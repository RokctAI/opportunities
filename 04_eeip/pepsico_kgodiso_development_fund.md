# EEIP Program: PepsiCo Kgodiso Development Fund

## Quick Stats
- **Multinational Company**: PepsiCo
- **Administrator / Fund Manager**: Kgodiso Development Fund (independent trust with its own Executive Director and Board of Trustees)
- **Application Status**: ONGOING
- **Target Audience**: Black-owned emerging farming enterprises and food-system SMMEs
- **Focus Area**: Enterprise Development / Agricultural Development / Localization
- **Investment / Funding Type**: Grants / Loans / Blended Finance
- **Region / Territory**: South Africa
- **Website**: https://kgodisofund.co.za/

## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: R600 million fund, including R300 million allocated to agricultural development for black-owned emerging farming enterprises; grant funding and preferential-rate loans via initiatives such as the Mzansi Black Business Accelerator (with the PepsiCo Foundation and FoodBev SETA).
- **Non-Financial Support**: Wraparound developmental support, portfolio management, technical assistance, training, and integration into PepsiCo/Pioneer Foods value chains.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Black-owned emerging farmers and black-owned SMMEs in the food value chain (agriculture, agro-processing, manufacturing, logistics, foodservice) in South Africa.

## Program Description
The Kgodiso Development Fund is PepsiCo's R600 million enterprise development fund, established as a public-interest commitment arising from PepsiCo's acquisition of Pioneer Foods. Note: it is an equity-equivalent-style enterprise development programme from a multinational, not a dtic-approved EEIP under the B-BBEE Codes. Run as an independent fund, it supports the growth and scaling of black-owned farming enterprises and SMMEs, promotes regenerative agriculture, and funds agro-processing and value-addition businesses. As of 2026 the fund remains active and continues to make investments across South Africa's food system.
