# EEIP Program: Automotive Industry Transformation Fund

## Quick Stats
- **Multinational Company**: AITF (Automotive Industry Transformation Fund)
- **Administrator / Fund Manager**: AITF Board / Edge Growth
- **Application Status**: ONGOING
- **Target Audience**: Black-owned automotive component manufacturers, suppliers, and dealerships
- **Focus Area**: Supplier Development / Localization / Job Creation
- **Investment / Funding Type**: Debt Financing / Grants / Supplier Development
- **Region / Territory**: South Africa
- **Website**: https://aitf.co.za/

## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: Access to business financing, capital expenditure funding for machinery, and grant-funded support services.
- **Non-Financial Support**: OEM supplier onboarding, market access, engineering and productivity diagnostics, and quality systems implementation.

## Eligibility Criteria
- 51% or more Black-owned automotive suppliers, commercial vehicle dealers, or panel beaters seeking integration with major car manufacturers (Toyota, BMW, VW, Mercedes-Benz, Nissan, Ford, Isuzu).

## Program Description
The AITF is a collective Equity Equivalent Investment Programme co-founded by seven major automotive manufacturers in South Africa. The fund aims to accelerate B-BBEE transformation within the automotive industry supply chain by financing and developing Black-owned auto-component suppliers and dealerships.
