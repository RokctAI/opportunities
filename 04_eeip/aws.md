## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: AWS cloud credits, technical resources, and business development support.
- **Non-Financial Support**: Access to global AWS partner networks, technical training, architectural reviews, and business mentorship.

## Eligibility Criteria
- 51% Black-owned ICT startups, software developers, or digital product businesses with high scalability.

## Program Description
The AWS Equity Equivalent Investment Programme is designed to accelerate the growth of Black-owned ICT small businesses. It provides high-potential businesses with AWS cloud credits, technical training, business mentorship, and access to the global AWS Partner Network.
