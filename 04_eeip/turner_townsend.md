- **Website**: https://www.turnerandtownsend.com/locations/south-africa/

## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: Enterprise development funding for black-owned SMMEs in the construction and built-environment sector (R19.5 million committed across the approved programme phases).
- **Non-Financial Support**: Mentorship of women-owned and black-owned SMMEs, professional skills transfer in quantity surveying and project management, and access to mainstream construction project pipelines.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Black-owned or women-owned small, micro and medium enterprises (SMMEs) operating in the construction, cost management, or built-environment professional services sector in South Africa.

## Program Description
Turner & Townsend, the UK-headquartered global construction consultancy, runs one of the longest-standing dtic-approved Equity Equivalent Investment Programmes in South Africa. First approved in 2011 and renewed in 2021 for a further 10-year period, the programme focuses on enterprise development in the construction sector, mentoring women-owned and black-owned SMMEs to gain access to markets and integrate into the mainstream built-environment economy. It is one of only two approved EEIPs in the construction sector recorded by the dtic.
