# EEIP Program: MSC Mediterranean Shipping Company SA EEIP

## Quick Stats
- **Multinational Company**: MSC (Mediterranean Shipping Company)
- **Administrator / Fund Manager**: MSC South Africa
- **Application Status**: ONGOING
- **Target Audience**: Black-owned maritime and logistics SMEs, and Black youth entering the maritime industry
- **Focus Area**: Enterprise Development / Skills Development
- **Investment / Funding Type**: Grants / Training / Supplier Development
- **Region / Territory**: South Africa
- **Website**: https://www.msc.com/

## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: R102 million committed over the programme period towards enterprise development of black-owned businesses in the maritime value chain.
- **Non-Financial Support**: Maritime skills development, including MSC's annual learnership programmes for unemployed youth in Durban, Johannesburg and Cape Town covering shipping documentation, cargo movement, customs processes and supply chain logistics.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Black-owned SMEs operating in the maritime, shipping, and logistics value chain in South Africa; Black youth for the skills development and learnership components.

## Program Description
MSC (Swiss-Italian, the world's largest container shipping line) received dtic approval in 2019 for a R102 million, 10-year Equity Equivalent Investment Programme focused on enterprise development and skills development in South Africa's maritime sector. It is the only dtic-approved EEIP in the maritime sector recorded to date, running to approximately 2029.
