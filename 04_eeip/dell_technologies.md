# EEIP Program: Dell Technologies Khulisa Academy & SMME Fund

## Quick Stats
- **Multinational Company**: Dell Technologies
- **Administrator / Fund Manager**: Dell / Khulisa Academy
- **Application Status**: ONGOING
- **Target Audience**: Black youth, ICT SMMEs, and high-performance computing startups
- **Focus Area**: Skills Development / Enterprise Development
- **Investment / Funding Type**: Incubation / Grants / Training
- **Region / Territory**: South Africa
- **Website**: https://www.dell.com/en-za

## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: Technology infrastructure grants, computing hardware, and operational enterprise grants.
- **Non-Financial Support**: Unemployed Black graduate development in high-performance computing, data science, and AI via Dell Khulisa Academy; incubation for technology startups.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Black-owned ICT businesses or unemployed Black youth graduates in engineering, science, or technology disciplines.

## Program Description
Dell's EEIP incorporates the Khulisa Academy, which provides advanced training in ICT, high-performance computing, and data science for unemployed Black graduates. Additionally, Dell supports enterprise development by providing technology infrastructure and funding to emerging Black-owned ICT businesses.
