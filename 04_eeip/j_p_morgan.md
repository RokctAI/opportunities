# EEIP Program: J.P. Morgan Abadali Fund

## Quick Stats
- **Multinational Company**: J.P. Morgan
- **Administrator / Fund Manager**: Edge Growth
- **Application Status**: ONGOING
- **Target Audience**: Black-owned businesses, entrepreneurs, and social enterprises in South Africa
- **Focus Area**: Enterprise Development / Funding / Job Creation
- **Investment / Funding Type**: Debt Financing / Grants
- **Region / Territory**: South Africa
- **Website**: https://www.jpmorgan.com/

## Program Benefits
- **Financial Support**: R300 million low-interest debt fund for SMEs and R40 million in grant funding for business development.
- **Non-Financial Support**: Edge Growth post-investment support, business diagnostic tools, and operational capacity building.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Black-owned or Black-managed small and medium enterprises (SMEs) with viable business models showing high growth and job-creation potential.

## Program Description
The Abadali Fund is a R340 million Equity Equivalent Investment Programme by J.P. Morgan, designed to support financial inclusion and entrepreneurship in South Africa. The fund consists of a R300 million debt fund managed by Edge Growth and a R40 million grant fund.
