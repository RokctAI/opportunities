### Tender Description
The National Arts Council (NAC) invites qualified service providers to submit quotations for the provision of an API-based Identity Verification Service to integrate with the Grants Management System (GMS). The purpose of the solution is to enable real-time verification of applicants, beneficiaries against Department of Home Affairs records to improve data integrity, reduce fraud and strengthen compliance processes.
