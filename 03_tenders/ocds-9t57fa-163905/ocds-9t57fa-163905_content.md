Tenders will be evaluated and awarded as follows:
Stage 1: Eligibility Criteria
• Attendance of the compulsory tender clarification meeting.
• Bidder must be an Authorized System Integrator of the Adroit SCADA software
• Bidders must furnish proof of qualification in the form of a training certificate
to work on the SCADA OPC driver interface utilised in the existing system. This
certificate must be issued in the name of the bidder OR in the name of the
proposed programmer who is currently employed by the bidder, if not then a
letter stating such intent to employ this person, including this person’s
signature of willingness & acceptance for the intended duration of the project.
Stage 2: Functionality
Only Tenders scoring a minimum of 70 out of 100 will be further evaluated in terms of
Stage 3: Price and Preference.
Stage 3: Price, Preference and Specific Goals
Tenders will be evaluated and adjudicated in terms of the Preferential Procurement Policy
Framework Act (Act 5 of 2000) Regulations 2022; the George Municipality’s Supply Chain
Management Policy as well as the George Municipality’s Preferential procurement Policy,
where 80 points will be scored for price and 20 points for B-BBEE status and specific
goals. Refer to the Tender Data for detailed breakdown of the evaluation process.
