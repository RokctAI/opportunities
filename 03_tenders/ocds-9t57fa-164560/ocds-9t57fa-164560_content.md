BACKGROUND (TERMS OF REFERENCE)\nThe South African National Energy Development Institute (SANEDI) is a statutory body\nestablished in terms of the National Energy Act No. 34 of 2008. The Act provides for SANEDI\nto direct, monitor, and conduct energy research and development, promote energy research\nand technology innovation, as well as undertake measures to promote energy efficiency\nthroughout the economy.\nThe Department of Science, Technology and Innovation (DSTI) established the Energy\nSecretariat at SANEDI in 2020, which created a platform that allows for both private and\npublic sectors to participate in the following energy related research, development and\ninnovation (RDI) Flagship Programmes supported by the DSTI:\n• Hydrogen South Africa (HySA);\n• Renewable Energy Hub and Spokes;\n• Coal CO2 to X; and\n• Energy Storage.\nThe Energy Secretariat is mandated to assist the DSTI in:\n• Monitoring the implementation of the Energy Science, Technology, and Innovation\n(STI) Plan, which is part of the Department’s Decadal Plan. The Energy STI plan\ntakes into consideration and builds on the HySA Strategy, Energy Storage RDI, Solar\nEnergy Technology Roadmap, Coal CO2 to X RDI Roadmap, and the Hydrogen\nSociety Roadmap (HSRM);\n• Monitoring the implementation of innovation policies relevant to the energy space\nthrough the four flagships (namely hydrogen economy, carbon dioxide captures and\nutilisation, renewable energy and energy storage);\n• Managing and monitoring large scale deployments in partnership with public, private\nsector and academia in support of the Presidential District Development Model\n(DDM), such as the Platinum Valley, clean energy deployments in partnership with\nlocal and district municipalities, as well as other government departments; and\n• Coordinating the training of Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET)\ngraduates and University of Technology (UoT) graduates on emerging technologies\n(i.e., fuel cells, batteries, renewables) in partnership with the relevant entities under\nthe Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET), such as the Energy &\nWater Sector Education Training Authority (EWSETA).\nThe DSTI developed the National Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Technologies (HFCT) Strategy\nthat was approved by Cabinet in 2007 to create knowledge, human resource capacity and\nenable the development of high-value commercial Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Technologies\nwhile adding value to the country's platinum group metal (PGM) resources. The Strategy\nwas formally launched by the DSTI in 2008 and branded HySA.\nOne of the key recommendations that came from an independent review HFCT\nStrategy/HySA emphasised the need for the Strategy to transition from niche to broader\napplications through a well-coordinated approach across government in order to achieve\naccelerated deployment and to support the RDI and commercial efforts of the HySA\nProgramme. Cabinet approved the HSRM, and its\nrelease to the public, on 14 September 2021.
