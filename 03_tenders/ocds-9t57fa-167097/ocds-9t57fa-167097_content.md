NAME OF THE PROJECT: APPOINTMENT OF A SERVICE PROVIDER FOR THE
PROVISION OF POUND MANAGEMENT SERVICES FOR A PERIOD OF THIRTY-SIX
(36) MONTHS
1. INTRODUCTION
The Greater Kokstad Municipality intends appointing a suitably qualified and experienced Service
Provider to provide comprehensive Pound Management Services for a period of thirty-six (36)
months.
The appointed Service Provider shall provide the services in accordance with the KwaZulu-Natal
Pound Act, 2006 (Act No. 3 of 2006), the Greater Kokstad Municipality Pound By-law (2019), the
Animal Protection Act, 1962 (where applicable), and any other relevant legislation governing the
humane treatment and management of animals.
1. PROJECT BACKGROUND
Greater Kokstad Municipality is responsible for managing stray and impounded animals within its
area of jurisdiction.
The Municipality requires a dedicated, competent and compassionate Service Provider who will
not only safely impound animals but also ensure that animals are treated humanely, receive
appropriate daily care, and are kept in a clean, safe and well-maintained facility throughout the
impoundment period.
The Service Provider will be responsible for the overall operation, cleanliness and maintenance of
the municipal pound.
1. AIM OF THE PROJECT
The objective of this appointment is to provide an efficient, humane and legally compliant pound
management service that promotes:
• Animal welfare;
• Public health and safety;
• Compliance with applicable legislation;
• Proper management and maintenance of the municipal pound.
1. SCOPE OF WORK / SPECIFICATIONS
The appointed Service Provider shall be responsible for the following:
Animal Collection and Impoundment
• Capture and safely remove stray, abandoned, dangerous, vicious or wandering animals.
• Respond to complaints received from the Municipality.
• Transport animals safely and humanely.
• Ensure animals are handled with minimum stress or injury.
25

Animal Care
The Service Provider shall:
• Provide sufficient clean drinking water at all times.
• Feed animals daily with appropriate food.
• Monitor the health and condition of every impounded animal.
• Arrange veterinary treatment where necessary.
• Immediately report sick, injured or deceased animals to the Municipality.
• Ensure humane handling of all animals.
Pound Management
The Service Provider shall:
• Operate and manage the Municipal Pound.
• Open and close the pound during approved operating hours.
• Ensure security of the facility.
• Prevent escapes.
• Ensure only authorised persons have access.
Facility Maintenance
The Service Provider shall maintain the Municipal Pound in a clean, hygienic and safe condition
by:
• Cleaning all kennels, pens and holding areas daily.
• Removing animal waste regularly.
• Maintaining grass and vegetation.
• Controlling odours.
• Maintaining fencing, gates and basic infrastructure.
• Keeping food storage areas clean.
• Maintaining drainage systems.
• Reporting maintenance requirements immediately.
The pound must remain clean, neat and presentable at all times.
Record Keeping
Maintain accurate records including:
• Date animal was impounded;
• Species;
• Breed;
• Colour;
• Sex;
26

• Identification marks;
• Location where captured;
• Date owner was notified;
• Release date;
• Disposal or adoption details;
• Veterinary treatment provided.
• Registers must be made available to the Municipality upon request.
The Service Provider shall comply with:
• KwaZulu-Natal Pound Act, 2006;
• Greater Kokstad Municipality Pound By-law;
• Animal Protection legislation;
• Occupational Health and Safety requirements;
• Municipal policies and procedures.
1. DELIVERABLES
The successful Service Provider shall provide:
• Comprehensive pound management services;
• Daily care of impounded animals;
• Daily cleaning of the facility;
• Monthly operational reports;
• Updated pound register;
• Incident reports;
• Veterinary treatment reports where applicable; service provider will be responsible to
conduct veterinary services for impounded animals annually.
• Monthly maintenance report identifying defects requiring municipal attention.
1. EXPECTED OUTCOMES
The Municipality expects:
• Humane treatment of all impounded animals.
• Improved cleanliness of the Municipal Pound.
• Well-maintained pound infrastructure.
• Accurate record keeping.
• Reduced public complaints.
27

• Compliance with all applicable legislation.
• Efficient response to animal-related complaints.
• Professional management of the Municipal Pound.
1. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS / KEY COMPETENCIES
The Service Provider shall demonstrate:
• Registration as a Company, Non-Profit Organisation (NPO), or Non-Governmental
Organisation (NGO).
• Proven experience in animal handling and pound management.
• Experience working with livestock and domestic animals.
• Knowledge of the KwaZulu-Natal Pound Act and municipal by-laws.
• Availability of suitably trained personnel.
• Ability to respond to emergencies.
• Ability to maintain accurate records.
• Commitment to animal welfare and humane treatment.
Preference may be given to Service Providers who can demonstrate previous experience in animal
welfare, rescue services or municipal pound management.
1. PERFORMANCE STANDARDS
The Service Provider shall:
• Treat all animals with care, dignity and compassion.
• Maintain the pound in a clean and hygienic condition at all times.
• Ensure no animal suffers unnecessary neglect.
• Feed and water animals daily.
• Attend to complaints promptly.
• Maintain professional conduct.
• Cooperate fully with municipal officials during inspections.
• Allow unannounced inspections by the Municipality.
• Failure to maintain acceptable standards may constitute breach of contract.
