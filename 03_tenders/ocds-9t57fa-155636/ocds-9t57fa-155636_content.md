2. SCOPE OF WORK
2.1 Background:
The National Radioactive Waste Disposal Institute (NRWDI) currently operates without
dedicated data centre facilities at its Operational Office in Vaalputs–Springbok. This absence
poses significant risks to the Institute’s information security, operational efficiency, service
continuity, and regulatory compliance.
To address these gaps, NRWDI seeks to procure professional services to design, build, and
commission a fully equipped mini data centres (approximately 6 square meters each) that will
support secure, efficient, and compliant IT operations across both sites.
The establishment of mini data centres at both offices ensures that NRWDI can maintain
resilient, secure, and compliant infrastructure that supports current operations, future digital
growth, and emerging AI/data-driven requirements.
2.2 Compliance With International Standards
The proposed data centres must be designed to meet key international IT governance and
security standards, including:
 ISO/IEC 27001 – Information Security Management - Ensures the protection of
confidentiality, integrity, and availability of NRWDI’s data through structured security controls.
 ISO 20000-1 – IT Service Management - Promotes consistent service quality, predictable
IT operations, and improved service delivery.
 ISO/IEC 42001 – Artificial Intelligence Management - Enables responsible AI governance,
risk management, and transparency as NRWDI increasingly adopts digital and AI-supported
systems.
 ISO 22301 – Business Continuity Management - Ensures resilient operations and rapid
recovery in the event of disruptions.
 ISO 9001 – Quality Management Systems - Supports consistent, repeatable processes and
continuous improvement across IT environments.
