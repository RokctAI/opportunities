TERMS OF REFERENCE (TOR)
Provision of Terrain Maintenance and Gardening Services for Onderstepoort
Biological Products (SOC) Ltd
1. Background
Onderstepoort Biological Products (SOC) Ltd (OBP) is a Schedule 3B National
Public Entity responsible for the development, manufacture, and distribution of
animal vaccines to support animal health and food security.
1. Purpose
OBP intends to appoint an experienced and financially stable service provider to
deliver comprehensive terrain maintenance, landscaping, and gardening services
across its facilities.
1. Scope of Services
The service provider will be responsible for maintaining:
• OBP Main Site, including perimeter fence areas.
• OBP Residential Houses and surrounding areas.
• Experimental Animal Facilities, including stables and paddocks.
• Stormwater drainage systems, channels, and inlets.
• Roads, paved areas, parking areas, lawns, flowerbeds, and veld grass
areas.
1. Key Responsibilities
The service provider shall:
• Provide a minimum of eight (8) gardeners and one (1) onsite supervisor
daily.
• Maintain approximately 364,574 m² of grounds and landscaped areas.
• De-bushing of Agricultural land estimated 310577 m2, inclusive of trees in
the designated area
• Mow lawns, trim edges, and maintain veld grass on a weekly basis.
• Remove weeds from fence lines, paved surfaces, and landscaped areas.
• Maintain flowerbeds, shrubs, hedges, and trees.
• Keep paved areas, roads, parking lots, drains, and gutters clean and free
of debris.
• Maintain fire breakers twice annually by means of a Motor Grader.
• Fire breaker in the inside of the OBP fence and OBP owned houses
(3021m X 6m X 0,1m) up to topsoil
• Remove and legally dispose of all gardening waste on the same day it is
generated.
• Ensure stable and paddock areas remain weed-free without the use of
herbicides.

Fire Breaker Route (Bi-annual)
De-bushing Area (Bi-annual)

1. Equipment and Consumables
The service provider shall supply and maintain all equipment, tools, consumables,
and PPE required for service delivery, including:
• Ride-on and petrol lawnmowers
• Brush cutters
• Leaf blowers
• Chainsaws
• Tractor and slasher
• Gardening hand tools
• Herbicides, pesticides, fertilizers, and seasonal plants
• Generator for electrical equipment where required
• Motor Grader for firebreak maintenance
The service provider shall ensure equipment is maintained and replace any faulty
equipment within 24 hours.
1. Service Standards
The service provider must:
• Maintain a clean, safe, and aesthetically pleasing environment.
• Ensure continuous service delivery throughout the contract period.
• Have sufficient financial and operational capacity to support uninterrupted
service provision.
• Comply with all health, safety, and environmental requirements.
