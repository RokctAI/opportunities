5.1 BACKGROUND AND WHY THE NEED TO SOURCE THE GOODS OR SERVICES
Johannesburg Water is mandated to monitor and ensure the quality of drinking water in compliance with the
South African National Standard (SANS 241), which includes the analysis of trihalomethanes. In addition,
Johannesburg Water is responsible for assessing wastewater treatment plant performance through the analysis
of organic compounds in both influent and effluent streams. The organisation is further required to investigate
potential surface water contamination as part of its industrial effluent monitoring programme.
These analytical requirements necessitate the use of a high-resolution Gas Chromatograph coupled with a Mass
Spectrometer (GCMS), which is capable of accurately detecting and quantifying a wide range of organic
constituents. Beyond routine compliance monitoring, such instrumentation also enables the analysis of
emerging contaminants, including antiretroviral drugs (ARVs), petrochemicals, polycyclic aromatic
hydrocarbons (PAHs), agrochemicals, and selected pharmaceutical compounds.
The laboratory’s existing GCMS instruments have surpassed their operational lifespan and are no longer
supported by the original equipment manufacturer, resulting in increased maintenance challenges and risks to
analytical reliability. To ensure continuity of operations, maintain compliance with regulatory standards, and
enhance analytical capabilities, Johannesburg Water requires the procurement and installation of new, modern
GCMS instrumentation.
5.2 CONTRACT DESCRIPTION
5.2.1 The successful tenderer for Commodity 1 will be required to supply, deliver, install, and
commission a single-quadrupole GC-MS system coupled to a purge-and-trap system at Cydna
Laboratory.
5.2.2 The successful tenderer for Commodity 2 will be required to supply, deliver, install, and
commission a time-of-flight (TOF) GC-MS system equipped with a liquid autosampler, also at
the Cydna Laboratory.
5.2.3 Both successful tenderers will be responsible for providing training for six (6) employees on
the operation of their respective instruments. In addition, each tenderer must provide annual
maintenance services for their supplied instrument for a period of 36 months.
5.2.4 The successful tenderer shall be responsible for disconnecting the current Agilent GCMS
systems prior to installation of the new equipment.
5.2.5 Each successful tenderer will conduct annual maintenance exclusively on the instrument they
have supplied for the full 36-month period.

1. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
• Contractor must issue their employees SABS approved PPE. A copy of the PPE issue register
signed by the employee issued with the PPE must be kept in the SHE file.
• Contractor supervisor are required to conduct continuous inspections of the PPE issued to their
employees to ensure that they are still in good condition to be used by the employee or they still
comply with manufacture requirements.
• The contractor is responsible for ensuring that employees are trained on the safe use of the PPE
issued to them, how to maintain it and the limitations of the PPE.
• NO SHORTS OR DRESSES WILL BE ALLOWED ON SITE
1. WORKPLACE SIGNAGE
• Appropriate symbolic signage must be displayed where it is required by legislation.
• Appropriate warning, mandatory and information signs must be placed where required.
• All signs must comply to SANS/SABS requirements.
• Contractors shall use mandatory and prescribed symbolic safety signs at their lay down and site
areas.
1. INCIDENT REPORTING AND INVESTIGATION
• All incidents shall be reported to the Client before the end of the shift or within 24hrs of
occurrence.
• Section 24 incidents shall be reported to DOL using the prescribed format.
• The contractor shall develop an incident management procedure and communicate with all
employees.
1. NOTIFICATION OF CONSTRUCTION WORK
• The contractor shall notify the DOL in the prescribed format of the intended work prior to work.
1. COMPLIANCE MONITORING
• Weekly inspections and monthly audits will be conducted on site.
1. PROJECT COMPLETION
• Upon completion of the project the SHE file shall be returned to the Client for retention and close
out
