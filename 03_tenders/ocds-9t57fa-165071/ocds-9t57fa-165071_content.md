1. Opening and Welcome
Velaphi Gumede welcomed attendees and confirmed that the meeting formed part of
the mandatory scope clarification process for the Saturation Dive System
Refurbishment Tender. Attendance at the briefing and subsequent site inspection was
confirmed as mandatory for participation in the tender.

Key Points Discussed
• Tender submissions must be made exclusively through the PetroSA
procurement portal. Email submissions will not be accepted.
• Tenderers were advised to regularly monitor the PetroSA portal for any tender
bulletins, amendments, or clarifications.
• Meeting minutes and any future communication will be circulated to
attendees.
• The original tender closing date was noted as 09 September 2026 at 13:00.
• PetroSA outlined its evaluation process:
o Pre-qualification stage.
o Technical/compliance assessment.
o Commercial evaluation.
o Due diligence.

would apply.
1. Scope of Work Presentation
Refurbishment Options
Tenderers were advised that two execution options are available:
1. Refurbishment of entire or partial systems in Mossel Bay.
2. Relocation of entire dive system from Mossel Bay to Cape Town Harbour then
to the contractor's workshop for refurbishment.

Logistics Support by PetroSA
• For Option 1 - PetroSA will provide lifting services in Mossel Bay harbour for
the few items the successful vendor intends to relocate to Cape Town by road
transport
• For Option 2 - PetroSA will lift and transport the entire dive system from Mossel
Bay to Cape Town Harbour using one of its vessels. The vessel to be docked
at a berth booked and paid for by the service provider. Lifting from the vessel
onto road transport from harbour to service provider yard is part of the service
providers responsibilities.
Major Scope Elements
The following activities were highlighted:
Chambers
• Repair of areas affected by wall-thickness loss.
• ABS has indicated that weld repairs will not be accepted.
• Affected sections must be cut out and replaced with new plate material.
Utilities and Hoses
• Inspection and refurbishment/replacement of hoses and utility lines.
• Review of threaded chamber connection points and rectification where
required.
Winch and Hydraulic System
• Complete inspection and refurbishment of hydraulic systems.
• Assume PetroSA to free issue winch new winch wires
• Hydraulic cylinder for band breaks to be replaced.
• Electrical cables between the winch deck and the various system to be
megger-ed and only replaced if failed insulation test.
Procurement of Replacement Parts
• PetroSA expressed preference for contractors to procure replacement
materials and components directly at a cost plus percentage mark-up basis.
• PetroSA to compile and share with bidders defects list or items that must be
assumed for complete replacement.

1. Installation and Integration Requirements
Following refurbishment, the successful contractor will be required to:
• Install the system onto the selected vessel.
• Integrate all equipment and utilities.
• Conduct commissioning and testing.
• Design, fabricate and install any required interface platform.
• Liaise with vessel classification authorities regarding all the design and
installation on the vessel.
• Complete stability assessments and certification requirements.
• Employ the services of marine architecture for the needed services including
vessel stability analysis for the final dive system layout.
The vessel onto which the system will ultimately be installed has not yet been
procured. PetroSA expects the vessel procurement process to be concluded while
refurbishment activities are underway. Upon securing the vessel PetroSA shall share
its details. In the meantime bidders to quote PetroSA based on the previous system
layout and vessel details.
1. Technical Questions and Responses
Documentation
PetroSA confirmed that:
• Historical installation information will be shared where available.
• Previous ABS survey reports and IMCA audit information will be provided to
assist tenderers.
Regulatory Compliance
The refurbished system must comply with:
• ABS requirements.
• IMCA requirements.
• Relevant IMO regulations.
• Applicable South African regulatory requirements.
Vessel Integration
• PetroSA indicated preference for a vessel with a moon pool arrangement.

• Previous vessel layouts and information may be shared to assist tenderers
with proposals.
Testing
• Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT) may be performed during refurbishment.
• Full integration and operational testing will be conducted after installation on
the vessel.
1. Commercial Clarifications
Payment Terms
• Tenderers may propose milestone-based payment structures.
• Advance payment requests may be included in proposals.
• Payment terms will form part of commercial negotiations and evaluation.
Cost Plus Arrangements
• Known refurbishment requirements should be priced in the tender submission.
• Unknown defects identified during execution may be managed through a cost-
plus mechanism.
• Tenderers were advised to clearly state cost-plus provisions in their proposals.
• PetroSA to compile and share with bidders defects list or items that must be
assumed for complete replacement.
Tender Submission Documentation
Tenderers were requested to:
• Clearly label all supporting documents.
• Avoid submitting large, unstructured files.
• Organize technical and commercial information in a logical and easily
reviewable format.
