This document serves as a bid specification for the appointment of a panel of suppliers for the supply and delivery of

1. BACKGROUND INFORMATION
The KwaZulu Natal Region has over 2000 km of perway track where OHTE infrastructure is installed and requires
to be maintained by internal staff from the Electrical Department. Staff require materials that will part of Warehouse
Inventory to be replenished as and when the minimum levels are reached. This panel of suppliers will assist with
ensuring compliance with RSR provisions, regulatory requirements within the Region of PRASA KZN in ensuring
that material is available to keep the infrastructure in working order.
2.1 STATUS QUO
Currently these materials are sourced as and when required but the poor performance of contracted suppliers is
impacting on compliance.
2.2 PROBLEM STATEMENT
The operating license model was changed from where the operating license was issued to PRASA as an
organization to where the operating license was issued per Region, with that change it means each Region is now
responsible of ensuring compliance with RSR requirements to maintain the operating license. One of the operating
license requirements is ensuring maintenance of infrastructure through service providers and also some
maintenance is done by internally qualified staff. It for this reason that inventory material needs to be available easy
at the Warehouse where it can be easily drawn when needed during awkward hours of the day for maintenance
purposes.
