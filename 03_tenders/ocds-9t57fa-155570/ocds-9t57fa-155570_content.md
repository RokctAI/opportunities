B. REQUEST FOR QUOTATION FOR SNAKE TRAINING
1. BACKGROUND TO SANSA
The South African National Space Agency (SANSA) has a mandate, as outlined in the South
African National Space Agency Act, 2008 (Act No 36 of 2008), to co-ordinate and integrate
national space science and technology programmes and conduct long-term planning and
implementation of space-related activities in South Africa, for the benefit of the citizens of
South Africa
2. SCOPE OF WORK (TERMS OF REFERENCE)
SANSA requires a service provider to provide the Certificate in Preventive and Predictive
Maintenance (CPPM) training for 6 employees who are based in SANSA Space Operations
Farm No 502JQ in Hartebeesthoek.
The course content should include, but not limited to:
a. Maintenance overview
b. Common issues in an organization
c. Introduction to asset management
d. Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) set-up
e. Work identification and requesting
f. Work planning and estimating
g. Work scheduling and execution
h. Completion and work control:
i. Data analysis techniques
j. Maintenance replacement decisions
k. Downtime reduction
l. Performance reporting
m. Root Cause Analysis
n. Action plan
NB:
The facilitator must have the training certificates and accreditations to facilitate this course
and have at least 3 years of experience. The facilitator must be a South African by
naturalisation. The Facilitator’s ID copy, CV/profile, qualifications, accreditations, course
overview, and manual sample must be attached to the standard bidding documents.
The training must be carried out over three days.
The theory and practical training should be carried out at the SANSA Space Operations
facilities in Haartebeesthoek (Farm No 502JQ in Hartebeesthoek). The attendees must
receive certificates of competence.
