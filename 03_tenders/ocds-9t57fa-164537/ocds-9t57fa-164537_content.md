SCOPE OF WORK / SPECIFICATIONS\nBackground\nThe Playhouse Company hires out various event venues, rehearsal venues and recording studio to members of the public\non a fixed hourly and daily rate. The current booking process relies on manual bookings, payment verification and schedule\nupdates, creating risks associated with double-bookings, booking errors and bookings being confirmed before payment is\nreceived. The Playhouse Company seeks to modernise this process through an integrated online booking and payment\nsolution.\nVENUE PRICES – FIXED RATES\nTHEATRES DAILY RATE\nOpera R 27 000.00\nDrama R 15 000.00\nLoft R 6 500.00\nFUNCTION VENUES DAILY RATE\nGrand Foyer R 27 000.00\nAlhambra R 14 000.00\nVIP Room R 4 000.00\nTudor Room R 28 000.00\nREHEARSAL VENUES HOURY RATES\nRoom 503 R 390.00\nRoom 506 R 110.00\nRoom 507 R 110.00\nRoom 508 R 110.00\nRoom 410 R 340.00\nRoom A1 R 260.00\nRECORDING STUDIO HOURY RATES\nStudio -3 R 650.00\n\nScope\n1. System Configuration and Implementation\nThe service provider shall:\n• Supply and configure a cloud-based booking platform.\n• Configure all Playhouse venues on the system.\n• Implement user roles and administrative controls.\n• Ensure the system is fully operational and accessible through web browsers and mobile devices.\n1. Online Venue Booking Functionality\nThe proposed solution must:\n• Allow customers to view available venues.\n• Display venue images and descriptions.\n• Display venue rates per hour/day.\n• Allow customers to select venues, dates and timeslots.\n• Prevent double-bookings.\n• Automatically calculate booking charges.\n• Provide a shopping cart, confirmation and checkout process.\n• Generate booking confirmations automatically.\n1. Playhouse Website Integration\nThe proposed solution must integrate with The Playhouse Company website to allow customers to access venue\ninformation, availability, booking functionality and secure payment processing directly from the website or through a\nseamless website-linked booking portal.\n1. Online Payment Functionality\nThe solution must integrate with third-party payment gateways including, but not limited to:\n• PayFast\n• Yoco\n• Paystack\n• iKhokha\n• Automatically confirm bookings upon successful payment.\n• Generate payment confirmations and receipts.\n• Maintain a secure audit trail of all transactions.\n1. Scheduling and Calendar Integration\nThe solution must:\n• Provide a central booking calendar.\n• Synchronise or integrate with Microsoft Outlook calendars.\n• Automatically update venue schedules upon booking confirmation.\n• Provide daily, weekly and monthly booking views.\n• Allow authorised staff to export schedules.\n1. Reporting\nThe system must provide reports including:\n• Bookings by venue.\n• Revenue generated by venue.\n• Customer booking history.\n• Cancelled bookings.\n• Outstanding payments.\n• Venue utilisation statistics.\n2\nREQUEST FOR PROPOSALS IN TERMS OF PPR 2022\n\n1.
