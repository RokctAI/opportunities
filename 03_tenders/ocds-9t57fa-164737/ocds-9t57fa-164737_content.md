General description\nKendal Power Station is situated approximately 40km South West of Witbank in the Mpumalanga province.\nConstruction started in 1982 and took 11 years to complete.\nKendal Power Station comprises six generating sets (Units) of 686 MW each, the station capacity is 4116\nMW, which is indirectly dry-cooled. The generators produces electricity at a voltage of 22KV, the generator\ntransformers steps up the transmission voltage to 400kV.\nKendal Power Station receives its coal supply of over a million tons per month from a neighbouring mine\nKhutala as well as other sources with varying characteristics. The ash that remains after the combustion of\ncoal has two grading i.e. coarse ash (5% of total ash) and fly ash (95% of total ash). The fly ash together\nwith waste gasses passes through the electrostatic precipitators where 99.9% of the ash is collected. The\ncoarse ash is removed by a submerged scraper conveyor to the apron conveyor. The fly ash is moistened\nand mixed in the ash conditioners. Both gratings of ash are transported via the overland conveyors to the\nash dump, where it is spread by an ash spreader, levelled and covered with topsoil and finally regressed.\nThe maximum continuous rating of each boiler at the turbine stop valves is 577kg/s with superheated steam\ntemperature and pressure of 540 degrees Celsius and 17,24MPa respectively.\nThe water usage at Kendal Power Station is minimal (0.1 litre per kilowatt-hour). The water comes from the\nVaal and Usutu Water Schemes alternatively. The WTP produces potable and demineralised water,\nconducts water balancing and effluent management, chemistry control and monitoring of all process water.\nThe CP plants, one at each unit, are used for the polishing of condensate water on the units while the CP\nRegeneration plant does the transfers and regeneration of the Unit CP resin and is located in the Water\nTreatment Plant.\nThe power station had a DIIR of 0.2 in 2005 but is continuously striving towards a DIIR of 0 which has been\nachieved on 11 August 2006. Kendal Power Station has been graded as a NOSA Integrated Platinum Five\nStar facility in June 2006.\nThe power station is a Zero Liquid Effluent Discharge Station and ISO 14001 compliant.\n1.1 Contractor's yard\nShould the Contractor qualify for a site, the Employer will provide a site within the premises of the\nPower Station for the Contractor to establish himself for the execution of the works.
