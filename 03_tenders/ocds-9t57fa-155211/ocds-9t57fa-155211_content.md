SCOPE OF WORK
The successful vendor will be responsible for the following:
 Training delivery: Provide physical training sessions to employees. The sessions
should cater to different skill levels and focus on the following areas:
o Basic user training (e.g., Outlook & calendar management, Word, Excel,
PowerPoint, OneDrive, Teams, Sharepoint)
o Intermediate user training (e.g., basic Excel, Teams collaboration tools,
Planner, OneNote, Mobile Integration)
o Security awareness and best practices (e.g., phishing awareness, multi-
factor authentication, securing data in Teams and SharePoint)
 Design and customisation of training materials: Develop and customise training
content to align with Greater Taung Local Municipality’s specific business use case.
The content should include practical, scenario-based examples relevant to the
organisation’s operations.
 Training format: The vendor should provide training in the following formats, as
required:
 Live, instructor-led onsite sessions.
 Documentation and guides for reference
 Training assessment: Develop assessments or surveys to evaluate the
effectiveness of the training and identify any gaps or additional training needs.
TRAINING APPROACH
The vendor should propose a comprehensive approach to training that includes the
following elements:
 Needs assessment: Prior to training delivery, conduct a thorough needs
assessment to understand the current proficiency levels and specific business
needs of employees.
 User-centric content: The content should be tailored to users' specific job roles
and their level of familiarity with Ms O365. The training should be practical and
engage employees with real-life examples that are directly applicable to their daily
work.
 Engagement and interactivity: The training should not be limited to passive
content delivery. It should include interactive elements such as quizzes, polls, group
discussions, and hands-on exercises.
 Blended learning model: Use a mix of synchronous and asynchronous learning
methods to accommodate different learning preferences and schedules.

TIMELINE
 Kick-off and planning phase: The vendor will begin with a planning phase to
customise training content based on Greater Taung Local Municipality requirements
and user needs.
 Delivery of training sessions: The training should be completed within a time
frame of 06 Day; Group 1 – 02 days. Group 2 – 02 days, Group 3 – 02 Days.
Specific training timelines can be provided once the needs assessment is
conducted.
TRAINING DELIVERABLES
The following deliverables are expected:
 Customised training materials for various Ms O365 tools and features.
 A list of recommended best practices and FAQs tailored to Greater Taung Local
Municipality’s use of Ms O365
 Cybersecurity awareness training covering phishing and social engineering
awareness, Password management & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), safe use
of Microsoft 365 tools, data protection, ransomware and malware awareness.
 Attendance certificates for participants who successfully complete training.
VENDOR QUALIFICATIONS
The vendor should have the following qualifications:
 Proven expertise and experience in delivering Microsoft 365 training at an
enterprise level.
 Knowledge of Ms O365 features, functionalities, and best practices
 Experience in customising training content based on organisational needs.
 Ability to deliver engaging, interactive, and effective training to employees with
varying levels of technical proficiency.
 Familiarity with the security and compliance features of Microsoft 365.

1. General Conditions
1.1. Preferential Procurement Regulations, 2011 (Regulation 9) makes provision for the
promotion of local production and content.
1.2. Regulation 9.(1) prescribes that in the case of designated sectors, where in the award of
bids local production and content is of critical importance, such bids must be advertised
with the specific bidding condition that only locally produced goods, services or works or
locally manufactured goods, with a stipulated minimum threshold for local production and
content will be considered.
1.3. Where necessary, for bids referred to in paragraph 1.2 above, a two stage bidding process
may be followed, where the first stage involves a minimum threshold for local production
and content and the second stage price and B-BBEE.
1.4. A person awarded a contract in relation to a designated sector, may not sub-contract in
such a manner that the local production and content of the overall value of the contract is
reduced to below the stipulated minimum threshold.
1.5. The local content (LC) expressed as a percentage of the bid price must be calculated in
accordance with the SABS approved technical specification number SATS 1286: 2011 as
follows:
LC = [1- x / y] *100
Where
x is the imported content in Rand
y is the bid price in Rand excluding value added tax (VAT)
Prices referred to in the determination of x must be converted to Rand (ZAR) by using the
exchange rate published by the South African Reserve Bank (SARB) at 12:00 on the date
of advertisement of the bid as required in paragraph 4.1 below.
The SABS approved technical specification number SATS 1286:2011 is accessible on
http://www.thedti.gov.za/industrial development/ip.jsp at no cost.
1.6. A bid may be disqualified if –
(a) this Declaration Certificate and the Annex C (Local Content Declaration: Summary
Schedule) are not submitted as part of the bid documentation; and

(b) the bidder fails to declare that the Local Content Declaration Templates (Annex C, D
and E) have been audited and certified as correct.
2. Definitions
2.1. “bid” includes written price quotations, advertised competitive bids or proposals;
2.2. “bid price” price offered by the bidder, excluding value added tax (VAT);
2.3. “contract” means the agreement that results from the acceptance of a bid by an organ of
state;
2.4. “designated sector” means a sector, sub-sector or industry that has been designated by
the Department of Trade and Industry in line with national development and industrial
policies for local production, where only locally produced services, works or goods or
locally manufactured goods meet the stipulated minimum threshold for local production
and content;
2.5. “duly sign”means a Declaration Certificate for Local Content that has been signed by the
Chief Financial Officer or other legally responsible person nominated in writing by the Chief
Executive, or senior member / person with management responsibility(close corporation,
partnership or individual).
2.6. “imported content” means that portion of the bid price represented by the cost of
components, parts or materials which have been or are still to be imported (whether by the
supplier or its subcontractors) and which costs are inclusive of the costs abroad (this
includes labour and intellectual property costs), plus freight and other direct importation
costs, such as landing costs, dock duties, import duty, sales duty or other similar tax or
duty at the South African port of entry;
2.7. “local content” means that portion of the bid price which is not included in the imported
content, provided that local manufacture does take place;
2.8. “stipulated minimum threshold” means that portion of local production and content as
determined by the Department of Trade and Industry; and
2.9. “sub-contract” means the primary contractor’s assigning, leasing, making out work to, or
employing another person to support such primary contractor in the execution of part of a
