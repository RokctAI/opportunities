REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
DUBE TRADEPORT CORPORATION
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS FROM COMPETENT CONTRACTORS FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF
AGRIZONE 1 FLUSHLINE RISING MAIN PIPE FROM FLUSH POND D TO THE HOLDING POND
REF NO.: DTP/RFP/04/IFR/01/2026
Dube TradePort Corporation (DTPC) was created as a provincial public entity to develop Dube
Trade Port. Refer to www.dubetradeport.co.za for more information.
DTPC hereby invites proposals for the appointment of a contractor for the construction of AgriZone
1 flushline rising main from flush pond D to the holding pond.
The work includes, but is not limited to, the following:
 Construction of a flushline rising main from flush pond D to the holding pond.
 Include necessary mechanical and electrical components such as pumps, valves, and air
release mechanisms to ensure uninterrupted operation of the rising main.
It is estimated that tenderers must have an active CIDB grading of 5CE or higher by the closing
date of the bid. No Potential Emerging (PE) status is allowed.
EVALUATION OF THIS BID
All proposals will be evaluated in various stages – compliance and functionality will be assessed
and thereafter all responsive proposals will be evaluated in accordance with the PPPFA
regulations, 2022 using the 80/20 preference points system.
The 20 preference points will be allocated to the following as included in the bid document:
 10 Points will be allocated to enterprises who are 51% Black Owned.
 10 Points will be allocated to enterprises who are located in KwaZulu-Natal.
REQUESTS FOR DOCUMENTS & ENQUIRIES
Bid documents will only be available via email from 10 JULY 2026 up to 16 JULY 2026 during
the weekdays (Mon – Fri) between 08h30 and 16h00. A non-refundable document fee of R500.00
(Five Hundred rands) must be paid via EFT and must be emailed in order for the Bid Documents
to be sent via email. No cash will be accepted and proof of payment must be emailed to
tenders@dubetradeport.co.za Please allow 2 days for funds to reflect in the account. DTPC will
