2 PPECB Background
The PPECB is a Schedule 3A national public entity that is constituted and mandated in terms of the PPEC Act,
No. 9, of 1983 to perform cold chain services. The PPECB also delivers inspection and food safety services
as mandated by the Department of Agriculture under the APS Act, No.119 of 1990.
The PPECB’s Executive Authority is the Minister of the Department of Agriculture who appoints the board
members. The board comprises of representatives from the perishable product industries.
The PPECB employs ± 1 200 people, who deal with more than 200 products and 500 varieties. There are
more than 50 service types, over 30 offices in 13 production regions, at more than 1,500 locations. A large
percentage of staff are inspectors and therefore not office bound. In addition to these offices the PPECB also
has several sub offices that operate on a seasonal or ad-hoc basis.
The PPECB, mandated by the Minister of the has been delivering end-point inspection services on perishable
products destined for export since 1991. Inspectors stationed across the country; deliver inspection services
on 200 product types at more than 1500 locations.
The PPECB is responsible for South Africa's cold chain management and ensures that products for export are
handled, stored and transported at specific temperatures and optimum conditions.
Please visit the PPECB’s website on www.ppecb.com for more information on the PPECB.

4 TERMS OF REFERENCE
4.1 Scope of Service
Background and Purpose
In terms of the requirements of the Auditor-General of South Africa (AGSA), the PPECB is required to appoint
an external audit firm to perform the annual external audit of its financial statements. The appointment of the
external auditor is subject to the approval and annual confirmation of the Auditor-General of South Africa.
Accordingly, the PPECB invites suitably qualified and experienced audit firms to submit proposals for the
provision of External Audit Services in accordance with these Terms of Reference.
These Terms of Reference set out the scope of work and deliverables for the provision of External Audit
Services to the Perishable Products Export Control Board (PPECB) in terms of section 53 of the Public Finance
Management Act, 1999 (Act No. 1 of 1999) (PFMA), as required by the Office of the Auditor-General of South
Africa.
The contract will cover the external audit of the PPECB's annual financial statements for a five-year period,
commencing with the 2026/27 financial year (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027) and ending with the 2030/31
financial year (1 April 2030 to 31 March 2031). The contract shall be effective from 01 April 2027 to 31 March
2032, subject to the terms and conditions of the contract and satisfactory performance.
The duration of the appointment shall be subject to annual performance assessments and annual confirmation
by the Auditor-General of South Africa. In accordance with the recommendations of the Office of the Auditor-
General of South Africa, the appointment shall not be renewed beyond a maximum period of five (5) years.
PPECB utilises several business systems to support its operations, including Microsoft Dynamics 365 (D365),
Oracle, CRS, TITAN, and Navision. As part of the audit scope, the appointed service provider will be required
to evaluate the adequacy and effectiveness of systems controls within these platforms, including access
management, segregation of duties, data integrity, workflow approvals, change management, and compliance
with relevant policies and regulatory requirements.
TITAN 2.0® is a next-generation system that connects people, processes, and compliance in a single,
integrated portal. It provides a central hub for managing inspection and operational services efficiently, with
real-time visibility across the export value chain.
The appointed service provider shall be required to:
a) Conduct the external audit in accordance with the Public Audit Act, 2004 (Act No. 25 of 2004), any General
Notices issued in terms thereof, and the International Standards on Auditing (ISAs);
Page 7 of 19

b) In accordance with the Public Audit Act, 2004, and any General Notices issued in terms thereof, report on
findings relating to performance against predetermined objectives, compliance with applicable legislation and
regulations, and internal control deficiencies, without expressing an audit opinion thereon;
c) Communicate all significant audit findings and matters arising from the audit to management and the Audit
Committee to ensure that they are fully apprised of the implications for the PPECB's operations and
governance processes;
d) Submit a final audit report, together with recommendations for improvement, to management and the Audit
Committee;
e) Attend all scheduled Audit Committee meetings and, where required, provide input on matters relating to
the external audit;
f) Liaise with the Auditor-General of South Africa and comply with any directives, requirements, or reporting
obligations issued by the Auditor-General in relation to the audit engagement.
The contract shall commence on 1 April 2027. The PPECB reserves the right to terminate the contract, without
penalty, should the successful bidder fail to comply with or fulfil any of the terms and conditions of the contract
or if AGSA does not approve the appointment in subsequent financial years.
The appointment of the external audit firm shall be for a maximum period of five (5) years and shall be subject
to annual renewal based on:
a) A satisfactory evaluation by the PPECB Audit Committee of the effectiveness, independence, and objectivity
of the External Auditors; and
b) Annual consultation with and approval by the Office of the Auditor-General of South Africa (AGSA) prior to
the confirmation of the appointment for the relevant financial year.
The service provider must meet the following mandatory competency requirements as Pre-qualification:
• Are independent from the PPECB and its directors and able to maintain its independence throughout
the engagement - Service Provider - to submit a detailed statement.
• Must be registered as a public auditor in public practice with the Independent Regulatory Board of
Auditors (IRBA). The firm must provide evidence of a satisfactory result on their latest IRBA review
results for the firm and for the engagement partner.
After fulfilling the mandatory criteria as set out above for the external audit services, the short-listed firms will
be evaluated in terms of the following criteria:
• Professional Competence and Integrity – The firm should be professional and competent in the
practice of external audit (attest function) as well as have high ethical standards.
• Relevant Experience: Have at least three years’ experience in conducting government or similar
audits. A firm which is familiar with the regulatory environment under which the PPECB operates
will be preferred. Knowledge of and exposure to the PFMA and Treasury Regulations are non-
negotiable as AGSA has specific requirements that auditors must cover.
• Relevant Engagement Partner Experience: Public Audit experience of at least three years is
required.
• Independence and Objectivity: No conflict of interest must exist between partners and staff members
of the firm and Board and staff members of the PPECB. Any firm which is currently performing
advisory services to the PPECB is also not eligible. A detailed statement on the independence of the
Bidder and the proposed External Audit Team is required.
• Track record and Reputation: Preference will be given to a firm that has demonstrated technical
ability, a good track record and reputation in the industry.
• Capacity to conduct audit at the PPECB: Regard will be given to the availability of staff to be assigned
to the PPECB at the time of the audit, which is typically around January to February for planning and
April to May for final audit.
• The successful service provider and the appointed engagement partner shall be based in Cape Town
for the duration of the audit. Any travel and accommodation costs incurred in the performance of the
services shall be for the service provider's own account and shall be deemed to be included in the
bid price.
• The successful service provider must ensure that any proposed resource will be replaced with
someone of similar skill, experience and qualification should the proposed resource no longer be
available at the time of contracting.
• Size of firm: A firm with a minimum of three partners, who must be members of SAICA.
