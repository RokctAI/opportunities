PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS
SUPPLY AND DELIVERY OF DEWATERING PUMPS FOR THE OPERATIONS EASTERN
1. Introduction
The tender enquiry calls for the supply of dewatering pumps for general use at dams in the Operations
Eastern. The pump shall be portable, self-priming, submersible non clog dewatering vertical type
pump. This shall be a compact unit, fitted with single impeller at the bottom and sealed by means of
double mechanical seal arrangement. This shall be specially wound with PVC insulated special
winding wire for suitability of underwater operation.
The construction of the pump is - pump below and motor above) and suction inlet is at the bottom so
that water is pumped from the lowest level and discharge outlet is at top. The pump shall be provided
with a strainer.
The motor is protected against the contamination from surrounding water by double mechanical seal
arrangement on the shaft and a double cable entry seal. All parts are made from specially selected
material to withstand against high abrasion and corrosive fluid.
The medium pumped will be seepage water in the gallery. The water temperature is expected to be
close to room temperature with specific gravity of one.
1. Scope of work
The project includes following:
• The supply and delivery off five (5), 0.55KW submersible pumps.
• The supply and delivery off five (5), 0.75KW submersible pumps.
• The supply and delivery off five (5), 5.6 kW submersible pumps.
2.1 Supply and delivery of submersible pumps
The pump shall be portable, self-priming, submersible non clog dewatering vertical type pump. This
shall be a compact unit, fitted with single impeller at the bottom and sealed by means of double
mechanical seal arrangement. This shall be specially wound with PVC insulated special winding wire
for suitability of underwater operation.
The construction of the pump is - pump below and motor above and suction inlet is at the bottom so
that water is pumped from the lowest level. The motor is protected against the contamination from
surrounding water by double mechanical seal arrangement on the shaft and a double cable entry seal.
All parts are made from specially selected material to withstand against high abrasion and corrosive
fluid. The medium pumped will be seepage water in the dams with some suspended silt. The water
temperature is expected to be close to room temperature with specific gravity of one. The pumps shall
be provided with a float switch.
