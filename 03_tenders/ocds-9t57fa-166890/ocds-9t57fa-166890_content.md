1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this project is to appoint suitable service provider to supply and maintain multifunction printers for
a period of 36 months
1. Background
JoGEDA’s primary objective in issuing this bid is to enter into agreement with a successful bidder who will achieve
the following:
- Provide JoGEDA with a suitable service provider that will supply & maintain multifunctional printing machines,
that is reliable and will maintain a high level of satisfaction in line with the service levels;
- Provide JoGEDA with a dedicated key contact person that is suitably qualified for the duration of the contract.
1. Scope of Printing Solution Equipment
Must be demonstrated on the bid provided. All the requirements below are compulsory.
• Device: Multifunction network Printer/ Scanner/ Printer
• Security feature: User Authentication\ ID & Print
• The printers should be capable of doing colour and monochrome printing
• The printers should provide USB port and secured wireless connectivity
• The print speed should be a minimum 60 pages per minute
• The Internal Hard Disk drive should be a minimum of 250GB or more
• Minimum 1GB RAM
• The main unit must have multiple feeder drawers and be able to accommodate multiple types and sizes of
paper as part of the feeder (A4 and A3)
• The printers must have an automated document feeder
• The printers must be capable of network printing, scan to mail/network and colour scanning
• The finishing of the printers must be able to produce a staple/stacker/punching and page numbering
• Estimated number of people connected to the machine should be 30
• The printers must be able to provide security relevant chronological records such as audit trail and other reports
• Black & White and colour copies
• Minimum Resolution: 1800 dpi (printing) 600 dpi (scanning)
• Network printer interface
• Multi-tray/stapler/punch finisher/ Bypass tray
• Colour management (user defined)
• Booklet creation
• Two holes punching
• Automatic reverse document feeder
• Duplex standard
• Network scanning
• User Codes
• Touch Screens
• Service & Maintenance Plan
• Technical assistance response time
- Adhoc Support: 24 hours
- Consumable delivery: Next business day response
