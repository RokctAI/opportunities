CONTEXT OF THIS PROCUREMENT
The SAEON seeks to appoint a service provider to connect the electricity of a freestanding instrument
housing unit (container office) to the electricity distribution board of the main reception building at the
MRC/Wits Agincourt Field Office (Mpumalanga).
CONTRACT PERIOD
Once off contract.
DESCRIPTION OF GOODS/DETAILED SPECIFICATION
The Expanded Freshwater and Terrestrial Observation Network (EFTEON) requires cables and circuit
breaker and steel electrical conduit, a connection to the electricity distribution board, and an electricity
certificate of compliance for a container standing loose from the main building at the MRC/Wits Agincourt
Field Office.
• 100 m x 10mm Twin-and Earth (T&E) cable suitable for medium-duty, high-current applications
(60Amp circuit breaker) and 20 mm diameter and 100 m length steel electrical conduit
• Installation of cable and connection to the mains electrical distribution board at MRC/Wits Agincourt
Field Office

Installation:
Electrical connection: Connect the primary cable to the main power supply
Conduit installation: Route the cable through a 20 mm diameter steel electrical conduit around the building
perimeter
Excavation: Excavate a trench measuring 300 mm wide, 500 mm deep and 100 meters long from the main
building to the air quality container
Voltage drop: The service provider should maintain a total voltage drop of less than 5% from the main
power supply to the container (Please show your calculation upon job completion)
Circuit breaker: Connect the cable to the circuit breaker in the air quality container
Completion: Backfill the trench and mechanically compact the soil to ensure stability
