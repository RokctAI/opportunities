(EOI No. 8129/25/08/2026)
Expression of Interest (EOI) for the Appointment for a Panel of Service Providers for
the Provision of services for the development and implementation of Integrated
Management Systems (IMS) (ISO9001:2015, ISO14001:2026 and ISO45001:2018) for
the companies supported by the CSIR for a period of 4 years
On Friday, 07 August 2026, the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) invited bidders to submit
proposals for the above-mentioned EOI, closing date Tuesday, 25 August 2026.
The CSIR appreciates your interest in responding to the Request for Proposal.
Please note the following amendment in the advertised tender document:
1. Inclusion of Express Term:
Express Term:
Successful bidders responding to this EOI will be appointed in a consulting and implementation capacity for the
development, establishment, and implementation of Integrated Management Systems (IMS). In accordance with
the principles of auditor independence, objectivity, and impartiality as contemplated in ISO 19011 (Guidelines
for Auditing Management Systems), any bidder, consortium member, subcontractor, or personnel involved in
the development, implementation, maintenance, or support of a management system for a beneficiary company
shall not be eligible to perform, participate in, or be appointed to undertake any audit, verification, assurance,
certification, or independent assessment activities relating to that same management system. This restriction is
intended to preserve the integrity and impartiality of subsequent conformity assessment activities and to avoid
any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest.
The closing/submission date for the tender is amended to Tuesday, 01 September 2026, at 16h30.
The CSIR wishes to encourage you to submit a response to this EOI, and to apologise for any inconveniences
caused because of this erratum.
Enquiries may be directed to tender@csir.co.za (Please use EOI number as subject reference)
