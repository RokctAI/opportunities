1. Scope of work
An evaporation pond was commissioned for the disposal of uranium-contaminated sludge. Available records
indicate that sludge was discharged to the pond between 1991 and 2017. Based on the available design
drawings, it is understood that approximately 200 mm of sand was placed over the pond liner prior to the
commencement of sludge disposal. The pond has a reported surface area of approximately 8 560 m².
Waste Management requires suitably qualified and experienced service providers to undertake a bathymetric
survey of the evaporation pond. The objective of the survey is to quantify the water and sediment profiles and
determine the following:
• The depth and volume of the water column, measured from the water surface to the top of the
sediment layer across the surface area of the pond.
• A bathymetric profile of the pond illustrating the spatial distribution and thickness of the sediment
across the pond. The profile should use the pond liner as a reference point.
• Contour plans and digital survey data illustrating the pond bed and sediment surface elevations.
• The volume of the sediment, measured from the top of the sediment layer to the pond liner.
• A comprehensive survey report detailing the survey methodology, equipment used, assumptions,
results, calculations, and any limitations encountered during the survey.
In addition, Necsa requires for raw data files (provided in x; y; z coordinates) to be provided as separate soft
copy file.
It should be noted that the equipment to be used in the pond will be exposure radiological contamination.
Necsa will employ all available measures to decontaminate the equipment. Should these measures not yield
the desired results, the equipment will be replaced at its current book-value.
