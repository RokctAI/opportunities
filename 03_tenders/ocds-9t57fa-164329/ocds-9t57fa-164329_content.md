SITE IDENTIFICATION AND LAND AVAILABILITY
SURVEY OF GOVERNMENT/STATE OWNED LAND
The section 34 Independent Power Producer Procurement Programme (IPPP
Programmes) is a competitive public procurement programme established by the
Department of Electricity and Energy to procure new electricity generation capacity from
Independent Power Producers (IPPs) to contribute towards security of electricity supply.
The IPPP Programmes are implemented primarily pursuant to the Electricity Regulation
Act, 2006 as amended (ERA), Ministerial Determinations issued under section 34 of the
ERA, and the Integrated Resource Plan (IRP). Under the IPPP Programmes, the private
sector would be required to develop, finance, construct, own, operate and maintain the
energy facilities.
The Department of Electricity and Energy (the Department), working through the
Independent Power Producers Office (IPP Office), is committed to the successful execution
of the Integrated Resource Plan 2025 (IRP 2025) through Ministerial Determinations to be
issued pursuant to the ERA.
Against this policy and regulatory backdrop recognizing that competitive procurement under
the IPPP Programmes has historically been largely site-agnostic (with IPPs responsible for
identifying and securing land), the IPP Office is exploring the feasibility of alternatives for
geographically targeted procurement approaches.
This Request for Information (RFI) is a market sounding exercise targeted at
Government/State Owned Entities and is aiming to gather information on
Government/State Owned land parcels that could be potentially earmarked for competitive
procurement of new generation capacity from a combination of energy sources including
Renewable Energy (RE), Energy Storage and other renewable energy projects.
The outcomes and information gathered pursuant to this RFI is intended to support the
exploration of potential opportunities for alternative geographically targeted procurement
approaches that could be used in the development of the IPPP Programmes, and to gather
information on site availability and readiness of any Government/State Owned land parcels.
This RFI could help determine the feasibility of, and whether, sufficient opportunities exist to
augment the traditionally used purely developer/IPP-led site selection model in designing
an appropriate procurement framework that is more location-specific / a designated

THE SURVEY
The IPP Office is conducting the studies pursuant to this RFI to explore the feasibility of
more proactive procurer-led development models especially where grid capacity in South
Africa is currently constrained and project development timelines need to be shortened for
accelerated delivery of the roll out of new generation capacity procured under the IPPPP
Programmes.
