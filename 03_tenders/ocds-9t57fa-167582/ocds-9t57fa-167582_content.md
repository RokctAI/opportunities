1. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
KZN Growth Fund Agency (KZNGFA) seeks to appoint a suitably qualified service provider for a payments
platform for the implementation, operation and support of a card-based/voucher grant disbursement solution
for Twelve (12) Months.
The Platform will support the end-to-end administration of non-repayable grant disbursements through a secure
digital OVF Card system for approved grant beneficiaries. The solution must support beneficiary management,
OVF Card generation and activation, approved goods management, supplier claims processing, approvals
workflow, payment processing support, post-disbursement auditing, reporting and analytics.
The system must be scalable, secure, auditable and compliant with all applicable South African regulatory and
governance requirements.
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES REQUIRED
The purpose of this tender is to appoint a qualified service provider to provide a payments solution that
allows for:
An electronic card/voucher disbursement administration platform enabling purchasing by approved
beneficiaries enabling payment from accredited supplier enabling suppliers.
The system must include:
• Prevent cash withdrawal
• Prevent person-to-person transfers
• Prevent unauthorised merchant payments.
• Merchant category controls (MCC) restrictions,
• Supplier whitelist,
• Product catalogue controls,
• Transaction limits.
• Provide beneficiary onboarding and card/voucher administration functionality;
• Support bulk beneficiary uploads from existing Microsoft Excel spreadsheets;
• Integrate SMS and email notification services;
• Ensure compliance with financial, information security and regulatory standards;
• Provide training and technical support.

5.3 OVF Card/Voucher Administration Platform
The platform shall include:

• Support CSV and XLSX imports;
• Validate uploaded data;
• Detect duplicate beneficiaries;
• Capture beneficiary demographic details;
• Capture grant approval information;
• Capture approved grant amounts;
• Maintain beneficiary status tracking;
• Store supporting documents;
• Track beneficiary orientation attendance;
• Track signed contracts;
• Allow beneficiary search and filtering;
• Maintain complete beneficiary audit trails.
5.3.2 OVF Card/Voucher Generation and Management
The system must:
• Generate unique OVF Card/Voucher;
• Generate unique activation codes;
• Support OVF Card/Voucher expiry dates;
• Support OVF Card/Voucher account spending limits;
• Restrict vouchers to approved suppliers only;
• Restrict purchases to approved products and quantities only;
• Support partial redemption functionality;
• Support voucher suspension and cancellation;
• Automatically deactivate expired OVF Cards/Voucher;
• Maintain Cards/Voucher redemption history;
