REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
ESTABLISHMENT OF AN EVENTS MANAGEMENT PANEL OF SERVICE PROVIDERS TO MANAGE
AND EXECUTE ENTERPRISE ILEMBE EVENTS AS AND WHEN REQUIRED FOR A PERIOD OF
THIRTY-SIX (36) MONTHS (TENDER NUMBER: T03-2027)
Enterprise iLembe hereby requests proposals from suitably qualified and experienced Events management
agencies to assist the entity with the implementation of events management programmes.
Please visit our website www.enterpriseilembe.co.za for copies of the Terms of Reference or e-mail your
request to the contact person listed below. Any requests for further information for clarity purposes will be
made available via e-mail.
Please place your completed proposal in a sealed envelope endorsed “PROPOSAL FOR: ” ESTABLISHMENT
OF AN EVENTS MANAGEMENT PANEL” in the Tender Box at Sangweni Tourism Centre, Cnr Link Road &
Ballito Drive, Ballito on or before 22 September 2026 at 11h00.
All proposals will be evaluated and adjudicated in terms of the approved Supply Chain Management Policy of
Enterprise iLembe, the 2022 Preferential Procurement Regulations and the 2011 B-BBEE Regulations as
detailed in the Terms of Reference. Enterprise iLembe reserves the right not to accept any or portion of a
proposal received under this call or give reasons for doing so.
Please note: All proposals must be submitted by hand to the tender box as no e-mailed or fax submissions will
be accepted. Bidders using a courier service to deliver documents are responsible for ensuring that such
delivered documents are physically deposited in the tender box before the tender closure. Tenders must be
submitted during office hours as stated in the terms of reference. No submissions will be accepted after the
closing date and time, as stipulated above.
