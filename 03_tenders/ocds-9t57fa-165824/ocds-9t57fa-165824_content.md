1. Technical tender evaluation procedure
The technical evaluation procedure is specific to,
The Provision of SPU Disconnections and Reconnections services in the North West Operating Unit for
a three year period. The evaluation method has two main parts: desktop and equipment & vehicle
assessment, which are related.
3.1 Desktop Evaluation
This evaluation exercise is performed by the Eskom evaluating representatives. This part of the
evaluation starts when submissions are opened for the first time. The technical evaluation will be
evaluated as follows: the mandatory criteria and then proceeds to functionality.
The Eskom assessment representatives will go through the details of the returnable submissions that
are required and will ensure that mandatory / gatekeeper qualification criteria are met.
Only submissions that pass mandatory / gatekeeper requirements will proceed to functionality phase.
Scoring/rating for functionality criteria will be assessed out of 100 points. Suppliers need to maintain a
minimum threshold of 80 points to be recommended.
Gate keeper
Proof of registration as an Electrical Contractor with the Department of Labour (this is a replacement of
ECB registration requirement since it has been discontinued)
3.2 Equipment Assessment
This assessment is performed on the basis of assessing the supplier’s capability to enter into a
contract with Eskom with respect to the
The Provision of SPU Disconnections and Reconnections services in the North West Operating Unit for
a three year period..

1. Disconnection and reconnection
Small Power User (SPU) - North West Operating Unit.
The schedule and/or number of customers will be determined age analysis that determines
debtor’s status daily. All Small Power User SPU meters referred to in this contract can be
identified, disconnected and reconnected by means of the information and equipment that will
be provided by the relevant Eskom office:
➢ the physical address
➢ the premise number
➢ a meter identification number visible on or in the meter kiosk
➢ single line diagrams to find installations where necessary Electrical Network Schematic
(ENS)
➢ All documentation/equipment to perform the required disconnection and reconnection must
be collected from the relevant Eskom office.
➢ Note: Eskom pays contractor per Premise, three phases with three meters is regarded as one
premise.
• In addition to the above the contractor must have:
➢ Knowledge of the geographical area to easily locate the premises without delay and timeously
disconnect and reconnect the customer within that date. Eskom will in addition provide contractor
with network information and /or assist contractor with line owner who knows the area and Eskom
network.
➢ technical and geographical aspects of Eskom’s networks

recorded on CC & B system. Eskom will audit the remarks and take action accordingly
• On completion of each disconnection and reconnection, the data must be provide to the Works
Coordinator as per daily activity to update them on Maximo & CC & B systems
