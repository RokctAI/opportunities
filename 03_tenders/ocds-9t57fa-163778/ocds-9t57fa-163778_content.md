The South African Revenue Service (SARS) hereby informs all prospective bidders of an
erratum to the above-mentioned Request for Proposal. The erratum relates specifically to the
mandatory requirement in the published tender documentation for bidders to submit proof of
registration with the National Contract Cleaners Association (NCCA).
Following the briefing session held on 5 August 2026, SARS formally confirms that bidders are
no longer required to submit proof of registration with the National Contract Cleaners
Association (NCCA). Bidders must, however, still submit a valid Letter of Good Standing issued
under the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act (COIDA) by the
Department of Employment and Labour or its agencies.
