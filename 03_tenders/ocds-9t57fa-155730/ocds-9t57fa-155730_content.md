1. INTRODUCTION
The Greater Kokstad Municipality (GKM) is one of four local municipalities within the Harry Gwala
District Municipality and is responsible for providing a variety of services to the communities within
its jurisdiction. One of the critical services the municipality offers is the collection and safe disposal
of domestic and commercial waste. As part of its waste management strategy, the municipality seeks
to appoint a service provider for the supply and Delivery of municipal refuse bags for a period of 36
months.
2. BACKGROUND OF THE PROJECT
The Greater Kokstad Municipality (GKM) has prioritized waste management, particularly focusing
on the safe disposal of waste. In line with this, the municipality has been procuring black refuse bags
through a service provider to ensure that waste management activities are conducted effectively,
with a focus on maintaining compliance with environmental regulations and supporting waste
management goals as per the approved municipal Integrated Waste Management Plan (IWMP).
3. AIMS OF THE PROJECT
The objective of this project is to source a service provider to supply and deliver black plastic bags
to Greater Kokstad Municipality for a period of 36 months. The service provider will be responsible
to ensure that black refuse bags are supplied and delivered on time as and when requested by the
municipality as per the order issued.
The project also aims to:
• To acquire black municipal Refuse Bags for the Greater Kokstad Municipality to enhance
service delivery to the community of Kokstad.
• The black municipal refuse bags will be used for the collection and removal of waste from
residential, light industrial and commercial areas of the Greater Kokstad Municipality
(GKM).
• The municipality has also adopted a strategy to distribute black municipal refuse bags to
residential areas quarterly.
