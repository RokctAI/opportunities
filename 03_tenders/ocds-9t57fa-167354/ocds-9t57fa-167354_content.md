Bid Notice
BID NO.: 07/26/27
APPOINTMENT OF A FULLY QUALIFIED AND COMPETENT ELECTRICAL
CONTRACTOR FOR THE MAINTENANCE AND HANDOVER OF FOUR (4)
TRANSFORMERS WITHIN WARD 3
Bidders are hereby invited from suitably qualified service providers for the appointment of a
fully qualified and competent electrical contractor for the maintenance and handover of four (4)
transformers within ward 3. Only bidders registered with the CIDB in contractor grading
designation 2EP or higher will be eligible. In the case of a joint venture, proof of a consolidated
CIDB grading designation meeting this requirement shall be submitted.
The physical address for collection of bid documents is Mandeni Municipality’s Civic Centre, 2
Kingfisher Road, Mandeni, Cashiers Office, upon payment of a non-refundable fee of R600.00
(inclusive of VAT). Bid documents will be available from Wednesday 2nd September 2026 to
Tuesday, 15 September 2026 during working hours, Monday to Friday from 08h00 to 15h30.
Bidders who pay by EFT may request the municipal banking details from the Manager: Supply
Chain Management.
A compulsory clarification meeting and site inspection will take place at the Technical Services
Boardroom, 2 Croton Place, Mandeni, on Wednesday, 16 September 2026 at 10h00, after
which bidders will proceed to the sites. Attendance and completion of the attendance certificate
are mandatory. Failure to attend the compulsory clarification meeting with the bid document will
disqualify the tender. The doors to the venue will be closed at 10h00 and no bidder will be
permitted to enter afterwards. Bidders that do not bring the document to the briefing will be
disqualified.
Bids must be completed in accordance with this document, sealed in an envelope marked “BID
NO.: 07/26/27 APPOINTMENT OF A FULLY QUALIFIED AND COMPETENT ELECTRICAL
CONTRACTOR FOR THE MAINTENANCE AND HANDOVER OF FOUR (4)
TRANSFORMERS WITHIN WARD 3”, and deposited in the tender box at the Civic Centre, 2
Kingfisher Road, Mandeni, no later than 12H00 on Wednesday, 23rd September 2026. Late,
emailed or faxed bids will not be considered. Only bidders registered on the Central Supplier
Database will be considered.

The Mandeni Municipality subscribes to the preferential procurement policy framework Act, Act
No. 5 of 2000 and its associated Regulations of 2022. Bids will be evaluated and adjudicated
