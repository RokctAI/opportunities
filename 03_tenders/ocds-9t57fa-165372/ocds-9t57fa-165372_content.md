1. BACKGROUND
The South African Human Rights Commission (SAHRC) is a Constitutional body established in terms of the
Constitution of South Africa, 1996, as well as the South African Human Rights Commission Act, 40 of 2014. The
SAHRC is under the strategic leadership of Commissioners. The administration of SAHRC is overseen by a
Chief Executive Officer (CEO).
The Commission has recently received negative sentiments, especially on social media and in the press, over
internal governance challenges and the promotion and protection of the rights of non-nationals. Despite
statements issued to correct negative sentiments, misinformation and disinformation on the Commission’s work
and its composition continue to be shared on social media.
2.PURPOSE
To appoint a suitably qualified Brand Strategist to strengthen the South African Human Rights Commission's
brand, improve its public image, and address negative perceptions through strategic brand management over
a six-month period.
1. OBJECTIVE
The objective of this Request for Quotes (RFQ) is to appoint a suitably qualified Brand Strategist who will render
brand management services for the Commission for a period of 6 months. The Brand Strategist will be responsible
for developing and implementing strategies to shape the Commission’s public image and perception.
The SAHRC requires a Brand Strategist with at least 10 years of development of brand strategies, social media
imaging, and strategies to counter negative perceptions and create a positive brand.
The strategist should have some knowledge of human rights, be dynamic, have a proven record of repositioning
brands, and be able to work under pressure and irregular hours.
4.SCOPE OF SERVICES
• Conduct research and survey: Analyse data to determine the best strategy to reposition the
Commission’s public image, identify trends and opportunities.
• Develop Brand Strategy: Define the brand's purpose, positioning, personality, and core messaging.
• Create brand voice and messaging: Counter misinformation and disinformation, especially on social
media, and ensure a consistent tone and story are told across all communication channels.
• Formulate marketing plans: Develop and implement strategies and campaigns that align with SAHRC’s
promotion mandate across all communications channels.

• Analyse performance: Quarterly monitoring of SAHRC brand perception, customer sentiment, and
campaign effectiveness to make necessary adjustments.
Deliverables
• Development of a Research Report and Analysis of the survey
• Development of a Branding Strategy
• Reposition the Commission’s public image
• Social media: Monitoring, implementation and management of social media channel campaigns to
reposition the public image of the SAHRC
• Quarterly performance reports showcasing positive repositioning of the public image of the SAHRC
5 . PROJECT TIMELINES
The project duration shall be six (6) months from the day of the contract signing.
