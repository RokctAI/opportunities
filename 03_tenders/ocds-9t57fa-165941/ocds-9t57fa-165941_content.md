1. PURPOSE
To request information (RFI) from qualified vendors regarding solutions to transition our current
backup infrastructure from Linear Tape-Open (LTO) tape-based backups to disk-based backups
or other advanced backup technologies.
1. BACKGROUND
SABC currently relies on LTO tape technology for corporate data backups. Given the rapid growth
of data volumes and the increasing need for faster and more reliable backup and recovery, we
are exploring modernizing our backup infrastructure. Potential solutions include disk-based
backup systems, cloud-integrated backups, deduplication appliances, or hybrid models.
1. OBJECTIVES
The primary goals for the new backup solution include:
➢ Significantly reduced backup and recovery windows to support business continuity
➢ Enhanced data reliability and integrity
➢ Scalability to support anticipated data growth and retention policies
➢ Improved operational efficiency through simplified management and automation
➢ Cost-effective total cost of ownership (TCO) over the solution lifecycle
➢ Compatibilityor integration with existing backup softwareor recommendations for
alternative platforms
➢ Capability for secure offsite replication or cloud integration, where applicable

1. Information Requested
We invite vendors to respond with detailed information covering the following:
a) Company Profile
• Overview of the company’s history and expertise in backup and data protection
• Relevant certifications, partnerships, and local presence in South Africa
b) Proposed Solution Details
• Description of the proposed backup technology (disk-based, hybrid, cloud-enabled, etc.)
• Technical architecture and deployment model
• Support for data deduplication, compression, and encryption
• Compatibility with or recommendations for backup management software
• Performance benchmarks and scalability limits
• Security features including data encryption (in-flight and at rest) and access controls
c) Implementation and Support Services
• Typical deployment timelines and methodology tailored to enterprise environments
• Professional services availability including consulting, installation, and training
• Local support availability and service level agreements (SLAs)
d) Pricing Structure
• Detailed pricing model including hardware, software licenses, and maintenance/support
fees
• Any subscription or cloud service fees, if applicable
e) Client References
• Relevant case studies or references from similar-sized enterprise or broadcast sector client
