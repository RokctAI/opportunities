PROJECT PURPOSE\nThe Perishable Products Export Control Board (PPECB) is inviting accredited service providers to submit\ntheir proposals for delivering of Quality Council for Trade and Occupations (QCTO) skills programme\n(SP220319). The programme aims to equip learners with the knowledge, practical skills and workplace\nexperience required to effectively facilitate learning and development intervention within an occupational\ncontext.\n1. BACKGROUND\nThe PPECB is a Schedule 3A entity in terms of the Public Finance Management Act 1 of 1999. The PPECB\nis established in terms of Section 2 of the Perishable Products Export Control Act 9 of 1983. The main\npurpose of the PPECB is to promote the orderly, efficient, and sustainable export of perishable products\nfrom South Africa. The PPECB manages and monitors the cold chain for the export of perishable products\nfrom South Africa. The PPECB operates under two mandates, namely the Agricultural Products Standards\nAct and the Perishable Products Standards Act 9 of 1983.\n1. SCOPE\n• The programme should cover the QCTO curriculum components, including:\n• Knowledge Modules\n• The Statutory Learning and Development Environment\n• Facilitation of Learning in Occupational Contexts\n• Practical Skills Modules\n• Plan the Delivery of an Occupational Learning Intervention\n• Facilitate Different Methodologies, Training Styles and Techniques within an Occupational\nLearning Context\n• Work Experience Modules\n• Conduct Learning and Development Planning and Implementation Processes\n• Facilitate an Occupational Learning Session\n• Training to be conducted online via MS Teams\n\nREQUIREMENTS\nProvider Requirements:\n• Interested providers must:\n• Be accredited by the QCTO to offer SP220319.\n• Submit proof of current accreditation.\n• Provide qualified and experienced facilitators.\n• Submit facilitator CVs and qualifications.\n• Demonstrate previous experience in delivering Learning and Development Facilitator programmes\nProgramme Details:\n• ProgrammeTitle: Learning and Development Facilitator\n• NQF Level 5\nSkills Programme ID:\n• SP 220319\nCredits:\n• 36Credits (or as prescribed by the relevant QCTO curriculum)\nTarget Group:\n• The Programme is intended for Chief Inspector and Senior Inspector (Workplace mentors and\nSupervisors)\nEntry Requirement:\nHave access to learners or candidates for assessment practice\nLearning Outcomes:\n• Upon successful completion of the Programme, learners should be able to:\n• Understand the statutory learning and development environment\n• Plan and prepare occupational learning intervention\n• Select and adapt learning resources\n• Facilitate learning using appropriate methodologies and techniques\n• Evaluate and improve facilitation effectiveness\n• Facilitate learning sessions within an occupational environment\n• Apply adult learning principles and learner – centred approaches\n• Maintain ethical and professional standards in assessment practice.\nDeliverables:\n• The appointed provider shall provide:\n• Learner guides and training materials.\n• Facilitation and assessment services.\n• Workplace evidence requirements and guidance.\n•
