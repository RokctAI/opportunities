1. INTRODUCTION
1.1. The Department of Sport, Arts and Culture (DSAC) hereby invites proposals for the provision
of office space in the Johannesburg Metropolitan Area for a period of five (5) years, to
accommodate:
• National twenty-five (25) sports federations, and
• Seventeen (17) Cultural and Creative Industries sector cluster organizations.
1.2. This RFP seeks suitably qualified landlords or service providers with the capacity to provide
modern, secure, and compliant office accommodation that meets the functional needs of
DSAC and its stakeholders.
1.3. This must be ready to occupy Office Space with all the amenities and services specified below
in the scope of work.
