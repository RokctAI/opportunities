BACKGROUND OF THE RAF\nThe Road Accident Fund (RAF) is a schedule 3A public entity established in terms of the Road\nAccident Fund Act, 1996 (Act No. 56 of 1996), as amended. Its mandate is the provision of\ncompulsory social insurance cover to all users of South African roads, to rehabilitate and\ncompensate persons injured as a result of the negligent driving of motor vehicles in a timely and\ncaring manner, and to actively promote the safe use of our roads.\nThe customer base of the RAF comprises not only the South African public but also all foreigners\nwithin the country's borders. The RAF head office is in Centurion, and the RAF intends to establish\nCustomer Experience Centres (CEC) in each province in the country.\n1. INTRODUCTION\nThe Road Accident Fund (RAF) invites eligible Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and\naccredited partners or resellers to provide information on Cloud-Hosted Observability Solutions\ncapable of supporting enterprise-wide visibility across RAF’s hybrid Technology and Digital (T&D)\nenvironment.\nThis RFI is a non-binding market-information exercise and does not constitute a Request for Bid,\nProposal or Quotation, nor a commitment by RAF to procure any product or service.\n1. BACKGROUND OF THE RFI\nRAF is modernising its Technology and Digital (T&D) environment to improve operational\nefficiency, service delivery and business resilience. The environment spans on-premises\ninfrastructure, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, enterprise\napplications, databases, storage platforms and network services.\nRequest for Information – Cloud Hosted Observability Solution\nPage 5\n\nExisting monitoring capabilities provide fragmented and largely reactive visibility across this\nenvironment. This contributes to delayed issue detection, prolonged root cause investigation,\nlimited cross-domain correlation, and insufficient predictive insight as the hybrid estate expands.\nRAF therefore requires a clearer understanding of modern cloud-hosted observability approaches\nthat can unify telemetry and operational insight across infrastructure, applications, networks,\ndatabases, cloud services and digital user experiences. Relevant capabilities include real-time\ntelemetry, distributed tracing, application performance monitoring, service dependency mapping,\nbusiness transaction monitoring, AI-driven analytics, predictive alerting, automated diagnostics\nand cloud cost optimisation.\n1. OBJECTIVES OF THE RFI\nThrough this RFI, RAF intends to obtain sufficient market information to:\n• Identify viable cloud-hosted observability platforms and their supported functional\ncapabilities.\n• Compare hosting, deployment, integration and implementation models suitable for RAF’s\nhybrid environment.\n• Assess security, compliance, data residency, scalability and interoperability considerations.\n• Understand implementation timelines, skills requirements, support services and relevant\nsupplier experience.\n• Establish indicative licensing, subscription, consumption and total-cost structures for\nmarket analysis.\n• Identify current industry practices, product roadmaps and emerging observability\ncapabilities.\n• Generate evidence to refine RAF’s business requirements, technical specifications and\nassessment criteria for any future competitive procurement process.\n1.
