The erratum aims to notify bidders of the amendment to the tender document that
was published on 29th of July 2026 on National Treasury e-tender portal and on
SANParks website.

Returnable schedules required as compulsory:
1. SBD 1 – Eligibility in Respect of Risk to the Employer
2. The updated standard Health & Safety Specifications.
Returnable schedules required as non- compulsory:
1. Letter of good standing from compensation commissioner
2. Preference Points claimed in terms of the Preferential Procurement
Regulations 2022 (Form SBD 6.1) (Completed and Signed)
1. An original and valid B-BBEE sworn affidavit, SANAS or DTIC certificate,
or certified copy thereof. For Joint Ventures, a SANAS certificate in the
name of the JV must be provided
The amended bid document is issued and published with this erratum notice.
