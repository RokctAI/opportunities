SCOPE OF WORKS
Introduction
The UMzinyathi District Municipality (UDM) constantly strives to improve in the areas of compliance and
governance as this is vital to maintaining accountability, transparency, and public trust. Compliance ensures
that the municipality adheres to legal and regulatory requirements, reducing the risk of penalties, lawsuits,
and adverse audit findings that could tarnish its reputation. Strong governance fosters efficient resource
management, ethical decision-making, and effective service delivery, ensuring that municipal funds are
used responsibly to meet the needs of the community. This establishes a foundation for sustainable
development by promoting stability, fairness, trust among stakeholders and strengthens its ability to fulfill its
mandate and improve the quality of life for its residents.
UmDM recognizes that Information technology (IT) plays a transformative role in supporting administrative
processes by automating routine tasks like financial reporting, procurement, and record-keeping, which
reduces errors and ensures compliance with regulatory requirements. Digital platforms enable real-time
tracking of municipal activities, providing transparency, accountability in decision-making, and better
communication and engagement with stakeholders, such as residents and oversight bodies. These systems
enhance informed decision making by providing actionable insights from vast amounts of data.
The purpose of this bid is for the appointment of a service provider that has experience in the development
and implementation of integrated IT systems, that target and support the areas of compliance and
governance across all areas of the municipal business.
To ensure continuity the appointment will be for a period of three (3) years. A schedule of items to be
costed has been included as part of the bid requirements. This schedule covers a wide range of
requirements and for the duration of the appointment period the appointed provider may be required to
implement all or a selected range of the items detailed. This is dependent on municipal capacity and budget
availability.
Phase 1: Needs Assessment
The UmDM is aware that there are areas of the business that require improvement. To ensure that as many
of the opportunities for improvement are identified, a needs assessment must first be carried out.
This will provide a blue print that will help to ensure that the chosen solutions will align with the
municipality's requirements and objectives. By thoroughly analyzing current processes, challenges, and
resource constraints, the needs assessment will identify gaps and inefficiencies in existing systems. This
will help to ensure that inappropriate or redundant IT solutions are not selected for implementation. This
process will also allow for prioritization of features and functionalities that directly address critical issues,
such as compliance, service delivery, and data management.
While at the time of proposal this is an unknown, the service provider is expected to have a thorough
knowledge of municipal process and systems to anticipate the necessary requirements.
Phase 2: Customisation / Implementation
The second phase of the project will require the implementation of the approved needs assessment based
on priority and budget availability. The service provider will carry out the customisation and implementation
of required systems that fully meet the requirements of the municipality as identified in Phase 1. The
16

following items need to be addressed:
1. Approval: Given the priorities, a program and cost estimate must be presented for approval before
any work may commence.
1. Integration: Any new systems will be developed to co-exist with the existing systems and will be
developed and deployed using compatible technologies and software development platforms
1. Scalability: Proposed systems must allow for expansion based the municipality's needs,
accommodating an increasing population, expanding services, or additional functionalities in the
future.
1. Ownership / Buy-In: System ownership is a key requirement. The appointed provider will be required
to propose and implement strategies to ensure that the proposed systems obtain an identity and
ownership within the municipality
1. Data Security and Privacy: All proposed systems and technologies must protect sensitive municipal
and citizen data, ensuring compliance with data protection regulations.
1. Open-Source Technologies: UmDM supports the use of open-source technologies because they offer
cost-effective, customizable, and collaborative solutions. The successful service provider will be
expected to deliver the solutions in a manner which will allow other service providers to take over the
support and enhancements at the end of the contract period should the incumbent service provider
not be re-appointed. To this end, the following should be noted: The service provider will provide the
municipality with the source code, or a suitable product API to enable the municipality to undertake
full customisation of the software. If an API is provided, the service provider needs to provide detailed
API documentation.
Phase 3: Monthly Support
This phase of the project involves ongoing monthly support of the systems and applications as well as
attending to data maintenance. The appointed service provider will be required to:
1. Software Support: Attend to any bugs in the systems deployed
2. New Features: Make minor modifications to the software in respect to changes requested by the
UmDM. This will be restricted to minor point releases.
1. Data Maintenance: Providing support through backend database, capturing data through the front-
end or assisting with the collecting of specific information for systems deployed.
1. System Maintenance and Updates: Perform routine maintenance, such as software patches, bug
fixes, and updates, to enhance system performance and security.
1. Monitoring and Performance Analysis: Continuously monitor the system’s performance and user
activity to identify inefficiencies or bottlenecks and optimize processes.
1. Statutory Reporting: Assist with the development and preparation of statutory reporting to meet the
legislated requirements. Attend any meetings as directed to provide support to the IT team.
1. Helpdesk Support: Establish a dedicated helpdesk team to assist users with troubleshooting,
addressing technical issues, and providing guidance on system usage.
Conclusion
Whilst the UDM seeks to appoint a provider that is both skilled and competent in the deployment and
support of municipal systems, an equally important requirement is that the appointment is viewed as a
partnership between the provider and the municipality. This is intended to fosters collaboration, mutual
trust, and long-term benefits for both parties. The service provider will invest in understanding the
municipality's unique challenges and goals, allowing for tailored solutions that drive better outcomes. This
cooperative approach is intended to encourage flexibility and innovation, as both parties must work together
to address evolving needs rather than adhering rigidly to predefined contractual terms. This shared
accountability, will assist in ensuring that both parties are equally invested in achieving success.
