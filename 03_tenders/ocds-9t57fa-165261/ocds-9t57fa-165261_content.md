Annexure 7
PPA Risk Scoring Methodology
Each marked up PPA submitted by bidders as Appendix 10 Marked Up Offsite Wheeled PPA Term Sheet will be evaluated
and scored out of a total possible score of 100 using this methodology. The allocated score from the evaluation is based on
the re-allocation of key risks as indicated in Appendix 10 Marked Up Offsite Wheeled PPA Term Sheet, and the resultant
balance of these allocations between the Seller and the Foskor. The methodology follows a structured process as outlined
below.
Individual risk identification
A total of 29 risk items were identified for detailed review. These risks were derived from an understanding of the typical
commercial and contractual risks inherent in similar PPA structures. Definitions of the individual risk items, category, control
and weight of each item were defined.
Risk Control Owner
The 29 individual risk items were further allocated to the party with primary control or influence of the risk, i.e. which party is
better positioned to effectively manage or mitigate the risk. The primary risk owners are
 Tenderer as the primary party in the role of the Generator / Aggregator / Trader
 Foskor as the primary party in the role of the Off taker / Customer
 Secondary parties with external control:
o System Operator
o Market Operator
o Funders

Risk rating and scoring
Each of the 29 risk items will be evaluated and scored as per the individual weights detailed in table 1 below, representing
the degree of exposure between the Seller and Foskor. A lower percentage allocation favours the Seller and a higher
percentage allocation favours Foskor. The total of the scores allocated for each risk is then the score that is taken as the
score for item 3 in Table 5 Technical and Functional Evaluation Criteria of the RFP document. This scoring approach when
combined with the category weightings allows for a quantitive and objective assessment of risk allocation between the Seller
and the Buyer.
