1 INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND
(For purposes of this specification, the term ‘Landlord shall refer to the Service Provider/ Bidder)
1. The Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) is an independent and autonomous
organization that was established by the Labour Relations Act of 1995 (LRA) to deliver dispute
prevention and resolution services to the people of South Africa. The core mandate of the CCMA, as one
of the organizations charged with implementing the LRA, is derived from the purpose of the LRA which,
amongst others, is to advance economic development, social justice, labour peace and the
democratization of the workplace.
1. The CCMA invites prospective bidders who can supply office accommodation and parking within
Marshalltown in Johannesburg. The required CCMA office space comprises of a Gross Lettable Area
(GLA) of 2928.60m2 determined by the attached Space Planning Calculations (See Annexure A), for a
lease term of five (5) years. The anticipated occupation date is 01 July 2027. Any renovations/alterations
must be completed a week prior to the occupation date.
1. The characteristics of the building / premises should be a typical high people traffic volume facility
with offices, boardrooms, a training room, mediation rooms, and typical open plan office space
accommodation which need to be easily assimilated on the premises.
1. Preferably, the location of the facility should be within 2.5km walking distance to public transport
services, clearly visible from major local traffic routes and unimpeded entrance to the public area from
street level, ideally within proximity of public parking facilities. It is a further pre-requisite that the
proposed new premises must provide fifty (50) parking bays. With a minimum of twenty -one (21) onsite
parking bays including one (01) which must comply with the disability requirements, and twenty-nine (29)
parking bays, which should not be more than 100 meters away from the office.
1. The CCMA would like to lease office accommodation in a secured location. This will be applicable
irrespective of whether the lease is a single or multi-tenant building. Landlord to provide building physical
guarding services at all main entrance and exit points 24hours/7days a week at their own cost.
