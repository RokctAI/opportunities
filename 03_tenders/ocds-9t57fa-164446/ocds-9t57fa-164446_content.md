Question:
1. Procurement of On-Site "Crash Kits"
How would this be addressed? The contracting partner will immediately procure a
dedicated pool of refurbished, OEM-certified, and
stress-tested replacement parts. This inventory will
be stored locally on-site and must include identical
3PAR controller nodes, power distribution units, host
bus adapters (HBAs), and cache batteries. This
eliminates reliance on the OEM supply chain and
avoids cross-border shipping delays.
1. Utilization of In-House Engineering
Capabilities
Because the OEM will not provide field engineers for
physical replacements, the contracting partner will
leverage their capable, certified in-house storage
engineers. These technicians will be designated as
the primary responders to perform physical node

1. Strict Configuration and Firmware
Baselining
To safeguard the system during independent
maintenance, the 3PAR units will be locked at their
current, most stable microcode and firmware
versions. No configuration modifications or firmware
updates will be attempted without an isolated
sandbox validation, ensuring the integrity of the
existing HA fabric is never compromised.
1. Lifecycle Transition Planning
This parts-only and internal engineering approach
serves as a tactical bridging strategy. Concurrently,
SANRAL and the contracting partner are outlining a
phased technology refresh plan to systematically
migrate critical workloads off these legacy units and
onto modern, fully supported storage infrastructure.
