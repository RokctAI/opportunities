1. Background
Robben Island Museum requires a logistics solution to transport visitors safely and
comfortably from the mainland to Robben Island. The solution must provide a high-quality
and safe visitor experience.
1. Objectives
2.1 The primary objective of this tender is to appoint Service Providers to provide passenger
ferry services to and from Robben Island.
2.2. The secondary objectives are to identify suitable solutions to:
2.2.1 Provide reliable ferry services for visitors from the mainland, Nelson Mandela Gateway to
Robben Island, and back.
2.2.2 Ensure compliance with all maritime safety regulations and legal requirements.
2.2.3 Deliver a safe, comfortable, and enjoyable visitor experience.
1. A Passenger Boats
3.1. Vessel must be able to operate safely in Beaufort Scale 1–6 sea conditions.
3.2. Vessel length must not exceed 35 meters.
3.3. Maximum loaded draft must not exceed 2.2 meters.
3.4. Vessel beam must not exceed 10 meters.
3.5. Have at least 1 wash closet per 50 passengers.
3.6. Certified to carry a minimum of 50 passengers.
3.8. Passenger spaces must be air-conditioned.
3.9. Vessel must have universal accessibility, sufficient space in the passenger compartment
for at least four wheelchairs and a universally accessible wash closet.
3.10. Passenger spaces must have proper audiovisual sets and a multimedia player, for the
broadcast of RIM-provided videos.
1. B Luxury Boats
3.1. Vessel length must not exceed 35 meters.
3.2. Maximum loaded draft must not exceed 2.2 meters.
3.3. Vessel beam must not exceed 10 meters.
3.4. Passenger spaces must be air-conditioned.
3.5. Vessel must have universal accessibility, sufficient space in the passenger compartment
for at least one wheelchair and a universally accessible wash closet.
3.6. Passenger spaces must have proper audiovisual sets and a multimedia player, for the
broadcast of RIM-provided videos.
3.7. All seating to be properly cushioned and upholstered.
3.8. Have at least 1 wash closet per 50 passengers, or part thereof.
1. Compliance and Certification
4.1 The vessel and crew must be able to operate legally and safely between Cape Town
Harbour and Robben Island.
