9. PREFACE
1.1 The Western Cape Department of Social Development hereby calls for experienced
and registered Health and Welfare Sector Education and Training Authority (HWSETA)
Skills Development Providers (SDP’s) accredited with the Quality Council of Trades
and Occupations (QCTO) within the Western Cape area to implement the Child and
Youth Care Worker NQF Level 5 Learnership within the Department of Social
Development (herein after referred to as the Department) for a period of twenty-four
(24) months.
1.2 At present there is no accredited Child and Youth Care Worker NQF Level 5
Learnership presented by the Provincial Training Institute (PTI). It is therefore the
intention of this Department to procure a HWSETA registered and accredited with the
QCTO, external service provider that adheres to the Department of Labour and
HWSETA prescripts to fulfil this need.

1. BID SPECIFICATIONS
3.1 Scope:
3.1.1 To implement and present the Health and Welfare Sector Education and Training
Authority (HWSETA) Occupational Certificate: Child and Youth Care Worker, SAQA
ID: 99510 (NQF Level 5) accredited by the Quality Council of Trades and
Occupations (QCTO).
3.2 Minimum Requirements
3.2.1 Accreditation requirements
a) Bidders must submit a valid accreditation with the QCTO as an accredited
Child and Youth Care Worker NQF Level 5 Learnership Provider (SAQA ID:
99510, NQF Level 5). The accreditation letter must clearly indicate the
qualification, programme approval or scope of accreditation applicable to
the Occupational Certificate: Child and Youth Care Worker (SAQA ID 99510,
NQF Level 5). The Department reserves the right to verify the accreditation
status of any bidder directly with QCTO.
b) Bidders are required to provide proof from the South African Council for Social
Service Professions (SACSSP) that the learning programme is recognized by the
South African Council for Social Service Professions (SACSSP).
3.2.2 Experience
a) Bidders must provide a minimum of three (3) verifiable and contactable
references. (Annexure A)
b) Bidders must have a minimum of three (3) years’ experience in the
implementation of learnership programmes, supported by testimonial letters
confirming successful delivery of similar projects, which must be on an official
letterhead and signed.
3.2.3 Project Plan
a) Bidders must provide a detailed Project Plan with expected payment plan,
detailed cost breakdown, and monitoring plan. The Project Plan must include a
proposed implementation schedule indicating the roll-out of face-to-face and
virtual training sessions in accordance with the requirement that academic
lesson time be delivered through 50% face-to-face and 50% virtual group-
based training.
5

3.2.4 Capacity and Resources
a) The service provider has the required digital infrastructure, Learning
Management System (LMS), virtual classroom capabilities and technical
support resources to deliver training both physically and virtually to all learners.
Academic lesson time needs to be in a group setting which consist of 50% in
person and 50% virtual. All lessons to be recorded and made available for self-
study purposes.
b) Bidders must provide proof that they have the capacity to deliver training to
eighty (80) learners in accordance with the SAQA requirements for the
qualification. Information on the following must be provided:
• The training facility must be located within 100 km from the Cape Town
CBD.
• The training venue must be able to accommodate 40 officials comfortably
• Availability of facilities, i.e ablution facilities.
c) Bidder to provide the number of facilitators available to implement the training.
A min number of 3 facilitators is required in relation to the number of learners.
Facilitators must be accredited and experience to provide the required
training. Proof of experience and accreditation of facilitators must be
provided.
d) Bidder must provide a list of management staff that will be involved in the
project as per Annexure B.
3.2.5 Mentoring and Learner support
a) Bidders must provide a mentoring plan and learner support plan, which
includes assessment schedule and learner material workbooks
3.2.6 Financial Sustainability
a) Bidders must demonstrate sufficient financial capacity and sustainability to
successfully implement and complete the contract for the full twenty-four (24)
month contract period, including evidence of adequate cash flow to sustain
operations during the initial implementation period prior to receipt of
Departmental payments Bidders must submit one or more of the following to
demonstrate financial sustainability:
• Latest audited or independently reviewed financial statements;
• A letter from a registered auditor or accountant;
• A bank letter confirming financial standing.
Failure to submit any of the above will result in disqualification.
b) The Department reserves the right to verify any submitted information.
6

3.3 The learnership and associated activities should be focused on the following
interventions:
3.3.1 Programme Implementation
a) The CYCW Learnership will be conducted over a period of twenty-four (24)
months and will target eighty (80) officials, split into two block groups of forty
(40) officials per block, for the duration of the contract
b) Facilitate the Learnership.
3.3.2 Accredited Learnership Training Material and Delivery
a) Provide all accredited Learnership training manuals and learnership material to
learners and mentors, necessary to assist and guide them in executing their
assigned functions relating to the Learnership.
b) Customization of case studies and theory to include Child and Youth Care
Workers working within a secure care setting to address the needs of the
organisation.
c) The learnership will be delivered through a blended learning approach
comprising face-to-face classroom sessions and virtual instructor-led training
sessions. Learners will be able to participate through an accredited online
learning platform where appropriate, ensuring continuity of learning,
accessibility and flexibility. All virtual learning sessions will be facilitated by
accredited facilitators and supported by electronic learning materials, learner
support services, online assessments and mentoring activities.
d) Academic lesson time needs to be in a group setting which consist of 50% in
person and 50% virtual. All lessons to be recorded and made available for self-
study purposes.
e) The service provider must provide:
• Virtual classroom capability;
• Learning Management System (LMS);
• Recorded sessions for replay/self-paced revision must remain available until
the end of the final examination of the learnership;
• Technical learner support.
7

3.3.3 Mentorship and Learner Support
a) Train the 16 identified mentors.
b) Provide appropriate training, supportive monthly meetings and feedback of
learners, and ongoing support to mentors.
c) Provide additional supportive and remedial learning sessions and
supplementary examinations whenever necessary to learners.
d) The service provider must provide access to recorded learning sessions and
supplementary self-paced learning resources to support learners who miss
facilitated sessions or require additional learning support.
3.3.4 Facilities and Logistics
a) The service provider must provide the training venue.
b) Must provide catering: mid-morning tea, coffee with a muffin or sandwich and
lunch in the form of a meal with juice/soft drink when learners are attending
class at the premises of the selected service provider.
c) Water to be provided throughout training, during sessions.
d) Where learners attend virtual group-based sessions from a Departmental
venue catering arrangements will be done by the Department.
3.3.5 Monitoring and Reporting
a) Bidder to provide a submission and implementation of the Learnership
Communication methods (i.e, email, sms, whatsapp, ect.).
b) Submission of quarterly project reports and learner progress reports with learner
intervention plans when required.
3.3.6 Programme Close-Out
a) Final evaluation report and certification of the learnership must be provided
within 12 months after the completion of the final exams.
