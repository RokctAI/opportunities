The Department of Agriculture seeks the services of a local, suitably qualified and experienced security
company to render security services for a period of 36 months.
The Department of Agriculture requires service of qualified service provider to offer security management
services to ensure safety of the departmental assets (including biological assets), information, buildings and
personnel, service in terms of the following legislation:
a) Control of Access to Government Buildings and Vehicles Act, No 54 of 1985,
b) Criminal Procedures Act, No 51 of 1977,
c) Firearm Control Act, No 60 of 2000,
d) Private Security Industry Regulatory Act, No 56 of 2001,
e) Occupational Health and Safety Act, no 85 of 1994 as amended,
f) Trespass Act, No 6 of 1959.
The employer reserves the right not to award more than two contracts, to be executed
simultaneously, to a single service provider.

rates and in line with National Bargaining Council for the Private Security Sector and PSIRA. Service
providers quoting salaries below these rates will be passed over.

to be conducted to view the offices.
