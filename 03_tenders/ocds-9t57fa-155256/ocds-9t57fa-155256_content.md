3. INTRODUCTION
TCTA, as a Schedule 2 entity under the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA), is
required by law to ensure that all its Supply Chain Management (SCM) and related business
processes are conducted in accordance with the prescripts of the National Treasury and
other regulatory authorities. Among these activities is the evaluation of bid documents in
line with the original Request for Bid. Additionally, TCTA’s bid request template specifies
that successful bidders may be subject to supplier vetting processes.
TCTA manages highly regulated and sensitive information, including personal, financial,
operational, and internal corporate data. The current manual or ad‑hoc data‑masking
practices are no longer adequate and exposes the organization to several risks.
A data masking solution is needed to enhance TCTA’s Information Management and Data
Governance capabilities by providing secure, controlled, and compliant handling of sensitive
data across operational, analytical, and non‑production environments.
4. SCOPE OF WORK
4.1. DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF GOODS/SERVICES
TCTA seeks to appoint a qualified and experienced service provider to design, implement,
configure, integrate, and support an enterprise-wide Data Masking solution across its ICT
environment, including on-premises, cloud, and hybrid infrastructure.
The scope will include, but not be limited to, the following:
4.1.1. Static data masking
• The solution must have the ability to mask data at rest
4.1.2. Dynamic data masking
• The solution must have the ability to mask real time data
Page | 3

4.1.3. Tokenization
• The solution must have the ability to replace sensitive data with tokens or fake data
4.1.4. Anonymization
• The solution must have the ability to permanently replace or delete sensitive data
4.1.5. Role-Based Access Control
• Implementation of granular role-based access control aligned to job functions.
• Policy-based access enforcement across systems and applications.
4.1.6. Data discovery and classification
• Ability to locate, identify and label data based on sensitivity across TCTA applications
4.1.7. Masking across structured and semi‑structured data
• The solution must be able to mask data across TCTA repositories and databases.
There are 11 applications with an estimated 10 terabytes of data.
4.1.8. Ability to integrate with TCTA databases that contains Personal Identifiable
Information
• Integration with Microsoft365 Directory, Azure AD, and other directory services.
• Integration with Single Sign-On (SSO) environments.
• Secure management of privileged access to virtual machines, databases, and cloud
workloads
4.1.9. Audit, logging, and reporting features
• Ability to produce real-time and scheduled reports/dashboard
• Customizable reports aligned to governance and audit requirements.
4.1.10. Implementation, Training and Knowledge Transfer
• Solution design, deployment, and configuration.
• Pilot implementation and phased rollout approach.

4.1.11. Support and Maintenance
