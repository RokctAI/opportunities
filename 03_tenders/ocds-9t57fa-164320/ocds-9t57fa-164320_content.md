1 INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND
1. INTRODUCTION
1.1. The mandate of the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation, and Arbitration (CCMA) is espoused
by the Labour Relations Act, 1995 (Act No.66 of 1995) as amended (the LRA), to deliver
expeditious social justice to the challenging South African labour market. The CCMA’s Head Office
is in Johannesburg, while it is represented in other Provinces as Provincial and Regional Offices.
1. BACKGROUND
2.1. The CCMA seeks to appoint an authorised Financial Services Provider (FSP), registered as a
Short-Term Insurance Broker, with demonstrable experience and capacity to provide short-term
insurance brokerage services to the CCMA for a period of three (3) years commencing on 1 May
2027 and ending on 30 April 2030.
1. INVITATION TO BIDDER
3.1. Bidders are hereby invited for the Provision of short-term insurance brokerage services to the
CCMA. The successful bidder will be appointed as the CCMA's insurance broker and will be
responsible for providing brokerage, advisory, intermediary, insurance placement, claims
administration, annual insurance renewal management, insurer engagement and representation of
the CCMA with insurers, underwriters and reinsurers.
3.2. The appointed Broker shall provide brokerage services over a three (3) year contract period. During
each annual insurance renewal cycle, the Broker shall review the CCMA's insurance requirements,
obtain quotations from appropriate insurers and underwriters, negotiate competitive terms and
premiums, recommend the most suitable insurance programme, and facilitate the placement and
ongoing management of the insurance portfolio. The CCMA may conduct reference checks/
verification at any stage of evaluation.
3.3. Any misrepresentation will result in a potential bidder being disqualified and excluded from any
further involvement in this process.
