TENDER NOTICE
Tenders are hereby invited for the Works included in the following Contract which is to be implemented by the
Richards Bay Industrial Development Zone SOC Ltd (RBIDZ). It is estimated that only Tenderers who are registered
with the CIDB as Grade 5CE or higher Contractors shall be eligible to Tender for:
TENDER NO. 1A/ZDO/038/26: MAINTENANCE OF CIVIL ENGINEERING AND RELATED INFRASTRUCTURE IN
PHASE 1A
Tender documents together will be available as from 31 August 2026 to 10 September 2026 and will be emailed upon
an indication of interest and submission of proof of payment of the tender charge from the prospective tenderer. A non-
refundable cash payment of R500.00 paid into the RBIDZ’s bank account, namely Absa, Branch Code: 632005
Account No.: 4072219190. Proof of Payments should be emailed to tenders@rbidz.co.za. No Tender documents will
be sold after 10:30 am on the date of the compulsory briefing meeting.
Tenders are to be completed in accordance with the conditions attached to the documents and must be sealed and
endorsed with the relevant Contract number and must be personally deposited in the official Tender box at the RBIDZ,
RBIDZ Complex, 4 Harbour Arterial, Alton, Richards Bay not later than 12:00 on 29 September 2026, at which time
Tenders will be opened in public.
A compulsory briefing meeting and site inspection will be conducted by the RBIDZ on 10 September 2026 at 11:00 on
site at RBIDZ Phase 1A Zone, GPS 28°46'43.58"S, 32°3'56.48"E). The Employer will not be available for inspection
purposes on any other occasion. Tenderers are required to bring their Tender documents to the site inspection for
signature. Tenderers without original Tender documents and Tenderers with original documents arriving after
11:00 will not be allowed to attend the briefing meeting. Tenderers are to bring along their own PPE and without
PPE there will be no entry allowed to site. All queries relating to this Tender and documents must be forwarded via
email to tenders@rbidz.co.za.
Tenders will first be evaluated in terms of functionality, and thereafter price and preference on an 80/20 system as
contemplated in the Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act and must score a minimum of 60% on functionality to
be considered further in terms of price and preference. Tenderers who do not obtain the minimum functionality scoring
will be regarded as non-responsive that will lead to the disqualification of the Tender Submission. It is a condition of
tender that only tenderers that are EMEs or QSEs and are B-BBEE Level 1 shall be eligible to tender. It is also
a condition of Tender that a Tenderer makes provision for involving emerging contractors through a competitive
process from the RBIDZ list of pre-qualified emerging contractors.
The RBIDZ is not bound to accept the lowest price, highest scoring or any Tender and reserves the right to accept
or not to accept any Tender in whole or part thereof. The RBIDZ reserves the right to appoint a Tenderer that best
meets the requirements of the company on reasonable grounds.
Telegraphic, telephonic, telex, electronic, e-mailed and late Tenders will not be accepted.
