1. PURPOSE
The Directorate: Institutional Capacity Building and NPO Support seeks to appoint a suitably qualified, accredited, and experienced
training provider to facilitate the accredited NQF Level 5 Train-the-Trainer programme, “Facilitation of Learning Using a Variety
of Given Methodologies,” for sixty (60) Social Services Practitioners. The appointed provider must be accredited to offer training
aligned with the relevant SAQA-recognised qualification and unit standard requirements. In particular, the provider should possess
accreditation linked to SAQA Qualification ID: Further Education and Training Certificate: Youth Development, which
incorporates the required unit standard.
The programme aims to strengthen the facilitation, training, and learning delivery competencies of Social Services Practitioners to
enhance capacity-building initiatives and service delivery within the social development sector.
1. PROJECT SCOPE
The appointed service provider will be required to:
• Deliver an accredited NQF Level 5 training programme in Facilitation of Learning Using a Variety of Given Methodologies.
• Train sixty (60) Social Services Practitioners over a period of three (3) consecutive days
• Provide all learning and assessment materials required for the programme.
• Conduct pre-assessment orientation and learner support.
• Facilitate theoretical and practical learning sessions.
• Conduct formative and summative assessments in line with the relevant Quality Council and SETA requirements.
• Moderate assessments internally and arrange external moderation where required.
• Provide administrative support for learner registration, attendance management, portfolio development, and certification
processes.
• Submit comprehensive training reports and learner achievement records to the Department.

Programme Target Group
Programme 01 District Finance Officials
Programmes 02–04 Social Work Coordinators
Programme 05 Community Development Practitioners
Head Office Institutional Capacity Building and NPO Support Officials

strengthening the Department's capacity to provide training and support to NPOs and community structures.
The training will be conducted at a government-owned facility within the Durban metropolitan area. The proposed venue is the
Wentworth Youth Development Centre, Durban, which is adequately equipped to accommodate the training requirements of the
programme and provide a conducive learning environment for participants.
