SUBJECT : REQUEST FOR SERVICE – FOR THE PROCUREMENT OF
IMPLEMENTATION PARTNERS FOR THE SAP S/4HANA MIGRATION
PROJECT (PHASE 2)
PURPOSE:
1. The purpose of this memorandum is to request approval from the General Manager
Facilities and Infra Structure Management to proceed with the procurement processes
to obtain implementation partners/service providers for the implementation of the SAP
S/4HANA Migration Project, commonly known as SAP S/4HANA Phase 2.
BACKGROUND:
1. Transnet Engineering has been making use of Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
software since its first SAP ERP modules were implemented in 1997. Since the initial
implementation, the organization has continued to add ERP solutions to its landscape
such that the ERP software is now pervasive in all areas of the organization.
1. The ERP system at Transnet Engineering needs to be technically upgraded and
prepared for the eventual migration from the current SAP ECC6 version to S/4HANA.
1

DISCUSSION/ MOTIVATION:
1. Project CAPEX funding has already been approved and allocated to Transnet
Engineering by Transnet Group the implementation process.
1. Transnet Engineering seeks to appoint a competent and qualified Service Provider or
an Implementation partner for the services:
• Data Volume Management (DVM)
• Migration the current SAP ECC6 applications to the SAP S/4HANA Platform
1. As part of the governance process, a go-ahead is requested for the project team to go
into an open market to source the services of a qualified implementation partner.
FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS:
1. The Capex in the amount of R60,000,000 (sixty million rand) has been duly approved.
An amount of R5,818,132.74 (five million eight hundred and eighteen thousand one
hundred and thirty-two rand and seventy-four cents), designated for contingency and
representing a shortfall against the approved business case, shall, if and to the extent
required, be made available at the project award stage. Such funding shall be sourced
through Transnet Engineering (TE) funds reallocation and shall be subject to and in full
compliance with all applicable accounting standards, governance frameworks, and
requisite approval processes.
BUDGET IMPLICATIONS:
1. Capital expenditure (Capex) funding amounting to R60,000,000 (sixty million rand)
excluding VAT), has been approved by the Transnet Group Investment Committee for
the implementation of this project, excluding the contingency allocation of
R5,818,132.74 (five million eight hundred and eighteen thousand one hundred and
2

thirty-two rand and seventy-four cents), which will be made available when required
at a later stage after the award, in necessary.
RECOMMENDATION:
1. It is therefore recommended that the General Manager: Facilities and Infrastructure
Management approve the procurement of services required for the implementation of
the SAP S/4HANA Migration (Phase 2) project, which forms part of the Transnet Group
SAP S/4HANA Modernisation Programme. This approval is recommended to ensure the
continuation of the modernisation initiative, enable improved system performance and
operational efficiency, mitigate risks associated with legacy systems, and support the
organisation’s strategic objective of digital transformation.
