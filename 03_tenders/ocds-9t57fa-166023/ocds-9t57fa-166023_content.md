1. INTRODUCTION
The Mpumalanga Economic Growth Agency, hereinafter referred to as “MEGA” is a
schedule 3D entity of the Mpumalanga Provincial Government with a mandate of
stimulating economic growth in the Province through inter alia:-
(i) the provision of funding to SMMEs, Co-operatives and Agricultural enterprises,
as well as the provision of housing loans to citizens of Mpumalanga within the
gap market;
(ii) proactively identifying investment opportunities within the Province, attracting
investment into such opportunities through various trade and investment
promotion initiatives, as well as facilitating that such investments into the
province derive maximum development impact;
(iii) delivering massive infrastructure within the Province;
(iv) the development and management of property in the form of industrial parks
which stimulate economic activity in the Province.
In exercising its powers and functions as articulated in the Act, [“the MEGA Act 1 of
2010”], the entity must continually broaden the participation of Mpumalanga citizens
in all the sectors of the economy and accordingly contribute, whether directly or
indirectly, to the alleviation of poverty, unemployment and inequality.

1. PURPOSE OF THE REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS (RFP)
The purpose of this Request for Proposal (“RFP”) is to solicit proposals from interested
suitably qualified, competent, experienced, and accredited training service providers for the
establishment of a panel of service providers for the provision of training, skills development
and support services for Mpumalanga Economic Growth Agency (“MEGA”) on an "as and
when required" basis for a period of three (3) years.
As part building capacity for employees, the Skills Development Act 97 of 1998 emphasizes
the improvement of skills of workers by promoting education and training in the workplace.
In order to perform their activities, gain more knowledge, and develop skills in each unit, it is
of authoritative importance that all officials be equipped with training that is in line with their
job descriptions.
The issuance of this RFP does not constitute a commitment to award a contact or pay any
costs incurred in the preparation of this RFP. Appointment to the panel does not guarantee
any minimum work or income. Training interventions will be commissioned according to
organisational requirements, budget availability, and operational priorities.
1. OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
The objectives of this appointment are to:
I. Establish a panel of qualified training service providers capable of delivering quality
learning interventions.
II. Enhance employee skills, competencies, and performance through structured learning
programmes.
III. Ensure compliance with applicable skills development legislation and regulatory
requirements.
IV. Provide access to specialised training across various disciplines.
V. Improve efficiency in procuring training services.
