1. PURPOSE OF THE REQUEST FOR INFORMATION
The purpose of this Request for Information (RFI) is to obtain market insights to
support business decision-making for a once-off Request for Tender relating to Under
Pressure Drilling services.
2. BACKGROUND
Johannesburg Water invites suitably qualified service providers to submit information
for a solution aimed at under-pressure drilling on a live operational water reticulation
pipeline without interrupting the flow or supply. the under-pressure of miscellaneous
pipe materials and sizes for a period of 36 months.
This RFI is issued solely to gather market-related information from potential bidders.
It does not constitute an offer, commitment, or any obligation to enter into a business
agreement with Johannesburg Water
3. SCOPE OF WORK AND SPECIFICATIONS REQUIREMENTS
3.1 LOCATION OF SITES
Depots – all regions.
3.2 SPECIFICATION & SCOPE OF WORK
Provide Under Pressure Drill services on miscellaneous pipe sizes and materials.
The pressure rating of 16 bar on all pipe supplied pipe fittings, the rates are to
include the supply of all materials, labour, equipment, plant, safety measures, etc, to
complete the works successfully.
Drilling holes for T-piece installation into under-pressure flowing water pipe systems
without leaking or interrupting the water system flows (Wet tapping).
Pipe materials and sizes applicable are indicated in classification of
their prevalence within the water network.
mPVC CL16 (SANS 966-Part 2) DN 20mm – DN600mm, mPVC CL16 (High Impact)
(SANS 1283) DN 20mm – DN500, AC Pipes DN 63mm - DN 450mm, HDPE PE100
PN 16 SDR 11 To SANS ISO 4427 DN 20mm-600mm (Butt welding or Electro-fusion

to SANS 0269), Steel pipe Grade 42 to SANS 719 – DN 900mm and above, OPVC
CL16 to SANS 16422 – DN 63mm – DN 700mm.
The required material and accessories
Cutting head size of all available ranges, Clamp coating -250-micron fusion
bonded epoxy, Hole drilling saw, Drilling ratchet.
Isolation valve (clockwise closing) RSV’s type size up to and including 150mm,
and for bigger sizes the Wedge Type Gate valves are used and should adhere
to SANS 664. Note that the valves must be manufactured in accordance with
the local content requirement of 70% and be SANS 664 certified with the
certification bodies mark of approval on the valve.
