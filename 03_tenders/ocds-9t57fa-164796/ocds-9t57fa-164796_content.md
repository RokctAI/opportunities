SPECIFICATION T 2
1. PROJECT SCOPE AND SCHEDULE OF WORK
BACKGROUND AND CONTEXT
Nkomazi Municipality has a responsibility to render pauper and indigent burial service to
qualifying household who are indigent and unknown persons of Nkomazi Community hence
the request for a panel of funeral parlours which will render the services at a reasonable cost
on behalf of the municipality.
1. SCOPE
The scope of work is provision of burial services that are in line with the Indigent and Paupers Burial
Policy of the Nkomazi Municipality as well as Health Standards as Regulated by the relevant
legislations.
• To render pauper burial on behalf of Nkomazi Local Municipality
• The service provider should be available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week.
• The service provider shall provide after hours’ emergency service and support to Nkomazi Local
Municipality on a dedicated phone number (mobile or land line)
• The potential service provider should demonstrate the following key competences:
• Proof of registration as mortuary
• Proof of having funeral parlour within the jurisdiction of Nkomazi Local Municipality area.
• Valid certificate of compliance from District Municipality Health Section
• Proven record in providing pauper and indigent service for a for municipalities or government.
1. NATURE OF THE CONTRACT
This is a thirty-six (36) months contract in which a maximum of six (6) appointed service providers will
be appointed to the panel to render pauper burial services on behalf of Nkomazi Local Municipality. The
Municipality intends to apply a three (3) stage evaluation process in the adjudication of this tender,
namely:
1. Stage One (1): Administrative Compliance and Pre-Qualification
Evaluation of compliance with all mandatory administrative requirements and pre-qualification criteria as
stipulated in the tender document.
1. Stage Two (2): Technical Evaluation
Evaluation of functionality, experience, expertise, capacity, and any other technical criteria relevant to
the respective work package.
