• Maintenance of traffic signal installations;
• Installation and replacement of traffic signal poles;
• Installation and termination of traffic signal cables;
• Detector loop installations and repairs;
• Installation and replacement of lanterns and pushbuttons;
• Installation and maintenance of traffic signal controllers;
• Emergency response to traffic signal knockdowns and faults;
• Excavation, backfilling and reinstatement works;
• Testing, commissioning and certification of installations.

1. TRAFFIC SIGNAL CABLE TERMINATIONS
3.1 Traffic signal cable terminations shall include 2-core, 4-core, 8-core, 12-core, 24-
core, 27-core and 37-core cables or combinations thereof.
3.2 All cables shall be terminated in accordance with the approved phase allocation
drawings and intersection specification sheets.
3.3 Strip connectors and mounting boards may be supplied by the Municipality unless
otherwise specified.
3.4 Cable terminations shall comply with the following requirements:
• Traffic phases shall be terminated in sequence;
• Pedestrian phases shall follow traffic phases;
• Pushbutton common cores shall be clearly separated;
• Unused cores shall be neatly terminated;
• A minimum slack of 1 meter shall be provided within each pole;
• Cable armouring and sheathing shall be removed neatly without damaging
conductors;
• Earth continuity conductors shall remain intact and properly bonded.
3.5 All terminations shall be tightly secured and clearly identifiable.
1. CONTROLLER TERMINATIONS
4.1 All metallic components and cable armouring shall be properly bonded and earthed.
4.2 Controller wiring shall comply with approved wiring schedules and phase
allocations.
4.3 All cable connections shall be secure and properly labelled.

4.4 Earth electrode resistance shall not exceed 2 ohms.

SARTSM requirements.

appointed Representative.
5.3 The Contractor shall provide suitable bedding material where existing material is
unsuitable.
5.4 Reinstatement of roads, sidewalks, kerbs and paved areas shall be undertaken to
municipal standards.

5.6 Temporary reinstatement shall be maintained until permanent reinstatement is
completed.
5.7 The Contractor shall remove all rubble and surplus material from the site
immediately upon completion of the works.
1. INSTALLATION OF DETECTOR LOOPS
6.1 Detector loops shall be installed using approved road-cutting equipment.
6.2 Slots shall generally be:
• 6 mm wide;
• 30 mm to 40 mm deep.
6.3 Loop wire shall be installed in accordance with approved loop layouts.
6.4 Loop wire joints within the loop itself shall not be permitted.
6.5 All loops shall be tested prior to sealing and commissioning.
6.6 Loop slots shall be sealed using approved epoxy or loop sealant materials.
6.7 The Contractor shall ensure minimal disruption to traffic during installation.
6.8 The Contractor shall provide traffic accommodation and safety measures.
