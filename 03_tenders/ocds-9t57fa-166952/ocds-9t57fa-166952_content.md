JOB EVALUTION PRICING SCHEDULE
1. PRICING SCHEDULE
Bidders must provide firm pricing for all deliverables. Rates must be expressed per job (for evaluation
and job description work) and per hour (for facilitation, presentations, and advisory services).
Note: Pricing Schedule and the Technical Proposal must be delivered separately. If the Pricing
Schedule is including in the Technical Proposal, the bidder will be disqualified in participating in the
tender process.
Note: For ad hoc services, the unit rate quoted will apply for the full five (5)-year contract period. The
service provider must provide the annual escalation percentage for the pricing. No rate escalation
will be permitted without prior written agreement from the DBSA.
