What Is Train the Trainer?
• Following the walkthrough sessions, CRP validations sessions configuration discussions, the
existing processes were validated, and necessary configuration changes were made and
processes and functionality confirmed.
• In this phase (TTT), we train the subject matter experts (SME) and potential instructors and
enable them to train other application users in SITA.
• Following these sessions, the participants need to practice in a training instance and impart
training to the end users.
