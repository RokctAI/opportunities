SPECIFICATION
1. PURPOSE
The main purpose of this Request for Proposals (RFQs) is to appoint a suitably qualified service
provider and implementing partner to:
- Render Project Management services to conceptualise, mobilise partners and resources
(financial and non-financial) to promote key economic activities and niche industries as well
as investment into the Inner City;
- Coordinate premium activities / events and projects that promote the niche economic
activities in the inner city;
- Revive the inner city economic revitalisation as part of the Inner City Economic
Development and Investment Roadmap and other interventions such as the Presidential
Johannesburg Working Group on Inner City and Townships; and
- Increased competitiveness and innovation capacity of identified clusters.
2. LOCATION
This project is targeted for the confines of the Johannesburg Inner City (CBD). The Inner City of
Johannesburg is important due to its central location in respect of the Gauteng City Region and the
City of Johannesburg. The Gauteng City Region is the highest provincial contributor of South
Africa’s Gross Domestic Product (GDP) at 34%. The City of Johannesburg economy plays a
central role within the Gauteng City Region although agglomeration economies are also significant,
specifically the close proximity of Johannesburg, Tshwane and Ekurhuleni.
The focus is on three macro interventions namely spatial reconfiguration, township economy
revitalisation and massive infrastructure investment. To this end five development corridors are
planned one of which is a Central Development Corridor which sees the City of Johannesburg as
the hub of finance, services, ICT and pharmaceutical industries, with a strong focus on
regenerating the Inner City.

events with the aim of showcasing areas of excellence in business and investment, tourism, urban
management and rejuvenation interventions.
