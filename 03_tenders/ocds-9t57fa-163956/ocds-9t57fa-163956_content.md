1. INTRODUCTION
The Media, Information and Communication Technologies Sector Education and Training
Authority (MICT SETA) is a public entity established in terms of Section 9(1) of the Skills
Development Act (Act No. 97 of 1998). The MICT SETA plays a pivotal role in achieving South
Africa’s skills development and economic growth within the 5 distinct sub-sectors it operates in,
i.e., Advertising, Film and Electronic Media, Electronics, Information Technology, and
Telecommunications. To deliver on its mandate, key amongst the priorities of the organisation is:
The MICT SETA has adopted digital transformation initiatives to enhance operational efficiency,
ensure regulatory compliance, and support a paperless working environment. One of the critical
enablers of this digital transition is the planned implementation of an automated Declaration of
Interests and Gift Register System for a period of three (3) years.
This system will serve as a critical governance and compliance tool to enhance transparency,
accountability, and ethical conduct within the organization of digital governance and
efficiency.
1. BACKGROUND
The MICT SETA operates within a regulated public sector environment and is required to
comply with governance frameworks, including:
• The Public Finance Management Act (PFMA);
• The Code of Conduct for Public Servants;
• Auditor-General South Africa (AGSA) audit requirements; and
• Internal governance, risk, and compliance policies.
Currently, the management of declarations of interests and gifts involves manual and semi-
automated processes, which pose risks such as:
• Incomplete or inaccurate disclosures;
• Limited audit trails;
• Delays in reporting and verification; and
• Increased risk of conflicts of interest going undetected.

1. PURPOSE
The MICT SETA would like to invite suitably qualified and experienced service providers to submit
proposals for the supply, implementation, customization, and support of an automated
Declaration of Interests and Gift Register System for a period of three (3) years. The
implementation of the system will enable the MICT SETA to:
• Implement a fully automated system for declarations of interest and gift management;
• Enhance governance, compliance, and ethical accountability;
• Provide real-time visibility and reporting to management and auditors;
• Ensure secure, auditable, and tamper-proof records; and
• Align with digital transformation and paperless initiatives.
