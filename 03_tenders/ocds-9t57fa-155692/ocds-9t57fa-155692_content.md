10) A programme to accelerate transformation in the construction industry, increase the capacity of the
construction industry to deliver infrastructure and support the growth of emerging contractors, was
launched on 14 March 2024 by the Deputy Minister of Public Works and Infrastructure and the
Construction Industry Development Board.
Details of the B.U.I.L.D. Programme were published in a Government Gazette in 2020 (GG 43726) and
B.U.I.L.D. has gradually been phased in at various levels of government and the private sector. The
CIDB, a public entity with the mandate to promote improved performance in construction, oversees the
programme and manages the B.U.I.L.D Fund.
The B.U.I.L.D programme determines that public sector entities which implement construction
projects, that meet certain minimum requirements, must include developmental goals to the
deliverables defined in the tenders. If applicable, Contractors are required to include these goals in the
plans and pricing when they submit their tender bids.

C3.1: PROJECT DESCRIPTION AND SCOPE OF CONTRACT
C3.1.1 Description of Works
The proposed construction of a 36 m reinforced concrete pedestrian bridge over the Ohlanga
River in the Amaoti, Zambia area is in response to the river widening to approximately 30 m. The
existing 12 m steel pedestrian bridge, supported on gabion abutments, has been severely
undermined and is being washed away, now terminating within the stream of the Ohlanga River.
As a result, the current crossing is unsafe and no longer serves the community effectively.
The new pedestrian bridge will provide a safe and reliable crossing, significantly improving
connectivity for local residents. It will facilitate access to essential services, including public
transportation (minibuses and municipal buses), schools, healthcare facilities, and local economic
opportunities. This intervention will enhance mobility, improve access to social services, and
support economic participation within the community.
Scope of Work:
• Site establishment and clearance
• Setting out of the works and survey control
• Construction of a 36 m span reinforced concrete pedestrian bridge, including:
o Piling foundation and reinforced concrete abutments
o Centre Pier
o Reinforced concrete deck slab
o Parapets beams
• River diversion and temporary crossing works
• Earthworks and approach paths to the bridge
• Gabion protection work
• Dismantling of the existing 12 m steel pedestrian bridge
• Removal of existing gabion abutments
• Transportation of salvaged bridge components to a new site
• Construction of new abutments at the relocation site
• Reinstallation and commissioning of the steel pedestrian bridge at the new location
• Site rehabilitation and environmental restoration
C3.1.2 Description of Site and Access
The site is located in the North of Durban, in the Amaoti area (29°41'22.68"S; 30°58'30.80"E)

PS.20 Fairing Compound
A fairing compound defines the required characteristics, application methods, and performance standards for
a filler material—usually epoxy or polyester-based—used to smooth surface imperfections on boats,
composites, or concrete before painting. These specifications typically cover adhesion strength, density, sag
resistance, and curing times.
Key Aspects of Fairing Compound Specifications:
• Type & Composition: Generally two-component systems (resin + hardener/catalyst) incorporating
lightweight fillers like hollow microspheres to ensure ease of sanding.
• Substrate Compatibility: Often specified for use on fiberglass (GRP), steel, aluminum, wood, or
concrete.
• Physical Properties:
o Density: Lightweight formulations (often < 1.0 g/cm³) are preferred for marine applications
to minimize added weight.
o Sag Resistance: Crucial specification for vertical or overhead application; high-quality
compounds often exhibit no sagging up to 20-35mm thickness.
o Shrinkage: Low to no shrinkage (100% solids) is required to ensure the surface remains
level after curing.
o Sandability: Must produce a smooth, easily sanded surface that does not clog sandpaper.
• Application Requirements:
o Temperature: Surface and ambient temperatures must generally be at least +5°C, with an
ideal range of 20°C–35°C.
o Surface Prep: Surfaces must be clean, dry, and free from contaminants, typically requiring
grit blasting (Sa 2 ½) for steel or sanding (80-100 grit) for fiberglass.
o Mixing Ratio: Precise volumetric or weight ratios (e.g., 1:1 or 2:1) are required for proper
curing.
