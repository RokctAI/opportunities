### Special Conditions
Tenders are hereby invited from Contractors with a CIDB grading of 7 CE/ 7ME or higher for the UPGRADE OF MOORDKUIL RAW WATER PUMP STATION. Contractors to use unskilled local labourers according to the EPWP method and regulations. Contractors to sub-contract a minimum of 5% of the contract to a local EME, QSE or Level 1 B-BBEE registered contractor with a CIDB grading of up to 3CE in the discipline relevant to the subcontracted works.
