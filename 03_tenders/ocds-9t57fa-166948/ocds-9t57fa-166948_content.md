1. INTRODUCTION
2.1 Over the past decade, the Government of South Africa through the Department of Electricity
and Energy (the “Department”) has been undertaking the Independent Power Producers
Procurement (IPPP) Programmes for the establishment of private sector participation and
investment in the generation of electricity. The IPPP Programme has been running for over
10 years.
2.2 Ministerial Determinations are the instrument utilized by the Department to implement the
Integrated Resource Plan (IRP). The 2025 IRP was promulgated in October 2025 and new
Determinations will give effect to the future procurement rounds under the IPPP
Programmes.
