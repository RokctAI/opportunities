Description of the The services are for the Capital Allocation team within Eskom Group,
works/goods/services responsible for The Evaluation, Prioritisation, And Recommendation of Capital
Investment Proposals. The training will support team members involved in
capital planning, investment analysis, and preparation of reports submitted to
EXCO and the Investment and Finance Committee (IFC), as well as other
stakeholders involved in capital budgeting and strategic resource allocation
processes.
Description of Service/ product that needs to be provided
Provision of capital allocation training for Capital Allocation team employees.
The training will cover:
1. Capital allocation frameworks and strategic decision-making.
2. Evaluation of investment proposals using ROI, NPV, IRR, and payback
methods.
1. Scenario planning and risk-based decision-making.
2. Prioritisation of competing capital investments
3. Development and presentation of strategic business cases.
4. Governance and accountability in capital allocation processes.
Scope of Work/Supply
The scope of this request is the provision of targeted capital allocation
training aimed at enhancing the analytical, financial evaluation, and strategic
decision-making capabilities of the Capital Allocation team. The training will

The training is expected to be delivered through a structured learning

Specification of Product or Goods
Specification of the service is the provision of capital allocation training aimed
at building technical and strategic competency in capital investment decision-
making within the Capital Allocation team.
The training must include structured content on capital allocation principles
and frameworks, investment appraisal techniques (including ROI, NPV, IRR,
and payback period), scenario and risk analysis, prioritisation of competing
capital projects, and development of robust capital investment
business cases. The programme should also cover governance and
accountability in capital allocation decision-making, including transparent
evaluation and reporting of investment decisions.
The training should be delivered as a structured, facilitator-led programme
incorporating both theoretical instruction and practical, case-based
application exercises. Learning activities should enable participants to apply
concepts to real-world capital planning and investment evaluation
scenarios relevant to the organisation. The training must conclude with
certification of completion for all participants.
