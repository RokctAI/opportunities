close of business.
2.8 The Joburg Market distributes monies due to the Producers into the Agents’ trust accounts, on a daily
basis.

2.10 The Joburg Market collects monies on behalf of other parties and thus have merchant/speed
points fees applicable.
2.11 As a government institution, the Joburg Market is required to only work with funds that are reflected
