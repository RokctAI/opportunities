INTRODUCTION TO MHSC
The Mine Health and Safety Council (MHSC) is a national public entity (Schedule 3A)
established in terms of the Mine Health and Safety Act, No. 29 of 1996, as amended. The
MHSC is mandated to advise the Minister of the Department of Mineral and Petroleum
Resources (DMPR) on mine health and safety (MHS) issues in the mining industry, relating to
the development and implementation of the MHSC annual MHS research programme,
reviewing and development of MHS legislation and dissemination (knowledge and technology
transfer) of MHSC research outcomes to improve MHS conditions in the South African Mining
Industry (SAMI).
The MHSC Website is built in WordPress, and the e-Library is developed on Linux, Apache,
MySQL, PHP (LAMP) stack. They are custom designed platforms that support information
dissemination while promoting interaction and collaboration amongst key users/stakeholders.
The MHSC Website serves as one of MHSC’s core business systems that interfaces large
number of stakeholders through the internet, whilst the MHSC e-Library is used as a
centralized repository for storing, managing, and providing access to research publications,
research reports, Mine Health and Safety guidelines, and other knowledge resources,
enabling engagement with a large number of the SAMI stakeholders via the internet. Thus,
the website and e-Library should be maintained, available and performing at all times for the
upkeep of the good name of the MHSC.
CONTEXT OF PROCUREMENT
The MHSC requires the services of a service provider to perform minor theming (as in when
required), support and maintain the current website and the e-Library, using some of the
existing content as well as newly updated content but with the revised and updated look and
feel. The website and the e-Library must continue to clearly communicate the objective of
MHSC as well as highlighting key projects, research reports, events, newsletters, subscription
to portal activities, and relevant resources.
