1. BACKGROUND
The Eastern Cape Department of Public Works & Infrastructure provides support to Micro, Small,
Medium and Macro Enterprises (MSMEs) within built environment to be established companies
within the Eastern Cape Province.
MSMEs are critical to the departments’ construction, maintenance, infrastructure support,
localization and property development objectives. However, many MSMEs face systematic
barriers, including:
i) Inability to meet collateral requirements
ii) Insufficient credit score history or financial track record
iii) Weak or poorly packaged funding applications
iv) High perceived risk by commercial banks
v) High cost of capital and unfavorable lending terms
vi) Long approval cycles from Development Finance Institutions
vii) Lack of bridging finance during project execution.
These barriers expose the Department to operational risks such as delayed project delivery,
underspending and non-achievement of transformation targets.
1. OBJECTIVES
To address the challenges indicated above, the Department of Public Works and Infrastructure
(DPWI) wishes to establish a 3-year dedicated Panel of Financiers to submit their interest in
providing structured financing, advisory services, and risk mitigation support to MSMEs that are
participating in departmental programmes, including:
• Integrated Contractor Development Programme (ICDP)
• Property Incubator Programme
• Development of Material Suppliers and Manufacturers
The Panel will ensure immediate, accessible, de-risked, and developmentally aligned funding,
supporting transformation, localization, and contractor development objectives.
1. SCOPE OF WORK
3.1. Successful panel members will:
i) Provide direct finance (loans, bridging finance, asset finance, contract-backed finance)
ii) Offer financial advisory and investment readiness services
iii) Offer financial management training
iv) Support MSMEs with credit readiness, financial management, and record keeping
v) Implement structured risk mitigation instruments (e.g. cessions, direct disbursements,
guarantees)
vi) Track funded MSMEs’ performance, provide quarterly reports to DPWI
vii) Facilitate payments where applicable, including workers’ stipends, as per project milestones
