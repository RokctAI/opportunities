SECTION 2
BACKGROUND OVERVIEW AND SCOPE REQUIREMENTS
1. INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND
The Housing Development Agency (HDA) is a national public sector development agency that, inter alia, has the
mandate to identify, acquire, hold, prepare and develop suitable land for human settlements. The agency also
undertakes the programme and project management for development of housing and human settlements. The
HDA carries out its functions in partnership with a range of stakeholders, including national, provincial and local
government and municipalities, as well as with communities, developers and financiers. Established in 2009, the
Agency was established by an Act of Parliament in 2008 and is accountable through its Board to the Minister of
Human Settlements. For more information about the HDA, please visit our website: www.thehda.co.za.
The HDA has, in the past years, repositioned itself as an implementation agent for government; packaging and
delivering human settlements projects on behalf of both national, provincial and local government.
•The main purpose of the invitation to tender is to appoint a suitable Service Provider that can render cleaning and
hygiene services to the HDA at 4 Kikuyu Road, Sunninghill, Johannesburg for a period of thirty-six (36) months.
•The service provider will further be required to provide cleaning equipment as well as cleaning and hygiene
consumables monthly.
1. SCOPE OF WORK AND AREAS OF FOCUS
The appointed service provider will be required to provide cleaning and hygiene services to the HDA Head Office
as per Section 5.1 below. The size of the office is approximately 3500 sqm with about 180 parking spaces.
• The service provider will be required to provide six (6) cleaning staff working on a daytime rotational
shift basis of forty-five (45) hours per week.
• The Service Provider must submit a cleaning schedule, with the bid, ensuring that all vacuuming and
polishing of offices is completed by 08h00 daily.
• The service provider will be required to provide monthly cleaning and hygiene services as per Section
5.1 below.
• The service provider will be required to provide equipment that is in a good working order for the
purpose for which it is intended as per Section 5.2 below.
• The service provider will be required to provide cleaning and hygiene consumables as set out in per
Section 5.3 below.
GENERAL DUTIES OF THE SERVICE PROVIDER:

The service provider will be required to ensure the following:
• The Service Provider is required to appoint a supervisor who will meet with the HDA Representative on a
weekly basis to receive feedback on services rendered.
• CV’s for all proposed employees to be contracted to the HDA are to be submitted with the bid, together with
criminal checks and South African Identity documents.
• The Service Provider’s training and induction plan for the allocated resources must be submitted with the
bid.
• The Service Provider must submit proof that the company is a member of the Bargaining Council for the
Contract Cleaning Services Industry.
• Rates to be paid by the Service Provider must be at least at the minimum wage gazetted at R33.27 per
hour - R1,497.15 per week (based on 45 hours worked per week)
• The salary adjustment must be made annually in line with all gazetted changes. Evidence must be
