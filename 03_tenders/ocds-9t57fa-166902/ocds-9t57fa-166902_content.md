SECTION 3: BACKGROUND, OVERVIEW AND SCOPE OF REQUIREMENTS
1 BACKGROUND
The Multi-Purpose Terminal (MPT) operates seven (7) Quayside berths with various cargo being exported or
discharged. This type of operations requires manpower (Labor) for cleaning within specified times. There are
various stockpiles and rail sidings that feed cargo to these berths with interchangeable or multiple loading zones
that require extensive cleaning.
2 EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Transnet Port Terminals is a division of Transnet SOC Limited whose core business is to provide cargo handling
to a wide spectrum of customers, including shipping lines, freight forwarders and cargo owners. Operations are
divided into five major business segments, namely containers, bulk, break bulk, automotive and national port
authority.
The Multi-Purpose Terminal (MPT) operations focus on the efficient loading bulk and break-bulk vessels across
the Terminals Seven Berths and various rail siding operations, offloading cargo via back actor operations. The
