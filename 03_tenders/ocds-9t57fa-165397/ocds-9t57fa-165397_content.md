PURPOSE
The purpose is to appoint a suitably qualified and experienced service provider to conduct a
detailed market demand study for the two (2) proposed projects sites: (i) Erf 3138 Witbank
Extension 16, Mpumalanga and (ii) Erf 13120 Queenstown (Komani), Eastern Cape, as follows:
Erf 3138 Witbank Extension 16, Mpumalanga
Undertake a detailed market demand study to determine the appropriate income, housing
typologies and mix in terms of residential development on the property. The surrounding
residential development typologies, mixes should be referenced and analysed to concretise the
recommended residential development for the property.
Erf 13120 Queenstown (Komani), Eastern Cape
Undertake a detailed market demand study to determine the appropriate affordability, housing
typologies and mix in terms of a student housing/accommodation development on the property.
The surrounding residential and student housing/accommodation development typologies,
mixes should be referenced and analysed to concretise the recommended development for the
property.

SCOPE OF WORKS
The scope of work for the appointed service provider includes but is not limited to the
activities indicated below. The successful service provider will be required to:
(a) Undertake a thorough macro and microeconomic market demand and supply
assessment of the Mpumalanga (i.e., Witbank/Emalahleni) and Eastern Cape (i.e.,
Queenstown/Komani) regions.
(b) Undertake a real demand study to determine a suitable target for a housing residential
development market for the proposed development in Witbank Extension 16 and a
suitable target for student accommodation in Queenstown (Komani), while taking into
consideration the mandate of the National Department of Human Settlements,
Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET), National Student Financial Aid
Scheme (NFSAS), policies, programme and funding.
(c) Compile comprehensive reports with data and information analysis, findings, conclusion,
and recommendations that include the following:
• Location delineation defining clearly the area of coverage and rationale including
what modes of transport, education and health amenities exist and are planned, and
link to potential beneficiaries.
• Location analysis defining what is the total need for various forms of affordable
housing and student housing/accommodation in the area. What is the size of a social
housing portfolio and any other housing responses if available such as student
housing, First Home Finance (FHF), etc. How is the current housing response
segmented e.g. what is the percentage of informal vs formal settlements, subsidized
vs privately funded, ownership vs rental, what is the land need to address current and
future housing needs;
• Economic Profiling. What is the size of each of the regional economies, the
dominant contributors, what are the current and future forecast growth and
performance patterns of the various segments of these economies (i.e., Witbank –
Mpumalanga and Queenstown – Eastern Cape). Provide an analysis of the economic
profile in relation to the provision of affordable housing, student
housing/accommodation and first home finance programme target market;
• Demographic Market overview – housing and student housing/accommodation
market area delineation and population size, household sizes, age profile, racial
profile, levels of education, employment status and what is the split of occupations in
which sectors, what are people choosing for dwelling types and tenure status, annual
household incomes, living standard measurement, level of indebtedness and
affordability, dominant household expenditure patterns, what percentage across all
these aspects represents the affordable housing, student housing and first home
finance programme target market; and affordability.
11

• Residential Market Analysis – South African affordable housing overview, local
residential market activity in the surrounding areas, residential demand modelling,
what is an overview of the residential market trends underlining the source market
area.
• Social Housing sector analysis – size of the existing portfolio, what is the size of
the planned and approved developments, what are the occupancy vs vacancy rates,
what are the average vacancy filling turnaround times, what are the unit types and
sizes for which the market shows most appetite.
• Student Housing sector analysis – size of the existing portfolio, what is the size of
the planned and approved developments, what are the occupancy vs vacancy rates,
what are the average vacancy filling turnaround times, what are the unit types and
sizes for which the market shows most appetite. Number of existing and planned
educational institutions within the study area.
• First Home Finance sector analysis – size of the existing portfolio, what is the size
of the planned and approved developments, what are the occupancy vs vacancy
rates, what are the average vacancy filling turnaround times, what are the unit types
and sizes for which the market shows most appetite.
• Up-market sector analysis – size of the existing portfolio, what is the size of the
planned and approved developments, what are the occupancy vs vacancy rates, what
are the average vacancy filling turnaround times, what are the unit types and sizes
for which the market shows most appetite.
• How these properties are aligned to strategic initiatives for the Department of Human
Settlements.
