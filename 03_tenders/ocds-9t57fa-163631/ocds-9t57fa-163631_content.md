The Government of the Republic of South Africa through its Department of Science, Technology and
Innovation (“DSTI”) has received grant funding support from the Government of the Federal Republic
of Germany through its Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development ("BMZ") for the
"Support of Vaccine Development and Production in South Africa" Project. The Project provides
equipment and infrastructure (including construction and Information technology) support with the
purpose of strengthening vaccine research, development, pre-clinical testing, pilot-scale production and
regulation in South Africa. KfW Development Bank implements the project on behalf of the BMZ and
the South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC) implements the project on behalf of the DSTI.
The South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC) is a national public entity as listed in Schedule
3A of the Public Finance Management Act, 1999 (PFMA). The SAMRC has been established through
the South African Medical Research Council Act, 1991. The SAMRC’s mission is “to improve the
nation's health and quality of life through promoting and conducting relevant and responsive health
research”. The SAMRC’s head office is in Cape Town (Western Cape Province) and it is also
represented in Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal Provinces through regional offices. The SAMRC has seven
campuses and several satellite research sites with diverse business activities i.e. offices, biochemistry
and microbiology laboratories, health research clinics, animal research centres, and tenants renting
space. Further information about SAMRC can be found on the webpage, www.mrc.ac.za.
One of the conditions of the grant is that all procurement under the Project will be implemented by the
SAMRC in compliance with the South African and German public sector procurement frameworks with
the fundamental principles of fair, equitable, transparent, competitive, and cost-effective procurement.
In this regard, Bidders should note that the equipment and infrastructure will be procured by the
SAMRC on behalf of Project Beneficiaries. Equipment and infrastructure contracts will be paid for
directly by SAMRC OR KfW to the suppliers / service providers.
The SAMRC commenced the implementation of the Project on 1 April 2024 and subsequently issued
Request for Applications inviting role-players in the vaccine production, research and development,
training and regulation industry to apply for equipment and infrastructure support under the Project. The
RFA processes concluded in December 2024.
The SAMRC invites bids from suitably qualified, technically competent and experienced service
providers for the Provision of a Backup and Archiving Storage Appliance for the South African
Products Regulatory Authority (SAHPRA) which is a Project Beneficiary.
