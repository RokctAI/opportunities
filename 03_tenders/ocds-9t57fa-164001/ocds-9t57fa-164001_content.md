o Different respondents might provide a very different suite of analytes. In this case, the

Quality Guidelines for Coastal Marine Waters: Natural Environment and Mariculture Use for
ad hoc chemical and microbiological laboratory analysis has been added into the amended
Annex C: Pricing Schedule to be made available to bidders via publication platforms on 17
August 2026.
