Arc Protection Personal Protective Equipment Specification and Standards
For the procurement of Arc Protection Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) in South Africa,
the technical specification should ensure compliance with SANS, IEC, ISO, and Occupational
Health and Safety (OHS) requirements from procurement through acceptance (buy-to-
handover). The PPE must be suitable for electrical workers performing work on LV and MV
systems (400 V to 11 kV and above) where arc flash hazards exist.

Recommended Technical Specification: Arc Flash Protective Clothing and PPE
Supply, deliver and commission complete Arc Flash PPE suitable for work on low- and
medium-voltage electrical systems. All PPE shall comply with the latest editions of SANS/IEC
61482-1-1, SANS/IEC 61482-1-2, SANS/IEC 61482-2, SANS 20345, SANS 50352, the
Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993, and applicable Electrical Machinery
Regulations. PPE shall be arc-rated in accordance with the Employer's Arc Flash Hazard
Assessment, with certified ELIM, ATPV and/or EBT values. The supplier shall provide full
type-test certificates, certificates of conformity, quality documentation, warranties, user
manuals, and training. Acceptance shall be subject to verification of compliance, inspection,
and successful submission of all required documentation.
