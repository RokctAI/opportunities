NOTICE T08/26/27
SUPPLY AND DELIVERY OF TRAFFIC & LAW ENFORCEMENT UNIFORM FOR THE PERIOD ENDING
30 JUNE 2027
Swartland Municipality invites tenders for the Supply and Delivery of Traffic & Law Enforcement Uniform
for the Period Ending 30 June 2027 as per the specifications stated in the official bid documentation.
The documents can be obtained from Jo-Maré van der Westhuizen at (022) 487-9700 or
vanderwesthuizenj@swartland.org.za during normal office hours at the Municipal Offices, Kerk Street,
Malmesbury. A non-refundable cash fee of R190.00 is payable to Swartland Municipality for collection of hard
copy tender documents. The tender document is available in electronic format on request, at no charge.
Quotations and all relevant documentation in a sealed envelope, clearly marked “Tender T08.26.27 –
“Supply and Delivery of Traffic & Law Enforcement Uniform” must be deposited in the tender box at
the Municipal Head Offices, on the cnr of Kerk and Voortrekker Street Malmesbury, before 12h00 on
Friday 28 August 2026. No late quotations, electronic or faxed tenders will be accepted.
Bids will be evaluated in accordance with the Council’s Preferential Procurement Policy and the attached
specifications. The 80/20-preference point system will be used for the evaluation of the contract. The
Preferential Procurement Policy will be available for perusal during normal office hours at the municipal offices.
The Swartland Municipality endorses and supports the Government’s Black Economic Empowerment
Programme and is committed to rectify the inequalities of the past. For bidders to qualify for the advantages
of the B-BBEE Act, they must thoroughly complete the attached Annexure MBD 6.1. There will be no
benefits if the schedule is not thoroughly completed. Preference points MUST also be claimed in
paragraph 6.1. Bidders are required to submit original and valid B-BBEE Status Level Verification
Certificates or certified copies thereof together with their bids, to substantiate their B-BBEE rating claims.
Bidders are also required to submit Proof of Locality in order to claim points for locality. This Bid is
subject to the General Conditions of Tender and if applicable, any other Special Conditions of Tender.
All Technical queries can be directed to Mr Perold Barron at 022-4879400.
Late tenders will not be accepted and the Municipality will not be compelled to accept the lowest or any tender.
The Municipality reserves the right to omit any part of the tender.
