1. INTRODUCTION
Limpopo Tourism Agency is a schedule 3c entity mandated in terms of Limpopo
Tourism Act of 2009, as amended, to amongst other things to promote and offer a
sustainable and diverse tourism experience through strategic marketing
(destination marketing) and support and facilitate tourism, trade and investment
development programmes, collaborations with stakeholders and sectors
transformation.
2. PURPOSE
The invitation to bid is intended to secure services of a service provider with a
capacity to design and construct exhibitions stands on behalf of Limpopo Tourism
Agency.
3. SPECIFICATIONS FOR THE BID:DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF EXHIBITION STANDS
Service providers are required to provide a comprehensive proposal on design and
construction of exhibition stands which will position and market a representative
tourism and travel offering of the Limpopo Province. The LTA will book and pay for
exhibition space in tourism exhibition and events. The Service provider is then
required to design and construct the stand as per requirements from the LTA. The
contract is for a period of thirty-six (36) months upon the date of signing.
The following constitute the main areas of delivery:
I. Develop, design and build the LTA exhibition stands as per the concept and
the requirements of the client.
II. Provide LTA with a turn-key solution to exhibitions and shows.
III. Pay attention to the brand manual guidelines as per the marketing needs of
the Limpopo Tourism Agency.
IV. Provide LTA with the floor plan as part of the design and execution plan of
the stands per exhibition.
V. Provide rate price for the square meters (m²) to be charged per consumer
and trade exhibition stand which will increase annually.
VI. Facilitate hospitality services during exhibitions/shows for the duration as
some venues offer exclusive deals.
VII. All infrastructure will need to comply with all relevant laws and regulations
including engineer safety certificate and public liability compliance.

4. STANDS APPEARANCE
The stand of each exhibition should be inviting, attractive and colourful with large
visual graphics which portray the characteristics of destination Limpopo. High
resolution graphics and logos will be provided by Limpopo Tourism Agency for
printing purposes and some of the images to be sourced from stock footage as and
when necessary.
5. STAND REQUIREMENTS
I. Each stand should be built in such a manner that it can be re-used. The service
provider shall be responsible for erecting, dismantling and storage of the stand
at and after every show/exhibition. Transportation and storage of stand
material is to be included in the bid amount
II. Collection and transportation of LTA promotional materials, branding to the
events when necessary.
III. The stand to include placement of furniture and appropriate décor.
IV. The design must be open plan and free flowing.
V. The exhibition space at trade shows/exhibitions is dependent on availability.
LTA has the right to choose the appropriate stand based on space availability at
the mentioned shows/exhibitions.
VI. Over and above the mentioned shows, where LTA is required to participate the
service provider will be engaged based on the contract. There may be added
requirements which will be shared with stand builder based on the m².
