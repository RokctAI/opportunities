1. BACKGROUND
The ARC developed new sweet potato varieties aimed at improving wilt disease
tolerance, dry matter content in combination with keeping ability, root quality and
suitability for agro-processing. These varieties show great potential for
commercialization (cultivation and processing). It is envisaged that the new varieties
will provide alternatives for the limited number of varieties currently available.
1. PURPOSE
The ARC requires the services of a service provider to assist in the evaluation and
commercialization of ARC sweet potato varieties. The scope of work should be
defined through the Expression of Interest to the Bid Evaluation Committee by
providing 1) a document with compulsory bid requirements; 2) expression of
interest form which should be completed and 3) catalogue of available varieties.
1. MOTIVATION/S FOR CONSIDERATION
Fusarium wilt is caused by Fusarium oxysporum, this disease spreads with planting
material and infected plants also infect the soil (soil-borne disease). Wilt disease can
cause severe yield loss in susceptible cultivars such as Blesbok. When cutting through
the stem of an infected plant, a reddish-brown discoloration of vascular tissues can be
seen. The leaves turn yellow, blacken and die. The most effective to curb the problem
is use of varieties which are more tolerant than Blesbok such as ARC-SP-11, ARC-SP-
12 and ARC-SP-14 (refer to Catalogue of Varieties). There are also cream and orange-
fleshed varieties with tolerance. Purple-fleshed (ARC-SP-17,18,19 and 20) and
orange-fleshed varieties (Bophelo, Khumo, ARC-SP-2, 5, 6, 7, 10 and 16) offer
excellent options for agro-processing (refer to Catalogue of Varieties).
