1. SCOPE
This document specifies technical requirements for the supply of steel materials to be delivered at
Transnet Engineering premises within South Africa, depending on individual item requirement at the
respective premises or plant.

1. SPECIFICATIONS AND TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Please refer to Annexure 1 Document number PD_BR_NAT_001A (Purchase Order Long
text Detailed Description) for individual items specification and technical requirements.
- Supplier can Supply EN 10028 P355NL1 in place of EN 10025 S355 J2 + N
- As minimum where an EN 10204 type 3.1 certificate is required from on a commercial
quality grade, atleast chemical composition must be supplied to EN 10204 type 3.1.
- Any Violation of the long text requirements on any material will result in the material
being rejected and send back to the supplier.
- If there is a material equivalence (Both Mechanical and Metallurgical) to a specific item
which a supplier want to propose as an alternative then the supplier must request for a
concession/approval before they can supply the item.
- First Article inspection should be sent and approved by Transnet Engineering before the
supplier can start supplying more quantities as specified.
- Raw Material tolerance shall be in accordance to EN 10029 Class B unless specifically
stated otherwise on the long Purchase Order text.
1. MATERIAL CERTIFICATE
An EN 10204:2004 type 3.1 inspection certificate must be supplied with every delivery and No rust or
corrosion on the surface will be allowed. The following must be clearly visible on the inspection
certificate:
- Product manufacturer company name
- Product manufacturer company address
- Authorisation of the certificate, i.e. certificate must be signed by the manufacturer inspection
representative independent of the manufacturing department.
- Designation of person signing for manufacturer
- Clearly stipulates that the inspection certificate is in accordance with EN 10204: 2004 type
3.1
- Certificate date or manufacture date
- Supplied product name
- Dimensions/sizes must be visible
- Packaged unit weight
- Package unit: Box/spool/bag
- Full EN ISO specification / classification
- Chemical analysis section according to EN 10204:2004 type 3.1, with specification
requirements and actual test results

1. STORAGE AND HANDLING
- Materials should be stored in dry areas and must be free of dust/corrosion and dimensions
that are within specification upon delivery to Transnet Engineering premises.
- No Mechanical Damages will be accepted upon delivery to Transnet Engineering.
1. DELIVERY CONDITIONS
Please note that delivery conditions may differ from item to item so it is important for the bidder to
refer to long text Purchase Order Description ( Annexure 1: Doc. No. PD_BR_NAT_001A) for the
delivery condition of specific items.

1. TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE
The bidder must have personnel in place able to offer technical assistance in case challenges are
encountered during the use of the product.
1. CONCLUSION
This document provides all technical requirements that a bidder must comply with prior to bidding or
supplying TE with raw material. In addition to all the requirements, the bidder must be aware that
should the brand name which they tender with change within the duration of a contract, then the
bidder will have to submit all test certificates including First Article Inspection at their own cost.
