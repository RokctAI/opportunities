Description of the services The scope of this project encompasses the implementation and operation of
the Mobile Health Clinic Programme across the designated provinces. The
programme shall provide accessible healthcare services through fully
equipped and adequately resourced mobile clinic units.
The Mobile Health Clinic and all healthcare professionals and support
personnel deployed on the mobile units must be appropriately qualified,
accredited, and compliant with the requirements of the National Department of
Health. The service provider shall ensure that all mobile clinics are duly
registered and licensed and operate in accordance with all applicable national,
provincial, and local healthcare regulations, standards, and legislative
requirements. Furthermore, all services must comply with relevant health,
safety, quality assurance, and clinical governance standards to ensure the
delivery of safe, effective, and professional healthcare services to
beneficiaries.
Provide fully equipped and adequately resourced Mobile Health Clinic services
across all nine provinces. Services will include a comprehensive health
screening questionnaire, optometry, vital signs assessment, respiratory health
evaluation, and the screening of lung conditions associated with exposure to
emissions and other environmental risk factors.
