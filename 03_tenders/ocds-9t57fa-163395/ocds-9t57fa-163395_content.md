REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
DUBE TRADEPORT CORPORATION
PROVISION OF ICT SECURITY AND CYBERSECURITY SERVICES FOR DUBE TRADEPORT CORPORATION FOR
A PERIOD OF THREE YEARS
DTP/RFP/12/COR/04/2026
Dube TradePort Corporation (DTPC) was created as a provincial public entity to develop Dube
TradePort. Refer to www.dubetradeport.co.za for more information on DTPC.
DTPC invites qualified and experienced service providers to submit proposals for the provision of ICT
Security and Cybersecurity services. The prospective service provider must be capable of providing
a managed SOC-as-a-Service solution that includes security information and events management
(SIEM) capability to enhance DTPC cybersecurity posture through real-time monitoring, advanced
analytics, automation, and threat intelligence integration.
The project seeks to achieve broadly the following items:
• Ensure DTPC’s critical assets are protected.
• Strengthens DTPC’s overall security posture.
• Decreases DTPC’s exposure to cyber threats.
• Assist DTPC to manage risks more effectively and
• Assist DTPC to deploy and report on cybersecurity controls.
The bidder must be a registered partner authorized to sell the OEM’ products and have the
following ISO Certification:
o ISO / IEC 27001- Information Security Applied to Cyber and Digital Security and Governance
certificate
o ISO 9001 – Quality Management System Standard certificate
EVALUATION OF THIS BID
All proposals will be evaluated in various stages – compliance and functionality will be assessed and
thereafter all responsive proposals will be evaluated in accordance with the PPPFA regulations, 2022
using the 80/20 preference points system.
The 20 preference points (Specific Goals) will be allocated to the following as included in the bid
document:
I. Enterprises that are at least 51% owned by black people
