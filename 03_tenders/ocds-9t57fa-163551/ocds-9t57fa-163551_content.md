1. ADDENDUM: BID NO. LDE/B10/2026/27 – ASSISTIVE DEVICES
Prospective bidders are advised that certain technical specifications contained in the bid
document require correction and/or clarification to ensure that the required items are clearly
defined and can be priced consistently.
The following items are affected:
1. Item No. 60 – Deaf-Blind Communication Device, appearing on page 7;
2. Item No. 28 – Audio Labelling Device, appearing on page 6;
3. Item No. 20 – Large Print Books, appearing on page 5;
4. Item No. 16 – Maps, as reflected in the bid specification; and
5. Braille Embosser – the required size and/or capacity is to be clarified.
Prospective bidders must take note of the revised and/or clarified specifications contained
in the addendum when preparing their technical and financial proposals.
1. ADDENDUM: BID NO. LDE/B11/2026/27 – SCHOOLS OF SKILLS EQUIPMENT,
TOOLS, MACHINERY AND SPECIALISED RESOURCES
Prospective bidders are advised that the administrative compliance requirements relating
to manufacturers' and/or suppliers' certificates or confirmation letters and applicable
regulatory requirements are hereby clarified.

Where bidders are required to submit certificates and/or confirmation letters from registered
manufacturers, companies or suppliers for purposes of authenticating the products offered,
such documentation must contain sufficient information to establish the authenticity of the
issuing entity, including the applicable company registration details.
Where applicable, the relevant certification and regulatory requirements may include:
• International Organization for Standardization certification, including ISO 13485 in
respect of relevant medical equipment; and
• South African Health Products Regulatory Authority (SAHPRA) requirements in
respect of products falling within the applicable regulatory framework.
For avoidance of doubt, bidders are specifically advised that:
“SAHPRA registration/certification is not applicable to non-medical equipment.
Where the requirement is applicable, bidders shall submit the relevant documentary
evidence as prescribed in the bid document.”
Bidders must therefore comply only with the certification and regulatory requirements
applicable to the particular category of equipment or products being offered.
1. ADDENDUM: BID NO. LDE/B12/2026/27 – REFRACTION AND FITTING OF
SPECTACLES
Prospective bidders are advised that the specifications and related requirements for the
supply, delivery, refraction and fitting of spectacles have been reviewed and, where
applicable, corrected and/or clarified.
Bidders are required to consider all amendments and clarifications contained in the
addendum when preparing and submitting their bids.
1. IMPORTANT NOTICE TO BIDDERS
Prospective bidders are required to ensure that they obtain and consider the applicable
addendum together with the original bid document when preparing their submissions.
Bidders who have already submitted their bids may, if they so wish, withdraw, amend or
replace their submissions before the revised closing date and time, subject to the
applicable bid submission requirements.
All bids must be submitted by no later than 11h00 on 27 August 2026 at the address and
in the manner stipulated in the original bid documents.
All other terms and conditions contained in the original bid documents remain
unchanged.
The addendum forms an integral part of the respective bid documents and must be read
together with the original bid documentation.
The Limpopo Department of Education appreciates the continued interest and cooperation
of all prospective bidders.
