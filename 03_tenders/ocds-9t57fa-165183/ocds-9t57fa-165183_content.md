RFP: NEF 07/2026/27
REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL:
HEAD OFFICE REFURBISHMENT FOR THE NATIONAL
EMPOWERMENT FUND (NEF)
The National Empowerment Fund (NEF), established by the National Empowerment Fund Act No. 105 of 1998,
promotes and facilitates economic equality and Broad Based Black Economic Empowerment by providing
development finance to Black-empowered South African businesses. As an agency of the Department of Trade,
Industry and Competition (the dtic), the NEF is mandated to advance Broad-Based Black Economic
Empowerment (B-BBEE) through innovative financing, investment products, and support for inclusive growth.
The NEF herewith invites potential service providers to submit proposals for the Head Office Refurbishment
services of the NEF. The NEF will make available the comprehensive RFP document that will outline the
specifications and functional requirements of the proposal. The NEF reserves the right to cancel the tender should
the needs of the NEF change or should the NEF regard the submissions not to be adequated based on its needs.
TENDER SUBMISSION
All proposals should be submitted clearly marked with the RFP: NEF 07/2026/27 Head Office Refurbishment
services for the National Empowerment Fund to headoffice@nefcorp.co.za. RFP documents will be available
on the NEF website: www.nefcorp.co.za on 11 August 2026.

Sandown, 2195
