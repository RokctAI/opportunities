PART C3 SCOPE OF WORK / SPECIFICATION
C3.1 Description of the Service
The work comprises of supply and delivery of Road Maintenance Materials to Mkhondo Local
Municipality as and when required by the Municipality. An official purchase order stating the required
items will be issued prior to every delivery. A quotation with the item unit prices as per the submitted
bill of quantities will be required prior to purchase order issuing.
The product specification in detail include:
Aggregate
The aggregate consist of crushed and washed angular pieces of hard rock within the range of 2mm
to 27mm. A mixture of sizes produces the best interlocking effect and therefore a much denser patch.
For general patching jobs, an ideal aggregate- which has been tested as giving the best interlocking
results (Stone Mastic Asphalt) is a mixture of 6.7 and 9.2mm washed aggregate. The aggregate can
tolerate some degree of dust, and the on board water supply can damp the aggregate and dust to
good effect, however, dusty aggregate increases the consumption of emulsion as the emulsion has
to coat the dust as well as the aggregate.
Uncrushed river gravel is not a suitable material, as it does not interlock
Specialised Bitumen Emulsion (KRS 65)
Viscosity at 50 degree Celsius (51-200), binder content % m/m 65-68, fluxing agent content % m/m
5, residue on sieving g/100ml 0.25max, positive particle charge, binder deposit on the cathode after
30 min. g, Min 1,0. Zero sedimentation after 60 complete rotations and typical density in kg/litres
1,000.
Specialised Bitumen Rejuvenator Emulsion
This rejuvenator extends the life of asphalt and chip seal surface by preventing damaging moisture
and ultraviolet rays from penetrating the surface. The single component, black bitumen compound,
contains no solvents, promotes elasticity, retards binder ageing and rejuvenates dry asphalt surfaces.
Viscosity at 50 degree Celsius (10-30), binder content % m/m 20-25, Nano modifier % 0.1 – 0.5,
residue on sieving g/100ml 0.25 max, positive particle charge, zero sedimentation after 60 complete
rotations and typical density in kg/litre 1,000.
Crusher Sand
Crusher sand is a fine aggregate consisting of angular to roughly cubical shaped particles with a
characteristically vesicular structure and rough surface texture. All types of construction including:
General fill and paving sand.
80mm Paving Block
Class 25 blocks when tested in accordance with clause 7.4.1 of construction materials of SANS 1058
shall have an average wet strength of at least 25 MPa. Blocks shall be of the type (S-A, S-B or S-C)
scheduled or given on the drawings or required in terms of the project specification, as applicable,
and shall comply with the relevant requirements of SANS 1058.
