1. INTRODUCTION TO THE NRF
The National Research Foundation (“NRF”) is a juristic person established in terms of the
National Research Foundation Act, Act 23 of 1998, and a Schedule 3A Public Entity in terms
of the Public Finance Management Act (PFMA). The NRF is the government’s national agency
responsible for promoting and supporting research and human capital development through
funding researchers, provision of the National Research Platforms, and science outreach
platforms/programs to the broader community. The NRF provides these services in all fields
of science and technology, including natural science, engineering, social science, and
humanities.
The NRF is a schedule 3A entity under the PFMA (Act 29 of 1999), which is required to plan
and report on its activities and organizational performance, and which is to be audited by the
Auditor-General of South Africa (AGSA) on an annual basis. As part of the AGSA audit
requirements, the NRF has to collect / document and store details, data and/or information of
all persons and activities that form part of its performance record as proof thereof. In terms of
this requirement, all persons making use of NRF facilities, platforms, equipment, tools etc., for
research and related purposes are required to provide their personal details/data/information
as per the template below or other similarly appropriate format. By completing your information

2.2 Background of SAAO Facilities:
SAAO manages a range of amenities at its Sutherland site, comprising of residential
buildings inclusive of a hostel, telescope buildings, office buildings, workshops, and medium-
voltage substations with step down transformers and 11–22 kV switchgear. These facilities
require highly specialized electrical skills for troubleshooting, repairs, maintenance,
upgrading, servicing, compliance and future proofing of all electrical infrastructure across the
entire site. The service provider will on request, be required to travel to Sutherland for
inspections for quoting purposes or the delivery of services after receiving an official
Purchase Order.
Advance fault finding, troubleshooting and tracing skills will come highly recommended as
some of the older buildings lack electrical schematics, layouts, blueprints and drawings. The
SAAO will request of the contractor where necessary, to draw new or amend existing circuit
diagrams, electrical floor plans, DB layouts, electrical site layouts, single line diagrams and
control circuits in accordance with the national drawing code for electrical installations (NRS
002-200).
Where necessary and/or requested by the SAAO, Certificates of Compliance (CoC’s) shall
be issued after work completion. Either an Initial or Supplementary CoC will be requested for
new installations or additions to an existing electrical system with supporting documentation
such as deviation reports, calculations and pictures. The main purpose is to get SAAO
facilities to a safe, reliable and electrically compliant state that conforms to SANS regulations
for the electrical code.

2.4 EMERGENCY CALL-OUT SERVICES
The contractor must provide emergency call-out services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365
days a year, as and when required. SAAO’s normal operating hours are 08:00 to 16:30 on
weekdays, with a skeleton crew on weekends and public holidays. Emergency call-outs must
be responded to within 12 hours of notification by SAAO. This requirement must be strictly
adhered to for the full contract period, and the contractor must ensure that suitably qualified
staff are available to respond to emergency call-outs when required.
The contractor must provide SAAO with the contact details of the coordinator responsible for
both normal-hours work and emergency call-outs. The contractor must also provide the
details of the response team, including each team member’s role, level of seniority, and
estimated time of arrival for emergency repairs.
Normal call-outs must be responded to within 48 hours of notification by SAAO. The
contractor must strictly comply with all response-time requirements. Non-compliance will be
monitored and may result in termination of the contract if the contractor contravenes this
agreement. SAAO cannot afford system downtime caused by delayed communication or
slow response times.
2.5 ACCOMODATION
Subject to availability and the duration of the relevant project or work instruction, limited on-
site accommodation may be made available at the SAAO Sutherland hostel. Meals are
provided daily between 07h30 and 18h00. Contractors requiring accommodation must make
prior arrangements with the Facility Manager and provide full details of all personnel who will
be accommodated and performing work on site. Any approved accommodation and meal
costs for the applicable stay will be recovered from the contractor and deducted from the
contractor’s quotation or invoice, as applicable.
2.6 CIVIL WORK
The contractor shall be responsible for trenching, excavating, grinding, drilling and chasing to
install cables and cableways in ground, walls and ceilings. Cable and pipe detectors shall be
used to ensure safe location of hidden utility lines such as electrical cables, gas lines and
water pipes either buried underground or concealed inside building walls and roofs.
Backfilling will commence once cables are laid, as well as replastering and repainting of walls
where chasing was done for wire installation. All civil work must comply with SANS 10400
and the contractor need to ensure that the areas referenced are restored to a safe and
aesthetically pleasing condition.
