A BUSINESS CASE AND CONCEPT PLAN FOR THE REVITALISATION OF AN INDUSTRIAL
INCUBATION HUB: SOWETO - OLD VISTA BUILDING FOR A PERIOD OF 6 MONTHS
SPECIFICATION
1. OBJECTIVES
The objective of this project is to revitalize the existing Incubation Hub by identifying its current
challenges and repositioning it as a sustainable, demand-driven, and high-impact facility. The aim
is to create a dynamic, responsive, and sustainable platform for supporting entrepreneurship,
innovation, and small enterprise development, particularly in high-impact economic sectors.
The revitalization process will involve:
• Assessing the current state, performance, and relevance of the existing hub;
• Identifying infrastructure, operational, and strategic gaps;
• Identify best-performing local and international industrial incubation hubs and extract
lessons in governance, funding, sector focus, and innovation programming.
• Re-aligning the hub’s focus with current economic development priorities and sector
demands;
• Introducing innovative service offerings, digital tools, and capacity-building programs;
• Establishing a sustainable operational and governance model; and
• Attracting stakeholder support, partnerships, and potential funding for implementation.
• Develop a high-level project implementation framework (including a 3-D model).
• To propose various vehicle options for implementation.
• Define high level project resource requirements.
The ultimate goal is to transform the Industrial Incubation Hub into a high-performing, future-ready
facility that accelerates business growth, enhances job creation, and contributes to local economic
development.
2. SCOPE OF WORK
The Service Provider will be required to present a feasible approach/methodology to develop the
business case and concept plan that is in line with the City’s objectives. Details around the
proposed methodology, stakeholder engagement and other approaches should be included
amongst other relevant topics. This project stage shall further outline detailed work plans,
timelines, project deliverables, and risk and mitigation measures to prevent possible challenges
that may be encountered during the project. The Service Provider is required to include the
following:
• Project Workflow Diagram;
• Project plan;
• Resource & Capacity Plan (project team, skills, experience)
• Project Milestones;
• Detailed stakeholder engagement plan
• Risk Plan with mitigating measures; and
• Detailed budget and Invoice Plan
The scope of work for this study shall include the following:
4.1 Situational Analysis
• Locational attributes of the study area / site (level of connectivity to public transport
infrastructure, surrounding uses).
Initials

• Assess the current entrepreneurship and innovation landscape in the region. This should
include establishing a clear understanding of entrepreneurial activity by various factors
(formal/informal, size (big/medium/small), current and future needs and requirements.
• Identify key economic sectors with high growth potential.
• Socioeconomic dynamics to understand the potential end-user (employment/unemployment
level, education levels, income levels)
• Identify best-performing local and international industrial incubation hubs and extract
lessons in governance, funding, sector focus, and innovation programming.
4.2 Market and Competitor Analysis
• Overview of other incubation hubs or similar facilities in the city and in other municipalities.
• Comparison of services, cost of development, and success rates.
• Identification of unique value propositions to differentiate the hub.
4.3 Stakeholder Engagement
A community participation specialist will design the project program with assistance from other
members of the project team. The program should allow stakeholders to offer input in a proactive
and accessible manner. To ensure equity in the public participation process, a mechanism must be
established.
In addition to regular meetings, the project team may need to provide presentations to the City and
update other committees, commissions, and stakeholder groups. The bidder will need to consult
with city departments, and other government agencies, and conduct work sessions with a Project
Steering Committee/Technical Committee. Key internal stakeholders are listed below (but not
limited to):
• City Departments/MOEs.
• Section 79 Committee;
• Development Planning

• Smart City Office
• Department of Human Settlements
• Joburg Property Company
• Ward Governance;
• Department of Economic Development
The successful bidder must engage with external stakeholders to ensure that their needs, inputs,
and concerns are heard. Key external stakeholders are listed below (but not limited to):
• Residents;
• Resident Associations;
• Businesses;
• Business Associations
• Property Owners;
• Community Participation Consultants on previous COJ projects in the area
4.4 Business Case Development
• Define the problem/opportunity statement.
• Articulate strategic alignment with policy and development objectives.
• Determine potential partnerships and opportunities for local tertiary institutions and TVET
Colleges.
• Outline options and a preferred implementation approach.
• Conduct financial viability assessment (cost-benefit analysis, financial sustainability model,
risks, ROI).
• 3D model of the proposed Industrial Incubation Hub.
• Include sustainability components and smart city principles.
Initials

4.5 Concept Plan Development
• Propose design and functional layout (including digital infrastructure).
• Define services and programs to be offered in the Industrial Incubation Hub, while
accentuating and optimizing the existing offerings.
• Propose a governance and operational model.
• Develop a monitoring and evaluation framework.
• Produce detailed architectural drawings for implementation (as per SACAP architectural
project stages).
