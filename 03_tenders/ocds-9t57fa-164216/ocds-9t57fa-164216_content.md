Invitation to Submit Bids
Bid Number: SAMRC/ITSD-SDSMS/2026/07
Provision of Software Development, Support and Maintenance Services
The South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC) based in Parow Valley, Cape Town
wish to procure the services of a suitably qualified and experienced service-provider to provide
Software Development, Support and Maintenance services for the SAMRC Information
Services Technology Division.
A non-compulsory virtual briefing session will be held on 14 August 2026 at 11H00. Prospective
supplier(s) who wish to attend the briefing sessions should email their Microsoft Teams credentials to:
tenders@mrc.ac.za before the meeting date.
Completed bids must be submitted in a sealed envelope bearing the bidder’s company name, address,
bid description and number, and hand delivered to: South African Medical Research Council, Tender
Box No. 2, Francie van Zijl Drive, Parow Valley, Tygerberg, 7580, Western Cape; and must arrive
before the closing date and time. Late, faxed, or e-mailed bids will not be accepted. One (1) original
hard copy of the completed bid document and 1 USB flash drive containing full and same tender
information as in the hard copy (PDF format) must be submitted.
Alternatively, bidder(s) can submit their completed bid proposals with supporting documents through
the SAMRC website ( Current Tenders | South African Medical Research Council (.ac.za)
SCAM ALERT: We have received reports of scammers impersonating SAMRC employees to solicit
money from prospective bidders. SAMRC will never call you for tender-related matters. Any
communication requesting payment, gifts, or favors in exchange for procurement-related assistance
should be treated as fraudulent. Do not be a victim! Report scams immediately at:
https://scmfraudreporting.samrc.ac.za/
Responsive bids will firstly be evaluated on functionality and thereafter on an 80/20 preference point
system in terms of the Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act (Act 5 of 2000) and Preferential
Procurement Regulations, 2022.
Please note that:
• This bid is valid for one hundred and twenty (120) days after the closing date for the submission of
bids;
• It is compulsory that bidders obtain bid documents, available for downloading on the SAMRC website
(http://www.mrc.ac.za/Tenders/tenders.htm ) from 31 July 2026 to 28 August 2026. No hardcopy bid
document will be issued by the SAMRC;
• Bidders must register on the National Treasury’s Central Supplier Database (CSD) prior to submitting
bids, by logging onto https://secure.csd.gov.za/. With effect from 1 July 2016, SAMRC may not award
any bid to a supplier not registered as a prospective supplier on CSD. Submit with this bid your CSD
supplier number and unique registration reference number for SAMRC to verify your company’s
registration on CSD;
• Negotiations for the bid award will only be concluded with the qualifying bidder(s) who is/are
registered on the CSD and whose key information outlined in this bid document is in order.
• Bidder(s) are required to submit 1 original hard copy document and 1 USB flash drive with full
information as per the original hard copy (PDF format), as indicated above in this advert;
• Non-compliance with all submission requirements, such as the USB for traditional submission, amongst
other criteria as per section 1.21, will lead to the disqualification of the bidder(s);
• The SAMRC is not compelled to accept the lowest or alternative bid, and reserves the right to accept
part or the whole of any bid or cancel the bid;
