SECTION 3: BACKGROUND, OVERVIEW AND SCOPE OF REQUIREMENTS
1 BACKGROUND
The Port currently receives its freshwater feed from eThekwini Municipality, due to the ageing infrastructure and
environmental exposure around the Port of Durban, the water quality testing will help determine if the water
around the Port is suitable for drinking.
2 EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Whereas Transnet is seeking a partner(s) to provide solutions for the Provision of Water Quality Sampling Services
at various locations within the Port of Durban on an “as and when required” basis for a period of sixty (60)
months nationally, it also seeks to improve its current processes for providing these Services to its end user

1. BACKGROUND
The Port of Durban currently receives its potable water supply from the eThekwini Municipality. However, due
to the Port's operational activities and exposure to various environmental conditions, there is a need to
periodically assess the quality of water supplied and distributed within the Port precinct. Drinking water
quality testing is therefore required to determine whether the water complies with applicable health, safety,
and regulatory standards.
In addition to supporting Port operations, the Port of Durban supplies freshwater to berthed vessels for use
by crew members and passengers. Vessel operators and shipping agents frequently request water quality
information and certification to verify that the supplied water is safe for drinking and domestic use onboard.
Regular testing and monitoring will provide assurance that the water meets required potable water standards,
identify any potential contamination risks, and demonstrate compliance with health and safety requirements.
The results of the water quality assessment will enable the Port to maintain confidence in the potable water
supplied to employees, contractors, visitors, vessels, and ensure that freshwater supplied remains fit for
human consumption.
