Notice T02.26.27
UPGRADING OF BULK WATER PIPELINE: WESBANK RESERVOIR TO MALMESBURY /
ABBOTSDALE, PHASE 2
Swartland Municipality hereby invites tenderers for the Upgrading of Bulk Water Pipeline from Wesbank Reservoir to
Malmesbury / Abbotsdale: Phase 2.
Tenderers must be registered with the CIDB in a CE Class of construction works and have a grading designation equal
to or higher than that determined in accordance with the sum tendered or a value determined in accordance with
Regulation 25(1B) or 25(7A) of the Construction Industry Development Regulations, 2004 (as amended). The estimated
contract value is between R 10 million to R 20 million.
Preferences are offered to tenderers who satisfy criteria stated in the Tender Data. Only tenderers who satisfy the eligibility
criteria stated in the Tender Data are eligible to submit tenders.
A non-refundable tender fee of R 530-00, payable in cash or EFT in favour of Swartland Municipality is required prior to
collection of the tender documents. Should tenderers opt for an EFT Payment, Payments can be made to:
STANDARD BANK Malmesbury
Account Type: Cheque
Account Name: Swartland Municipality: Sundries Account
Account Number: 372865143
Branch Code: 051001
Reference: Tender No & Company Name
Documents are only available in hard copy; tenderers should allow 2 business days for EFT payment to clear
prior to collection. Documents will only be available for collection from Tuesday, 11 August 2026. Queries relating to the
issue of these documents may be addressed to Mr Johan du Plessis, Tel. No. 082 561 8697 or e-mail:
johandp@bviwc.co.za.
The physical address for collection of tender documents is the Offices of the Director: Civil Engineering Services,
Swartland Municipality, Piet Retief Street, Malmesbury.
A representative of the Municipal Civil Engineering Services Department will be available on request if assistance is
needed with the preparation of the tender submission – excluding the determination of a tender price. For any enquiries
regarding the completion of the schedule or for information on the tender, the tenderers are requested to contact Mr. Esias
de Jager at 022 487 9400 before the tender closing date.
A Compulsory Clarification Meeting with representatives of the Employer will take place at the Banquet Hall of
Swartland Municipality, 1 Kerk Street in Malmesbury on Friday, 21 August 2026, at 10:00 (Registration closes at
10:05). Any person who is later than 10:05 will be denied access. The person attending on behalf of the Tenderer
must be a senior technical person involved with bidding for the company or someone from their team. Tender
documents must be collected prior to the meeting.
Bids will be evaluated in accordance with the Council’s Preferential Procurement Policy (available on their website) and
the attached specifications. The 80/20-preference point system will be used for the evaluation of the contract. Preference
points will not be allocated if Annexure MBD 6.1, included in the tender documents, is not completed. Preference
points MUST also be claimed in paragraph 6.1. Bidders are required to submit original and valid B-BBEE Status
Level Verification Certificates or certified copies thereof together with their bids, to substantiate their B-BBEE rating
claims. Bidders are also required to submit Proof of Locality in order to claim points for locality.
Duly completed tenders in a sealed envelope marked: “TENDER NO. T02.26.27: UPGRADING OF BULK WATER
PIPELINE: WESBANK RESERVOIR TO MALMESBURY / ABBOTSDALE, PHASE 2” together with the name and
address of the Tenderer must be deposited in the tender box at the Municipal Offices, 1 Kerk Street, Malmesbury before
12:00 on 11 September 2026, upon which the tenders will be made public.
Late tenders will not be accepted and the Municipality will not be compelled to accept the lowest or any tender. The
Municipality reserves the right to omit any part of the tender. Tenders by telephone, facsimile and e-mail will not be
accepted.
