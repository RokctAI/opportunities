1. PURPOSE OF THE BID
The purpose of this bid is to appoint a suitably qualified service provider to conduct Personnel
suitability checks on prospective candidates for employment and criminal record verifications on
service provider directors, members, key personnel, or representatives, intending to conduct
business with the Eastern Cape Department of Public Works and Infrastructure (ECDPWI), in
order to support informed decision-making in recruitment and supplier management processes.
1. OBJECTIVES OF THE BID
The objective of this bid is to ensure compliance with the Public Service Regulations, which
require that the Accounting Officer must be satisfied that a candidate qualifies in all respects for
appointment, and that all claims made in the application have been verified and recorded in
writing prior to appointment.
The verification process is intended to ensure that only suitably qualified and verified candidates
are considered for employment within the Eastern Cape Department of Public Works and
Infrastructure (DPWI).
In addition, the bid aims to support the Department's risk management, integrity management,
and security objectives through the verification of criminal records of directors and/or key
persons associated with service providers, where such verification is required by the
Department. This process is intended to assist the Department in identifying potential security,
fraud, corruption, and reputational risks associated with individuals conducting business with the
Department.
The overall objective is to enhance integrity management, mitigate security and reputational
risks, promote ethical conduct, safeguard departmental resources and information, and protect
the interests of the Department through the provision of reliable and legally compliant
verification services for a period of twenty-four (24) months.
1. SCOPE OF WORK
The Service Provider shall conduct personnel suitability checks, verification services,
and criminal record verifications for the Eastern Cape Department of Public Works and
Infrastructure (DPWI), including its six (6) District Offices and Head Office, on an as-
and-when-required basis, as requested by the Department.
The scope of verification will vary per candidate, and only requested checks shall be
performed. Payment will be based strictly on verification services actually rendered per
candidate. (Please take note of page 17 that is below the pricing schedule)
