1.1 Tender Notice and Invitation to Tender
1.1.1 Tender Notice
The Department of Agriculture, Eastern Cape Provincial Government, invites tenders from Authorized
re-sellers / distributers for supply and delivery of Irrigation Design software (Model Maker OR
SIMILAR) for a period of 3 years. The output of the software should be compatible with .dxf, dwg, and
dr4 files.
Key Functions of the software to be supplied:
• Digital Terrain Modelling (DTM): Builds 3D surfaces from survey points to visualize terrain.
• Contour Generation: Creates contour lines for land representation.
• Volume Calculations: Determines cut and fill volumes for earthworks.
• Infrastructure Design: Specialized modules for designing roads, pipes (sewer, stormwater,
water), and irrigation systems.
• CAD Integration: Links survey data with CAD functions for creating detailed drawings on-
screen.
• Survey Data Processing: Reduces survey observations to coordinates and manages data.
• Point Cloud Handling: Imports and analyses point cloud data for enhanced 3D visualization.
• Reporting & Plotting: Generates reports and plots cross-sections, long-sections, and other
designs.
