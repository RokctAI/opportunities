1. INTRODUCTION
Eskom Holdings SOC Ltd is a strategic national asset responsible for the generation, transmission and
distribution of electricity throughout South Africa. The organisation relies extensively on the uninterrupted
supply, transportation, storage and utilisation of coal, heavy fuel oil (HFO), fuel and other petroleum-
based products to ensure the sustainability of its power generation operations. Criminal activities
targeting these commodities have emerged as a significant threat to Eskom’s operational performance,
financial sustainability, security of supply and overall energy security.
Eskom’s Security Investigations (SI) is mandated to investigate high-impact incidents, organised crime,
economic offences and other security-related matters affecting the organisation and its subsidiaries.
These investigations include, but are not limited to, coal theft, coal quality manipulation, fuel and heavy
fuel oil theft, diversion of fuel products, collusion involving transport contractors, organised criminal
syndicates operating within the coal supply chain, procurement-related irregularities, corruption, fraud,
sabotage and other serious offences that negatively affect Eskom’s operations and revenue. These
investigations are conducted through a combination of internal investigative resources and specialised
outsourced service providers.
The coal and fuel value chain remains highly vulnerable to organised criminal activities due to the
complexity of logistics operations, multiple stakeholder interfaces, extensive transportation networks and
the large volumes of commodities handled daily. Criminal syndicates often exploit weaknesses within
processes, systems and controls to facilitate the theft, diversion, adulteration and unlawful sale of coal,
heavy fuel oil and fuel products. These criminal activities may involve employees, contractors, service
providers, transport operators and external organised crime networks. The methods employed by
perpetrators are often sophisticated, coordinated and difficult to detect, requiring specialised
investigative methodologies and advanced intelligence capabilities.
Eskom continues to suffer significant financial losses and operational risks arising from coal and fuel-
related crimes. These losses not only impact revenue and operational expenditure but also threaten
power station performance, fuel security, generation reliability and ultimately the security of electricity
supply to customers. The increasing sophistication of organised criminal syndicates necessitates the
deployment of specialist investigative skills, intelligence-driven methodologies and multidisciplinary
investigative approaches capable of identifying, disrupting and dismantling criminal networks operating
within Eskom’s coal and fuel supply chains.
Traditional investigative approaches alone have proven insufficient to effectively address complex and
organised criminal activities associated with coal and fuel theft. Accordingly, Eskom requires access to
specialist investigation capabilities, including intelligence gathering and analysis, surveillance operations,
undercover investigations, data analytics, asset tracing, evidence analysis, witness interviewing, litigation
support services. These capabilities are necessary to support successful criminal prosecutions,
disciplinary actions, civil recoveries and preventative interventions.
The Security Investigations therefore requires the services of suitably qualified, experienced and
competent specialist investigation service providers to supplement and enhance Eskom’s existing
investigation capacity. These services will be rendered for a period of three years across Eskom
Holdings SOC Ltd and its subsidiaries, with a specific focus on the investigation of coal theft, coal
diversion, coal quality manipulation, fuel theft, heavy fuel oil theft, fraud, corruption and related organised
criminal activities affecting the organisation.

Due to the volume, complexity and geographic spread of incidents across Eskom’s operational footprint,
internal resources are not always sufficient to respond effectively and within required timeframes. The
appointment of a specialised investigation service provider will enable Eskom to rapidly deploy additional
investigative resources and specialised expertise in support of high-priority investigations, proactive
disruption operations and strategic interventions aimed at protecting Eskom’s assets, revenue and
operational integrity.
Security Investigations therefore intends to establish a contract for the Provision of Specialised
Security Investigation Services into Coal and Heavy Fuel Oil/Fuel Theft Incidents Affecting
Eskom for a Period of Three (3) Years.
The successful service provider shall provide specialised investigation and support services which may
include, but are not limited to:
• Coal theft and diversion investigations;
• Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO) and fuel theft investigations;
• Coal quality and coal supply chain investigations;
• Intelligence gathering, analysis and threat assessments;
• Organised crime investigations;
• Surveillance, counter-surveillance and covert operations;
• Tracking and tracing of suspects, assets and criminal proceeds;
• Crime scene management and evidence preservation;
• Witness interviewing and statement-taking services;
• Evidence analysis and litigation support;
• Support to criminal, civil and disciplinary proceedings;
• Asset tracing and recovery support;
• Joint disruption operations with law enforcement agencies; and
• Skills transfer and capacity building for Eskom investigators.
The appointment of specialist investigative service providers will strengthen Eskom’s capability to detect,
investigate, disrupt and prevent coal and fuel-related crimes, thereby contributing to improved
operational resilience, reduced financial losses and enhanced security of electricity supply.

criminal activities affecting Eskom’s coal, heavy fuel oil (HFO), fuel and oil value chains, including
Generation coal-fired power stations and associated supply chain operations. The scope shall include,
but not be limited to, the following:

The Service Provider shall:
• Conduct intelligence-driven investigations into coal theft, fuel theft, heavy fuel oil theft, fuel diversion,
stockpile adulteration, contamination, sample tampering and related criminal conduct.
• Investigate allegations of fraud, corruption, collusion, bribery, procurement irregularities and
misconduct involving employees, contractors, suppliers, transporters and external stakeholders.

2.12 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
