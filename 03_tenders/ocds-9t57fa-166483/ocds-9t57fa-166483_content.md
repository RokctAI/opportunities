WORKING AT HEIGHTS SCOPE OF WORK
Unit standard 229998 – SAQA accreditation (please submit a valid proof of
accreditation)
SPECIFICATION
1. Course Details
• Demonstrate basic knowledge of the limitations of a limited range of fall arrest
equipment and regulations.
• Inspect, assemble and store fall arrest equipment.
• Select suitable anchor points
• Use fall arrest systems with a double lanyard.
• Use pre-installed vertical and horizontal life-lines and explain reducing risks during
rescue.
The candidate should at the end of the course be competent in understanding and
carrying out functions as outlined below:
• Know the legislation and regulations governing the safety of work at heights
• Explain the use and limitations of a limited range of fall arrest equipment and fall
arrest plan
• Hazard Identification and Risk
• Inspect, assemble and storing fall arrest equipment.
• Identification and Risk Assessments in The Workplace
• Select suitable anchor
• Risk Assessment Methods for Different Circumstances
• Use fall arrest systems with double lanyard
• Using pre-installed vertical and horizontal lifeline
• Able to use relevant PPE
Legislation governing the safety of work at height refers to the Occupational Health and
safety act (Act 85 of 1993 and its amendments).
Minimum free space PPE or personal protective equipment refers to any device or
appliance designed to be worn or held by an individual for protection against potential
hazard
