1. Introduction
The Tshwane Automotive Special Economic Zone (TASEZ) is Africa’s first automotive city,
an industrial hub that provides investors with a wide range of offerings to help their
business flourish.
We are a Special Economic Zone (SEZ) that is focused on growing South Africa’s
automotive manufacturing industry. This means we are ready to provide a number of
financial and infrastructure incentives to make doing business that much easier. SEZs
were set up to accelerate South Africa’s industrial development by helping promote
industrial agglomeration, build the required industrial infrastructure, promote
coordinated planning among key government agencies and the private sector, and
guide the deployment of other necessary development tools.
Our mandate is to transform the sector, acting as a catalyst for employment, boosting
the country’s economic growth, and enabling socio-economic development. TASEZ is
well placed to do just that, providing a much-desired location close to an already
established automotive industrial complex. The hub provides world-class customised
solutions, incentives, support services and systems for manufacturers seeking
excellence.
Our partners come from all three tiers of government, the Department of Trade, Industry
and Competition provides financial support for investor-related infrastructure through its
SEZ Fund, while the Gauteng Department of Economic Development and the City of
Tshwane provide financial support and the resources necessary to manage and
implement the SEZ.
This Request for Proposals (RFP) is for the appointment of two (02) service providers for
the provision of HVAC maintenance services for a period of thirty-six (36) months.

1. Scope of Work
3.1. TASEZ hereby invites proposals from suitably qualified bidders for the provision of
maintenance services for all HVAC systems within its facilities. Due to the number of
air-conditioning units across TASEZ facilities, TASEZ intends to appoint two (2) service
providers to undertake the preventative and breakdown maintenance of the air-
conditioning units. Each service provider will be allocated a predetermined number
of units at specified locations, for which they will be responsible for the maintenance
and management thereof.
NOTE: Air conditioner units will be accessed via ladder and cat ladder to rooftop
access. Safety harnesses and general accepted safe height work procedure
required.
3.1.1. Bidders are required to provide the comprehensive maintenance of
equipment in accordance with the relevant SANS and manufacturer
recommendations of the following HVAC systems installed at Phase 1 and
Phase 1A.
3.1.2. Preventative maintenance
3.1.2.1. Unless superseded by more stringent requirements of these
specifications or code requirements, all maintenance service shall
conform to the requirements of the following standards: SANS
10400, SANS 10139, SANS 1929:200, SANS 10147
3.1.2.2. Perform the minimum service as per the manufacturer’s
recommended maintenance schedule for each HVAC
item/system as per the maintenance frequency schedule table
below, or at more frequent intervals when it is necessary to
maintain satisfactory operation or recommended by the
equipment manufacture, following the manufacturer
recommended service procedures. This includes preventative and
reactive maintenance of the following systems:
• Air conditioning systems
• CRAC units
• Fresh air ventilation systems
