C3.1.1 PROJECT OVERVIEW
The continued expansion of industrial development within the Coega Special Economic Zone
(SEZ), together with associated residential growth in surrounding areas including Motherwell
North, has resulted in increasing wastewater treatment demands within the region. The existing
Fish Water Flats Wastewater Treatment Works (WWTW), which historically accommodated flows
from these areas, has reached its hydraulic and process capacity limits and cannot support
projected future growth.
In response to these constraints, the Coega Development Corporation (CDC) initiated the
development of new wastewater treatment infrastructure within Zone 5 of the SEZ. The overall
infrastructure strategy provides for the development of separate Domestic and Industrial
Wastewater Treatment Works, allowing each facility to be designed in accordance with the
specific characteristics of its influent stream.
The Domestic WWTW forms the first phase of implementation and has been prioritised to
address immediate treatment requirements.
CDC’s intention is to construct the Coega Wastewater Treatment Works in phases to
accommodate domestic effluent from the Motherwell areas, including future developments, as
well as effluent from industrial areas within Zone 5 of the SEZ.
The proposed extension of Alumina Street forms part of Phase 1 of the Coega Wastewater
Treatment Works. This extension will provide access for heavy vehicles to the sludge drying pads
for sludge removal, as well as access for operational and maintenance vehicles during the
operation and maintenance phases of the facility.
It must be noted that a Contractor, in terms of the prescribed category of CIDB, will be appointed.
The Contractor must familiarize themself with the above-mentioned requirements and price the
document accordingly.
C3.1.2 EMPLOYER’S OBJECTIVES
The Employer’s objective is to appoint a Contractor for the construction of an access road to
provide access to the Wastewater Treatment Works (WWTW).
The Employer intends to award the Contract as soon as possible. The Contract will be deemed
to commence upon acceptance of the Notification of Award by the successful bidder. The formal
contract agreement will be concluded as soon as practicable thereafter.
