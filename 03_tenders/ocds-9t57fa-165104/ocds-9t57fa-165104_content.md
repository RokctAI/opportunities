Invitation to Submit Bids
Bid Number: SAMRC/FM-HVAC&R/2026/11
MAINTENANCE AND INSTALLATION OF HEATING, VENTILATION, AIR-CONDITIONING
AND REFRIGERATION SYSTEMS - PRETORIA
The South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC) invites bids from suitably qualified, competent, and
experienced service providers with a professional team capable of rendering Heating, Ventilation, Air-
conditioning and Refrigeration (HVAC&R) supply and installation, maintenance and repair services at the
SAMRC premises located in 1 Soutpansberg Road, Arcadia, Pretoria, Gauteng Province. Bidders must be
registered in the mechanical category of the CIDB and have CIDB grading of 6ME or higher. Bidder’s division
or sub-contractors doing electrical must have CIDB grading of 4EB/EP or higher.
A compulsory briefing session and site inspection will be held on 27 August 2026 at 11H00 at the below-
mentioned address: SA Medical Research Council, Soutpansberg Road, Acardia, Pretoria, Gauteng Province.
Completed bids must be submitted in a sealed envelope bearing the bidder’s company name, address, bid
description, and number, and hand delivered or mailed to: SA Medical Research Council, Tender Box, 1
Soutpansberg Road, Arcadia, Pretoria, Gauteng Province; and must arrive before the closing date and time. Late,
faxed, or e-mailed bids will not be accepted. One (1) original hard copy of the completed bid document and 1 USB
flash drive containing full and same tender information as in the hard copy (PDF format) must be submitted.
Alternatively, bidder(s) can submit their completed bid proposal with supporting documents through the SAMRC
website( Current Tenders | South African Medical Research Council (SAMRC.ac.za)) before the closing date and
time.
Responsive bids will firstly be evaluated on functionality and thereafter on an 80/20 preference point system in
terms of the Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act (Act 5 of 2000) and Preferential Procurement
Regulations, 2022.
Please note that:
• This bid is valid for one hundred and twenty (120) days after the closing date for the submission of bids;
• It is compulsory that bidders obtain bid documents, available for downloading on the SAMRC website
(hhttp://www.mrc.ac.za/Tenders/tenders.htm ) from 07 August 2026 to 11 September 2026. The SAMRC will
issue no hardcopy bid document.
• Bidders must register on the National Treasury’s Central Supplier Database (CSD) prior to submitting bids, by
login onto https://secure.csd.gov.za/. With effect from 1 July 2016, SAMRC may not award any bid to a supplier
not registered as a prospective supplier on CSD. Submit with this bid your CSD supplier number and unique
registration reference number for SAMRC to verify your company’s registration on CSD;
• Negotiations for the bid award will only be concluded with the qualifying bidder(s) who is/are registered on the
CSD and whom key information outlined in this bid document is in order;
• Failure to comply with all submission requirements, such as the USB for traditional submission and others, will
lead to disqualification of the bidder(s).
• The SAMRC is not compelled to accept the lowest or alternative bid, and reserves the right to accept part or the
whole of any bid or cancel the bid;
• Bidder(s) must be able to supply all the required items as indicated in the bid specifications;
• Documents and USB flash drives submitted on time by bidders shall not be returned;
• Appointment will be subject to the successful bidder agreeing to sign a service level agreement with the SAMRC
to adhere to the terms and conditions set out in the bid document;
• It is envisaged that SAMRC will conclude a service contract with a successful bidder for a period of 36 months,
reviewed annually with an option to extend by a further 24 months, subject to budget availability/performance.
All technical enquiries may be directed in writing to Stanley Modiba (tenders@mrc.ac.za), and any other enquiries
related to the bid process may be directed in writing to Supply Chain Management (tenders@mrc.ac.za) before
the cut-off time of 16:00(SAST) on 04 September 2026. The bid number should be quoted in all correspondence.
No telephonic enquiries will be responded to.
The closing date and time for the submission of bids is Friday, 11 September 2026, at 11H00.
