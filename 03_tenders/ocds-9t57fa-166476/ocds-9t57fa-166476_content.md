2.1.5 Procurement procedures
2.1.5.1 General
The CCT intends to appoint two (2) tenderers, consisting of the highest ranked acceptable tenderer
("the Winner") and one (1) Alternate Tenderer per category. Should insufficient responsive and
acceptable tenders be received, the CCT reserves the right to appoint fewer tenderers or not to make
any appointment.
The contract period shall be for a period of 36 months.
Subject to operational requirements, orders shall ordinarily be offered to and allocated to the Winner.
The Winner shall be afforded the first opportunity to supply the required goods, subject to its ability to
comply with the contract requirements, delivery obligations and specified timeframes.
The CCT shall not be obliged to purchase any minimum quantity of goods and does not guarantee
any minimum expenditure under the contract.
Refusal of Orders
Where the Winner declines or refuses an order, the CCT may offer the order to the Alternate Tenderer.
Stock Availability and Capacity Constraints
The CCT may allocate an order to the Alternate Tenderer where the Winner:
 advises that sufficient stock is not available;
 is unable to meet the required delivery timeframe;
 lacks the capacity to fulfil the order;
 repeatedly fails to deliver goods within the prescribed period; or
 is otherwise unable to fulfil the order in accordance with the contract requirements.
Product Quality and Contract Performance
The CCT may utilise the Alternate Tenderer where the Winner:
 repeatedly supplies defective or non-conforming goods;
 fails to replace rejected goods within the period prescribed by the CCT;
 fails to honour warranty obligations;
 repeatedly breaches delivery obligations; or
 repeatedly fails to comply with any material obligation of the contract.
Continuity of Supply
The CCT reserves the right to utilise the Alternate Tenderer where reasonably necessary to
ensure continuity of supply, service delivery requirements or operational continuity. The utilisation
of the Alternate Tenderer shall not give rise to any claim for compensation, loss of profit or damages
by the Winner.
No Exclusivity
Appointment to this contract shall not confer an exclusive right to supply the goods.
