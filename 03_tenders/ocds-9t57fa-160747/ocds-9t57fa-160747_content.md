ERRATUM TO NGB
EXPRESSION OF INTEREST TO
BLOCK ILLEGAL ONLINE
GAMBLING WEBSITES

1. PARAGRAPH 2: PURPOSE OF THE EXPRESSION OF INTEREST
1.1 Paragraph: 2.1 is amended as follows:
1.1.1 South Africa is facing serious challenges in combatting illegal interactive
gambling offering to South Africans. The enforcement challenge is
exacerbated by the offshore location of these illegal operators, as they
are coming from foreign jurisdictions.
1. PARAGRAPH 3: PROJECT OUTCOMES
2.1 Paragraph: 3.1 is amended as follows:
2.1.1 Service Providers are required to benchmark with other foreign
jurisdictions where similar services were rendered and indicate what
their observations, findings and recommendations are in relation to
website blocking. Service providers must indicate the potential areas of
revenue generation in utilising the models that they propose, if any
1. PARAGRAPH 4: EXPRESSION OF INTEREST
3.1 Paragraph: 4.3 is amended as follows:
3.1.1 The purpose of this EOI is to solicit proposals from potential service
providers willing to be invited to participate in a subsequent Request for
Proposal (RFP) or Tender process.
3.2 Paragraph: 4.4 is amended as follows:
3.2.1 Potential service providers will be invited to do a demonstration how
they will be blocking the websites as part of this EOI process. Service
providers are required to detail the approach and methodology they will
follow to provide the required services; who are their stakeholders that
they will likely to partner with to properly execute their mandate; what
levels of approvals and LOC they will require to operate in the country
and at what stage; how the service provider intends to address the
challenges that the NGB is currently exposed to; and if there is any
potential revenue streams or how can such potential be exploited to
sustain this services.

3.3 Paragraph: 4.4 is amended as follows:
3.3.1 Interested service providers must submit the following:
a) Company profile and relevant experience
b) Tax clearance certificate (if not an international service provider)
c) Any relevant certifications and/or licensing.
3.4 Paragraph: 8 is amended as follows:
3.4.1 All EOI submissions and related enquiries are to be addressed to the
Procurement Practitioner via email at eoi-
illegalonlineblocking@ngb.org.za, indicating Reference 01/2026_ED
and shall be received no later than 04 September 2026 at 12h00
Central African Time (CAT).
3.4.2 The maximum capacity of the email attachments is 50mb. Please
ensure that all information submitted complies with these requirements
as the NGB will not be liable for any submissions that do not reach this
domain due to size constraints.
3.4.3 Service providers are permitted to submit more than one email if the
email attachments exceed the 50mb size.
