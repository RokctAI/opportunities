ENQUIRY NO: CTT26927
DESCRIPTION: THE PROVISION OF A COMPREHENSIVE FEASIBILITY
ENGINEERING STUDY FOR PetroSA’s GAS-TO-POWER
PROJECT
1. INTRODUCTION
PetroSA invites suitably qualified and experienced service providers to undertake a
comprehensive Feasibility Engineering Study for the proposed Gas-to-Power Project
at the GTL Refinery in Mossel Bay. The project aims to assess the development of a
300-360 MW Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) power plant utilising indigenous
offshore gas resources and existing infrastructure.
1. SUMMARISED SCOPE OF WORK
The appointed Consultant will perform a feasibility-level engineering study to confirm
the technical and commercial viability of the proposed Gas-to-Power facility and
develop a bankable AACE Class 3 cost estimate. The scope includes:
• Development of the Basis of Design (BoD).
• Feasibility engineering for the power island and associated balance-of-plant
facilities.
• Assessment of existing utility infrastructure and required upgrades.
• Electrical system design, grid integration, and power evacuation studies.
• Identification of project risks, execution strategies, and implementation
schedules.
• Support for future Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) and EPC
