1. BACKGROUND AND INTRODUCTORY PROVISIONS
The Skills Development Act, (Act No. 97 of 1998) requires that the skill of the department
be developed according to the identified needs so that employees performance can
improve and the strategic goals of the department can be achieved. This Act was also
introduced to maximize the quality of production and the services delivered by the South
African workforce.
ECDOH embark on the process to capacitate its employees on Occupational Health and
Safety, Safety Health Representative and Fire Fighting to possess a requisite of skills
complying with the requirements of the Department of Labour. It is in process of
appointing a qualified and accredited service provider to conduct training in Occupational
Health and Safety, Safety Health Representative and Fire Fighting in all Health Care
Facilities. The bid will be a rate-based contract and the estimated number of employees
to be trained is about 1850 employees in the Province. The training will be conducted in
all Districts in the Province of the Eastern Cape Province.
