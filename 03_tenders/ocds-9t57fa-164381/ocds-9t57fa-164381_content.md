INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND\n2.1 The Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment (DFFE) is the custodian of South Africa’s\nforest resources. The Department has a legal mandate to ensure that the country's forests are developed\nand managed in a sustainable manner. Sustainable Forest Management (SFM) in South Africa is attained\nthrough the implementation and administration of two forestry legislations, namely, the National Forests\nAct, 1998 (Act No. 84 of 1998) as amended to be referred to as NFA in the document and the National\nVeld and Forest Fires Act (Act number 101 of 1998) as amended to be referred as NVFFA.\n2.2 As per section 6 (3) of the NFA, the Minister is mandated to report to parliament at least every three years\non the status of forests within the republic. The Minister is required to report on the following:\n• the facts and trends which emerge from forest monitoring;\n• report on whether the facts and trends observed are of the national interest;\n• the measures being implemented to address negative trends; and\n• other matters he or she considers appropriate.\nNB: This information is presented in the form of the State of the Forest (SoF) report. The current project\nis for the reporting period between 2022 and 2024.\n2.3 Accordingly, collection and updating of the State of the Forests within the republic is very important.\nThis is because the report provides an update on progress towards achieving sustainable forest\nmanagement within the country. The report outlines achievements and challenges faced by the forestry\nsector within the reporting period. Therefore, serve as a source of information for decision processes,\npolicy formulation and planning processes of the Department and entire forestry sector.\n2.4 Due to capacity challenges it may lead to delayed publishing of the report. The delayed or failure to\npublish the report will be a contravention of section 6(3) of the NFA.\n3\n\n1. OBJECTIVES\n3.1 The objective is to appoint a professional service provider to compile and produce the triennial State of\nthe Forests Report for the period 2022-2024, and the objectives of the SoF report are to:\n3.1.1 Present facts and trends on the status of the country’s forests as per the NFA definition of forest.\n3.1.2 Identify the drivers of change and present the possible implications of the trends considering whether\nthey are of national interest.\n3.1.3 Report on the socio-economic benefits of forests to the citizens of the country and other issues of\nnational importance.\n3.1.4 Identify the gaps in existing utilisation, protection and conservation efforts in-situ and ex-situ, in all forest\ntypes.\n3.1.5 Make recommendations on specific actions and future directions required to advance South Africa’s\nprogress towards SFM, especially for improving conservation efforts on all forest genetic resources\n3.1.6 Provide information that assist in planning and decision making by policy makers, local communities,\nconservation agencies, timber growers, timber processing enterprises and research institutions.\n3.1.7 Provide information that will enable the country to meet its national (contribution to the NDP 2030)\nregional and international reporting obligations such as but not limited to the implantation of the UN\nForest Instrument and the achievement of the Global Forest Resources Assessment led by FAO,\nreporting on progress towards achieving the Global Forest Goals led by UN Forum on Forests, as well\nas the global report on Sustainable Development Goals 2030.\n1.
