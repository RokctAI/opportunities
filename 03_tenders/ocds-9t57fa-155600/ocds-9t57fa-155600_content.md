Title:
Appointment of a competent and legally compliant waste management service provider to
manage the collection, segregation, treatment, handling, transportation, and disposal of all
waste streams generated on site, including the provision of an on-site waste sorter/operator
and the operation of an autoclave for waste treatment for a period of 6 months.
Scope:
The scope includes the collection, sorting, treatment, recycling, and lawful disposal of multiple
waste streams, as well as the provision of a dedicated on-site waste sorter and autoclave
operation.
1. WASTE STREAMS
The appointed service provider shall manage the following waste streams:
1.1 General Waste
• Non-hazardous domestic and office waste
• Collection, transportation, and disposal at a licensed landfill
1.2 Recyclable Waste
• Paper and cardboard
• Plastic
• Glass
• Steel and other recyclable metals
• Supply of clearly labelled recycling containers
• Separation, baling (where applicable), and removal
1.3 Animal Waste
• Animal by-products and manure
• Bedding and contaminated animal waste
• Carcases (Removal and Disposal)
1.4 Pharmaceutical Waste
• Expired, damaged, or recalled pharmaceutical products
• Contaminated pharmaceutical packaging
• Controlled and non-controlled pharmaceutical waste
1.5 Hazardous Waste
• Chemical waste
• Contaminated materials
• Laboratory waste

1.6 Anatomical Waste
• Human and/or animal anatomical waste
• Pathological waste
• Handling and treatment in compliance with biohazard and healthcare waste
regulations
1.7 Electronic Waste (E-Waste)
• Computers, monitors, printers
• Electrical and electronic components
• Batteries and cables
• Data destruction certification where applicable
• Empty printer cartridges
2. On-Site Waste Sorting and Treatment
2.1 Waste Sorter
The service provider shall provide a dedicated, trained waste sorter on site, whose
responsibilities shall include:
• Ensuring correct segregation of waste streams
• Preventing cross-contamination
• Monitoring compliance with waste segregation rules
• Reporting non-conformances to SHE Department
• Maintaining housekeeping standards in waste areas
2.2 Autoclave Operation
The waste sorter/operator shall:
• Operate the on-site autoclave for treatment of applicable waste streams
• Ensure correct loading, cycle selection, and validation
• Maintain treatment records
• Ensure treated waste is rendered non-infectious prior to disposal
• Report breakdowns or non-compliance immediately
3. Recycling and Rebates
The service provider must offer rebates for the recovery and recycling of the following
materials:
• Steel
• Electronic waste (E-waste)
• Paper
• Cardboard
Rebate requirements:
• Clear rebate structure and rates must be provided by the service provider
• Rebates to be reflected as credits on monthly invoices
• Weight tickets or recycling certificates to be supplied as proof

4. Service Levels
• Scheduled collections as agreed (Weekly on Friday’s collections of general
waste)
• Emergency and ad-hoc collections when required (for other waste streams)
• Replacement of damaged containers
• Clean and hygienic waste areas are always maintained
5. Compliance and Legal Requirements
The service provider must submit the following compulsory requirements:
• Produce a valid Waste Accreditation Permit to collect hazardous waste issued by
the City of Tshwane and/ or any relevant regulatory statutory requirements. The
bidder must provide proof of a valid Waste Accreditation Permit and relevant
permits relating to waste management.
• Use licensed treatment and disposal facilities (Proof of license or
Agreement/MOU with a destruction facility)
• Submit waste destruction methodology used for waste destruction
• Provide waste manifests, safe disposal certificates, and treatment records
• Maintain valid insurance (public liability, environmental liability)
6. Reporting and Documentation
The contractor shall provide:
• Monthly waste reports by waste stream
• Quantities collected, treated, recycled, and disposed
• Autoclave treatment records
• Certificates of safe disposal and destruction
7. Health, Safety, and Environmental Controls
• Contractor to submit a Safety, Health, and Environmental (SHE) File (Before
commencement)
• Update safety file as in and when required
• Ensure staff are trained, competent, and medically fit (vaccinated for hepatitis A, B
and tetanus)
• Risk assessments and method statements for waste handling
• Use of appropriate PPE (Supplied by service provider)
• Incident reporting and investigation procedures
• All employees to attend induction upon approval of safety file
8. Ownership & Responsibility
8.1 All supplied containers and consumables shall remain the property of the Service
Provider, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
The Service Provider shall be responsible for:
• Cleaning and sanitising containers
• Replacing lost or damaged containers due to normal wear and tear
• Ensuring adequate quantities are available at all times
