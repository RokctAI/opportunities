BACKGROUND\nHypervisors are software platforms that create and run Virtual Machines (VMs). Hypervisor\nInfrastructure host Information Systems like SAP, Landmark, TVBMS, TV-License Debtor\nsystem, Audit- and Risk management, Contract management, Treasury management and the\nSABC websites and must have a reliable service that can respond to business requirements in\na quick and effective manner.\nFor the past 17 years SABC has successfully deployed VMware Hypervisor as solution to host\nabout 150 applications on Windows and Linux based operating systems. This enables hosting\nof multiple servers in a virtual environment and results in efficiencies of electricity, floor space,\nmanpower, networking and architectural simplicity as 99% of applications are supported on it\nand thus eliminate the need for procurement of servers to host every new application that is\ndeployed.\n1. REASON\nThis Request for Information (RFI) seeks to engage the market and gather information on\ncurrent hypervisor solutions.\nSABC operates a significant number of legacy applications and continues to rely heavily on on-\npremises storage infrastructure. While many modern hypervisor solutions offer cloud-native\ncapabilities and cloud-based storage integration, these approaches may not fully meet the\norganization's current operational requirements. Although SABC is progressing toward greater\ncloud adoption, the transition will take time.\nTherefore, it is essential to evaluate and compare available hypervisor solutions, considering\ntheir compatibility with legacy systems, support for on-premises storage, functionality,\nscalability, cost implications, and long-term strategic fit. This assessment will help the\norganization understand the trade-offs associated with each option and select the solution that\nbest supports its business and technology objectives.\n1.
