1 INTRODUCTION
The Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) is one of the leading scientific
research and technology development organisations in Africa. In partnership with national and
international research and technology institutions, the CSIR undertakes directed and
multidisciplinary research and technology innovation that contributes to the improvement of
the quality of life of South Africans. The CSIR’s main site is in Pretoria while it is represented
in other provinces of South Africa through regional offices.
The CSIR researches, develops, localises, and diffuses technologies to accelerate
socioeconomic prosperity in South Africa. The organisation’s work contributes to industrial
development and supports a capable state. The CSIR collaborates with a number of State
Departments, academia and the private sector to develop and demonstrate new technologies
for maritime systems and related applications. The CSIR seeks a potential, suitably qualified
partner for the development of above and below-water sensor platforms, systems and
technologies.
The Technical Specification in Annexure B details the proposal requirements.
