on street poles for a three-year term, which expired on April 30, 2020. The contract was not

the municipality has now decided to utilize all street poles and bus shelters in designated areas
across Nelspruit, Barberton, White River, and Hazyview, including bus terminus, park and
rides, taxi ranks and other Municipal Infrastructure for commercial advertising.
