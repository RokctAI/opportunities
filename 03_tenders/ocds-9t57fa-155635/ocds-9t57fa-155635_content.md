2. COMPANY OVERVIEW
South African Broadcasting Corporation (SABC) is a Public Entity founded in August 1936 and listed in terms of Schedule
2 of the Public Finance Management Act, Act No. 1 of 1999, as a public broadcaster in South Africa, and provides 19 radio
stations as well as five television broadcasts to the general public.
3. BACKGROUND
SABC Auckland Park operates a total of 31 transformers that supply power to both TV and Radio Park buildings. These
transformers are critical to the uninterrupted broadcasting operations of the SABC. To restore reliability, protect SABC's
electrical infrastructure, and optimize electricity usage and costs, comprehensive major maintenance is now urgently
required across all transformer units.
4. OBJECTIVES OF THE MAINTENANCE
• Restore transformers to optimal operational condition.
• Improve overall power factor and reduce electricity losses.
• Extend the lifespan of the equipment.
• Ensure compliance with electrical and safety regulations.
• Prevent equipment failure that could disrupt broadcasting operations.
• Ensure accurate readings and billing with the municipality.
5. TOTAL TRANSFORMERS ON SITE
TV and Radio Park: 31 transformers
• Sub A: 2x 1500kVA
• Sub B: 2x 1500kVA
• Sub C: 1x 1000kVA
• Sub D: 2x 1500kVA
• Sub F: 3x 1000kVA
• Ground Floor Sub: 1x 500Kva
• Chiller Plant: 1x 600kVA
• TV Side Transformers: 20x units (varied capacity)
