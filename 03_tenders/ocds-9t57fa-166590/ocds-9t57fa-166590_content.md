PRODUCT PARTICULAR SPECIFICATIONS
APPOINTMENT OF FACILITATOR FOR EXPOERT READINESS PROGRAM IN THE CHRIS
HANI, JOE GQABI, AMATHOLE AND SARAH BAARTMAN
1. BACKGROUND
The Department of Agriculture is prioritizing commercialization of 100 farms and revitalization of
irrigation schemes. The services required from the accounting service providers seek to provide
support on prioritise farms with accounting services. Farmers could not access loans on financial
institutions due to lack of financial statements, hence the need to provide support for accounting
services.
The Department is currently implementing South African Good Agricultural Practices (SA-GAP)
certification and domestic market access programs to improve the competitiveness of smallholder
farmers. These interventions have enabled participating farmers to meet local market requirements
and establish sustainable supply relationships within South Africa. To build on these achievements,
the Department intends to implement an Export Readiness Programme aimed at preparing selected
smallholder farmers to participate competitively in international markets. The programme will provide
farmers with the knowledge, technical skills, and practical support required to comply with export
market requirements, including international food safety standards, traceability systems,
phytosanitary and sanitary requirements, quality assurance protocols, export documentation,
logistics, packaging and labelling, and international market intelligence.
1. SCOPE
The training and catering service will be conducted as follows
a) over the course of 4 days at Chris Hani District; 4 days at Joe Gqabi District; 4 days in Buffalo
City; and 4 days in Gqeberha & Sarah Baartman with a focus on group training that is in
accordance with the material.
b) Thereafter, the service provider will group companies according to sectors and state of
readiness and provide mentorship to the participants.
c) A foundational training on the problems, processes, and advantages of exporting will be a
major focus of this phase.
d) To improve the effectiveness of the group session, the information gathered on the pre-
assessment training will be used to set the tone of the training.
e) The training provider is expected to also provide catering service. Catering menu is attached
as a guide. Service providers MUST submit their menu for the entire duration of the training.
The menu is expected to be a variety throughout the training.
