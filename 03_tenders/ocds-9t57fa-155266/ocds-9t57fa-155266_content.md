D. SPECIFICATION
1. BACKGROUND
Lot 220 Langalibalele street in PMB house two four story building which is Post office a heritage building and
The old Telkom exchange, LV/Switch gears and the Municipal transformer are located in the exchange build-
ing, which was broken into and Electrical cables, copper bazz bars, electric meter and components were stolen,
resulting in a disruption of electricity and preventing SAPO from trading and functioning
2. OBJECTIVE
The objective is to secure services an experience and qualified electrical company to repairs and restore elec-
trical supply to the Post Office
3. SCOPE OF WORK
3.1 Inspect existing LV panel and Test current panel functionality (identify damage, assess condition).
3.2 Disconnect and safely remove stolen/compromised cables.
3.3 Supply and install replacement 4-core, various dimensions and lengths cables including all sundries.
3.4 Ensure all electrical connections supplying electricity to the post office are correct
3.5 Conduct full testing and verification (voltage, continuity, safety checks).
3.6 Final report to include all the following, List cable types and sizes (eg, copper bazz bars, 4-core cables,
specific gauge) confirm all is safe and functional
3.7 Supply and install two burglar Gate, constructed of 25 x4.5 mm mild steel flat bar, welded both vertically
and horizontally to form a grid spaced at maximum 150mm centres to windows.( 2500 X 1800 mm)
4. LEGISLATIVE AND TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
4.1 The Occupational Health and Safety Act, 1993.
4.2 The Local Municipal Bye-Laws and the regulations of the Local Supply Authority
4.3 The Electrical installation must adhere to the SABS 0142 standards and in accordance with the ECB code
of conduct.
4.4 Supplier must be CIDB registered for category 2 EB – Active status.
4.5 Supplier must be registered and in good standing with the Compensation for Occupation Injuries and Dis-
eases Act (COIDA) of 1993 / Workman’s Compensations.
