BACKGROUND
The objective of this assessment is to determine how mature TCTA’s ethics management maturity
processes are. This will be determined by an independent evaluation of the degree to which ethical
programme elements are integrated within TCTA. The purpose of the assignment is to:
• Obtain an independent view of the effectiveness of the institution’s ethics framework.
• Assess key ethics risks impacting the organisation.
• Obtain high‑level employee insights through an anonymous ethics survey.
• Identify priority actions to strengthen ethical governance and culture.
SCOPE OF WORK
DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF GOODS/SERVICES

Engage an independent external consultancy firm to assess the maturity of TCTA’s ethical culture
by conducting an anonymous ethics survey and a comprehensive risk assessment, including
benchmarking against industry standards. This service aims to provide objective insights and
actionable recommendations to strengthen ethical compliance and improve culture maturity.
The service provider will be required to perform the following integrated activities:
• A desktop and interview-based assessment of the institution’s ethics arrangements, providing
TCTA with a concise ethics maturity assessment summary highlighting key strengths and gaps,
overall maturity positioning, and priority improvement areas.
• Design and administration of a short anonymous ethics survey to gauge employee perceptions
of ethical culture, providing TCTA with documented survey results summary and key insights
linked to ethics risks and maturity.
• An ethics risk assessment relevant to the institution’s mandate, operating environment, and
inherent risks.
COMPANY EXPERIENCE REQUIRED
An independent consultancy offering a range of services and products related to organisational
ethics. The company profile must demonstrate experience in ethics, governance, risk, integrity work,
experience in the public sector environment, and ability to administer anonymous surveys. The
company must demonstrate having successfully implemented a minimum of projects of similar scope
which mut be evidenced by signed reference letters on official company letterhead in conducting
ethics maturity assessments (which incorporate anonymous ethics survey and an ethics
(quantitative and qualitative) risk assessment).
PERSONNEL EXPERIENCE REQUIRED
The bidder must assign at least one (1) Ethics Officer certified by the Ethics Institute of South Africa,
with a minimum of five (5) years’ experience in conducting ethics maturity assessments,
incorporating anonymous ethics surveys and a combination of quantitative and qualitative risk
assessments.
DELIVERABLES
1. Organisational Maturity Assessment Summary Report:
Strengths and Weaknesses: Highlighting what’s working and what needs attention.
Recommendations: Prioritized actions for improvement.
Roadmap: Steps to move from current to desired maturity level.
2. Anonymous ethics survey outcomes analysis:
Overall and by dimension.
3. ERA Report, aligned with the TEI’s Ethics Risk Handbook which includes an ethical landscape
summary, full risk analysis, survey‑linked risk insights, control testing outcomes, and an
improvement roadmap. The Report must be supported by:
• A Risk Register as primary output- using TCTA’s adopted template.

• An Ethics Risk Heat map/Dashboard- a visual summary plotting the rating of each risk and
prioritisation of high-risk areas.
4. A presentation to management or a governance structure (virtual)
All deliverables must be concise, clearly written, and suitable for senior management and oversight
committees.
