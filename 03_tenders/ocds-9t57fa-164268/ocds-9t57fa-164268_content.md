1. BACKGROUND
The eThekwini Municipality operates a self-insurance scheme through its Self-Insurance Reserve
Fund, which provides cover for various municipal risks, including comprehensive insurance for the
municipal vehicle fleet. The fleet plays a critical role in supporting service delivery across the
municipality.
Due to the operational demands placed on the fleet, vehicles may from time to time sustain accident
or collision damage requiring specialised body repair and restoration services. While the Municipality
has limited internal repair capacity, certain repairs and restoration work must be outsourced to
suitably qualified and equipped external service providers.
This tender therefore seeks to establish a panel of competent panel beaters and body repairers who
can provide professional repair services to restore municipal vehicles to their pre-accident condition
when required.

body repair service providers to form part of an approved panel for the provision of vehicle body
repair services to the municipal fleet.

damage, collision damage, or other body-related deterioration are repaired and restored to their pre-
accident condition in accordance with acceptable industry standards.
