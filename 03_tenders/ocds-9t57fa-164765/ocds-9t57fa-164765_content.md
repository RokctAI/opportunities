BACKGROUND INFORMATION\nThe Industrial Development Corporation of South Africa Limited (IDC) is a development\nfinance institution mandated to promote economic growth, industrial development, and job\ncreation in South Africa. As part of executing this mandate, IDC undertakes regular\ninternational travel to support investment activities, project assessments, stakeholder\nengagements, and organisational objectives.\nIDC Corporate Travel is an internal function responsible for managing official travel\narrangements for IDC and its subsidiaries, employees and delegations. Due to the\norganization’s ongoing international engagements, there is a continuous need for reliable\nforeign currency services to support official travel requirements. These services include the\nbuying and selling of foreign currency, provision and management of multi-currency cards,\nand access to foreign currency through approved collection or delivery channels.\nThe IDC therefore seeks to appoint a competent service provider to ensure the efficient,\nsecure, and cost-effective provision of foreign currency services in support of its corporate\ntravel operations, while ensuring compliance with applicable regulatory and governance\nrequirements.\n1. SCOPE OF WORK/TERMS OF REFERENCE\nThe appointed service provider will be required to provide comprehensive foreign currency\nservices to support IDC Corporate Travel for official international travel. The scope of work\nincludes, but is not limited to, the following:\n3.1. Foreign Currency Services\no Buying and selling foreign currency at competitive and market-related exchange rates.\no Provision of a range of major and commonly used international currencies required by\nIDC travelers.\no Transparent pricing, clearly indicating margins, fees, and any applicable charges.\n3.2. Multi-Currency Card Services\no Issuing, loading, reloading, and cash-out of multi-currency travel cards.\no Ability to manage multiple currencies on a single card. (i.e. US Dollar, Euro, British\nPounds, Australian dollars, Chinese Yaun, Dirhams and Swiss Francs etc.)\n12\n\no Secure card management, including PIN control, lost/stolen card support, and balance\nenquiries.\no Online or electronic access to transaction history and balances, where applicable.\n3.3. Delivery and Collection Channels\no Foreign currency collection and/or delivery to designated IDC offices.\no Availability of foreign currency collection at major airports, including but not limited to\nJohannesburg, Durban, and Cape Town.\no Clearly defined turnaround times for currency orders, deliveries, and collections.\n3.4. Account Management and Support\no Dedicated account manager and support team for IDC Corporate Travel.\no Advisory support on foreign currency requirements, trends, and best practices for\ncorporate travel.\no Efficient handling of enquiries, issues, and escalations.\n3.5. Systems Integration\no Ability to integrate, or demonstrate capability to integrate, foreign currency services with\nIDC Corporate Travel’s online booking and travel management tools.\no Provision of system interfaces, data feeds or reporting outputs to support streamlined\nbooking, reconciliation, tracking, and management of foreign currency transactions.\n3.6. Compliance and Governance\no Full compliance with South African Reserve Bank (SARB) regulations, exchange\ncontrol requirements, and applicable legislation.\no Adherence to IDC governance, risk management, and internal control requirements.\no Compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in the handling of\npersonal and financial information.\n3.7. Reporting and Management Information\no Provision of periodic reports on foreign currency transactions, volumes, usage trends,\nand costs, as required by IDC.\no Ability to support audit, reconciliation, and management review processes.\n3.8.
