1. INTRODUCTION / SCOPE OF WORK
This specification defines the technical, operational, and contractual requirements for the appointment
of a competent service provider for the leasing, supply, maintenance, and support of forklifts and
shunting tractors in Transnet Engineering facilities Durban, Salt River and Koedoespoort, including
respective sub-regions.
The equipment will be used for material handling, logistics support, and rail vehicle shunting
operations, which are critical to operational continuity. The service provider is therefore required to
ensure high reliability, safety compliance, and availability of equipment at all times.
This specification establishes minimum requirements and does not relieve the contractor from
applying sound engineering practice, asset management principles, and continuous improvement.
The scope includes:
• Provision of equipment for continuous monthly operation (80, 100 and 260 hours on the
following site requirements Salt River, Koedoespoort and Durban respectively)
• Preventative and corrective maintenance in line with OEM standards
• Breakdown support and emergency response
• Equipment, performance monitoring and reporting
• Compliance with all statutory and safety requirements

and pneumatic tyres shall not be accepted.

against falling objects without restricting visibility.
5.3 All forklifts shall be fitted with standard safety features including a reverse alarm,
flashing or strobe light, mirrors, horn, and a mounted fire extinguisher.
5.4 To support operational control and monitoring, all units shall include a driver tracking
and telematics system with operator identification capability.

1. SERVICE, MAINTENANCE AND BREAKDOWN RESPONSE
6.1 The Service Provider shall be fully responsible for maintaining all equipment in a safe,
reliable, and operational condition throughout the contract period. Maintenance shall be
proactive and aligned to OEM requirements, including scheduled preventative servicing,
timely repairs, and proper record-keeping. All equipment must always remain compliant
with safety and statutory requirements
6.2 A structured breakdown response system shall be in place to ensure rapid fault
resolution. The Service Provider shall respond to breakdowns within a few hours and
complete repairs within 48 hours. Where this is not achievable, equivalent replacement
equipment shall be provided at no additional cost to Transnet Engineering.
For the purposes of this document, breakdowns shall be limited to failures arising from
manufacturing defects, mechanical defects, or normal wear and tear of the forklifts and
tractors during ordinary operation. Any breakdown, damage, or failure determined to
have been caused by user negligence, misuse, abuse, operator error, or any other user-
related fault shall be deemed a user default and will fall outside the scope of this
contract. The costs associated with such user-default incidents shall be excluded from
the contractor’s obligations under this agreement
6.3 The Service Provider shall base maintenance planning, service intervals, and resource
allocation on the defined operational usage requirements per site. For pricing and
service calculations, the following standard usage assumptions shall apply: Salt River
(SRX) – 80 operating hours per month; Koedoespoort (KDS) – 100 operating hours per
month; and Durban (DBN) – up to 260 operating hours per month.
Bidders are required to take careful note of the estimated operating hours provided for
each forklift when preparing their pricing. The annual maintenance cost must be
calculated based on the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) recommended
preventive maintenance schedule and service intervals applicable to the specified

Engineering.
