Purpose\nThis switching philosophy defines the safe operating sequences for transformer incomers and bus\ncouplers fitted with Masterpact MTZ air circuit breakers and mechanical trapped-key interlocking.\nThe philosophy ensures:\n• No paralleling of transformers\n• Controlled load transfer\n• Safe isolation for maintenance\n• Fail-safe operation independent of control power\nA2. Switchboard Configuration\nDesignation Description\nL1 Incomer – Transformer 1\nL3 Incomer – Transformer 2\nL5 Incomer – Transformer 3\nL7 Incomer – Transformer 4\nL2 Bus Coupler\nL4 Bus Coupler\nL6 Bus Coupler\nEach incomer feeds its own bus section. Bus couplers allow temporary load transfer between adjacent\nsections.\nA3. General Operating Rules\n1. Only one transformer may feed a bus section at any time.\n2. Bus couplers shall not be closed when both adjacent incomers are closed.\n3. Incomers shall not be closed when the associated bus coupler is closed.\n4. Switching shall only be carried out by authorised personnel.\n5. Mechanical key interlocking shall never be bypassed.\n6. If a key cannot be released, the operating condition is unsafe.\nA4. Normal Operating Condition\n• All incomer ACBs (L1, L3, L5, L7): CLOSED\n• All bus coupler ACBs (L2, L4, L6): OPEN\n• All keys trapped in respective breakers\nA5. Open Incomer L5\n• L5 key is released\n1. Insert L5 key into key exchange box\n• Releases L6 key\n1. Insert L6 key into Bus Coupler L6\n2.
