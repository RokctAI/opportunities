PRODUCT PARTICULAR SPECIFICATIONS
1. BACKGROUND
Department of Agriculture is seeking services from a suitably qualified service provider to render
training services on Hazardous Biological Agents Regulations of the Occupational Health and
Safety Act 85 of 1993. The training audience are the Veterinary Services employees. Content of
the training should be attached by prospective Service Providers.
The OHSA further states that an employer shall before any employee exposed or maybe exposed
to Hazardous Biological Agents (HBA) ensure that the employee is adequately informed and
trained on both practical aspects and theoretical knowledge with regard to the potential exposure of
HBA, the measures to be taken by the employer to protect the employee against any risk of being
exposed and the precautions to be taken by the employee to protect him or herself against the
health risks associated with the exposure of HBA.
Prospective service providers responding to this request must provide fully-fledged and operational
venue set up for conducive learning environment for adults. The trainings are expected to be
conducted as per different areas stipulated at the bottom of the document. The facilitator is also
expected to find its own venue for the training.
1. SCOPE OF WORK
The training required is for a group of one hundred and two 102 officials for a minimum of 2 days
each group, which must include the following:
• Learning material and stationery
• Venues and Facility
• Morning Tea, Lunch (a cooked meal with salads) and Afternoon Tea
• Certificate of competency on completion of training
At the end of the course the expected outcomes are as follows:
• The contents and scope of Hazardous Biological Agents Regulations.
• The potential risks to health caused by the exposure to Hazardous Biological Agents.
• Measures to be taken by the employer to protect the employee against any risk of being
exposed.
• Precautions to be taken by the employee to protect him or herself against the health risks
associated with the exposure of HBA including the wearing and use of protective clothing
and respiratory protective equipment.
• The importance of good housekeeping at the workplace and personal Hygiene requirements.

engineering control measures provided.
• The necessity of medical surveillance.
