BACKGROUND
TCTA utilises the Sage 300 Finance system as a core financial management platform, together with
various integrated third-party software components. The original contractual arrangement for the
system was only for Sage licences. Two variation orders were approved to increase the scope of the
contract to include Sage upgrade, support and maintenance, Orchid Systems installation. The last
variation order was approved for support and maintenance only.
Following further contractual amendments, the scope of the current contract was limited to support
and maintenance services only. The software licences procured under the prior variation remain in
use; however, they are not covered under the existing contractual arrangement and are approaching
expiry. The majority of the third-party software licences are due to expire in July 2026, while the Sage
300 Finance Premium licences are scheduled to expire in September 2026.

Failure to procure replacement licences presents a risk of service disruption to critical financial
systems. This requisition is therefore submitted to facilitate the procurement of the required Sage
300 Finance and integrated third-party software licences to ensure continuity of operations and
system stability.
