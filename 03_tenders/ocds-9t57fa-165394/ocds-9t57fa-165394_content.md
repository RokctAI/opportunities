1. BACKGROUND IZIKO
Iziko Museums of South Africa (Iziko) is a schedule 3A public entity and non-profit organisation,
partly subsidised by the National Department of Sport, Arts & Culture (DSAC), bringing together
12 national museums and a Social History Centre situated in the Western Cape under a single
governance and leadership structure. Iziko was established in terms of the Cultural Institutions
Act, 1998 (Act No. 119 of 1998) and is required to comply with the Public Finance Management
Act (PFMA), 1999 (Act No. 1 of 1999, as amended) and its concomitant Regulations.
1. OVERVIEW
The Iziko Museums of South Africa intends to source and procure, Mobile Portable Ablution
toilets and servicing of the ablution equipment to use at our site, located at Klein Drakenstein
Paarl, Schuurmansfontein Road, Iziko Mandela Prison House rented for a period of 24 months
(2 years)
1. SPECIFICATION
A series of serviced chemical toilets are required at the above-mentioned site. This
service must:
Scope of works:
• Supply female toilet facilities similar or equivalent to those indicated in Annexure A,
Figure 1 or 3, providing a minimum of 5 individual toilets (cubicles) in total. The
configuration of units is at the supplier’s discretion.
• Supply male toilet facilities similar or equivalent to those indicated in Annexure A Figure 2
or 3, providing a minimum of 2 individual toilets (cubicles) and 3 urinals in total. The
configuration of units (including urinals) is at the supplier’s discretion.
• Supply 1 accessible (paraplegic) male toilet.
• Supply 1 accessible (paraplegic) female toilet.
• All toilets to have lighting capability.
• Include delivery to site and full installation/setup of all units.
• Include weekly servicing, including cleaning and waste removal.
1. PERIOD
Toilets are expected to remain on site for a period of up to 24 months; please provide a monthly
rental rate. A provision should be made for a cancellation notice period of 2 months to apply.
Iziko museums of South Africa may, given budget, elect to terminate contract earlier/or extend
further
