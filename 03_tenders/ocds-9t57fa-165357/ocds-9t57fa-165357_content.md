APPOINTMENT OF A PANEL OF NATIONAL TERTIARY INSTITUTIONS TO PROVIDE
STRUCTURED LEARNING PROGRAMMES FOR A PERIOD OF 36 MONTHS
In terms of Section 110 of the Municipal Finance Management Act, 2003 (No. 56 of 2003), tenders
are hereby invited for the appointment of a panel of national tertiary institutions to provide structured
learning programmes for a period of 36 months.
Tender documents and specifications are available and will ONLY be sold at a non-refundable fee
of R500.00 from the 14th of August 2026. Tender documents will be sold at the Cash Point by the
Cashier’s (No EFT)
The closing time for receipt of tenders is 12:00hrs on 16/09/2026. No telephonic, e-mail, unmarked
and late tenders will under no circumstances be considered and accepted. The tender box will be
emptied just after closing time on the closing date. Hereafter all bids will be public.

Abbreviations and meanings:
Abbreviation Meaning
SAQA South African Qualifications Authority (a statutory body responsible for
overseeing the development, registration, and quality assurance of the National

DHET Department of Higher Education and Training (It is the government body
responsible for tertiary education, including universities, Technical and Vocational
Education and Training (TVET) colleges)
HET Higher Education and Training (the tertiary phase of the education system,
encompassing all post-school education and training that leads to formal
qualifications (such as Higher Certificates, Diplomas, and Degrees)
CHE Council on Higher Education (the Council on Higher Education. It is an
independent statutory body established by the Higher Education Act of 1997.)
HEQC Higher Education Quality Committee (is the permanent subcommittee of the
Council on Higher Education (CHE) in South Africa. Established by the Higher
Education Act, it is the statutory body responsible for auditing institutional quality,
promoting quality assurance, and accrediting academic programs across all public
and private higher education providers)
