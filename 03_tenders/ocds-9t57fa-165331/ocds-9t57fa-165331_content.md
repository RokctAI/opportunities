1 BACKGROUND
The SABC Auckland Park campus requires a standardized, long-term maintenance solution for its industrial
kitchen appliances. Given the high daily utilization of these assets, a usage-based maintenance schedule is
mandatory to mitigate the risk of unplanned downtime. The lack of regular maintenance since equipment
installation, has resulted in frequent adhoc repairs, inconsistent service delivery, unpredictable costs, and
potential health and safety non-compliance. The City of Johannesburg conducts regular inspections, and
noncompliance prohibits the issuing of the required certification, to provide canteen services on the premises.
Equipment failures during peak operating hours, disrupt service delivery, leading to disgruntled staff and
negatively impacts supplier performance
2 REQUIREMENTS AND SCOPE OF SERVICES
To ensure equipment longevity and safety:
• Preventative Maintenance (Scheduled): Conducting regular inspections, calibrations, and servicing on
a monthly or quarterly basis according to manufacturer requirements to mitigate failure risk.
• Corrective Maintenance (Repairs): Provision of all labour, tools, and technical machinery to fix existing
equipment on an "as and when required" basis.
• Emergency Callouts: Rapid response for critical breakdowns that threaten business continuity or safety
(e.g.cold storage failure).
• Asset Management: Maintaining an updated asset register and a detailed log of all repairs and part
replacements for each machine.
• Compliance: Ensuring equipment meets statutory safety standards.
2.2 Technical Scope
• Thermal/Hot Equipment: Servicing of industrial ovens, steam kettles, grills, deep fryers,etc.
• Refrigeration & HVAC: Maintenance of standalone fridge units, walk-in fridge/freezers, display units,
extraction/ventilation systems.
o Extraction & Ventilation Systems:
o Deep Cleaning: Specialized degreasing of the entire extraction system, including canopies, ducting, and
fan motors, to prevent fire hazards and ensure compliance.
o Filter Maintenance: Monthly cleaning or replacement of baffle and grease filters to maintain airflow
efficiency.
• Fan & Motor Servicing: Regular inspection of belts, bearings, and motor housing for kitchen exhaust fans
