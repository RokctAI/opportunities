The agency also undertakes the programme and project management for\ndevelopment of housing and human settlements. The HDA carries out its functions in\npartnership with a range of stakeholders including national, provincial and local government\nand municipalities, as well as with communities, developers and financiers. It was established\nin 2009 through an Act of Parliament passed in 2008. The HDA is accountable to the Minister\nof Human Settlements, Water and Sanitation, through its Board of Directors.\nThe Housing Development HDA (HDA) [North-West Regional Office] has an existing Medium-\nTerm Operational Plan (MTOP) agreement with the North-West Department of Human\nSettlements (NWDHS) from 2021/22 to 25/2026 FY. A new MTOP agreement has recently\nbeen signed between the HDA and NWDHS for a period of 4 years (2026 FY to 2029/2030).\nThe renewed agreement assigns the HDA a new mandate for implementing the asbestos\nprogram, however, the new agreement is only effective in April 2026.\nThe current MTOP provides for a platform for NWDHS to make ad hoc requests as and when\nrequired to HDA. Through this, the HDA received Instruction to Perform Work (IPW) from\nNWDHS to undertake the Asbestos project within the current 2026/27 FY. The asbestos\nproject shall include as part of the scope; to undertake roof removal and remedial as per the\nassessment report from NHBRC. The project as per the IPW shall take place in both the\nKetlengriver and JB Marks Local municipalities.\nAsbestos, known to cause fatal diseases such as asbestosis, lung cancer, and mesothelioma,\nposes a significant health risk. It is a silent and perilous threat, particularly when an individual\nis exposed to concentrated or moderate amounts regularly over an extended period. The\nsymptoms of these diseases typically manifest 20 to 30 years after the initial exposure.\nRegular exposure, even at moderate levels, may pose severe risk and may cause asbestosis\ndisease.
