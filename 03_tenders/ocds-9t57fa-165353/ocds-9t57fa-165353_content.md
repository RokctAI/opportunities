refundable fee of R 1000.00 from the 14th of August 2026. Tender documents will be sold
at the Cashier’s point only in Secunda main municipal offices, during office hours. Tender
documents are not allowed to be mailed to bidders.
The closing time for receipt of bids is 12:00hrs on 15/09/2026. Telegraphic, telex, facsimile,
e-mail, unmarked and late bids will under no circumstances be considered and accepted.
The tender box will be emptied just after closing time on the closing date. Hereafter all bids
will be public.
