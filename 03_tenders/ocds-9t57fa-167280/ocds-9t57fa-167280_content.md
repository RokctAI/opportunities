SECTION 1: PURPOSE
Ingquza Hill Local Municipality intends to conduct a Development of Lambasi Precinct; a rural administrative area and
coastal region located near Lusikisiki on the Wild Coast of the Eastern Cape.
SECTION 2: BACKGROUND
Ingquza Hill Local Municipality has identified Lambasi as one of its strategic growth areas requiring a detailed precinct-
level planning framework, therefore the municipality intends to appoint a service provider for the Development of
Lambasi Precinct in line with the Ingquza Hill Spatial Development Framework, Ingquza Hill Land Use Management
Scheme, Spatial Planning & Land Use Management By-laws 2016 as well as the Land Survey Act 1997.
The subject site is situated within Ingquza Hill local municipality, roughly North-East of Port St Johns and close to
Mkambati. The precinct is characterised by Traditional villages under customary land tenure, scattered homesteads,
strong cultural and traditional leadership structures. Additionally, the local economy is largely based on Livestock
farming, Maize production, Vegetable farming, Household food gardens and Small-scale crop cultivation therefore the
Development of the precinct will also assist in future plans of diversifying the economy in line with the vision and
objectives of the Ingquza Hill Spatial Development Framework as at the core of the municipality’s priorities are the
Economic and Social Development Strategies aimed explicitly at reducing inequalities within our communities,
increasing opportunities for all and improving everyone’s quality and standard of living.
Although the precinct is predominantly rural, its strategic location presents significant opportunities for integrated rural
development, tourism, agriculture, fisheries, community-based economic development, coastal development, human
settlements, environmental conservation, infrastructure investment and local economic development as its location
provides access to:
• Wild Coast beaches
• Coastal forests
• Rivers and estuaries
• Traditional rural settlements
• Agricultural land
• Eco-tourism attractions.
As part of these leading municipal strategies, IHLM is committed to optimising coastal opportunities for all its
communities and where possible, restore some equity in coastal land ownership through prioritising coastal
development that will connect previously disadvantaged communities to the coastline.
Proposals are invited from suitably qualified multidisciplinary consulting firms or consortiums with demonstrated
experience in detailed precinct planning, spatial and regional planning, urban design, environmental management, and
stakeholder engagement to undertake the preparation of the Lambasi Precinct Development Plan. The appointed
service provider shall be responsible for delivering a comprehensive precinct plan that complies with all applicable
legislative requirements and is suitable for adoption and implementation by the Municipality.
