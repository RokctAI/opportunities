1. SPECIFICATION OF CONTRACT/SCOPE OF WORK
2. PURPOSE
 The purpose of this specification is to appoint a suitably qualified supplier to supply genuine
AutoCad and Revit software licenses for the use by Municipal officials involved in architec-
tural, engineering, drafting, building design and technical documentation.
2.SCOPE OF SUPPLY
 The appointed service provider shall supply the following software licences:
NO. SOFTWARE
1 AutoCad
1 Revit
3.MINIMUM TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION.
3.1 AutoCad
 Genuine AutoCad software.
 Commercial subscription licence.
 Named-user licensing.
 Latest available AutoCad version at the time of supply.
 Software installation and activation.
 3 years subscription period.
 Latest supported version available at the time of activation.
 Installation and activation assistance.
 Access to applicable software updates.
 Ability to produce and edit 2D technical drawings.
 3D modelling and visualisation capabilities.
 Compatibility with standard DWG drawing files.
3.2 Revit
 Genuine Revit software.
 Commercial subscription licence.
 Named-user licensing.
 Latest available Revit version at the time of supply.
 Software installation and activation.
 3 years subscription period.
 Latest supported version available at the time of activation.
 Installation and activation assistance.
 Access to applicable software updates.
 Building Information Modelling (BIM) functionality.
 Architectural design and documentation capabilities.
 Structural and MEP modelling capabilities.
 Production of plans, elevations, sections, schedules and construction documentation.
 Compatibility with standard Revit project files and applicable formats.
