THE REQUIRED SERVICES AND SCOPE OF WORK
1. INTRODUCTION:
The Nelson Mandela Bay Stadium (NMBS), operated by the Mandela Bay Development Agency (NMBS),
a municipal entity of the Nelson Mandela Bay Municipality (NMBM) and acting as its development agent,
invites suitably qualified and interested service providers to submit proposals for rendering of Event
Ticketing Administration and related services for the Nelson Mandela Bay Stadium with the
objective of appointing a suitably qualifies and experienced ticketing service provider.
1. OBJECTIVES:
The objective of this Request for Proposals (RFP) is for the Nelson Mandela Bay Stadium (NMBS) to appoint
a suitably qualified and experienced ticketing service provider to provide Event Ticketing Administration
and Related Services. The appointed service provider will be responsible for delivering a reliable, secure
and efficient ticketing solution for events hosted at the Stadium, including ticket sales, online ticketing,
ticket distribution, verification, access control, reporting, reconciliation and customer support.
The service provider should have the necessary technical capabilities, operational capacity and industry
experience to effectively manage ticketing requirements for events of varying sizes and complexity,
thereby supporting the NMBS in delivering a professional and seamless experience for event organisers,
spectators and other stakeholders.
2.1. This RFP comprises of five (5) linked components. The event ticketing administration service
provider contract period is envisaged for a period of twelve (12) months with an option to
renew for a further two (2) twelve (12) months, provided that the successful event ticketing
service provider complies with all terms and conditions as well as performance objectives
contained in the agreement.
2.2. This RFP is directed at and the envisaged for the provision of the following services, listed
in components as an all-inclusive package of services provided by the successful single
service provider:
2.2.1. Component 1: Ticketing Administration Services – NMBS and all its sub
entities / divisions
2.2.2. Component 2: Events Management and related services
2.2.3. Component 3: Digital Services and online campaign/events services offering
2.2.4. Component 4: Sport / Stadium Related Services
2.2.5. Component 5: National ticketing location and distribution sites

1. SCOPE OF WORK:
The NMBS wishes to appoint a suitably qualified and experienced service provider for the provision of Event
Ticketing Administration and Related Services. The appointed service provider should demonstrate the
necessary technical capabilities, operational capacity, industry experience and commitment to delivering
reliable, efficient and customer-focused ticketing services. The service provider will be required to provide
a comprehensive range of ticketing-related services, including ticket sales and administration, online
ticketing, ticket distribution, ticket verification, access control, reporting, reconciliation and customer
support, as well as any other related services required to support events hosted at the Nelson Mandela Bay
Stadium.
Interested Ticketing Administration Service Providers are invited to submit proposals in accordance with the
requirements and specifications set out below.
3.1. Commission on ticket sales will be negotiated and agreed upon, prior to the signing of the
service level agreement.
3.2. Ticketing System and Infrastructure requirement:
3.3.1 The ticketing system should be able to allow multiple scans of the same ticket, for
example the entry at the outer gate to confirm legitimacy of the ticket, secondly
access into a specific area such as stand access points, suite or ramp access etc.
3.3.2 The scanners must be able to read in and out without having to reconfigure
turnstiles each time. The outer gates must be anti-pass back.
3.3.3 Level 2 turnstiles need to be bi-directional, and be able to be made free flow from
a central point.
3.3.4 The counting system must be live, real time and linked with the VOC.
3.3.5 There must be a service plan with back-up and support included in the contract,
spares kept locally and be replaceable immediately with swap outs.
3.3.6 There needs to be match day support depending on size of expected crowd.
3.3.7 Software costs must be included in the tendered price.
3.3.8 The system must also include back up in case of power failure.
3.3.9 Handheld scanners must form part of the package.
3.3.10 Training of Stadium staff to form part of the package.
3.3.11 Scanners and turnstiles must be able to process a minimum amount of 1000
spectators per hour into the Stadium, as per SANS 10366.
3.3.12 Access to tickets must be country wide and not restricted to limited outlets.
3.3.13 Electronic tickets will become the norm, so sufficient security measures need to be
implemented to prevent copying of E Tickets.
3.3.14 Access to tickets must be achievable by all and not restricted by language or any
other boundary.
3.3.15 Tickets should be available across various platforms such as banking apps, Cell
Phone Service Providers, Stadium web pages and various other platforms if possible.

3.3.18 The printed complimentary ticket system would become a thing of the past. Tickets
can only be given to specific individuals that qualify as per paying patrons.
3.3.19 New scanners will be needed at all entry points into the Stadium, including

3.3.24 Must allow end users to submit tickets, engage in chat, track ticket history, and use
