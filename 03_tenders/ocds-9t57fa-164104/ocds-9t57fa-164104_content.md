BACKGROUND
GIS is essential to bring all sources of information together on an Enterprise level to enable continuous
implementation, monitoring and reporting of the operations, and the governance of the administration. GIS is
key to achieving strategic business objectives. GIS is a critical building block and enabler for Municipalities.
Enterprise GIS is already playing a major role as an enabler to the Sol Plaatje Local Municipality. The GIS
platform and related technology is helping to manage the Municipality’s core information, impacting on
development planning, emergency management services and revenue collection to name but a few. It is in
the Municipality’s interest to ensure that the “enterprise” environment is catered for with GIS Enterprise
Licenses, Technical Support, Development and GIS training, where “enterprise” will allow for scalability. This
three (3) year agreement will realize the continued benefits of economy of scale in terms of license fees and
training, unlimited access to GIS software licenses, provision for additional new software and upgrades,
known license costing forecast as well as the procurement process benefits of three-year contract
INTRODUCTION
The Sol Plaatje Local Municipality intends to appoint a qualified service provider for the provision of
Enterprise Geographic Information System (GIS) software licenses, technical support, development
services, and GIS training for a period of three (3) years (2026/27 – 2028/29).
The purpose of this procurement is to ensure the continued operation, support and enhancement of the
Municipality’s enterprise GIS environment which supports spatial data management, integrated planning,
infrastructure management, revenue systems, and decision-making processes across multiple municipal
departments. The Sol Plaatje Local Municipality supports maintaining an enterprise GIS environment, as it
maximizes the value of GIS investments and spatial data, enhances productivity through automated
workflows, integrates diverse datasets, and improves collaboration, communication, reporting, and decision-
making for integrated planning and spatial analysis.
The three-year contract will include:
• Annual enterprise GIS licenses for all user departments.
• Enterprise GIS software and architecture.
• Support and development of customized GIS core applications.
• Management of enterprise GIS databases and online platforms.
• Formal GIS training through certified courses, delivered virtually, via e-learning, or in a classroom.
The required services include the supply of software licences, technical support, system development, data
management, and user training for a period of three (3) years.
