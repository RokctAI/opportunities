1. INTRODUCTION
The purpose of this submission is to obtain approval to appoint a panel of service providers to provide as
and when supply and deliver of cleaning chemicals and horticulture materials within the KwaZulu Natal
(KZN) Region for a period of thirty-six months on an as and when basis.
2. BACKGROUND INFORMATION
The Passenger Rail Agency of South Africa (“PRASA”) has identified the need to appoint a service provider
for as and when supply and deliver of cleaning chemicals and horticulture materials
Prasa has been requesting once for as and when and deliver of cleaning chemicals and horticulture
materials. Since going on tender every month is time consuming. Not all suppliers return the quotes.
The supply and deliver of cleaning chemicals and horticulture materials are used at station and depots to
ensure that station and depot are always cleaned as per the required standards.
Therefore, it is of importance to have an As and When (Period Contract) in place for the supply and deliver
of cleaning material. Thus, ensure that Prasa complies with OHS Act 85 of1993 and the image of the
company is not at stake.

and related processes.

edge technology and service delivery systems.

recommendations and substitutions.
2.6 PRASA must reduce costs by streamlining its acquisition of Services, including managed service processes
on a Group basis.
