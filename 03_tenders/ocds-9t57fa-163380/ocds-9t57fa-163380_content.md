REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
DUBE TRADEPORT CORPORATION
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS FOR THE PROVISION OF INSURANCE BROKING SERVICES, CLAIM
MANAGEMENT AND RISK MANAGEMENT SERVICES FOR DUBE TRADEPORT CORPORATION FOR A PERIOD
OF THREE (3) YEARS
REF NO.: DTP/RFP/07/FIN/02/2026
Dube TradePort Corporation (DTPC) was created as a provincial public entity to develop Dube
TradePort. Refer to www.dubetradeport.co.za for more information on DTPC.
Dube TradePort Corporation seeks to appoint a suitably qualified professional bidder to render
services related to insurance broking, comprehensive risk management and claims management
services of the DTPC insurance portfolio for a period of three (3) years). The service provider must be
registered with the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA), to provide insurance broking services,
risk management and claims services in all aspects of the DTPC business.
EVALUATION OF THIS BID
All proposals will be evaluated in various stages – compliance and functionality will be assessed and
thereafter all responsive proposals will be evaluated in accordance with the PPPFA regulations, 2022
using the 80/20 preference points system.
The 20 preference points (Specific Goals) will be allocated to the following as included in the bid
document:
I. Enterprises who are at least 51% owned by black people (10 Points)
II. Enterprises who are based in KwaZulu Natal (10 Points)
