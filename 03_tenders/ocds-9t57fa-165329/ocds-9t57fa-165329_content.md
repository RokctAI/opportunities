1. INTRODUCTION
The Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality intends to undertake a Baseline
Study for a Proposed Mixed-Use Township Establishment on municipal commonage
land situated on the south eastern portion of Bizana. The project forms part of the
municipality’s strategic spatial planning initiatives aimed at facilitating structured urban
growth, improving land development opportunities, and promoting integrated
settlement development within the Bizana urban area.
The proposed development area is located on portions of the Remainder of Erf 110,
Bizana Municipal Commonage, and has been identified by the municipality as a
suitable area for future urban expansion and mixed-use development. The proposed
township establishment is envisaged to accommodate a combination of residential,
commercial, institutional, and supporting land uses in line with the spatial restructuring
objectives of the municipality.
Property Description
The proposed development site is situated within the southeastern extent of Bizana,
headed to Umhlanga Location and forms part of municipal commonage land under the
ownership and administration of the Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality.
The area is strategically located within proximity to existing urban development and
key movement routes, making it suitable for future township establishment and urban
expansion.
Apart from limited pockets of privately-owned land within the Bizana urban area, a
significant portion of land within the Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality
remains state-owned or held in trust by the Minister responsible for Rural Development
and Land Reform. Land ownership and tenure arrangements within the municipal area
are often characterised by complex land administration systems and overlapping land
rights.
Furthermore, various portions of land within the Bizana urban area remain affected by
land claim processes lodged in terms of the Restitution of Land Rights Act, 22 of 1994.
These land-related complexities have historically constrained coordinated urban
development and delayed the implementation of strategic municipal projects.
6

The municipality therefore seeks to undertake baseline investigations to determine the
suitability, constraints, opportunities, and development potential of the identified site
for a future mixed-use township establishment.
1. BACKGROUND & PROBLEM STATEMENT.
The Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality has identified the need to
undertake a Baseline Study for a Proposed Mixed-Use Township Establishment within
the southeastern portion of Bizana as part of its broader spatial transformation and
urban restructuring agenda. This initiative is aligned with the strategic objectives of the
WMMLM Spatial Development Framework (2025), which identifies Bizana as a primary
urban growth node and promotes integrated settlement development, diversification of
land uses, densification, and improved spatial efficiency.
The WMMLM SDF (2025) emphasises the importance of directing urban growth
towards strategically located areas capable of supporting sustainable development
patterns, improved accessibility, and enhanced economic opportunities. In this regard,
the municipality has identified portions of the municipal commonage as potential land
parcels suitable for future township establishment and mixed-use development.
The proposed project seeks to investigate the feasibility and development potential of
the identified site through the undertaking of baseline studies and preliminary planning
assessments. The baseline study is intended to establish a clear understanding of the
existing spatial, environmental, cadastral, infrastructural, and land use conditions
affecting the site and to determine the suitability of the land for future township
establishment.
Currently, the municipality does not have sufficient baseline information and technical
assessments to guide informed decision-making regarding the development potential,
spatial structuring, and implementation requirements of the proposed mixed-use
township. The absence of this information limits the municipality’s ability to effectively
plan for future urban expansion, infrastructure investment, and coordinated land
development within the southeastern portion of Bizana.
Furthermore, the area may be affected by various development constraints, including,
environmental considerations, servicing limitations, and legislative compliance
7

requirements, all of which require detailed investigation prior to the commencement of
township planning processes.
The undertaking of a baseline study will therefore enable the municipality to:
* Assess the suitability and feasibility of the site for mixed-use township establishment,
* Identify development opportunities and constraints,
* Inform future township layout planning and infrastructure requirements,
* Ensure alignment with the WMMLM SDF (2025), SPLUMA, and other applicable
planning legislation, and
* Prepare the municipality for subsequent phases of planning, including township
establishment processes, engineering assessments, and land development
implementation.
This project therefore seeks to undertake comprehensive baseline investigations and
planning assessments for the proposed mixed-use township establishment within the
southeastern portion of Bizana in order to support future development planning and
implementation readiness.
1. SCOPE OF WORK.
The scope of work entails undertaking baseline studies and preliminary planning
assessments for the proposed mixed-use township establishment within the
southeastern portion of Bizana, and the surveying, zoning and registration of municipal
sites/properties and will also be subject to the need and desirability to conduct
surveying project
The scope shall include, but not be limited to, the following:
* Collection and review of existing spatial, cadastral, and land use information;
* Assessment of applicable planning policies and legislative frameworks, including
alignment with the WMMLM SDF (2025);
* Site analysis and identification of development opportunities and constraints;
* High-level assessment of environmental, topographical, and land suitability
considerations;
* Investigation of existing land tenure and cadastral information;
8

* Assessment of surrounding land uses and urban integration opportunities;
* Preliminary identification of infrastructure and service-related considerations;
* Preparation of a baseline analysis report and development informants plan;
* Recommendations to guide future township establishment and layout planning
processes.
The outcome of the project shall be a comprehensive baseline assessment intended
to inform future phases of the proposed mixed-use township establishment project,
including detailed planning, engineering investigations, and township establishment
processes. A detailed scope of work is as follows:

and implement a consultation process plan.

need may lead to discrepancy of approach.

captured within the required timeframe and ensure that all relevant stakeholders are

meetings and produce information material for stakeholders as required, but guided by
legislation or policies.

whether or not to proceed with a project.

ground conditions beneath the site and to identify any problems that may be associated
with the stability of the site or the subsoils with respect to the proposed development.
Tacky Survey
The Service Provider will be required to carry out tacky survey to determine the existing
features, fences etc.

The service provider will be required to prepare the storm water management plan for
the proposed road within the layout plan.
Traffic Impact Assessment
The service provider will conduct the necessary transport studies as per the layout
plan.
