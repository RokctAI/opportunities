C3.1 Employer’s Scope of Services
1. Introduction
Since inception, Airports Company South Africa Limited (ACSA) has transformed into a focused, profitable
and commercial enterprise that is market-driven and customer service oriented. The sustained growth in traffic
over the years and increase in aircraft traffic movements has resulting in various pavements at OR Tambo
International Airport (ORTIA) requiring rehabilitation to improve the infrastructure reliability. The project aims
to appoint a professional service provider to providing engineering design services from ECSA stages 1-6 for
the rehabilitation of the pavements and design of new access roads. Once the design is finalised, a contractor
will be procured to construct the works.
1. Background
The pavements to be rehabilitated includes service roads, runways, taxiways and taxi lanes. Selected airside
pavements at ORTIA requires rehabilitation due to audit findings from the South African Civil Aviation
Authority. The pavements have several surface defects such as longitudinal and transverse cracks, blocking
cracking, surface depressions and shear failures. The purpose of the project is to rehabilitate and strengthen
selected pavements at ORTIA to prevent pavement failures.
Runway thresholds 03L, 21R and 21L require rehabilitation. It is suspected that the runway thresholds and
other pavements are experiencing a ground water issue. The issue will need to be investigated further and
solved.
In addition, the scope includes the design or two new access roads adjacent to runway 03R – 21L. Currently
both the 03R & 21L Instrument Landing System (ILS) service roads are unpaved, and this has a major
negative impact on the operations when stakeholders are utilising these roads. Road users are often stuck in
the muddy roads reducing their response times thereby affecting operational efficiency. The key stakeholders
using these roads are, bird and wildlife for removing birds and wildlife from runway 03R-21L, electrical
maintenance for maintaining Automated Weather Observing System (AWOS), approach lights, ATNS for
maintenance of ILS, Fire and Rescue for maintenance of emergency gates, response to fire breaks and other
emergencies.
