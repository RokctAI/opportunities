CONTRACT OR PROJECT DURATION\nThe agreed contract period will be for a period of thirty-six (36) months.\n3.3 SCOPE OF WORK AND SPECIFICATIONS\n3.3.1 Service provider to collect, transfer and record the Municipal records from current external\nstorage facility to the new storage facility.\n3.3.2 Provision of a fully functional processing area for the provision of an Off- Site Storage\nFacility and Management of Records for uMlalazi Municipality from date of award.\n3.3.3 Provision of empty boxes (preferably acid free) with Lids (Minimum box side of 430mm x\n330mm x 260mm).\n3.3.4 Collection of Municipal records for storage as and when required.\n3.3.5 Data entry as and when required.\n3.3.6 Location handling of records as and when required.\n3.3.7 Retrieval handling, delivery, collection and return of records.\n3.3.8 Retrieval handling, scan to email and return handling.\n3.3.9 Destruction handling and provision of destruction certificate as and when required.\n3.3.10 Disposal of records to be done with prior approval of Municipality.\n3.3.11 Submission of regular reporting in terms of all files due for disposal in terms of the\nNational Archive Act.\n3.3.12 Digitalisation of records.\n15\n\n3.4 EXPECTED DELIVERABLES\n3.4.1 To provide from the outset clear labelling procedure to be followed by the Municipality for\neach type of record that is needed to be archived.\n3.4.2 To provide boxes for storage of records and to collect boxes of records from municipal\nbuildings/sites and relevant premises and deliver to the storage premises within 24 hours of\nreceipt of the request.\n3.4.3 To undertake/cater for movement of records from the storage facility to municipal\nDepartments/Units or to sites by the Municipality within 24 hours of receipt of the request.\n3.4.4 To provide for the collection and storage from the outgoing service provider of existing\nrecords in excess of ±3000 boxes. The quantity of records changes on a monthly basis.\n3.4.5 To provide for the retrieval of documentation to the Municipality within 8 office hours to\nthe records department of the municipality unless an emergency retrieval is requested within\n1 to 4 hours.\n3.4.6 To provide for the indexing and cataloguing of records/data to ensure easily access and\nretrieval of documentation.\n3.4.7 To provide on monthly basis update documentation to the Records/Archives Officer on all\narchived material housed in the offsite storage facility.\n3.4.8 To provide on a quarterly basis a monitoring management report and advising the\nmunicipality on any aspects that could compromise the integrity of the archiving.\n3.5 REQUIREMENTS OF THE STORAGE FACILITY\n3.5.1 The Storage Facility is to be located in the area that is close to arterial roads for ease of\naccess.\n3.5.2 The storage facility must be in such a way that records are protected from perils like: fire,\ndirectly sunlight, dust, theft, water, pests, extreme temperature & humidity\n16\n\n3.5.3 The storage facility is to be sufficiently ventilated ensuring that the humidity is between\n45% & 50% and temperature is between 18C & 20C for paper records.
