F.1.6 Procurement procedures

Unless otherwise stated in the tender data, a contract will, subject to F.3.13, be

F.1.6.2.1 Where the tender data require that the competitive negotiation procedure is to be

associated information. Notwithstanding the provisions of F.2.17, the employer may
