Bid Notice
BID NO.: 09/26/27
EXTENSION OF A PANEL OF SERVICE PROVIDERS FOR PROVISION OF IT GOODS
AND SERVICES (DATA CENTRE INFRASTRUCTURE, WEBSITE, APPLICATION AND
BUSINESS SYSTEMS DEVELOPMENT, LAN INFRASTRUCTURE AND DISASTER
RECOVERY and LAN INFRASTRUCTURE AND DISASTER RECOVERY) FROM
APPOINTMENT DATE TO 11 FEBRUARY 2029
Bidders are hereby invited from suitable service providers for the extension of a panel of service
providers for provision of IT goods and services (Data Centre Infrastructure, Website,
Application and Business Systems Development, Lan Infrastructure and Disaster Recovery and
Lan Infrastructure and Disaster Recovery) from appointment date to 11 February 2029.
The physical address for collection of bid documents is Mandeni Municipality’s Civic Centre, 2
Kingfisher Road, Mandeni, Cashiers Office, upon payment of a non-refundable fee of R600.00
(inclusive of VAT) (Debit Card, Cash or EFT: Ref no.: Bid No.: 09/26/27 Bidder’s Name). Bid
documents can also be emailed to bidders after submission of POP. The Municipal bank details
may be requested via email from the Manager SCM. Bid documents will be available from
Wednesday 2nd September 2026 to Tuesday, 15 September 2026 during working hours,
Monday to Friday from 08h00 to 15h30.
Bids are to be completed in accordance with the conditions attached to the Bid document and
must be sealed in an envelope and marked: “BID NO.: 09/26/27 EXTENSION OF A PANEL
OF SERVICE PROVIDERS FOR PROVISION OF IT GOODS AND SERVICES (DATA
CENTRE INFRASTRUCTURE, WEBSITE, APPLICATION AND BUSINESS SYSTEMS
DEVELOPMENT, LAN INFRASTRUCTURE AND DISASTER RECOVERY and LAN
INFRASTRUCTURE AND DISASTER RECOVERY) FROM APPOINTMENT DATE TO 11
FEBRUARY 2029 and must be deposited in the tender box situated at the reception: Civic
Centre, 2 Kingfisher Road, Mandeni no later than 12H00, on Friday, 2nd October 2026
at which time bids will be opened in public. Closing time will only be determined by the
Municipal watch displayed on the Municipal wall at the reception. The name and address of the
Bidder must be clearly written on the sealed envelope containing the Bid Document. Late
submission, e-mail or fax bids will no circumstances be considered. Only bidders
registered on Central Supply Database (CSD) will be considered.
The Mandeni Municipality subscribes to the preferential procurement policy framework Act, Act
No. 5 of 2000 and its associated Regulations of 2022. Bids will be evaluated and adjudicated
in terms of the Mandeni Municipality’s SCM Policy in two stages. First stage of evaluation is
functionality and second stage being price (form of offer). Bids shall be valid for a period of
120 days. The Mandeni Municipality does not bind itself to accepting the lowest bid, either
wholly or in part or give any reason for such action. All prospective bidders will be screened in
accordance with the National Treasury’s Defaulters Data Base. Canvassing in any form of gift
to Council is strictly prohibited and will lead to the disqualification of the bid.
