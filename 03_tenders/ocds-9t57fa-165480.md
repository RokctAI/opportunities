### Tender Description
Expression Of Interest pertaining to the engineering, supply, training and
support of a previously developed range of Protection, Control and
Substation Automation Equipment (predominantly based on the Hitachi
(Pty) Ltd and IST range of products) with the associated swing frame
panels and PIU Junction Boxes to Eskom Distribution over a period of up
to four (4) years. It is Eskom’s expectation that the previous design intent
will be sustained, in terms of the overall system architecture, brand and
types of IEDs utilized, guarantees and the provision of training and product
support.
