2 FUNCTIONS OF THE CGE
2.1 The Commission for Gender Equality (CGE) is an independent statutory body established in
terms of Section 187 of the Constitution of South Africa. The CGE is mandated to promote
respect for gender equality and the protection, development, and attainment of gender equality
in the Republic. The powers and functions of the CGE are outlined in the Commission for
Gender Equality Act No. 39 of 1996. Specifically, in terms of section 11(1)(a) of the CGE Act,
the CGE has the powers to monitor and evaluate policies and practices of state organs, state
agencies, public bodies and the private sector in order to promote gender equality, and to
prepare and submit reports to Parliament.
