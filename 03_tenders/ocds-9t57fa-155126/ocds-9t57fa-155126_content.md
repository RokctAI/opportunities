1. PECT BACKGROUND.
Port Elizabeth Container Terminal (PECT) operate in loading and offloading containers, and manganese
skip operations via STS cranes, 20/40ft spreaders and straddle carriers. The terminal has two
operational ship to shop cranes and one Mobile Harbour Crane and the demand for these cranes is high
due to high berth occupancy. The ship to shore cranes is utilised on a weekly basis for container and
skiptainer operations. The Port's main activities encompass a diverse range of commodities such as
automotive exports, container handling and manganese.
2. OBJECTIVE.
This contract entails the cleaning of Ship-To-Shore cranes, Straddle Carriers and Straddle workshop at
the Port Elizabeth Container Terminal (PECT). The equipment requires sufficient industrial cleaning to
ensure that all these machines stay in good clean operating condition, which is safe to be used by the
operator as well as the maintenance team, furthermore the contract entails that the machines need to
be cleaned as per request and as per schedule from the maintenance department.
