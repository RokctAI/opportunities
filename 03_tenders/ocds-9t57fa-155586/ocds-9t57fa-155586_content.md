2. BACKGROUND
The National Heritage Council (NHC) of South Africa is a public entity that is
responsible for the preservation, promotion and protection of the country’s heritage.
The important areas that the NHC focusses on are policy development for the sector
to meet its transformation goals, public awareness and education, knowledge
production in heritage subjects that were previously neglected, as well as making
funding available to projects that place heritage as a socio-economic resource.
The NHC is a Schedule 3A public entity in terms of the Public Finance Management
Act 1 of 1999 as amended by Act 29 of 1999, that came into existence through an
amendment of the Cultural Laws Second Amendment Act, no 69 of 2001, and was
officially constituted through the National Heritage Council Act, Act 11 of 1999).
