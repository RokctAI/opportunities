a) the erection, maintenance, alteration, renovation, repair, demolition or dismantling of an
addition to a building;
b) the installation, erection or dismantling of machinery;
c) the construction, maintenance, demolition or dismantling of any bridge, dam, canal,
railway, street, runway, sewer or water reticulation system or work on any similar project;
d) the moving of earth, clearing of land or making of an excavation or work on any similar
project." (General Administrative Regulations Clause 1).
It is recorded that the subject of this contract falls within the scope of the foregoing work
definition, and that the Employer in terms of this contract is the Mandator and that the
Contractor is the Mandatory in terms of the OHS Act.

a) the erection, maintenance, alteration, renovation, repair, demolition or dismantling of an
addition to a building;
b) the installation, erection or dismantling of machinery;
c) the construction, maintenance, demolition or dismantling of any bridge, dam, canal,
railway, street, runway, sewer or water reticulation system or work on any similar project;

project." (General Administrative Regulations Clause 1).

4.2.1 The electronic transfers account will receive EFT’s payments from customers with an 8-digit DI.

Electronic Transfers Account bank statement.

multiple Council account payments. These multiple payments must reflect on the Electronic
Transfers Account bank statement each with its own 8-digit DI.

Market receipting points lodged to the bank by a CIT company.
