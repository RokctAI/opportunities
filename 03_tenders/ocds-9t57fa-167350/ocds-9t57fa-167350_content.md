Bid Notice
BID NO.: 06/26/27
AN APPOINTMENT OF SERVICE PROVIDER TO SUPPLY AND DELIVER 2 x
RING MAIN UNITS (RMU) WITH PROTECTION SETTINGS
Bidders are hereby invited from suitable service providers for an appointment of service
provider to supply and deliver 2 x ring main units (RMU) with protection settings.
The physical address for collection of bid documents is Mandeni Municipality’s Civic Centre, 2
Kingfisher Road, Mandeni, Cashiers Office, upon payment of the non-refundable fee of
R600.00 (including VAT). Payments may be made by debit card, cash or EFT using the
reference: Bid No. 06/26/27 & Bidder’s Name. Bid documents will be available from
Wednesday, 2nd September 2026 to Tuesday, 15th September 2026 during working
hours, Monday to Friday from 08h00 to 15h30. Bidders who elect to pay by EFT may request
the Municipality’s banking details from the Manager: Supply Chain Management.
Bids must be completed in accordance with the conditions contained in this bid document and
sealed in an envelope clearly marked: “BID NO.: 06/26/27– APPOINTMENT OF A SERVICE
PROVIDER TO SUPPLY AND DELIVER TWO (2) CTC THREE-WAY RING MAIN UNITS
(RMUs) WITH PROTECTION SETTINGS”. Bids must be deposited in the tender box at the
reception of the Civic Centre, 2 Kingfisher Road, Mandeni, no later than 12H00 on Wednesday,
23rd September 2026 when bids will be opened in public. The closing time will be determined
by the municipal clock displayed at reception. The bidder’s name and address must be clearly
written on the sealed envelope. Late, e-mailed or faxed bids will not be considered. Only bidders
registered on the Central Supplier Database (CSD) will be considered.

No. 5 of 2000 and its associated Regulations of 2022. Bids will be evaluated and adjudicated
in terms of the Mandeni Municipality’s SCM Policy in two stages. Bids will first be assessed for
