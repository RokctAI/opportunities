SECTION 2: BACKGROUND, PURPOSE, AND SCOPE OF WORK
The Airports Company South Africa (ACSA) SOC Limited through its aviation, maintenance seeks to appoint a
registered professional Service Provider to provide e-auctioneering services for the disposal of the Company’s
fleet vehicles, equipment, furniture and IT equipment located at the various ACSA owned and operated Airports
within South Africa.
The e-auction contract will assist with the disposal of, amongst others, scrap stock items, movable plant and
equipment.
The e- auction disposal mechanism has proven extremely effective in reducing the risk associated with
stockpiling of highly sought after scrap resulting in generation of revenue for ACSA.
Assets Disposal forms part of the business’s ecosystem and carries a bountiful benefit for every organization,
provided it is managed properly. ACSA stands to gain enormously from this process, from risk management to
compliance management and the return on sales of assets.
In October 2024, ACSA Executive Committee made a decision that all asset disposals will be conducted through
Supply Chain Management for all disposals in the organization. This standardization was created as an effort to
streamline the disposal process and to curtail all risks associated therewith.
ACSA is calling for proposals from experienced service providers with a proven track record for the appointment
to conduct e-auctioning of ACSA’s - assets located nationally, that are no longer required due either being
replaced, become obsolete or scrapped on an “as and when” required basis, over a period of five years.
The Service Provider shall perform E-auctioneering services by advertising auctions
• Must have a proven digital online platform for provision of the e-auctioning services.
• Arranging lots by cataloguing the items with photographs, providing detailed descriptions and location of
the items,
• Conducting market pricing analysis to assist ACSA with the setting of reserve prices, formulating the terms
of sale, invoicing buyers and ensuring payment collection, timeous payment to ACSA, providing buyers with
collection documents, updating of collection details, and other related activities in order to realise the optimum
value from an E-auction for ACSA.
• The assets that would normally be sold on the E-auction are distributed across the entire operational area
within which ACSA operates, and the successful bidder must have resources to travel to each of the various sites
to catalogue assets identified for sale. Responsiveness for ‘quick’ cataloguing is crucial to post such assets as
quick as possible for sale. Such travel expenses will be for the account of the successful bidder and ACSA will not
be liable for any costs associated with the entire process.
When required to do so, at its own cost, the Service Provider must hire equipment such as forklifts, grabs etc. to
assist ACSA with the placement of scrapped assets for sale. All arrangements must be done in collaboration with
and be approved by an appropriate contract manager of ACSA.
The Service Provider must display professional skills in order to substantiate that the scrapped assets and/or
other material are sold to the best advantage of ACSA. The Service Provider must be in a position to propose

separate legal person for VAT purposes and, is in terms of section 51(1) of the VAT Act ,required to
register for VAT with the South African Revenue Services (“SARS”)where the body’s taxable supplies
exceed the registration threshold.
Should the joint venture or partnership or consortium not be registered for VAT, VAT cannot be levied
on any invoice that will be issued out to Airports Company South Africa for services rendered.
