1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
The Environment and Agriculture Management Department of the City of Tshwane
(CoT) requests the services of a qualified service provider to ensure AEL Application
and compliance with the Atmospheric Emission Licence (AEL) conditions for the
Rebecca Street Crematorium.
2. BACKGROUND
In accordance with Section 22 of the National Environmental Management: Air
Quality Act (NEM: AQA), 2004 (Act No. 39 of 2004), no person may conduct an
activity that results in atmospheric emissions without a Provisional Atmospheric
Emission Licence (PAEL) or an Atmospheric Emission Licence (AEL). Regulation
893 of 22 November 2013, issued under the Air Quality Act, identifies activities that
may have a significant detrimental effect on the environment, including Crematoria,
which are classified under Subcategory 8.2.
The City of Tshwane’s Rebecca Street Crematorium was issued an Atmospheric
Emission Licence (AEL) on 14 November 2024 by the Gauteng Department of
Agriculture and Rural Development (GDARD). In compliance with AEL Conditions
6.4, 9.4, and 9.8, the City is required to:
• Conduct stack emission testing for both stacks on a bi-annual basis.

• Include emission results in the annual report submitted to GDARD.
The Environment and Agriculture Management Department of the City of Tshwane
hereby invites qualified service providers to submit proposals for assisting in
compliance with the AEL conditions for the Rebecca Street Crematorium. The
appointed service provider will be responsible for conducting stack emission tests
and compiling atmospheric emissions reports in accordance with the regulatory
requirements.
Interested service providers must meet all relevant compliance and technical criteria
as stipulated in the bid documentation.
3. PROJECT SCOPE
The appointed service provider must ensure compliance with the National
Environmental Management: Air Quality Act (NEM: AQA), 2004 (Act No. 39 of
2004) and all relevant legislation. The scope of work includes, but is not limited
to, the following tasks for the Rebecca Street Crematorium:
A. Emission Measurements, Analysis, and Reporting
The service provider must conduct emission measurements, data analysis, and
compilation and submission of emission monitoring reports in compliance with
Subcategory 8.2 of the Air Quality Act. The required reports include:
• Bi-annual emission monitoring reports
• Annual compliance reports
Each compliance report must include, but is not limited to, the following:
Emission monitoring and bi-annual reports
• Sampling must be conducted within operational hours (06:00 -19:00) as
per the AEL.
• Sampling and Analysis – Conduct emission sampling and analysis in
accordance with ISO standards, as stipulated in Annexure A (Methods
for Sampling and Analysis) of the Air Quality Act.
• Mass Concentration of Pollutants – Determine and analyse pollutant
mass concentrations from the crematoria using isokinetic stack
monitoring instruments.
• Accredited laboratories must be used for the analysis, and accreditation
certificate must be included in the report.
• Declaration by the crematoria operator to the effect that normal operating
conditions were maintained during the emission tests (Declaration must
be included in the report).
Annual compliance reports

• The annual report must include, amongst others, the following items:
Pollutant emissions trend; Compliance audit report(s), Major upgrades
projects; and Greenhouse gas emissions.
• Management and Mitigation Measures – Outline measures implemented
to reduce atmospheric emissions and ensure regulatory compliance.
• The annual Report must be submitted to GDEnv by no later than 31
December 2026.
The service provider must ensure full compliance with all legislative requirements
and reporting standards as outlined in the Air Quality Act (NEM: AQA), 2004.
The appointed service provider will be responsible for ensuring compliance with the
Atmospheric Emission Licence (AEL) conditions for the Rebecca Street Crematorium
and application for Renewal of Atmospheric Emission Licence. The key project
