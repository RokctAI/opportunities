TENDER NUMBER 04/2627
ERRATUM
TENDER 04/2627: SUPPLY AND DELIVERY OF 80MM GREY INTERLOCK BLOCK PAVERS
Notice is hereby given that the highlighted paragraph in the tender advert was included in error.
The paragraph currently reads:
“Ndlambe Municipality hereby invites experienced service providers to submit bids for the supply
and delivery of various water and wastewater treatment chemicals to Ndlambe Local Municipality
over a period of 36 months. The selected service providers will be required to supply chemicals
as per the Municipality’s requirements on an ‘as and when required’ basis.”
The paragraph must be replaced with the following:
“Ndlambe Municipality hereby invites suitably experienced service providers to submit
bids for the supply and delivery of 80mm grey interlock block pavers in accordance with
the specifications and requirements contained in the tender document.”
All other tender conditions and requirements remain unchanged.
Ndlambe Municipality apologises for any inconvenience caused.
