SECTION 4: SPECIFICATIONS
1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 The National Nuclear Regulator (NNR) is a public entity which is established and governed
in terms of Section 3 of the National Nuclear Regulator Act, (Act No 47 of 1999) to provide
for the protection of persons, property and the environment against nuclear damage
through the establishment of safety standards and regulatory practices.
2. PURPOSE AND BACKGROUND
2.1 The purpose of this document is to outline the requirements for the appointment of a
suitable service provider for the design, supply, delivery and installation of a complete
lifeline system (fall protection system) for NNR head office. (420 Witch-Hazel Avenue,
Ego-Park, Highveld, Centurion).
3. SCOPE OF WORK
3.1. The successful service provider will be expected to design, supply, deliver and install
complete lifeline system (fall protection system) in accordance with applicable South
African health and safety standards and SANS 10313:2019.
3.2. The service providers are expected to conduct onsite verification of the dimensions and
design the lifeline system.
3.3. The successful service provider will be expected to provide quality method statement,
quality control sheet and inspection and testing plan.
3.4. The successful service provider must make allowance for all health and safety
requirements expenses.
3.5. The service provider must ensure lifeline system supports the safe attachment of
harnesses and complies with fall arrest regulations.
3.6. The service provider must test and certify the system after installation, providing a
certificate of compliance.
4. DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS
4.1. Service providers must visit the site to satisfy herself/himself as to the nature and extent
of the work to be done and the value of the materials required before finalizing and
submitting the quotation.
4.2. No claim for any variations of the contract sum in respect of the nature and extent of the
work or of inferior or damaged materials will be entertained.
