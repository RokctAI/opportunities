EMPLOYEE WELLNESS PROGRAMME
INTRODUCTION
Sarah Baartman District Municipality (SBDM) is committed to promoting and supporting the overall health,
safety, and well-being of its employees. In order to recognize the importance of employee wellness in the
workplace, SBDM undertook to establish a framework for promoting wellness for the benefit of employees by
identifying the mechanisms and resources that will be used to promote and encourage wellness among
employees. Sarah Baartman District Municipality views an Employee Wellness Programme (EWP) as a
strategic intervention designed to produce individual and organizational benefits by identifying and proactively
managing employee’s personal, health, and work-related concerns and challenges. Sarah Baartman District
Municipality staff complement is 120. However, the numbers may vary due to natural attrition and/or new
additions.
Sarah Baartman District Municipality’s Council approved an Employee Wellness Policy. The Policy outlines
how the municipality will assist its employees dealing with wellness challenges, including challenges relating
to:
• Health Screening
• Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)/Wellness Programme
• Disease and Disability Management
• Employee Training
• Primary Health Care
• Occupational Health
1. SCOPE OF WORK
The purpose of the SBDM Employee Wellness clinic is to holistically provide CORE employee wellness
services to the SBDM employees for a period of three (3) years. The service provider will be required to render
the following services:
Health Screening
The provision of on-site health screening facility once per annum through occupational healthcare practitioner
providing physical medical examinations for all employees. The primary purpose of the annual screening tests
is to detect early any disease or risk factors and direct the employee towards actively manage and mitigating
the identified factor. The medical screening will include the following:
• Blood pressure
• Cholesterol checks
• Diabetes test
• Mammograms and pap smears
• Prostate cancer screening

The Disability Management Program (DMP) will be used to assist employees, who are unable to work due to
