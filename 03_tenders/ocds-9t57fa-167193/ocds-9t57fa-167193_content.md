INTRODUCTION
Umuziwabantu Local Municipality hereby invites suitably qualified, experienced and
professional Town and Regional Planning Consultants for a period of 36 Months to submit
bids for appointment to a panel of professional service providers for a period to be
determined in the bid document.
The Municipality is committed to advancing integrated, sustainable and inclusive spatial
development that promotes economic growth, infrastructure investment, and improved
service delivery within its jurisdiction. In pursuit of this mandate, the Municipality seeks to
strengthen its technical capacity through the establishment of a panel of competent
planning professionals to support the implementation of its strategic development
objectives.
The appointed consultants will be required, as and when needed, to provide comprehensive
professional services including but not limited to:
o Preparation, review and alignment of municipal plans, including Spatial Development
Frameworks (SDFs) and Land Use Schemes;
o Development of feasibility studies and pre-feasibility assessments;
o Compilation of sector plans and precinct or master plans;
o Township establishment, subdivisions, consolidations and related land development
applications;
o Project conceptualisation, design development and costing;
o Property development advisory services; Infrastructure and development planning;
o Investment packaging, catalytic project preparation and bankable project
documentation;
o Enforcement support relating to illegal land uses and non-compliant developments;
o Land audits, property evaluation and development viability assessments;
o Conveyancing support and land transaction advisory services.
The purpose of this bid is to ensure that the Municipality has access to a multidisciplinary
planning panel capable of delivering high-quality, innovative and legally compliant planning
solutions that support spatial transformation, local economic development, and sustainable
human settlements.
Bidders must demonstrate relevant experience in municipal planning environments,
knowledge of applicable legislation, and the capacity to deliver projects within agreed
timeframes and budgets. All consultants must be professionally registered with the relevant
statutory bodies and comply with applicable procurement and regulatory requirements.
