2.1.5 Procurement procedures
2.1.5.1 General
The CCT intends to appoint a winner (the highest ranked tenderer (“the winner”)) and in addition one
“alternate tenderer” per region for the allocation of work. If insufficient responsive bids are received,
the CCT reserves the right to appoint fewer tenderers, or not to appoint any tenderers at all.
Each winner and each alternate tenderer may only be awarded a maximum of one region; however,
the CCT reserves the right to offer a tenderer more than one region, depending on the tender
evaluation and the tenderer’s capacity (staff and vehicles). This ensures that the allocation is done in
a fair and efficient manner, balancing the tenderer's capabilities and the requirements of each region.
Suppliers, once appointed and subject to operational requirements, will be invited to deliver the goods
or services on a “winner-takes-all” basis, whereby the order will always be offered and, if accepted,
allocated to the highest ranked tenderer (“the winner”), and only if he/she refuses, will the work be
offered to the next highest ranked tenderer from the alternate tenderers.
Tenderers are required to complete Schedule F14 on Page 104, indicating their order of
preference should they tender for more than one region.

1. OVERVIEW
As part of Cape Town’s Waste Strategy, the City, through the Urban Waste Management (UWM) Directorate, aims
to progressively increase access to recycling services to additional households in Cape Town, including low income
households. Research has confirmed that residents living in low-income areas often prefer to receive an incentive
such as money or goods in exchange for their recyclables.
The mobile Swop Shop model, where residents can exchange their packaging waste for grocery items or secondhand
items, has been shown to be a successful and cost effective model to deliver a waste minimization service in low
income areas. As such, the City has successfully trialed a custom Swop Shop trailer, both in 2020, where 98.7 tons
was diverted in 5 months, and in 2022/23, enabling the diversion of 82.7 tons in 12 months. The current Swop Shop
contracted service has diverted 252.9 tons in its first 9 months of operation.
The purpose of this tender is to appoint service provider/s (SP/s) to operate the City’s four (4) custom-built swop
shop trailers, making the weekly swop shop service available to a minimum of 10 low income communities per trailer.

in a minimum of 20 low income communities per region (using 2 trailers per region) awarded to them, ensuring a
