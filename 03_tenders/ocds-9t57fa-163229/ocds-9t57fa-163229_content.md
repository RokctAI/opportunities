1. SCOPE OF WORK
INTRODUCTION
Johannes Phumani Phungula Local Municipality, under Harry Gwala District Municipality, invites
qualified and experienced contractors to submit quotations/bids for the renovation and maintenance
of the Municipal Workshop Building. The project involves building repairs, painting, tiling,
concrete works, plumbing, carpentry, ceiling installation, and external works.
1. PURPOSE OF THE TERMS OF REFERENCE
• Define the scope of work required by the contractor
• Outline responsibilities and obligations of the contractor
• Specify technical and quality expectations
• Describe project management and delivery requirements
SCOPE OF WORK
• Site establishment including temporary facilities and storage
• Compliance with OHS and environmental regulations
• Provision of labour, materials, tools, and supervision
• Plastering of walls
• Wall and steel painting
• Concrete floor topping
• Floor tiling
• Installation of new doors
• Replacement of roof timber purlins
• Epoxy floor coating
• Installation of ceiling boards
• Installation of built-in cupboards
• Demolition / Removal of existing fittings
• Installation of new urinals and toilets
• Removal and replacement of windows
• Installation of burglar bars
• Sealing and waterproofing
NB: All materials must strictly comply with SANS standards, Compliance with municipal building
regulations & Use of approved quality materials
