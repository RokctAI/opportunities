3.2 Background Information
The Business Change and Technology (BCT) Strategy positions digital transformation and
capability enablement as critical enablers of Sasria’s strategic objectives. In particular, the
increasing adoption of data-driven decision-making, automation, and emerging Artificial
Intelligence (AI) technologies requires the organisation to strengthen its internal capability to
responsibly govern, oversee, and leverage AI-enabled solutions.
To support this strategic objective, Sasria is embarking on an organisation-wide AI adoption
journey, with a focus on building AI literacy, enabling responsible and compliant use of AI, and
developing internal capability to scale AI adoption sustainably. As a state-owned insurer
operating in a regulated environment, Sasria requires AI training that emphasises governance,
accountability, ethical use, and regulatory compliance (including POPIA, PFMA and King IV
principles), while supporting mindset shift and productivity improvement.
The training programme is expected to improve organisational readiness for AI initiatives,
enhance productivity, reduce onboarding and skills-acquisition timelines, and foster a
consistent, enterprise-wide understanding of AI risks, opportunities, and governance
requirements. Sasria SOC currently employs approximately 170 digitally literate staff
members operating from a single location. The organisation functions within a Microsoft
technology ecosystem and currently utilises OneDrive, SharePoint, and LinkedIn Learning to
host digital learning content
