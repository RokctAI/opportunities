1 Introduction
1.1 The Electoral Commission (IEC) seeks to procure twenty-eight thousand (28,000)
hand-held laser barcode scanners for deployment with its new tablet computers.
1.2 The scanners must be capable of connecting to the tablets via a USB Type-C
interface.
1.3 The scanners will be used primarily for reading barcodes on South African ID Books
and ID Cards
1.4 The Electoral Commission has invested extensively in ICT technologies, which
provide a platform to effectively support and enable its business processes and to
meet its goal of providing a free and fair election process in an open and transparent
environment. The Electoral Commission’s ICT Department intends to continue running
a highly efficient and stable ICT environment making full use of industry standards,
best practices and disciplines based upon stable and reliable technologies.

2 Technical Specifications
The technical specification for the required equipment is detailed below and it must be noted
that these are the minimum requirements. The only exception that may be accepted will be in
case where the bidder’s specification is higher, anything below the minimum specification will
be disqualified.
2.1 The scanners will be used primarily for reading barcodes on South African ID Books
and ID Cards. They must therefore:
2.1.1 Support 1D barcode formats commonly used on official identity documents.
2.1.2 Provide high-accuracy scanning even in low-light conditions or where
barcodes are partially damaged, smudged, or laminated.
2.1.3 Include anti-glare capability to ensure reliable performance when scanning
barcodes printed under plastic film or card lamination.
2.1.4 Deliver fast read speeds suitable for high-volume environments, minimizing
queue delays.
2.1.5 Be ergonomically designed for continuous use by operators, with durable
construction to withstand extended deployment.
2.1.6 Ensure secure data transmission to the tablet via USB Type-C, with
compliance to IEC’s information security standards.
