SPECIFICATION
Ablution Facilities at Durnacoal Cemeteries:
The Dannhauser Local Municipality requires ablution facilities for the new cemeteries in
Durnacoal, catering to males, females, and people with disabilities. The facilities must include
both flushing and pit toilets.
Scope for work:
The scope for work includes the approved design, construction, and installation of ablution
facilities, including:
Toilets:
• Male and female toilets with flushing and pit options
• Disability-accessible toilets with flushing and pit latrine options
• 3 female toilets
• 3 male toilets
• 1 female toilet with disabilities
• 1 female toilet with disabilities
Water Tanks:
• 2 x 5000Lt water tanks
• 1 water tank dedicated for disabilities ablution facilities (with a separate connection
Handwashing Basins:
• With soap dispensers and paper towel dispensers
Water Supply:
• Connection to a reliable water source for flushing toilets
• Water storage tanks for flushing toilets
Sanitation:
• Connection to a sewerage system or septic tank for flushing toilets
• Pit latrine emptying and maintenance services
Accessibility:
• Ramps and pathways for easy access
• Adequate space for wheelchair manoeuvrability
