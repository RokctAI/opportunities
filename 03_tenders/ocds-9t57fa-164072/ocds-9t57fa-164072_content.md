SPECIFICATIONS
Purpose
The purpose of this project planning document is for shopfitting, supply and delivery of street vendors
containers project and to outline how the project is going to be outlined, planned and executed. This will
create a clear pathway for how communication is going to ensue among different project stakeholders. This
will also outline the different deliverables and linked to different timeframes that is within scope and budget
but also to avoid scope creep.
Background
The project seeks to provide economic infrastructure through storage facilities to street-registered street
vendors within the Sol Plaatje municipality. This can be a valuable resource for street vendors, supporting
their business operations and livelihoods. Street vendors, especially in demarcated municipal stalls, struggle
with the storage of their produce or items that they sell, and must daily move stock between their houses and
stalls. The stalls will remain the property of the municipality, and each registered street vendor will be
expected to pay a nominal fee to the municipality.
