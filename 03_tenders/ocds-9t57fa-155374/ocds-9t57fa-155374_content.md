1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this request is to appoint a service provider for the upgrade of ablution facilities in the Tsitsikamma Section of
Garden Route National Park for a period of 24 months.
BACKGROUND
South African National Parks (SANParks) is a public entity functioning under National Environmental Management: Protected
Areas Act 57 of 2003 (Act 57 of 2003); with the mandate to conserve; protect; control; and manage national parks and other
defined protected areas and their biological diversity (Biodiversity). As a public entity, SANParks is also governed by the Public
Finance Management Act, Act 1 of 1999 (as amended by Act 29 of 1999), and it is listed as Schedule 3 Part A: public entity.
The primary mandate of SANParks is to expand, protect, conserve, and control the system of national parks, terrestrial and
marine, that it is assigned to manage. SANParks currently manages over 4 million hectares of terrestrial and 369 657 ha marine
protected biodiversity through a system of 21 National Parks and 10 Marine Protected Areas: (MPAs), representing approximately
67% of state owned terrestrial protected areas and 22% of State Managed MPAs. This places SANParks in an ideal position to
not only meaningfully contribute to the protection and sustainable use of the megadiverse biodiversity of South Africa, but also
to be a key player in building climate resilience through ecosystem-based mitigation and adaptation.
2. SCOPE OF WORK
1. Health and Safety Requirements
• A Health and Safety File shall be submitted only by the successful bidder prior to commencement of works.
• A valid Letter of Good Standing (COID) shall be submitted together with the Health and Safety File before site handover
and commencement of construction activities.
2. Objective
The objective of this project is to upgrade the Otter Camp Main Ablution Facility to a high-end, durable, and modern standard,
suitable for heavy public use and a coastal environmental setting, while improving aesthetics, functionality, hygiene, and
user comfort.
3. Description of Works
The Contractor shall provide all labour, materials, tools, equipment, supervision, transport, and incidentals required to
complete the upgrade works, including but not limited to:
• Demolition and removal of existing finishes where required
• Supply and installation of new finishes and fittings
• Plumbing and sanitaryware installation
• Electrical and lighting installation
• Finishing, testing, commissioning, and handover
All work shall be executed in a neat, professional manner and remain fully operational where required by SANParks.
4. Finishes and Fixtures Specifications (High-End Finish)
4.1 Floor and Wall Tiling

• Tile type: Ceramic tiles
• Tile size: 600 mm x 600 mm
• Finish: Non-slip suitable for wet areas
• Colour: White or off-white / light neutral tone (subject to SANParks approval)
• Joint width: Small joints (minimum practical joint width)
• Grout: Water-resistant, mould-resistant grout suitable for ablution facilities, dove grey
• Tile layout to be uniform and symmetrical with neat alignment
4.2 Sanitaryware – Toilets
• Toilet type: Front flush toilets
• Toilet seats: Hard cover, heavy-duty seats suitable for public facilities
• All sanitaryware to be of commercial-grade quality
• Installation to allow ease of maintenance and cleaning
4.3 Basins and Vanity Units
• Basin type: Square-shaped, modern design basins
• Vanity tops: Granite countertops, polished finish
• Basin installation to be neat, level, and securely fixed
• All exposed edges of granite to be finished and polished
4.4 Taps, Mixers and Shower Fittings
All taps, mixers, and shower fittings shall:
• Be black matt powder-coated finish
• Be suitable for coastal and corrosive environments
• Be of high-quality, commercial-grade manufacture
Specific requirements:
• Basin mixers: Black matt, modern design
• Shower mixers: Black matt, wall-mounted
• Shower heads: Black matt, long-arm type
4.5 Showers and Shower Doors
• Shower enclosures to be fitted with glass shower doors
• Glass type: Fully tinted grey glass to provide privacy
• Glass thickness: Suitable for public use (minimum safety glass standard)
• Frames and fittings: Black finish, heavy-duty and robust
• Doors and frames must be suitable for high-frequency public use
4.6 Lighting
• Light fittings: Suspended downlights mounted on a bar or frame system
• Finish: Contemporary, suitable for ablution environments
• Coverage: Lighting layout to sufficiently illuminate an area of approximately 40 m²
• All lighting to be moisture-resistant and suitable for wet areas
• Installation to comply with relevant electrical regulations
5. Materials and Standards
All materials and workmanship shall meet or exceed the following:
• Applicable SANS standards
• Manufacturer’s specifications and installation guidelines

• Products suitable for coastal environmental conditions
• High-durability finishes suitable for public ablution facilities
6. Quality Control and Workmanship
• All workmanship shall be of a high-end finish
• No cracked tiles, uneven joints, misaligned fittings, or visible defects will be accepted
• Contractor to rectify all defects at own cost
• Work areas to be kept clean and safe throughout the project
7. Completion and Handover
• All installations to be fully tested and operational
• Site to be cleaned and handed over in a usable condition
• As-built information and warranties to be provided where applicable
