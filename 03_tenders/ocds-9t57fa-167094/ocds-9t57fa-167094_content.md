1. TERMS OF REFERENCE
TENDER FOR SUPPLY, DELIVERY & INSTALLATION OF SPECIALIZED
MERANTI HARD-WOOD FLOORING AT TOWN HALL.
1.1 PROJECT BACKGROUND
The greater Kokstad Municipality seeks to appoint a suitable and experienced
service provider to undertake the removal of the existing timber flooring at the
Kokstad Town Hall and replace it with new Meranti Hardwood Flooring. All work
must be executed in a manner that ensures sustainability and full compliance with
applicable health, safety, and technical standards, including but not limited to
SANS 10400 and NHBRC Guidelines. The service provider is required to
undertake these services in accordance with the scope of work outlined below.
1.2 SCOPE OF WORKS
The contractor must supply, deliver, and install a 22mm x 102mm Meranti
Hardwood Tongue and Groove (T&G) Timber flooring. Certificate of compliance
for materials and coatings, together with a Quality Control Program, shall be
submitted to the Municipality for review before commencement of any work.
Inspection of materials for the confirmation of compliance to be signed off by
project manager at all deliveries for materials. Inspection to be requested at least
24 hours before delivery & no installation of materials is to commence before the
confirmation of compliance has been made.
The following shall be executed:
• Carefully remove/strip all existing timber flooring and dispose off at the
Kraansfontein landfill Site or as directed by the Project manager. All
recoverable materials are to be moved to the GKM Works yard.
• Remove and replace all skirting (66m) & quadrant with new meranti skirting,
including the four (2) staircases.
• Investigate, remove, and rebuild all defective supporting brick/concrete stub
columns.

Part
• Investigate, remove, and replace damaged timber beams.
• Carefully replace the existing meranti wooden floor with new meranti
hardwood tongue and groove (T&G) wooden flooring.
• Varnish/Wood-seal all areas with existing similar colour/finish
• Clear the site of all debris.
The Contractor shall provide all labour, materials, supervision, equipment, tools,
transport, and incidentals necessary for the execution and completion of the works.
The Service provider is to ensure all work undertaken adheres strictly to the OHS
Act, SANS standards, and building regulations. Maintain a comprehensive safety
file throughout the engagement, which must be submitted to GKM before the
commencement of any site-based work. The contractor must adhere to all statutory
safety regulations and best practices throughout the project.
NB: Any deviation from the specification or minimum standards must be approved
by the Project Manager prior to any work being done.
1.3 COMPLIANCE STANDARDS
The service provider to supply all materials and workmanship complying with the
latest applicable editions of:
• SANS 10082 – Timber Buildings
• SANS 10163 – Structural Use of Timber
• SANS 10400 – National Building Regulations
• Occupational Health and Safety Act, 85 of 1993
• Construction Regulations
• Relevant municipal by-laws
• Manufacturer specifications for timber treatment and sealants
Furthermore, the contractor must measure & confirm the working area, as Greater
Kokstad Municipality will not be held liable for incorrect/inconsistent
measurements.

1.4 GENERAL NOTES
The service provider should note the following:
• All building operations are to be confined within the property boundaries.
• Contractor to ensure that the building site is properly secured with controlled access
to and from the building site.
• Contractor to liaise with the municipality as to where to place building material and
rubble so as not to interfere with the movement of traffic.
• Contractor will be responsible for the provision of required safety and access signage
to all accesses to the building site.
• Contractor will be held liable for any damages to municipal property, directly or
indirectly, as a result of the construction process, delivery of materials, or as a result
of damages caused by any labour or subcontractors employed by the contractor. Any
damaged finishes shall be repaired or replaced at the Contractor’s cost.
• Once the required work has been performed, the site must be clean before handing
it back to Greater Kokstad Municipality. The completed flooring installation shall be
ready for immediate use upon handover.
• Contractor to note that all work is subject to a three (3) month retention period, that
10% of the project amount will be held back for that period, and it will only be paid
out when the defects that may occur within the 3 months have been repaired,
inspected, and signed off. The Contractor shall remedy any defects arising from: Poor
workmanship, Loose flooring, Structural movement, Defective finishes, Material
failures. During the defects liability period, at no additional cost to the Employer.

eyewear, hard hats, protective gloves, and reflective vests.
