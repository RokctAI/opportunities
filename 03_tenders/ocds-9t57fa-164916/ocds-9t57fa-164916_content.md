1. INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND
The City of Ekurhuleni operates more than 36 depots across its area of jurisdiction, with an estimated annual fuel
consumption of approximately 13,043,976 litres. Petrol and automotive diesel are essential commodities required
to sustain uninterrupted service delivery across critical functions, including Health, Disaster Management, Waste
Business Unit, Roads and Stormwater, and Ekurhuleni Metro Police Department.
This specification establishes a new Panel of appointed Service Providers to supply, deliver, and off-load petrol
and automotive diesel on an as-and-when-required basis from the date of award until 30 June 2029.
The City intends to appoint multiple Service Providers to a single Panel, rather than awarding separate, parallel
contracts per product line. This approach is set out in Form K, and is intended to improve security of supply,
prevent reliance on a single supplier for an essential commodity, and provide a documented, auditable basis for
allocating work among appointed Service Providers.
1. OBJECTIVES OF THE CONTRACT
The objectives of this contract are to:
• Ensure continuous, reliable supply of petrol and automotive diesel to all City depots for the duration of the
contract;
• Secure supply through a Panel of multiple qualified Service Providers, reducing the City's exposure to the
failure of any single supplier;
• Obtain fuel at a fair, market-related, and verifiable price through a transparent rebate mechanism
benchmarked against the DMRE-regulated wholesale price;
• Ensure that fuel supplied meets the applicable SANS specifications and is free from contamination or
adulteration;
67

• Promote the participation of EMEs, QSEs, and Historically Disadvantaged Businesses without
compromising the City's security of supply; and
• Establish a governance framework, including key performance indicators, reporting, and audit rights,
sufficient to detect and respond to underperformance, fraud, or non-compliance promptly.
