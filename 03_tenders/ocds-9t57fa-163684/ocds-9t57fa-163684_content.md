CLARIFICATIONS QUESTIONS
QUESTION 1
Can TCTA provide an indication of the current state and size of the enterprise landscape to be
captured in the tool, including the approximate number of business capabilities, processes,
applications, integrations, technology assets, and data entities that must be loaded as part of
the implementation?
RESPONSE
This will be finalised accordingly and shared with the appointed service provider.
QUESTION 2
Does TCTA have a preferred deployment model (SaaS, private cloud, on-premises, or hybrid),
and are there any data residency, security, or infrastructure hosting requirements that bidders
must comply with?
RESPONSE
SaaS, preferably SA Data Centre and Europe
QUESTION 3
Please provide details of the systems with which the Enterprise Architecture tool is expected to
integrate, including versions and technologies for Microsoft Active Directory, CMDB platforms,
SSO platforms, and any other systems that form part of the target integration scope.
RESPONSE
As stated in the RFB, these are the Microsoft Active Directory, CMDB platforms, and SSO
platforms
QUESTION 4
The RFB specifies five (5) core user licences and future growth of 10% new users. Can TCTA
clarify:
a. The expected number of read-only/viewer users?
b. Whether non-technical business stakeholders require access?
c. Whether bidders should price additional users beyond the initial five licences?

RESPONSE
Only core licenses, no read-only/viewer users. No, non-technical business stakeholders. No
pricing for additional users as TCTA cannot articulate the numbers.
QUESTION 5
For Stage 3 Live Demonstrations, will TCTA provide predefined demonstration scenarios and
use cases, or may bidders demonstrate the required functionality using their own reference client
environment? In addition, how will customisable functionality be assessed during the
demonstration?
RESPONSE
The demonstration will be conducted based on the key functional features outlined on pages 10
to 11 of the RFB. The bidder will be required to conduct the demonstration using their client’s
environment which the bidder will select from its client references.
QUESTION 6
Can TCTA provide the expected project commencement date and the targeted timeline for
implementation, configuration, training, migration of architecture artefacts, and production go-
live?
RESPONSE
TCTA anticipates that the commencement date will be approximately 8 to 10 weeks after the bid
closing date. However, TCTA is unable to reliably determine the duration required for
implementation, configuration, training, migration of architecture artefacts, and production go-
live at this stage. Accordingly, the timelines for these activities will be finalised in consultation
with the successful bidder
