SECURITY OPERATIONAL HOURS
• Two (2) 12-hour shifts equaling 24 hours a day
• All shifts are 12 hours and start at 5:50 for 06:00 and 17:50 for 18:00,
• The one shift will take over from the other at any specific station to ensure continued surveillance/control.
ACCESS CONTROL
The guards at the vehicle control gate must:
• Obtain clearance for and register every vehicle in the vehicle register.
• Obtain clearance for all pedestrian visitors or direct them to the relevant entrance gate.
• Obtain clearance for any SBDM property by leaving the premises at any given time.
• Always be visible and in close proximity to the main entrance points.
• Be customer-focused, patient, and polite and always remain professional in the execution of their
duties.
• The access gates are closed and opened as per site instructions or other instructions as issued by
the appropriate SBDM official on a weekly basis.
• Gates are locked/unlocked at designated times, or as required according to specific instructions
from appropriate SBDM staff.
• Ensure that contractors on site are escorted and remain on site with them.
ACCESS CONTROL (GROUND FLOOR)
• Obtain clearance for and register every visitor in the entry register.
• Obtain clearance for all pedestrian visitors and accompany them to the relevant official being visited.
Alternatively, the pedestrian visitor must be fetched from by the relevant official from the security
office.
• Always be visible and in close proximity to the main entrance points.
• Be customer-focused, patient, and polite and always remain professional in the execution of their
duties.
• The entry gates are closed and opened as per site instructions or other instructions as issued by the
appropriate SBDM official on a daily or as and when required.
ROAMING PATROLS
The guards are required to:
• Patrol all floors and parameters on an half-hourly basis 24-hours a day
• Service Provider to provide proof that the above is implemented.

• Provision, upkeep and maintenance of the security office space with the exception of the Winston
Ntshona Street, i.e., staff parking area, and toilet facility.
• Provision, upkeep and maintenance of remotes for gates and the access gates.
• Provision, upkeep and maintenance of necessary keys & padlocks.
