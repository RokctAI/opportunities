INTRODUCTION
Geographic Information Systems (GIS) enable the collection, management, analysis, and dissemination of spatially
referenced data and form a core component of modern decision-support systems. GIS plays a vital role in enhancing
planning, improving operational efficiency, and supporting evidence-based service delivery within municipalities, while
also enabling compliance with key legislative frameworks such as the Municipal Systems Act (No. 32 of 2000),
Municipal Structures Act (No. 117 of 1998), Spatial Data Infrastructure Act (No. 54 of 2003), and the Local
Government: Municipal Property Rates Act (No. 6 of 2004).
During the 2014/2015 financial year, the municipality developed a GIS web portal as a public-facing viewer that allows
users to access spatial information, visualize key features, and generate printable maps. The portal is accessible
through a link on the municipal website, however it is hosted externally due to the absence of internal GIS server
infrastructure and limited hosting capacity within the municipality.
In the 2022/2023 financial year, a service provider was appointed to upgrade, maintain, and host the GIS portal
externally under a three-year agreement with annual renewals, which has since lapsed.
Against this background, the Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality is undertaking a process to upgrade and
enhance the GIS web portal and to establish a sustainable hosting solution that aligns with current technological
standards and supports improved spatial data management and service delivery.
PURPOSE OF THE PROJECT
The purpose of the GIS web portal is to provide a centralized, user-friendly, and accessible platform for the viewing,
analysis, and dissemination of spatial information to support planning, decision-making, and service delivery within
the Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality. The portal is intended to serve both internal municipal users and
the general public by enabling easy access to up-to-date geospatial data, interactive mapping tools, and map
production capabilities.
The portal further aims to enhance transparency, improve operational efficiency, and promote data-driven governance
by integrating multiple spatial datasets into a single platform. It will support compliance with relevant legislative
frameworks, including the Municipal Systems Act (No. 32 of 2000), Municipal Structures Act (No. 117 of 1998),
Spatial Data Infrastructure Act (No. 54 of 2003), and the Local Government: Municipal Property Rates Act (No. 6 of
1. by ensuring the effective management, sharing, and utilization of spatial information.
In addition, the upgraded portal seeks to provide a reliable and sustainable solution for hosting and maintaining
geospatial data and applications, ensuring improved system performance, data security, and long-term accessibility
aligned with current technological standards.
OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT
The objective of these terms of reference for a GIS web portal project is to appoint a qualified and experienced
service provider to design, upgrade, implement, and maintain a secure, scalable, and user-friendly web-based GIS
platform for the Winnie Madikizela-Mandela Local Municipality. The service provider will be responsible for upgrading
the existing portal, updating and maintaining the spatial information available on the system, incorporating new
datasets where necessary, and hosting the portal and its data on a reliable external platform for a period of three (3)
years, with payments made on an annual basis.
ROLE OF A SERVICE PROVIDER
The successful service provider must assist the WMMLM to upgrade, update, maintain and host GIS web portal.
