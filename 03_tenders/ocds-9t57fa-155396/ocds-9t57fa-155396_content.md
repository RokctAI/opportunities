1. PURPOSE
1.1. The purpose of these terms of reference (TOR) is to appoint a service provider to perform
the Annual Financial Statement review, Annual Performance Report review and proactive
review of the 2027/28 Annual Performance Plan for the Directorate Internal Audit of the
Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET).
2. INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND
2.1. Paragraph 3.2.6 of the Treasury Regulations state that internal audit must be conducted
in accordance with the standards set by Institute of Internal Auditors
2.2. The Directorate Internal Audit of the Department of Higher Education and Training (DHET)
is located in the Office of the Director General (ODG). The Directorate currently has 7
(seven) occupied posts in its structure and is headed by Director Internal Audit reporting
administratively to the Accounting Officer and functionally to the Audit Committee
consisting of external members.
2.3. The Department normally make use a co-source partner to assist the Directorate Internal
Audit with its functions and mandate. The co-source partner’s contract has since expired
at the end of June 2025.
3. CONTRACT SCOPE
The appointed service provider shall perform the following audits:
• Annual Financial Statement review.
• Annual Performance Report review; and
• Proactive review of the 2027/28 Annual Performance Plan.

4.1. A report on the adequacy of the AFS Disclosures in line with the requirements of the

resources;
• Identification of risks that may impact the achievement of planned targets;
• Provide recommendations to improve the quality, credibility, and audit-readiness of
the APP.
