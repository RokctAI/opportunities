SUMMARY
This addendum clarifies and amends the Terms of Reference following questions raised by
prospective bidders during the briefing session held in respect of the tender published for the
appointment of service provider(s) for short-term vehicle rentals, chauffeur drive and bus
services for official funerals and state events for a period of sixty (60) months.
The addendum addresses the clarification raised regarding the R15 million financial
guarantee, the applicable preference point system, and the extension of the bid closing
date.
The amendment and correction below are intended to provide clarity to all prospective bidders,
afford prospective bidders sufficient time to consider the amendments to the Terms of
Reference and prepare their bids accordingly, and ensure that the requirements are applied
consistently during the evaluation of bids.
1. NATURE OF ADDENDUM
This document constitutes an addendum to the original tender documentation and provides
clarification and amendment to the Terms of Reference arising from questions raised by
prospective bidders during the briefing session.

1. CURRENT SECTION – FINANCIAL CONSIDERATIONS
2.1. R15 MILLION FINANCIAL GUARANTEE
TOR – Table 3: Mandatory Requirements, Item 6 – Financial Considerations
The Terms of Reference currently require the bidder to provide proof relating to access to
funding of R15 million.
Following the briefing session, a bidder requested clarification on whether an actual financial
guarantee confirming access to R15 million funding must be submitted, or whether a letter
confirming access to the required funding would be acceptable.
2.2. AMENDMENT
Following consideration of the question raised during the briefing session, the requirement
under Table 3: Mandatory Requirements, Item 6 – Financial Considerations is hereby amended
to read as follows:
“The bidder must submit either a letter from a financial institution or a bank guarantee
confirming that the bidder has access to funding of at least R15 million. The financial institution
issuing the letter or guarantee must be registered with the Financial Sector Conduct Authority
(FSCA).”
The amendment clarifies the type of supporting documentation that will be accepted as proof of
the bidder’s access to the required funding.
Accordingly, bidders may submit either a letter from a qualifying financial institution or a bank
guarantee, provided that the financial institution issuing the letter or guarantee is registered with
the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA).

The Terms of Reference contain a reference to an 80/20 preference point system in the heading
of the relevant evaluation table. However, the table provides for a total of 10 preference points,
which is indicative of a 90/10 preference point system.
During the briefing session, a bidder requested clarification on which preference point system
would apply to the procurement.
3.2. CLARIFICATION AND CORRECTION
Following consideration of the question raised during the briefing session, the Department
hereby confirms that the applicable preference point system for this procurement is 90/10.
The total preference points reflected in the relevant evaluation table amount to 10 points,
confirming that the procurement is subject to the 90/10 preference point system.
The reference to 80/20 in the heading of the relevant table is therefore an error and must be
disregarded.
The applicable preference point system is accordingly:
90 points – Price
10 points – Specific Goals
Bidders must therefore take the 90/10 preference point system into consideration when
preparing and submitting their bids.
