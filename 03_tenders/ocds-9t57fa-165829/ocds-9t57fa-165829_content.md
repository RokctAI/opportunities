3 Standby service provider where one service provider is appointed
3.1 For the award of one (1) service provider for hydraulic repairs and maintenance for Dredging Service fleet,
there is an intention to make an award to a “standby service provider” (2nd ranked bidder) for
circumstances where the 1st ranked bidder (main service provider) after having accepted the award of
business or has signed the contract, indicates that they will not be able to render the service or at any
time during the execution of the contract, the main service provider is unable to render the service.
3.2 This RFP contains this standard disclaimer which clearly states that Transnet intends to award to a
standby service provider under the circumstances where the evaluation award leads to one (1) service
provider.
3.3 Consequently, a customized standby service provider Master Goods Agreement (MGA) will be prepared
and entered into between Transnet and the standby service provider. The agreement will regulate the
relationship between Transnet and the standby service provider in relation to the circumstances where and
when each party can exercise its rights and obligations contained in the contract.
3.4 The award will be made to the “standby service provider” at the same time that an award is made to the
main service provider.
3.5 As the standby service provider will be considered for award of business, the price/s of the standby service
provider should be market related. If it is not market-related, negotiations must be conducted in line with
the approval delegations for negotiations to get the price to be market-related.
3.6 The standby service provider should also be tax compliant; not listed in the list of restricted bidders; have
no declared conflicts of interest; meet any other relevant legislative requirement.
3.7 The communication of the Letter of Award that will be issued to the standby service provider will clearly
state that:
3.8 The service provider is being awarded the status of a standby service provider;
