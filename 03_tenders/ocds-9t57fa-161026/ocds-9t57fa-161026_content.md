6.11 2) PHASE 2: TESTING OF SAMPLES:
TESTING OF SAMPLES
Tender Requirements
1. Material Collection:
• Bidders must collect standard samples of aggregates, cement, and fly ash from
the client's facilities for testing purposes during site clarification, or any time before
bid closing date.
• These materials will serve as the basis for replicating the client's mix design.
1. Mix Design Evaluation:
• Bidders are required to use the client's provided mix design.
• Bidders must calculate and demonstrate the optimal dosage of their additive for
the given mix design.
• Each bidder will prepare a test batch of concrete using the supplied materials and
the proposed admixture to meet specifications
• For the IVRCC Mix the dosage of the set retarding admixtures shall be stablished
to achieve an initial set of the IVRCC mix in excess of 15 hours and a final set of
less than 40 hours. The setting time will be measured according to ASTM C403.
The Bidder shall carry out the tests outdoors at different ambient temperature
(Please note C4-SITE INFORMATION – 3 WEATHER) and environmental
conditions. The test sample shall be the material passing through the 4,75-mm
sieve of the IVRCC mix and during the test it shall be protected to avoid loss of
moisture. Set retarding products, which results in a decrease of the IVRCC strength
by more than 10% at 28 days or at a later age, shall not be accepted.
1. Performance Testing:
• Bidders will provide results for:
• Workability (slump test). - SANS 5862-1:2006
• Compressive strength (28 days). - SANS 5863:2006
• Workability (Vebe test - RCC) - SANS 5862-3:2006
• Air content (where applicable). - SANS 6252:2006
• All testing must be performed at a SANAS-accredited laboratory. A detailed test
report must be submitted with the tender, together with the relevant SANAS
certificates confirming that the required tests (as specified above) were conducted.
1. Cost Submission:
• Bidders must submit the unit price of their additive per Liter.
• Each bidder must provide the total estimated cost of their additive for the given
mix design (based on their calculated dosage).

Cost Calculation Formula
The following formula will be used to evaluate the cost-effectiveness of each bidder:
Total Cost per Litre Calculation:
Total Litres Required=Dosage per m³×Quantity of Concrete (m³)
Total Cost = Unit Price per Litre × Total Litres Required
Evaluation Example:
• Bidder A:
- Dosage = 3.5 l/m³
- Quantity of concrete = 100m³
- Total Litres Required = 3.5 x 100 = 350 litre
- Total Cost = R50/litre × 350 litre = R17 500
• Bidder B:
- Dosage = 2.8 l/m³
- Quantity of concrete = 100m³
- Total Litres Required = 2.8 x 100 = 280 litre
- Total Cost = R60/litre x 280litre = R16 800
In this case, Bidder B would be more cost-effective despite the higher unit price due to lower
dosage requirements.

1. LOCATION AND ACCESS TO SITE
The dam site is situated on the Olifants River, in the Western Cape, approximately 2 km South- West of Clanwilliam
town in the Western Cape Province. Site
The site is immediately next to the N7 and accessed through a controlled gate. The gravel site roads will be regularly
maintained but could get challenging under abnormal rainfall conditions.
Directions to Site:
• From Cape Town International Airport: Take N2 freeway towards Cape Town
• Take exit 14 for M7/ Vanguard Drive
• Turn right onto Vanguard Dr
• Continue onto N7 for about 230 km
• The dam will be on the right about 2 km south of the town of Clanwilliam Dam in the Olifants River
1. ROAD CONDITIONS AND DISTANCE
Bidders are advised to acquaint themselves with roads, road conditions, distances, etc. on and to the site, before
bidding.

3 WEATHER
Temperatures
The Clanwilliam Dam site is situated in the Western Province in the winter rainfall region
with hot summers and cold wet winters. Average summer maximum and minimum
temperatures are in the order of 39°C and 11°C respectively. Corresponding winter
temperatures are 31°C and 4°C.
Relevant detailed meteorological information was obtained for the site from Weather Station
No 00846710 at Clanwilliam Dam, situated at RL 103 m. Average monthly minimum and
maximum temperatures obtained from that station are listed in Table 3.1. These records
will be used for tender purposes and will be compared to information from a weather station
to be installed at the dam site before construction commence. The prevailing wind
direction(s) is Southeast and Northwest.
