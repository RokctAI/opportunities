BACKGROUND\nThe PPECB is a Schedule 3A public entity as defined in the Public Finance Management Act 1 of 1999 and is\nestablished in terms of Section 2 of the Perishable Products Export Control Act 9 of 1983. The main purpose\nof the PPECB is to promote the orderly, efficient, and sustainable export of perishable products from South\nAfrica. The PPECB manages and monitors the cold chain for the export of perishable products from South\nAfrica.
