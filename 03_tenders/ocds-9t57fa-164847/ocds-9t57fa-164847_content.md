WE’RE HIRING: NEWS EDITOR
Why not join Arena Holdings?
Arena Holdings is a news and entertainment content provider and one of Africa’s largest English-language news
publishers. It owns iconic news titles including the Sunday Times, Sowetan, Business Day, Financial Mail, The
Herald and Daily Dispatch; It also broadcasts on DStv via Business Day TV, Ignition and The Home Channel and
owns three film production and distribution assets – Empire Entertainment, Indigenous Film Distribution and
Ochre Moving Pictures. A home to Africa’s longest running independent recording label and music publisher,
Gallo Music.
ROLE OVERVIEW:
Business Day is looking for a News Editor to drive our agenda-setting journalism. The News Editor is a senior
leader in the newsroom, working closely with other editors to set our strategic direction. They have sight of our
full editorial product, ensuring that Business Day’s coverage of news, politics and economics remains industry
leading. We’re looking for someone with exceptional editing and writing skills who can quickly commission, edit
and publish our journalism across print, digital and multimedia platforms. The News Editor is also unafraid to
produce stories themselves when the occasion calls.
Our newsroom is experienced but dynamic. We encourage all of our reporters and editors to plan their work
beyond the headlines of the day and not be confined by established storytelling techniques. If you think that you’d
be a good fit for that environment, then we’d love to hear from you.
EXPERIENCE:
• Relevant diploma/degree
• At least 7 seven years’ experience in journalism
• A demonstrated knowledge of politics and economics
• Excellent newsgathering, research, editing and writing skills
• Proven writing ability
• Ability to work quickly and accurately under pressure
• Excellent communication skills
• Unimpeachable integrity.
WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR
• Manage an editorial team
• Work professionally and ethically
• Manage the production of journalism that is well-edited for balance, accuracy and legal considerations
• Drive strategic planning of news coverage
• Oversee long-term planning diary
• Commission quick, well-written, relevant, accurate and balanced news stories
• Commission multimedia stories where appropriate
• Liaise with other section editors and reporters regarding stories for follow- up
• Proven ability to write clearly and authoritatively
• A receptiveness to feedback and openness to change.
