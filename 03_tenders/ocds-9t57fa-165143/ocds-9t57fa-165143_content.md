8.0 INSTRUCTIONS
8.1 Where surfaces are contaminated with oil or grease, this must be removed
before blast cleaning, by solvent or steam cleaning. Localised grease, or oil
spots can be treated using white spirit or other suitable organic solvent. Larger
areas must be treated using a water emulsifiable and rinsable solvent. After
de-greasing, all surfaces must be thoroughly washed with copious amounts of
fresh water to remove emulsified contaminants. Surfaces must then be dried.
8.2 Cover the clearance areas, as required, that must not be grit blasted or treated.
All bolt holes must be clean and plugged before blasting. Precautions must be
taken to protect areas not to be coated from blasting, and all openings in
equipment, nozzle entries or pipework must be adequately sealed to prevent
ingress of grit or coating.
RECORD IN THE HER
8.3 Wet blast the item.
NOTE: Before commencing work, erect a temporary enclosure to localise grit
blasting operations or remove parts to an appropriate grit-blasting
area.
Clean, dry, blasting abrasive must be used. The abrasive must be of steel or
chilled iron or boiler slag types. Particle size should give a profile between 50 to
100 microns peak to troughs with a maximum of 150 microns for rogue peaks.
(QC are to verify blast profile).
8.4 Before dry blasting, the relative humidity must be less than 85 % and ambient
temperature to be greater than + 5°C. Dryblast the item. Remove all
contaminants by air blowing with oil and moisture free air or by vacuuming.
(QC are to verify relative humidity and temperatures).
After dry blasting check the soluble salt levels using the Wattman Test Paper.
Salt levels are to be between 50-100mg/m². Repeat 8.3 and 8.4 until soluble salt
levels are within specification (QC to verify soluble salts levels).
RECORD IN THE EHR
8.5 Sweep-blast the tested area to remove all traces of the soluble salts tester.
Remove all traces of blasting media with oil and moisture free air or by
vacuuming.
RECORD IN THE EHR

8.6 Check the RH and substrate temperature. The RH in the immediate vicinity of
work must be less than 90% and the surface temperature of the steel must be
3°C or more above dew point (QC to verify RH, substrate temperature and
dewpoint).
RECORD IN THE EHR
8.7 If the environmental conditions are acceptable, continue with coating within four
hours of grit blasting. RH to remain below 90% during coating application.
(QC to monitor RH during application of coatings).
RECORD IN THE EHR
8.7.1 Apply primer coat of Corroglass PPA or ECP for concrete and PPV for steel.
8.7.2 Fill all cavities with Corroglass 602 or EA.
8.7.3 Apply five colour coats of Corroglass 632 or EB.
8.7.4 Apply two final coats of Corroglass 652 or EC (see data sheets for E-Series).
8.8 Check DFT upon completion if access is possible. Minimum 1200 microns thick
(QC to verify DFT).
RECORD IN THE EHR
8.9 Visually inspect and carry out a spark or wet sponge test (QC to verify testing).
8.10 Record the batch numbers of all the coatings used in the EHR (QC to verify
batch numbers).
