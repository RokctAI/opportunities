The purpose of these Terms of Reference is to clearly state and guide the service providers
when developing proposals and concepts for the project. Outline the service that would be

to submit proposals and quotations, in order to be considered for the appointment to supply,

1. BACKGROUND INFORMATION.
Beneficiary : Community of Umzimvubu Local Municipality
Contracting Authority : Umzimvubu Local Municipality (LED Depart)
Umzimvubu Local Municipality has identified a need to supply and deliver Rams for a period of
two year.
1. PROJECT OBJECTIVES.
➢ To provide sheep shearers for ULM shearing sheds.
➢ To enhance livestock production and farmer returns within ULM
➢ To increase income from wool production farming.
➢ To improve skills for production of quality wool.
➢ To boost local economic development
Umzimvubu Local Municipality therefore is looking for a service provider to supply and deliver sheep
Rams for a period of two years for Umzimvubu Local Municipality projects.
1. Project situation
The project is situated in 28 wards in Umzimvubu Local Municipality.
1. Request
Umzimvubu Local Municipality therefore is looking for a service provider to supply and deliver sheep
