REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
THE APPOINTMENT OF A SERVICE PROVIDER FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF BRAND,
MARKETING & COMMUNICATION CAMPAIGNS, PROGRAMMES AND PROJECTS
(TENDER NUMBER: T02-2027)
Enterprise iLembe hereby requests proposals from suitably qualified and experienced marketing,
communications and public relations agencies to assist the entity with the implementation of its Brand,
Marketing & Communication strategies.
Please visit our website www.enterpriseilembe.co.za for copies of the Terms of Reference or e-mail your
request to the contact person listed below. Any requests for further information for clarity purposes will be
made available via e-mail.
Please place your completed proposal in a sealed envelope endorsed “IMPLEMENTATION OF BRAND,
MARKETING & COMMUNICATION CAMPAIGNS, PROGRAMMES AND PROJECTS” in the Tender Box at
Sangweni Tourism Centre, Cnr Link Road & Ballito Drive, Ballito on or before 29 September 2026 at 11h00.
All proposals will be evaluated and adjudicated in terms of the approved Supply Chain Management Policy of
Enterprise iLembe using the 80/20 points system as set out in the 2022 Preferential Procurement Regulations.
Enterprise iLembe reserves the right not to accept any or portion of a proposal received under this call or give
reasons for doing so.
Please note: All proposals must be submitted by hand to the tender box as no e-mailed or fax submissions will
be accepted. Bidders using a courier service to deliver documents are responsible for ensuring that such
delivered documents are physically deposited in the tender box before the tender closure. Tenders must be
submitted during office hours as stated in the terms of reference. No submissions will be accepted after the
closing date and time, as stipulated above.
