TENDER NOTICE
The Richards Bay Industrial Development Zone Company SOC Ltd (RBIDZ) invites capable and competent service providers to
submit proposals for the following tender.
CONTRACT NO. MO/ADM/539/26 - APPOINTMENT OF A SERVICE PROVIDER SUPPLY, DELIVER, CONFIGARATION OF
CAMERA SYSTEMS AND UPGRADE THE ACCESS CONTROL
Tender documents will be available as from 28 July 2026 to 11 August 2026 and will be emailed upon an indication of interest
and submission of proof of payment of the tender charge from the prospective tenderer. A non-refundable cash payment of
R500.00 paid into the RBIDZ’s bank account, namely Absa, Branch Code: 632005 Account No.: 4072219190. Bidders should
forward a request for RFP document via email to tenders@rbidz.co.za with their company name, address and contact details quoting
the tender number and description on the subject line.
No briefing meeting or site inspection will be conducted for this project.
Tenders are to be completed in accordance with the conditions attached to the documents and must be sealed and endorsed with
the relevant Contract Number and must be personally deposited in the official Tender box at the RBIDZ, RBIDZ Complex, 4 Harbour
Arterial, Alton, Richards Bay (GPS 28°46'50.3"S 32°00'54.0"E) not later than 12:00 on 28 August 2026, at which time tenders
will be opened in public.
Tenders will first be evaluated in terms of compliance assessment, functionality, and thereafter price and preference on a 80/20
system as contemplated in the Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Act and must score a minimum of 70% on functionality
to be considered further in terms of price and preference. Tenderers who do not obtain the minimum functionality scoring will be
regarded as non-responsive and that will lead to the disqualification of the Tender Submission.
In accordance with the RBIDZ Preferential Procurement Policy and Broad Based Black Economic Empowerment Act and
associated Codes of Good Practice, targeted enterprises are companies that are Level 1 to 3 in terms of the B-BBEE Status
Level shall be eligible to tender.
The RBIDZ is not bound to accept the lowest price, highest scoring or any Tender and reserves the right to accept or not
to accept any Tender in whole or part thereof. The RBIDZ reserves the right to appoint a Tenderer that best meets the
requirements of the company on reasonable grounds. Telegraphic, telephonic, telex, electronic, e-mailed, and late Tenders will
not be accepted.
