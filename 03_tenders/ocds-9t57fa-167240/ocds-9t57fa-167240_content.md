TERMS OF REFERENCE FOR REVIEW OF INTEGRATED LAND USE SCHEME
SECTION 1: PURPOSE
TERMS OF REFERENCE: FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF A SERVICE PROVIDER TO REVIEW THE LAND USE
SCHEME FOR INGQUZA HILL LOCAL MUNICIPALITY FOR A PERIOD OF TEN (10) MONTHS.
Ingquza Hill Local Municipality requires the services of one (1) firm or a consortium of suitably qualified service providers to review the
Land Use Scheme for Ingquza Hill Local Municipality in the Eastern Cape Province.
As per Section 27 of the Spatial Planning and Land Use Management Act, Act No 16 of 2013 (SPLUMA) a Municipality may review its
land use scheme in order to achieve consistency with the municipal SDF and must do so at least every 5 years.
The present Ingquza Hill Land Use Management Scheme, 2018, has some shortcomings in alignment, managing land use in rural
and informal areas, and this issue is also evident in the Municipality.
The need therefore exists to review the land use scheme for the municipality to ensure harmonious and coordinated land use control
and systems within the municipality.
SECTION 2: BACKGROUND
2.1 Ingquza Hill Local Municipality is a category B municipality; that falls within the O.R Tambo District in the Eastern Cape Province.
2.2 Ingquza Hill is largely rural Municipality, with two towns situated within it, namely Lusikisiki in the Eastern part, and Flagstaff on
its Western edge. The municipal area of Ingquza Hill municipality encompasses both urban and rural regions, with a mix of
formal and informal settlements. This diverse composition presents a unique set of challenges and opportunities for the local
administration. The municipality must balance the needs of the urban and rural populations, while also addressing the
varied settlement patterns within its jurisdiction. Effective management of these complexities is essential for the
sustainable development of the region.
2.3 The area within the municipal boundary is governed by the Ingquza Hill Land Use Management Scheme, 2018.
SECTION 3: PROBLEM STATEMENT
The gaps expressed in the Ingquza Hill Local Municipality LUS have created challenges with its land use management. This has resulted
in misalignments with the adopted Municipal Spatial Development Framework (MSDF) and fragmented development patterns that hinder
the integration and sustainability of social, economic and environmental elements. Consequently, the institutional arrangements have
been weak and uncoordinated, which has led to incoherent planning procedures and systems within the municipality.
The current situation poses significant challenges for the municipality, and it is imperative that appropriate measures be taken to address
the issue. This requires a comprehensive approach that integrates the various elements of land use management and promotes
sustainable development practices. This will require the involvement of all stakeholders and the development of a coherent institutional
framework that fosters collaboration and coordination.
The LUS that has been in place for eight years now has become obsolete in light of the misalignment to the MSDF and missed

SECTION 4: PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES
The purpose of this project is to create a simplified yet comprehensive LUS that promotes single and harmonious
development of the municipal area while also preserving the natural environment. The LUS will encompass all areas within
Ingquza Hill Local Municipality's jurisdiction.
4.1 To develop a LUS that will give effect to and be consistent with the MSDF and determine the use and development
of land within the municipal area to promote economic growth, social inclusion, efficient development and to minimize
the impact on public health and natural resources.
4.2 To bridge the gaps and inconsistencies created by multiple planning tools used when considering development
proposals within the municipality.
4.3 In terms of Section 24 (2) of SPLUMA, the content of a LUS must:
4.4 Include suitable categories of land use zoning and regulations for the entire municipal area, including areas not
previously subject to a LUS;
4.5 Take cognisance of any environmental management instrument adopted by the relevant environmental management
authority and must comply with environmental legislation;
4.6 Include provisions that permit the incremental introduction of land use management and regulation in areas under
traditional leadership, rural areas, informal settlements, slums and areas not previously subject to a LUS;
4.7 Include provisions to promote the inclusion of affordable housing in residential land development;
4.8 Include land use and development incentives to promote the effective implementation of the spatial
development framework and other development policies;
4.9 Include land use and development provisions specifically to promote the effective implementation of national and
provincial policies;
4.10 Give effect to MSDF and integrated development plans; and
4.11 A land use scheme may include provisions relating to:
I. The use and development of land only with the written consent of the Municipality;
II. Specific requirements regarding any special zones identified to address the development priorities of
the Municipality and;
III. The variation of conditions of a land use scheme other than a variation which may materially alter or affect
conditions relating to the use, size and scale of buildings and the intensity or density of land use.
