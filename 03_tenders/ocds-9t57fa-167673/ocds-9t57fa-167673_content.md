AMENDMENTS TO THE TENDER DOCUMENT
The National Health Laboratory Service (NHLS) hereby issues Erratum No. 1 to the above-mentioned tender. Bidders
are requested to take note of the following amendments to the tender documentation:
1. Pricing Schedule – Year 2 to Year 5
The Pricing Schedule is amended to provide for the inclusion of pricing for Years 2 to 5 on the applicable pricing
schedules.
1. Pricing Formulas and Summary Calculations
The associated pricing formulas and summary calculations are amended to ensure that annual quantities, unit rates,
applicable escalation and five-year totals are correctly calculated and reflected in the Total Bid Offer.
1. SAHPRA Licence/Registration
A valid SAHPRA licence/registration is to be included under the Administrative Requirements.
1. Extension of Tender Closing Date
The tender closing date is extended from 30 September 2026 to 07 October 2026 to allow bidders adequate time to
conduct the required laboratory visits and finalise their submissions.
1. Amendment to Mandatory Administrative Requirement – Laboratory Visits
The current requirement under the Mandatory Administrative Requirements, which reads:
“Proof of attendance at the compulsory briefing session and proof of representation at a minimum of 50% of site
visits across all business units (comprising 50% small laboratories and 50% medium laboratories).”
is amended to read:
“Proof of attendance at the compulsory briefing session and proof of laboratory visits to all the below-listed
laboratories.”
IMPORTANT: Bidders are required to take the above amendments into account when preparing and submitting their
bids. All other terms and conditions of the tender remain unchanged unless specifically amended by this Erratum.
