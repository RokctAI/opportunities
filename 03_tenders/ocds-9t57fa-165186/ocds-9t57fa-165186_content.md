NOTICE T06/26/27
SUPPLY AND ERECTION OF HIGH SECURITY LARGE PANEL CONCRETE FENCING AT GENE LOUW SPORTS
GROUNDS, MOORREESBURG
Tenders are hereby invited for the Supply and Erection of High Security Large Panel Concrete Fencing at Gene
Louw Sports Grounds, Moorreesburg.
Tenderers must be registered with the CIDB in a CE or SQ class of construction works and have a grading designation
equal to or higher than that determined in accordance with the sum tendered or a value determined in accordance with
regulation 25 (1B) or regulation 25(7A) of the Construction Industry Development Regulations, 2004. It is estimated that
tenderers will need a grading designation of 3 (CE or SQ) or higher.
The physical address for collection of tender documents is Offices of the Director: Civil Engineering Services, Swartland
Municipality, Piet Retief Street (PEP building), Malmesbury. Documents may be collected during working hours after 09:00
from Tuesday, 11 August 2026. A non-refundable tender fee of R190-00, payable in cash, credit/debit card or Electronic
Funds Transfer (EFT) is required on collection of the tender documents. Payment needs to be completed at the cashiers’
desk (c/o Kerk & Hill Street) and proof of payment needs to be provided to the Civil Engineering Services office in Piet
Retief Street to obtain the document. Tenderers should allow 2 business days for EFT payments to clear prior to collection.
Tenders may only be submitted on the prescribed tender documents.
