Background
1. Hornbill staff accommodation
The 2 Hornbill house’s exterior and interior have sustained substantial damage, rendering it unsafe for occupancy.
Exterior damage
• The roof has been partially torn off, with significant damage to the roofing membrane and structural
components.
• The exterior walls have suffered cracks and damage, compromising the building’s structural integrity.
• Some windows are damaged, allowing water to enter the building.
• Exterior doors are not in good conditions
Interior damage
• Water has penetrated the building, causing damage to the ceiling, walls and floors.
• Ceiling boards have fallen, and some areas have exposed wiring and ductwork.
• Floors have been damaged by the water with some areas experiencing warping or buckling.
• Interior walls have suffered damage with some areas having cracks.
