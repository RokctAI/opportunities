OBJECTIVES
1. The objectives (aims) of this tender are:
• To invite bids from suitable service provider(s) for the procurement of IT services and expertise to facilitate the
maintenance of data center/server rooms, enabling live capture, smart ID card functionality, and other departmental
applications. To invite bids from suitable service provider(s) for the procurement of specialised Information
Technology (IT) services, technical expertise, and support resources to facilitate the maintenance, support,
optimisation, and continuous operation of the Departmental Data Centres and Server Rooms. The services are
required to ensure the stability, availability, security, and performance of the infrastructure that hosts and supports
mission-critical systems, including Live Capture services, Smart ID Card functionality, biometric systems, digitisation
services, and other departmental business applications for a period of three (3) years with an option of extending it
for further (2) years subject to satisfactory performance using SITA RFB 1183.
• The scope of the required services includes, but is not limited to:
➢ Preventative and corrective maintenance of data centre and server room infrastructure;
➢ Monitoring and management of servers, storage, backup, virtualisation, and network environments;
➢ Maintenance and support of power, cooling, environmental monitoring, and related infrastructure within the data
centre environment;
➢ Provisioning of specialised technical skills and expert resources for troubleshooting, incident resolution,
optimisation, and operational support;
➢ Support for disaster recovery, backup, business continuity, and system availability requirements;
➢ Infrastructure upgrades, configuration management, and technical advisory services;
➢ Compliance with Government ICT standards, security requirements, and departmental policies;
➢ Provision of 24/7 support services where required to ensure uninterrupted availability of critical systems and
services.
• The required services will support the Department in maintaining high availability, operational continuity, and reliability
of critical ICT systems that enable citizen services and internal business operations. The proposed contract period
is three (3) years with a possible extension of a further two (2) years subject to satisfactory performance.
BACKGROUND
1. The Department of Home Affairs (DHA) requires suitable and experienced service provider(s) for the provisioning of
support and maintenance services for its four (4) main Data Centres located in Gauteng, as well as Server Rooms in 210
modernized offices and Bank Branches across all nine (9) provinces. It must be noted that the number of modernized
offices and bank branches is expected to increase as the Department continues with its modernization programme. All
support and maintenance activities are centrally managed from the DHA Head Office.
Page 12 of 40

DHA13-2026
1. As part of its constitutional and legislative mandate, the Department must provide uninterrupted, secure, and efficient
civic and immigration services to both citizens and non-citizens. The Department’s ability to deliver these services is
heavily dependent on a robust and well-maintained Information and Communication Technology (ICT) environment.
1. Due to the DHA Modernisation Programme, there is a growing need for specialized technical services to support the roll-
out, operation, and maintenance of Data Centre infrastructure, including server hardware, storage systems, virtualization
platforms, and associated software infrastructure.
1. To ensure continued stability and service continuity, it is critical that a comprehensive support and maintenance contract
be in place immediately upon the expiry of the current contract. This will safeguard the Department’s ICT operations by
ensuring timely replacement, repair, and restoration of critical equipment and systems in the event of failures or disasters.
The transition to a paperless DHA environment has significantly enhanced service delivery efficiency but has also
increased reliance on the underlying ICT infrastructure. It is, therefore, imperative to ensure uninterrupted ICT support
and maintenance services to sustain the Department’s service delivery commitments to the South African public.
