TERMS OF REFERENCE/ SPECIFICATION
1. PURPOSE
1.1. The purpose of this Request for Quotation (RFQ) is to source the services of a
professional services firm to provide proposals/ advice in relation to the
restructuring/or repositioning of KBP property assets with focus on
redevelopment, acquisition and disposal strategy.
2. PROJECT BACKGROUND
2.1. Sedfa/KBP owns a portfolio of 45 properties in seven provinces with a GLA of 124
500 m², comprising of 26 industrial properties and 17 retail commercial
properties and 2 portions of vacant land. The properties are in the following cities
and towns; Johannesburg, Pretoria, Brakpan, Vereeniging, Gqeberha,

Bloemfontein, Odendaalsrus, Kimberley, Upington, Durban, Secunda and Cape
Town.
2.2. Almost half of the portfolio’s GLA is concentrated in Gauteng, followed by the
Western Cape and Kwa-Zulu Natal. The portfolio consists predominantly of light
industrial properties that are concentrated in Gauteng. The retail properties are
highly concentrated in Cape Town in the township of Mitchells Plain. The total
number of tenants in the portfolio is over 900.
