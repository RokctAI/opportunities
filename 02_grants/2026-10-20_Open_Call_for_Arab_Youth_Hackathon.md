# Grant Opportunity: Open Call for Arab Youth Hackathon

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: PepsiCo (in partnership with the Arab Youth Center)
- **Deadline**: 2026-10-20
- **Funding Amount**: prizes of $30,000 (first), $20,000 (second), $10,000 (third), plus one month of online incubation support from Plug and Play
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates (deadlines vary by country; October 20, 2026 is the Saudi Arabia deadline).
- Open to university students and early-stage entrepreneurs aged 18-35 focused on climate change and sustainability solutions; teams of 2-3 members (at least one member 21+); must be nationals or residents of the country where applying.
