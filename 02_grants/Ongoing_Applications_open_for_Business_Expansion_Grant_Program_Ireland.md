# Grant Opportunity: Applications open for Business Expansion Grant Program (Ireland)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Local Enterprise Office Waterford
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €150,000 (50% co-funding)
- **Focus Area**: Business Expansion, Employment Creation, Local Business Growth

## Eligibility
- Micro and small businesses in Ireland seeking to expand capacity and create employment. Funding is provided at up to 50% of eligible investment, up to €150,000.

## Description
The Business Expansion Grant is designed to assist businesses in expanding their enterprise beyond the initial start-up phase. It supports business development, machinery purchases, capital investments, and employment creation.
