# Grant Opportunity: EY SDG Accelerator: Freshwater x AI Program

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: EY / Bright Tide
- **Deadline**: 2026-03-16
- **Funding Amount**: Mentorship, coaching, investor readiness, and networking (Non-financial/accelerator program)
- **Focus Area**: Freshwater Systems, AI, Climate Resilience, Water Quality

## Eligibility
- Mission-driven enterprises (for-profit, nonprofit, hybrid, or cooperative) that use AI or advanced data tools to address freshwater challenges. Must operate in water-stressed regions like Africa, Latin America, South Asia, Southeast Asia. Must be legally incorporated, active for at least one year, and proficient in English.

## Description
Applications are open for the SDG Accelerator Freshwater x AI program to support ventures using advanced technologies to strengthen freshwater systems and improve climate resilience. The program offers comprehensive support including coaching from EY professionals, capacity-building workshops, investor readiness support, financial modeling guidance, and access to a global network of investors.
