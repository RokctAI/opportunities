# Grant Opportunity: U.S.-India Technology Innovation Partnership Grant

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: USISTEF
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Up to INR 15 million (approximately USD $165,000) per project, for a period of up to 24 months
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: India, United States (joint partnerships with at least one Indian and one U.S. partner for the complete duration)
- At least one partner must be an entrepreneurial entity, preferably a small or medium-scale enterprise
- Eligible participants include incorporated companies, startups, non-incorporated entities, academic institutions, government and non-governmental research institutions
- Projects must focus on critical minerals, quantum technologies, or related innovation areas
- Formal Partnership Agreement required once shortlisted
