# Grant Opportunity: KEPSA JGP Investor Readiness Program 2026 (Kenya)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: KEPSA (Kenya Private Sector Alliance)
- **Deadline**: 2026-03-28
- **Funding Amount**: Support program; Debt financing options range from Ksh. 100,000 to Ksh. 14 million
- **Focus Area**: Investor Readiness, Business Growth, Health, Agribusiness, Education, Manufacturing, Green/Circular Economy, Service Industry

## Eligibility
- Founders and business owners in Kenya operating registered businesses or companies with a valid registration certificate or company CR12. Businesses must have maintained records for more than two years, have an annual revenue above Ksh. 10 million, and be seeking business funding to support growth.

## Description
The Kenya Private Sector Alliance is inviting applications to participate in a support program designed to help businesses package their ventures, attract funding, and access growth opportunities. The program is structured as a two-month sequential support outreach aimed at assisting entrepreneurs in strengthening their businesses and preparing them to access financing.
