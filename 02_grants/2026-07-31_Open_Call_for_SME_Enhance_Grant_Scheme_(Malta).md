# Grant Opportunity: Open Call for SME Enhance Grant Scheme (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Fondi.eu / Measures and Support Division (Malta)
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: €6,000,000
- **Focus Area**: Innovation and Business Growth

## Eligibility
- Eligible applicants are micro, small and medium-sized enterprises engaged in an economic activity, regardless of their legal form.

## Description
The scheme falls under Policy Objective 1, which promotes a more competitive and smarter Europe through innovative and smart economic transformation and regional ICT connectivity. Its priorities include promoting entrepreneurship by facilitating the economic exploitation of new ideas and fostering the creation of new firms, reaping the benefits of digitisation for citizens, companies, research organisations and public authorities, and enhancing the sustainable growth, competitiveness, and job creation of SMEs through productive investments. The scheme has an initial budget of €6,000,000 and operates as an open rolling call with periodic cut-off dates.
