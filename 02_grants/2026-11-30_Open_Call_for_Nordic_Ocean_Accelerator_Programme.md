## Description
The Nordic Ocean Accelerator Programme was established to drive the blue transition by equipping ocean economy startups and SMEs with the tools, networks, and expertise needed to thrive in the Nordic region. The programme brings together leading Nordic ocean clusters and industry partners to foster innovation and sustainable growth in sectors such as seafood and blue food, maritime, ocean energy, ocean technology, ocean data and AI, and ports and logistics. Participants benefit from a comprehensive curriculum that includes six learning modules, an online kickoff, a hybrid final event, and ongoing support through an online learning environment.
