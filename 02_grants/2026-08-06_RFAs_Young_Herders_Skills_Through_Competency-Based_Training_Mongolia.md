# Grant Opportunity: RFAs: Young Herders’ Skills Through Competency-Based Training (Mongolia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: United Nations Development Programme (UNDP Mongolia)
- **Deadline**: 2026-08-06
- **Funding Amount**: UNDP Service Contract (Organizing competency-based training for 120 young herders across 4 aimags)
- **Focus Area**: Pasture management, livestock feeding, livestock business development, and climate-resilient herding practices

## Eligibility
- National legal entities in Mongolia with at least 3 years of experience in agriculture-related advisory services, training, and capacity-building activities

## Description
UNDP Mongolia is issuing a Request for Applications to national legal entities to organize and deliver competency-based training for 120 young herders across Khovd, Zavkhan, Dornod, and Sukhbaatar aimags, covering sustainable pasture management, livestock feed production, and business risk management.
