# Grant Opportunity: Zepto Nova Pitch in 10 Programme (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Zepto Limited / Startup India
- **Deadline**: 2026-05-22
- **Funding Amount**: Hyperlocal retail distribution and logistics support
- **Focus Area**: Manufacturing, consumer products, FMCG, retail tech

## Eligibility
- Startups recognised by DPIIT and registered on Startup India, as well as MSMEs and entrepreneurs with market-ready consumer products and sales traction in India.

## Description
Zepto Limited, in collaboration with Startup India, aims to accelerate India’s consumer product ecosystem by supporting startups and MSMEs with retail distribution, product visibility, and last-mile delivery through Zepto’s hyperlocal network.
