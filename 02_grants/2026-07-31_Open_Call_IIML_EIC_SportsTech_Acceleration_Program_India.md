# Grant Opportunity: Open Call: IIML EIC SportsTech Acceleration Program (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: IIM Lucknow Enterprise Incubation Centre (IIML EIC) & Legaxy
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Incubation support, seed funding opportunities from IIML EIC, VC network access, and super mentorship
- **Focus Area**: SportsTech, Fitness Technology, AI in Sports, Wearables, eSports, Sports Analytics, and Media

## Eligibility
- Open to startups registered in India across all growth stages building SportsTech and fitness solutions

## Description
The IIM Lucknow Enterprise Incubation Centre and Legaxy have launched a dedicated SportsTech Acceleration Program featuring super mentorship by Indian cricketer Bhuvneshwar Kumar, investor connections, and direct funding opportunities for Indian SportsTech startups.
