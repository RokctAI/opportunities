# Grant Opportunity: Apply for Affordable and Diverse Housing Fund (Australia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: City of Sydney
- **Deadline**: 2026-09-01
- **Funding Amount**: Up to $4 million per project or 30% of total project cost
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Australia (City of Sydney local area or demonstrating significant community benefits). Individuals, legal entities, or auspiced applicants; not-for-profit purposes only; active ABN if applying as a business; must comply with the City's ethics framework and be debt-free to the City; priority to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander-led projects.
