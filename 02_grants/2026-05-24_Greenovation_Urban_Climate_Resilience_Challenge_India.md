# Grant Opportunity: Greenovation Urban Climate Resilience Challenge (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: EarthON Foundation / Startup India
- **Deadline**: 2026-05-24
- **Funding Amount**: Up to Rs. 20 Lakh funding; Investment support up to Rs. 1 Crore
- **Focus Area**: Urban climate resilience, air quality, waste systems, heat stress

## Eligibility
- Startups and social entrepreneurs developing solutions for Indian urban climate resilience. Entity must be registered within the last ten years.

## Description
The Greenovation Urban Climate Resilience Challenge seeks applications to develop and scale affordable solutions that strengthen urban climate resilience across Indian cities, addressing risks like heat stress, air pollution, and water scarcity.
