- **Organization**: Eureka Network

- **Funding Amount**: varies by country (e.g. Czech Republic up to CZK 12,000,000; Lithuania and South Africa up to €300,000 per project; South Korea up to KRW 500 million per year for up to 3 years; Spain loans and grants)

- Eligible Countries: Austria, Belgium, Canada, Chile, Czech Republic, Finland, France, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Israel, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, Slovak Republic, South Africa, South Korea, Spain, Turkey, United Kingdom.
