## Quick Stats

- **Deadline**: 2026-09-30
- **Funding Amount**: Varies per project (Incentive Programme Sustainable Development)
- **Focus Area**: Sustainable Development, Youth Entrepreneurship, Sustainable Production

## Eligibility
- Youth-led organizations, startups, cantons, and municipalities operating in Switzerland.

## Description
The Youth in Action: Innovative Projects for a Sustainable Economy Grant supports youth-led projects that contribute to sustainable development and economic solutions in Switzerland. The focus areas of the call include the future of work and entrepreneurship, sustainable production and consumption, and innovation for strengthening a sustainable economy. The Sustainable Development Incentive Programme supports projects from cantons, municipalities, and other organizations that contribute to the Sustainable Development Goals and the strategic priorities of the Sustainable Development
