# Grant Opportunity: Funding to Support SMEs’ Business Reports and Plans (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: FONDI.eu (Government of Malta)
- **Deadline**: 2026-04-17
- **Funding Amount**: Lump sum of €4,000 (80% aid intensity)
- **Focus Area**: External consultancy reports, efficiency, competitiveness, investment readiness

## Eligibility
- Legally registered micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) engaged in economic activity.
- Organizations without independent legal personality must be properly registered under relevant civil law.
- Eligible to SMEs based in Malta.

## Description
The Business Reports for SMEs Grant Scheme offers funding to support SMEs in creating external consultancy reports to improve efficiency, competitiveness, and investment readiness. It provides financial support to contract external expertise needed to generate high-quality business reports that inform strategic decisions and strengthen operational performance.
