# Grant Opportunity: Funding Call for Space-Tech Wetland Sustainability Projects

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Space Agency (ESA)
- **Deadline**: 2026-05-15
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €75,000 per activity (75% of total project cost)
- **Focus Area**: Space-enabled applications for wetland sustainability, water purification, flood control, biodiversity support

## Eligibility
- Open to companies intending to develop space-enabled services and products.
- Teams must be based in eligible countries: Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

## Description
The European Space Agency is seeking applications to develop space-enabled applications that support wetland sustainability and create viable business opportunities. The opportunity supports feasibility studies over a six-month period for early-stage service concepts that respond to real customer needs while demonstrating technical feasibility and commercial potential.
