# Grant Opportunity: Call for Applications: Brackley Town Centre Grants (UK)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: West Northamptonshire Council / Brackley Town Council
- **Deadline**: 2026-08-24
- **Funding Amount**: Grant matching up to £10,000
- **Focus Area**: Town Centre Regeneration, High Street Business Growth, Storefront Improvement

## Eligibility
- Commercial property owners, local businesses, and independent retailers in Brackley Town Centre.

## Description
The Brackley Town Centre Grants offers to support improvements to the visual appeal, economic vitality, and overall condition of Brackley Town Centre while also supporting local business growth. The grant focuses on tackling visible signs of urban decay in Brackley, enhancing visual appeal, preserving historical integrity, improving economic vitality, supporting growth of local businesses, boosting footfall, strengthening the local economy, and creating a more vibrant and attractive high street. The grant is offered in partnership with Brackley Town
