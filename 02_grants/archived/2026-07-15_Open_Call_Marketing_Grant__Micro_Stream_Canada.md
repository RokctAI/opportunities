# Grant Opportunity: Open Call: Marketing Grant – Micro Stream (Canada)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Creative Saskatchewan
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-15
- **Funding Amount**: Up to 50% of the approved project budget, maximum $5,000 per applicant per fiscal year
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Active practitioners in creative industries recognized under The Creative Saskatchewan Act (2013): book publishing, craft and visual arts, digital game development, film and television, live performing arts, music
- Must demonstrate commercial intent within their project and a clear ambition to expand and grow their business
- Must be in good standing with Creative Saskatchewan
- Must meet Saskatchewan residency, establishment, and tax filing requirements
- Limited to one Marketing Grant under the Micro Stream per fiscal year
