# Grant Opportunity: Taiwan-Israel Research and Development Pilot Cooperation Program

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Department of Industrial Technology (DoIT), Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA), Taiwan and Israel Innovation Authority
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-21
- **Funding Amount**: Up to 50% of eligible and approved R&D costs from each country's implementing agency
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Israel, Taiwan; open to joint projects from all technology sectors except defense
- Joint proposals with at least one eligible science and technology company from Taiwan and one eligible company from Israel
- Taiwan applicants must be registered companies conducting R&D activities in Taiwan; Israeli applicants must be registered companies that perform R&D and operate in Israel
- Focus areas include Smart Technology, Advanced Manufacturing, Biotechnology, Green Energy, and Service Innovation
- Academic and research institutions are eligible only as subcontractors
