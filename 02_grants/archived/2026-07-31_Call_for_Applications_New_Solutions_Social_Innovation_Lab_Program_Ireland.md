# Grant Opportunity: Call for Applications: New Solutions Social Innovation Lab Program (Ireland)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: New Solutions
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Competitive seed grant of up to €5,000 (available to a small number of participants)
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Ireland; open to applicants who are residents of the Republic of Ireland
- Targets early-stage social innovators, people with lived experience of social issues, community activists, students, individuals, groups, and organisations interested in creating positive social change
- No prior experience or fully developed idea needed, but applicants must demonstrate a commitment to actively engage throughout the programme
