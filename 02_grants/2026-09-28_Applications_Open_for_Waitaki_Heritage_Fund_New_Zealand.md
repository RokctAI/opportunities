# Grant Opportunity: Applications Open for Waitaki Heritage Fund (New Zealand)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Waitaki District Council
- **Deadline**: 2026-09-28
- **Funding Amount**: up to 60% of eligible costs for projects under $15,000 (ex GST); up to 50% for projects of $15,000 or more
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: New Zealand (Waitaki District only).
- Applicants must own heritage buildings or land with historic sites that are heritage-listed, scheduled in the Waitaki District Plan, designated Wāhi Tūpuna/Wāhi Tapu, within recognized heritage areas, or likely to meet heritage listing criteria.
