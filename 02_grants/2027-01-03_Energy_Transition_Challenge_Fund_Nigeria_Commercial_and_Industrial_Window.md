# Grant Opportunity: Energy Transition Challenge Fund – Nigeria: Commercial and Industrial Window

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: AECF (Africa Enterprise Challenge Fund)
- **Deadline**: 2027-01-03
- **Funding Amount**: EUR 100,000 - EUR 1,000,000
- **Focus Area**: Clean Energy, Renewable Technology, Industrial Energy Transition

## Eligibility
- Legally registered private sector commercial and industrial enterprises operating in Nigeria that are adopting or supplying renewable energy solutions.

## Description
The Energy Transition Challenge Fund supports commercial and industrial enterprises in Nigeria to transition from diesel and fossil-fuel power to clean, renewable energy systems through matching grants and technical assistance.
