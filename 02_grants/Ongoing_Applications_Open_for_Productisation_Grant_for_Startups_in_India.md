# Grant Opportunity: Applications Open for Productisation Grant for Startups in India

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Kerala Startup Mission
- **Deadline**: 2026-12-31
- **Funding Amount**: up to ₹7 lakhs (up to ₹12 lakhs for women-led startups)
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: India. Eligibility criteria require applicants to be startups at the product development stage, incorporated as a Private Limited, LLP, or OPC with active status in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and possessing a KSUM Unique ID
