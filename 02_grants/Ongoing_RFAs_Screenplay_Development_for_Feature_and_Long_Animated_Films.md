# Grant Opportunity: RFAs: Screenplay Development for Feature and Long Animated Films

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Netherlands Film Fund (Nederlands Filmfonds)
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €35,000
- **Focus Area**: Creative Industry, Media, Animation, Film Production

## Eligibility
- Independent production companies and creative film enterprises developing feature films or long-form animated screenplays.

## Description
The Netherlands Film Fund provides financial support to independent production companies, screenwriters, and directors to refine and develop high-quality screenplays for feature films and animated productions.
