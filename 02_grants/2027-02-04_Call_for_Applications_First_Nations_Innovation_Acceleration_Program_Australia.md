# Grant Opportunity: Call for Applications: First Nations Innovation Acceleration Program (Australia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Queensland Government & Advance Queensland
- **Deadline**: 2027-02-04
- **Funding Amount**: Up to $100,000 AUD
- **Focus Area**: Indigenous Entrepreneurship, Business Growth, Technology & Innovation

## Eligibility
- Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander businesses and enterprises operating in Queensland, Australia. Applicants must be majority First Nations-owned commercial businesses.

## Description
The First Nations Innovation Acceleration Program provides targeted grant funding and acceleration support to empower First Nations entrepreneurs to grow and scale their businesses, enter new markets, and drive commercial innovation.
