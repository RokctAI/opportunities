# Grant Opportunity: Open Call: Marketing Strategy Support Grant for SMEs (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: FONDI.eu (Government of Malta)
- **Deadline**: 2026-04-17
- **Funding Amount**: Maximum €10,000
- **Focus Area**: Marketing strategy, business promotion, competitiveness, smart economic transformation

## Eligibility
- Micro and small enterprises engaged in economic activity.
- Irrespective of legal form, must have been actively operating for at least one year.
- Eligible to SMEs based in Malta.

## Description
The Marketing Strategy for Micro & Small Enterprises scheme supports micro and small businesses in obtaining professional guidance to design marketing strategies that boost their competitiveness and drive business growth. It part-finances costs incurred for external services contracted to deliver a marketing strategy aligned with the enterprise’s objectives.
