## Description
The Fast Track to the EIC Accelerator is part of the European Partnership on Innovative SMEs, co-funded by the European Union through Horizon Europe, and is designed to accelerate the growth of startups and SMEs that have participated in Eurostars-3 projects. This call enables eligible companies to bypass the initial short proposal stage and move directly to the full proposal phase of the EIC Accelerator, increasing their chances of securing support for scaling high-impact, deep tech innovations with the potential to create new markets or disrupt existing ones. The EIC Accelerator offers a unique blend of grant and investment funding, with a total allocation of 634 million euro in 2026, as well as coaching, mentoring, and access to investors and corporates for selected applicants.
