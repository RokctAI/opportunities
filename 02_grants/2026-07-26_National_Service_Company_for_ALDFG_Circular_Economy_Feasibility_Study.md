# Grant Opportunity: National Service Company for ALDFG Circular Economy Feasibility Study (Indonesia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: ATSEA-2 Program / UNDP
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-26
- **Funding Amount**: Consultancy / Grant funding (See programme guidelines)
- **Focus Area**: Circular economy, abandoned/lost/discarded fishing gear (ALDFG), waste management, marine environmental protection

## Eligibility
- National service companies, consultants, and organizations operating in Indonesia in circular economy and marine management

## Description
The ALDFG Circular Economy Feasibility Study supports national service companies in conducting feasibility studies on circular economy models for marine waste and fishing gear in Indonesia.
