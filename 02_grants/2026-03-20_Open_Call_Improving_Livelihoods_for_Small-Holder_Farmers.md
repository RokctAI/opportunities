# Grant Opportunity: Improving Livelihoods for Small-Holder Farmers

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Ashoka East Africa
- **Deadline**: 2026-03-20
- **Funding Amount**: Competitive seed funding potentially available
- **Focus Area**: Distributed Renewable Energy, Agricultural Productivity, Smallholder Livelihoods

## Eligibility
- Passionate innovators and social entrepreneurs based in or operating in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, or Rwanda. Must offer working distributed renewable energy solutions (e.g., solar, biogas, mini-grids) applied within agricultural value chains. Applicants should have a working prototype, pilot, or early implementation with an operational structure, and be founders or decision-makers.

## Description
Ashoka East Africa is launching Phase 2 to support social entrepreneurs in scaling distributed renewable energy solutions that enhance agricultural productivity and improve smallholder livelihoods. The program offers a 6-month experience combining capacity building, mentorship, ecosystem connections, and competitive seed funding to scale innovations.
