# Grant Opportunity: Film and TV Development Grant to Support Production Companies (Canada)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Creative Saskatchewan
- **Deadline**: 2026-10-08
- **Funding Amount**: varies by stream: Pre-Development up to $5,000; First Draft up to $25,000; Final Draft up to $15,000 (each up to 50% of budget); Slate Development up to $40,000 (up to 75% of budget)
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Canada (Saskatchewan only).
- Qualified Saskatchewan production companies incorporated in Saskatchewan (or federally incorporated and registered there), with principal place of business in Saskatchewan and substantial Saskatchewan ownership; cannot hold CRTC broadcasting licenses.
