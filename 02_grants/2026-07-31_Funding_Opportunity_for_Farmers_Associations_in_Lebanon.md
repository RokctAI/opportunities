# Grant Opportunity: Funding Opportunity for Farmers’ Associations in Lebanon

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: UNDP Lebanon / World Bank / CDR
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Grants up to $35,000 USD (requires matching contribution)
- **Focus Area**: Agri-food recovery, climate-smart infrastructure, food safety, processing facilities, marketing support

## Eligibility
- Legally registered farmers’ associations, cooperatives, and producer groups operating in Lebanon with at least 2 years of operational history

## Description
Implemented under the GATE project by UNDP and the World Bank, this opportunity offers grants to farmers’ associations and agri-food cooperatives in Lebanon to enhance agricultural resilience and infrastructure.
