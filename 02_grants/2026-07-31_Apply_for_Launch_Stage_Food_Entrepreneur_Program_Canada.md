# Grant Opportunity: Apply for Launch Stage Food Entrepreneur Program (Canada)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Foodpreneur Lab & City of Toronto
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: 16-week self-directed incubator, product prototyping support, and commercialization guidance
- **Focus Area**: Food product prototyping, branding, pricing, food safety compliance, and commercialization in Canada

## Eligibility
- Canadian citizens or Permanent Residents aged 18+ residing in or with businesses registered in the City of Toronto, possessing an early-stage food product concept

## Description
Supported by the City of Toronto BIC Grant Program, Foodpreneur Lab offers a 16-week structured incubator helping early-stage food entrepreneurs convert food concepts into market-ready prototypes compliant with Canadian food safety regulations.
