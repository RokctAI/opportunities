# Grant Opportunity: BSF Certification and Accreditation Grant Program 2026 (Malaysia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Bioeconomy Corporation & Ministry of Food Industry, Commodity and Regional Development Sarawak (M-FICORD)
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-30
- **Funding Amount**: Matching grant up to RM30,000 (80% funded by M-FICORD/Bioeconomy Corp, 20% co-contribution by participant)
- **Focus Area**: Black Soldier Fly (BSF) bioeconomy, accreditation, standards compliance, and commercial market access

## Eligibility
- Sarawakian-registered companies incorporated under the Companies Act 2016 with principal offices in Sarawak operating in Black Soldier Fly (BSF) activities

## Description
Bioeconomy Corporation and M-FICORD Sarawak offer a matching grant of up to RM30,000 (80/20 co-funding) to help Sarawakian Black Soldier Fly bioeconomy entrepreneurs acquire business certifications and accreditations needed for commercial market expansion.
