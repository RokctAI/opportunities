# Grant Opportunity: Project Funding Support for Institutes and Exhibitions (Germany)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Institut français
- **Deadline**: 2026-10-15
- **Funding Amount**: €1,000 to €5,000 per project (maximum 50% of total project budget)
- **Focus Area**: international travel costs, national travel costs, transport costs, insurance, taxes and permits, fees, accommodation costs, daily allowances, technical rental costs, exhibition material costs, technical fees, general technical costs, communication costs, publication costs, administration costs, and other specified eligible expenses

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Germany.
- Eligible applicants include non-profit organizations such as associations, cooperatives, and unions; direct operations of public administrations; freelancers; and other specified forms.
- Project focus: exhibition-related activities and projects.

## Description
The Project Funding for Institutes offers to support eligible institutions by providing financial assistance for approved exhibition-related projects and activities.
