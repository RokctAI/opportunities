# Grant Opportunity: Digitalise Your SME Grant Scheme (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Servizzi Ewropej f'Malta (SEM) / Measures and Support Division Malta
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €120,000 per enterprise (up to 50% co-funding)
- **Focus Area**: Business Digitalisation, Cloud Computing, E-Commerce, IT Infrastructure

## Eligibility
- Micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Malta seeking to adopt digital technology solutions, software, cloud systems, and e-commerce platforms.

## Description
The Digitalise Your SME Grant Scheme provides co-financing to Maltese SMEs to invest in digital technologies, hardware, software licenses, cybersecurity, and e-commerce solutions to modernize operations and enhance productivity.
