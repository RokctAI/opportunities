# Grant Opportunity: Orange Corners Innovation Fund (Benin)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Netherlands Enterprise Agency
- **Deadline**: 2026-06-04
- **Funding Amount**: €2.2 Million (allocated for Benin)
- **Focus Area**: Startup investment, entrepreneurship development

## Eligibility
- Fund managers that invest in early-stage startups led by young entrepreneurs in Benin. Focuses on private sector growth and youth employment.

## Description
The Orange Corners Innovation Fund subsidy programme provides capital to fund managers that invest in early-stage startups led by young entrepreneurs. The initiative is designed to help innovative businesses transform local solutions into practical and scalable business models.
