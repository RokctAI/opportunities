# Grant Opportunity: Funding for Agri-Food SME Value Chain Development (Moldova)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Union (Managed by Solidarity Fund PL in Moldova)
- **Deadline**: 2026-04-06
- **Funding Amount**: Maximum EUR 50,000 per project (Total budget EUR 250,000)
- **Focus Area**: Agri-food value chain, cooperation and association, seed sector, organic farming

## Eligibility
- Associative structures of SMEs in the agri-food sector in Moldova.
- Commercial SME associations and professional or representative SME associations.
- Priority for initiatives in the seed sector or organic farming with at least five economically active members.

## Description
The European Union is seeking grant applications to support the development of sustainable and competitive agri-food SMEs in the Republic of Moldova through strengthened cooperation and value chain initiatives. The call focuses on reducing economic fragmentation, optimizing costs, and supporting market integration and collective functioning.
