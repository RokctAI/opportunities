# Grant Opportunity: KSHITIJ 2.0 – The Blue Horizon Cohort (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) Fisheries Institutes
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Incubation support, ICAR technology transfer guidance, industry connection, and networking support
- **Focus Area**: Fisheries technology, fish farming, sustainable harvest, post-harvest processing, aquaceuticals, and cold chain preservation

## Eligibility
- Fish farmers, early-stage startups, student innovators, MSMEs, FFPOs, and FPCS operating in the blue economy / fisheries sector in India

## Description
KSHITIJ 2.0 is a 2-month virtual incubation programme focused on fisheries innovation, providing selected startups and MSMEs with ICAR technical guidance, technology transfer, and commercial networking across the blue economy.
