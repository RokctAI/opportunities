# Grant Opportunity: Take Your Shot Program Entrepreneurship Program (Canada)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: The Bridge Youth Resource Centre
- **Deadline**: 2026-05-08
- **Funding Amount**: Up to $20,000 (Scholarship/Awards)
- **Focus Area**: Entrepreneurship, mentorship, workshops, marketing, branding, financial planning

## Eligibility
- Entrepreneurs aged 17-29.
- Based in Windsor-Essex County and Chatham-Kent, Canada.
- Individuals who have a business idea or those who want to participate through the scholarship pathway.

## Description
The Take Your Shot program is an entrepreneurship program inviting applications from young entrepreneurs to develop their business ideas with funding and professional mentorship. The program includes mandatory workshops and a final pitch event where participants can present their ideas to a panel of judges.
