## Description
The programme focuses on encouraging the establishment of stronger research management capacities in Widening countries, supporting institutional reforms in research institutions and national research and innovation systems, strengthening human capital through well-trained and entrepreneurial research managers, promoting multidisciplinary and transnational cooperation in research management, fostering knowledge exchange and alignment of best practices, and advancing European Research Area priorities such as research integrity, responsible research and innovation, public engagement, gender equality, science education, open access, and knowledge valorisation. The initiative recognises that strong research management plays a critical role in improving grant acquisition, ensuring efficient resource allocation, maintaining compliance with funding regulations, and enabling effective strategic planning. It also supports high-quality research by streamlining administrative processes, encouraging interdisciplinary collaboration, attracting international researchers, and maximising the use of research infrastructures.
