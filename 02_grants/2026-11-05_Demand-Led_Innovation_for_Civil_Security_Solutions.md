# Grant Opportunity: Demand-Led Innovation for Civil Security Solutions

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Commission
- **Deadline**: 2026-11-05
- **Funding Amount**: €5,830,000
- **Focus Area**: Civil security, Pre-commercial procurement (PCP), R&D services

## Eligibility
- Any legal entity may participate, including those from non-associated third countries or international organisations, provided Horizon Europe eligibility conditions are met. Targets SMEs and startups in the civil security technology sector.

## Description
This initiative aims to support demand-driven innovation in civil security through public procurement of R&D services and the development of innovative technology solutions. It facilitates market access and scaling opportunities for innovative suppliers through pre-commercial procurement (PCP) mechanisms and early validation by first customers.
