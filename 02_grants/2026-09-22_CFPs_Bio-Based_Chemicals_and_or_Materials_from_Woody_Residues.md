# Grant Opportunity: CFPs: Bio-Based Chemicals and/or Materials from Woody Residues

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Unspecified
- **Deadline**: 2026-09-22
- **Funding Amount**: €170,760,699
- **Focus Area**: bio-based chemicals and materials from woody residues

## Eligibility
- any legal entity regardless of country of establishment

## Description
The initiative focuses on increasing the availability of bio-based chemicals and materials derived from woody residues, enhancing the sustainability of forest-based value chains through collaboration with local forest owners and cooperatives, and improving the end-of-life management of bio-based products in alignment with circular economy principles. Projects are also required to adopt a multi-actor approach by collaborating closely with local forest owners, cooperatives, and sector associations. They should optimise biomass supply chains, including sourcing, storage, and transport, and incorporate the Safe and Sustainable by Design framework to assess the environmental and safety aspects of developed materials and products.
