# Grant Opportunity: NourishNext West Africa: Youth Agri-Food Innovation Challenge

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Global Alliance for Improved Nutrition (GAIN), in partnership with the SUN Movement, supported by SDC and ECOWAS
- **Deadline**: 2026-09-08
- **Funding Amount**: USD 5,000 seed funding per selected innovator, plus technical mentorship and capacity-building
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Benin, Cote d'Ivoire, Nigeria, Senegal. Open to young innovators aged 18 to 35 years (upper age limit extended to 40 years for women-led innovations); solutions must be beyond the idea stage with a functional prototype, proof of concept, or MVP
