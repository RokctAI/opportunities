# Grant Opportunity: Open Call for Low Emissions Industry Program in Australia

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Australian Government (Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water)
- **Deadline**: 2026-06-09
- **Funding Amount**: $500,000 to $10 million
- **Focus Area**: Emissions reduction in mining and manufacturing, transition to net zero

## Eligibility
- Australian entities with a valid Australian Business Number (ABN).
- Must operate within the manufacturing or mining sectors (excluding oil and gas extraction).
- Must operate facilities that report emissions under the National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting Scheme.

## Description
The Low Emissions Industry Program supports projects that reduce emissions in mining and manufacturing facilities while strengthening their transition toward a low-carbon future. The program focuses on driving emissions reductions by co-funding projects, future-proofing industrial assets, and maintaining economic resilience.
