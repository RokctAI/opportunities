## Eligibility
- Open to registered companies, businesses, startups, or innovators operating in eligible jurisdictions.

## Description
The programme focuses on catalysing investment into the UK space sector, capturing market growth, raising Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs) of priority space capabilities, positioning UK space activities closer to commercial and scientific exploitation, growing the UK space economy, supporting innovation, increasing commercialisation of UK-developed space products and services, and capturing market share through new products and services for growing space markets. NSIP provides co-funded grants to reduce risks for high-reward research and development projects with clear target markets. The programme operates through two funding strands: NSIP Kick Starter, which supports highly disruptive early-stage innovations, and NSIP Major Projects, which focuses on commercialisation and growth of promising ideas from UK businesses, universities and research organisations.
