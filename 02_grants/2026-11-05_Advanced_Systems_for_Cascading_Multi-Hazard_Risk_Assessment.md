# Grant Opportunity: Advanced Systems for Cascading Multi-Hazard Risk Assessment

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Commission
- **Deadline**: 2026-11-05
- **Funding Amount**: €8,000,000 (Individual projects around €4,000,000)
- **Focus Area**: Multi-hazard risk assessment, predictive modelling, disaster resilience

## Eligibility
- Any legal entity may participate, including those from non-associated third countries or international organisations, provided Horizon Europe eligibility conditions are met. Private sector companies, SMEs, and research institutions are encouraged.

## Description
The European Commission is seeking applications to improve predictive modelling, risk assessment, and resilience planning for cascading and cumulative disaster impacts. The objective is to develop next-generation predictive systems that integrate single-hazard models into a unified framework capable of analysing cascading effects across meteorological, geophysical, and technological hazards.
