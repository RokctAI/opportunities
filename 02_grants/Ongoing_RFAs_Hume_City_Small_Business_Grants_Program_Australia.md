# Grant Opportunity: RFAs: Hume City Small Business Grants Program (Australia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Hume City Council
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: up to $20,000
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Australia. Eligibility criteria require applicants to be based within Hume City, hold a current Australian Business Number (ABN), have 20 or fewer full time equivalent employees (excluding casuals), possess appropriate permits and licences, demonstrate minimum trading periods (two years for established businesses and twelve months for startups), be up to date with council rates or on a payment plan, and have current public liability insurance
