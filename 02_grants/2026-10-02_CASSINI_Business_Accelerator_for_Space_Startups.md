## Quick Stats

## Eligibility
- European space startups and scaleups with proven commercial traction

## Description
The CASSINI Business Accelerator is designed to drive the commercial growth of Europe’s most promising space startups and scaleups by providing a comprehensive six-month acceleration experience. The programme brings together 20 top-performing companies that have already demonstrated product-market fit and commercial traction, supporting them as they expand into new markets and scale their operations. Participants benefit from tailored business and investment coaching, a robust skill and capability development plan, and access to a large network of local markets and stakeholders.
