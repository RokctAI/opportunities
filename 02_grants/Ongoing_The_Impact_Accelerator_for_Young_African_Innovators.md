## Description
The Impact Accelerator, now in its sixth edition, was created to bridge Africa’s opportunity gap by equipping young leaders, innovators, and entrepreneurs with the skills and mentorship needed to launch and scale impact-driven ventures across the continent. Participants engage in a 12-week immersive learning experience that combines expert-led masterclasses, live sessions, case studies, hands-on workshops , mentorship, and collaborative project work. The program is organized into Impact Cohorts aligned with key SDG themes, including health , education, agriculture, climate, gender equity, STEM, AI, policy, civic engagement, and economic empowerment.
