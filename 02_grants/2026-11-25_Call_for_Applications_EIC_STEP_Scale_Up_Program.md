# Grant Opportunity: Call for Applications: EIC STEP Scale Up Program

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Innovation Council (EIC)
- **Deadline**: 2026-11-25
- **Funding Amount**: EUR 10 million - EUR 30 million equity investment
- **Focus Area**: DeepTech, Critical Technologies, Scaleup Funding, Digital & Biotech

## Eligibility
- High-growth European SMEs, startups, and small mid-caps developing critical technologies under the Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform (STEP).

## Description
The EIC STEP Scale Up scheme provides large-scale equity investments to support European deeptech scaleups in accelerating late-stage development and industrial deployment of critical technologies.
