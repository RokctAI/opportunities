# Grant Opportunity: Request for Applications: Feasibility Study Scheme (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Fondi.eu / Measures and Support Division (Malta)
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: €100,000
- **Focus Area**: Health and Well-being, Sustainable Use of Resources for Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation, Smart Manufacturing, Marine & Maritime Technology, Aviation and Aerospace, and Future Digital Technologies

## Eligibility
- open to Micro, Small, Medium-sized, and Small Mid-Cap enterprises engaged in an economic activity.

## Description
The scheme focuses on Health and Well-being, Sustainable Use of Resources for Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation, Smart Manufacturing, Marine & Maritime Technology, Aviation and Aerospace, and Future Digital Technologies. The scheme aims to help enterprises evaluate innovative business ideas through feasibility studies that provide clear conclusions on business viability, identify the resources needed to implement the project, and assess its prospects for success. It also supports investment in research and innovation during the 2021–2027 programming period by fostering innovation, creativity, and entrepreneurship in line with Malta’s Smart Specialisation Strategy (S3) 2021–2027.
