# Grant Opportunity: Call for Residents: Culture Moves Europe 2027

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Culture Moves Europe (Creative Europe programme of the EU, implemented by the Goethe-Institut); residency hosted by IDRA Teatro
- **Deadline**: 2026-09-05
- **Funding Amount**: Travel allowance €400 (under 5,000 km) or €800 (5,000+ km), plus €85/day daily allowance and optional top-ups (green mobility €400, family €200/child, visa €120, OCT/OR €175)
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: The 40 Creative Europe countries (including Albania, Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Iceland, Kosovo, Liechtenstein, Moldova, Montenegro, Norway, Republic of Macedonia, Serbia, Tunisia, Ukraine). Open to artists, collectives, and performing arts companies legally residing in any of the 40 Creative Europe countries, excluding artists based in Italy
