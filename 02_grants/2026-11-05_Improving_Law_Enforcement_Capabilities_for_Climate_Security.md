# Grant Opportunity: Improving Law Enforcement Capabilities for Climate Security

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Commission
- **Deadline**: 2026-11-05
- **Funding Amount**: €8,000,000 (Individual projects around €4,000,000)
- **Focus Area**: Climate security, law enforcement technology, forensics

## Eligibility
- Any legal entity may participate, including those from non-associated third countries or international organisations, provided Horizon Europe eligibility conditions are met. Requires involvement of Police Authorities in Europe.

## Description
The European Commission is seeking grant applications to support research and innovation aimed at strengthening law enforcement capabilities to respond to emerging security threats and criminal activities linked to climate change, such as environmental crimes, illegal mining, and greenwashing fraud.
