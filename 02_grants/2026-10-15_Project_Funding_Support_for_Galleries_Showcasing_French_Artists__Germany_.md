# Grant Opportunity: Project Funding Support for Galleries Showcasing French Artists (Germany)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Office for Visual Arts / Institut français
- **Deadline**: 2026-10-15
- **Funding Amount**: €1,000 to €2,000 per grant (maximum 50% of total project budget)
- **Focus Area**: supporting exhibitions featuring contemporary visual artists, promoting the French art scene in Germany, strengthening collaborations between galleries and artists, and supporting monographic exhibitions by emerging artists

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Germany (galleries must be based there).
- German-based galleries with legal company/organization status, full-time managed, working with contemporary visual artists and regularly organizing exhibitions in physical spaces or at fairs.
- Exhibitions must feature artists from France or France-based artists; first and second exhibitions only (artists already in the gallery's regular program are not eligible).

## Description
The Project Funding for Galleries supports German galleries presenting exhibitions by artists from France or artists based in France, with a focus on strengthening artistic partnerships.
