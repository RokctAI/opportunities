# Grant Opportunity: NEB Academy | Skills Infrastructure Programme

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: EIT Culture & Creativity (Horizon Europe)
- **Deadline**: 2026-08-31
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €1,500,000 per project
- **Focus Area**: Skills Infrastructure, Education & Training, New European Bauhaus

## Eligibility
- Legal entities registered in Horizon Europe-eligible countries, including education providers, public bodies, and creative SMEs.

## Description
The NEB Academy | Skills Infrastructure call supports the development of lifelong learning programmes that help transform the lived environment through creative and cross-sectoral approaches. The call focuses on developing CPD, VET, and other lifelong learning formats, embedding New European Bauhaus values of sustainability, inclusivity, and aesthetics into learning design and delivery, addressing skills gaps in the building and lived environment ecosystem, supporting education and training providers, public bodies, decision-makers, civil society, and creative practitioners, building scalable and financially sustainable regional models, including creative, artistic, and design-led approaches in learning activities, defining clear learning outcomes and assessment methods, supporting regional transformation, future-proofing CCSI students through entrepreneurial and cross-disciplinary skills, deploying and scaling CCSI innovations, and accelerating and scaling CCSI market opportunities. is launched by EIT Culture & Creativity to support projects that create learning programmes for professionals, public bodies, education and training providers, and other relevant actors involved in the transformation of the lived environment.
