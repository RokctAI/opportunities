# Grant Opportunity: CFPs: Regional Tariff Response Initiative in British Columbia (Canada)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Government of Canada
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: funding of up to $10 million
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Canada. Eligible applicants include incorporated for-profit businesses operating in British Columbia with 10 to 499 full-time employees, at least three years of continuous and viable operations prior to March 21, 2025, and a minimum of two years of externally prepared or reviewed financial statements
