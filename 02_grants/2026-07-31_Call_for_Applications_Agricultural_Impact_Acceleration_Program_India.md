# Grant Opportunity: Call for Applications: Agricultural Impact Acceleration Program (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: riidl (Somaiya Vidyavihar University) & OmniActive
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Up to ₹10 Lakhs non-dilutive grant funding per selected startup
- **Focus Area**: Agritech innovation, sustainable agriculture, farm productivity, climate resilience, and farmer livelihoods

## Eligibility
- Open to Agritech startups registered anywhere in India developing innovative agricultural solutions across all growth stages

## Description
SoilBox Cohort 4 is an Agritech accelerator hosted by riidl at Somaiya Vidyavihar University, funded by OmniActive and the Improving Lives Foundation. Selected startups receive up to ₹10 Lakhs in non-dilutive grants, industry mentorship, and ecosystem access.
