# Grant Opportunity: Apply for Standards and Awards for SMEs Scheme (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: FONDI.eu / Government of Malta
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Maximum grant value up to €15,000 (or €20,000 for multi-standard applications) from an allocated €500,000 budget
- **Focus Area**: Quality management, international certifications, industry standards compliance, excellence awards

## Eligibility
- Micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) actively trading for at least one year in Malta

## Description
The Standards and Awards for SMEs Scheme provides financial support for Maltese SMEs to obtain external advisory services needed to achieve internationally recognized quality standards and certifications.
