# Grant Opportunity: Apply Now: AT&T Small Business Contest

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: AT&T Services, Inc. (She's Connected)
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Grand Prize of $50,000 grant plus 1-year AT&T connectivity; 4 Runner-Up grants of $5,000 each
- **Focus Area**: Small business growth, connectivity, community impact, and marketing amplification

## Eligibility
- Majority owners of small businesses in the 50 US states & DC with 1-5 locations and 99 or fewer employees

## Description
AT&T She's Connected Small Business Contest awards a $50,000 grand prize grant, free connectivity service, device, and nationwide storytelling amplification to a small business owner making local community impact.
