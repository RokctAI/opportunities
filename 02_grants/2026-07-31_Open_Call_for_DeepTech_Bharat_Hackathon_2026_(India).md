## Eligibility

## Description
The hackathon focuses on AI/ML, IoT, smart sensors and industrial automation, robotics, advanced manufacturing and Industry 4. 0, electric mobility, clean energy, sustainability and circular economy, AgriTech, smart cities and smart manufacturing, and defence and aerospace. The initiative is organised by Nashik Engineering Cluster’s Startup Incubation Centre in collaboration with Startup India, DPIIT, and aims to promote deeptech-driven innovation among students, innovators, startups, researchers, engineers, MSMEs, industries and ecosystem stakeholders.
