- Open to registered companies, businesses, startups, or innovators operating in eligible jurisdictions.

## Description
The program focuses on supporting startups in transitioning from prototype development to refined and market-ready solutions, providing curated mentoring and training workshops, validating and strengthening startup initiatives for future incubation, advancing the development of Minimum Viable Products (MVPs), and preparing participants for the next level of incubation support. The Exploration Program is designed for early-stage entrepreneurs and innovators who are at the prototyping or product development stage of their startup journey. It aims to help participants refine their innovations and prepare them for successful entry into the market.
