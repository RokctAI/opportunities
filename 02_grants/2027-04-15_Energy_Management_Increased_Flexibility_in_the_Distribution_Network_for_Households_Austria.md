# Grant Opportunity: Energy Management – Increased Flexibility in the Distribution Network for Households (Austria)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Climate and Energy Fund (Klimafonds Austria)
- **Deadline**: 2027-04-15
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €4,900,000 total (€2.45M for private households & small scale distribution)
- **Focus Area**: Energy Management, Smart Grids, Renewable Energy, Flexibility

## Eligibility
- Private households, small enterprises, and property management companies in Austria.

## Description
The Energy Management – Increased Flexibility in the Distribution Network for Households program supports the rollout of communication-enabled and automated energy management systems to improve energy efficiency, increase the use of renewable energy, reduce costs for households, and enhance the efficient use of electricity networks. The program focuses on the rollout of energy management systems, automated and communication-based control of electricity consumption, storage and generation facilities, energy efficiency, improved use of renewable energy, positive environmental and cost effects for individual users, increased flexibility in the distribution network, improved utilization of public infrastructure, transparent energy flows, increased energy literacy and energy awareness, automated use of dynamic electricity and grid tariffs, flexibility marketing, power limitation, reduced peak demand and feed-in, improved network capacity utilization, better integration of electrified heating and mobility, and enhanced communication and automation across the energy system.
