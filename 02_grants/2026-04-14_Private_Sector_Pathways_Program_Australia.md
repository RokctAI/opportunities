# Grant Opportunity: Applications open for Private Sector Pathways Program (Australia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Australian Government (Queensland)
- **Deadline**: 2026-04-14
- **Funding Amount**: Up to $100,000
- **Focus Area**: Commercialisation opportunities, SME-Corporate partnerships, advanced manufacturing, hydrogen, biomedical, aerospace

## Eligibility
- Queensland-based startups and SMEs with fewer than 200 full-time equivalent employees.
- Must have a minimal viable product being developed locally.
- Must hold an ABN registered in Queensland and be registered for GST.
- Must not have had previous commercial contracts with the participating corporate partners.

## Description
The Private Sector Pathways Program is inviting applications to support innovative small-to-medium enterprises in Queensland to collaborate with corporate partners and develop commercialisation opportunities. The program focuses on increasing partnerships between SMEs and corporate businesses or large organisations to solve problems and trial technologies.
