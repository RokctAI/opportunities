# Grant Opportunity: NEB Academy | Fashion Shift Programme

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: EIT Culture & Creativity (Horizon Europe)
- **Deadline**: 2026-08-31
- **Funding Amount**: Up to €2,500,000 per project
- **Focus Area**: Fashion & Textiles, Circular Economy, New European Bauhaus

## Eligibility
- Consortia including independent fashion SMEs, research institutions, and value-chain partners in Horizon Europe eligible countries.

## Description
The NEB Academy | Fashion Shift call supports collaborations that develop and scale circular, regenerative, and responsible fashion business models based on New European Bauhaus values. The call focuses on circular, regenerative, and responsible fashion business models, sustainability, inclusion, beauty, market deployment and scaling, fashion value chain collaboration, double fit with New European Bauhaus and EIT values, multi-actor engagement, learning and capacity-building, replication potential, deploying and scaling CCSI innovations, accelerating and scaling CCSI market opportunities, and future-proofing CCSI students through entrepreneurial and cross-disciplinary skills for green and digital transitions.
