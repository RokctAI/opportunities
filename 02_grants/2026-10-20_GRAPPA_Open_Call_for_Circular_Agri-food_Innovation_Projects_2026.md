# Grant Opportunity: GRAPPA Open Call for Circular Agri-food Innovation Projects 2026

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Ingredients for the Bioeconomy
- **Deadline**: 2026-10-20
- **Funding Amount**: €2,020,000 total budget; up to €60,000 for single SME projects, up to €90,000 for collaborative projects (up to 3 SMEs)
