# Grant Opportunity: Business Plan Competition

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: ROGEAP (Regional Off-Grid Electrification Project)
- **Deadline**: 2026-03-21
- **Funding Amount**: $5,000 first prize, five $2,500 awards, and a $4,000 equalisation award for the best woman-led enterprise.
- **Focus Area**: Off-Grid Solar Sector, Entrepreneurship, Sustainable Energy

## Eligibility
- Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in the off-grid solar sector operating within the 19 countries of implementation: Benin, Cape Verde, Burkina Faso, Côte d’Ivoire, The Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Niger, Nigeria, Senegal, Sierra Leone, and Togo. Focuses on young solar energy startups.

## Description
Applicants are now invited to submit applications for small and medium enterprises in the off-grid solar sector to participate in an innovation competition aimed at strengthening entrepreneurship and expanding access to sustainable energy solutions. The competition focuses on stimulating innovation within the off-grid electrification sector’s SMEs, facilitating access to financial and technical support schemes, and promoting sustainable and gender-sensitive enterprises.
