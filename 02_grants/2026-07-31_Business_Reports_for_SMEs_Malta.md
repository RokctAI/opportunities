# Grant Opportunity: Apply Now: Business Reports for SMEs (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: FONDI.eu / Government of Malta
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Co-financing under Policy Objective 1 of SFC2021 Programme 2021–2027 (Initial budget: €500,000)
- **Focus Area**: Business plans, feasibility studies, process optimization, digital transformation, and internationalisation strategies

## Eligibility
- Maltese micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs/SMEs) engaged in economic activity

## Description
The Business Reports for SMEs scheme supports Maltese micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises in acquiring external advisory services to prepare business reports and strategic studies.
