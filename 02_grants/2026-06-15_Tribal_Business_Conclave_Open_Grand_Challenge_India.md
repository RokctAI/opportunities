# Grant Opportunity: Tribal Business Conclave Open Grand Challenge (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Startup India
- **Deadline**: 2026-06-15
- **Funding Amount**: Rs. 5 Lakhs (1st), Rs. 4 Lakhs (2nd), Rs. 3 Lakhs (3rd)
- **Focus Area**: Healthcare, education, livelihoods for tribal communities

## Eligibility
- All DPIIT-recognised startups working on innovative solutions aligned with healthcare, education, and livelihoods for tribal populations in India.

## Description
The Tribal Business Conclave Open Grand Challenge aims to support innovative startups developing scalable solutions to improve the quality of life for tribal communities. Winning startups receive financial incentives and structured hand-holding support.
