# Grant Opportunity: Ignite Program – Stream A Planning in Australia

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Department of Industry, Tourism and Trade (Northern Territory Government)
- **Deadline**: 2027-06-30
- **Funding Amount**: Up to 5,000 matching grant
- **Focus Area**: Business Innovation, Planning, Product Development, Mentoring

## Eligibility
- Small to medium enterprises (SMEs) and registered businesses operating in Northern Territory, Australia.

## Description
The Ignite Program – Stream A Planning provides funding and specialist mentoring to support Northern Territory businesses in investigating and developing innovative ideas for new businesses, products, or services. The Ignite Program is a Northern Territory Government initiative designed to strengthen the region’s innovation ecosystem by supporting the development and commercialisation of innovative products and services. The program aims to increase investment in local start-ups and contribute to economic growth and industry Stream A – Planning specifically assists eligible applicants in investigating, validating, and developing their innovation ideas through access to specialist mentoring and planning
