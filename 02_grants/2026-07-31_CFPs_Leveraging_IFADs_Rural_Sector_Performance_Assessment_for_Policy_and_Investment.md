# Grant Opportunity: CFPs: Leveraging IFAD’s Rural Sector Performance Assessment for Policy and Investment

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD)
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Up to US$1.2 million
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Selected recipient may be a non-profit organization, private sector company, university, think tank, or research institute
- Applicants must provide a counterpart contribution of at least 20% of the IFAD grant amount (private sector institutions at least 25%); in-kind contributions are acceptable
- The selected organization will produce assessments for approximately 80 countries
