- **Deadline**: 2026-08-10
- **Funding Amount**: Mentorship, Acceleration and Seed Grants
- **Focus Area**: Cleantech / Water, Renewable Energy & Agriculture

## Eligibility
- Open to registered companies, startups, SMEs, and private sector ventures.

## Description
Deadline Date: August 10, 2026 The Cleantech Innovation Competition is a joint initiative by the Kenya Climate Innovation Center (KCIC), the Embassy of Switzerland in Kenya, SICPA, and Strathmore University that seeks to support early-stage businesses in the cleantech sector by enabling them to develop and scale solutions addressing pressing environmental challenges. The competition focuses on water, renewable energy and energy efficiency, agriculture, commercial forestry, and waste management. ...
