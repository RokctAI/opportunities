# Grant Opportunity: RFPs: Marketing Strategy for Micro and Small Enterprises (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: FONDI.eu / Government of Malta
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Maximum grant value up to €10,000 from an initial budget allocation of €500,000
- **Focus Area**: Marketing strategy development, market positioning, branding, customer acquisition, promotional planning

## Eligibility
- Micro and small enterprises (MSEs) engaged in economic activity in Malta

## Description
The Marketing Strategy for Micro and Small Enterprises scheme provides non-repayable grants to help small Maltese businesses contract expert advisory services for comprehensive marketing strategies.
