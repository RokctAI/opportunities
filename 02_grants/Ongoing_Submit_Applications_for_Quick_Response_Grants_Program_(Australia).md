# Grant Opportunity: Submit Applications for Quick Response Grants Program (Australia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Funds for Companies Partner Network
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: $5,000
- **Focus Area**: Innovation, Technology, Business Growth

## Eligibility
- Eligible applicants must operate within the City of Sydney local government area or demonstrate significant benefits for the area’s residents, workers and visitors. Applicants may include eligible not-for-profit organisations, incorporated associations, sole traders, individuals without an ABN, and unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible not-for-profit organisation. For-profit entities are also eligible to apply under this program.

## Description
