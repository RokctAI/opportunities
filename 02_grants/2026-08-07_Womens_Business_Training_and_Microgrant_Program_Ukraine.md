# Grant Opportunity: Women’s Business Training and Microgrant Program (Ukraine)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: SILab Ukraine
- **Deadline**: 2026-08-07
- **Funding Amount**: Free 4-day intensive business training (covering travel, accommodation, meals) plus microgrant funding opportunities
- **Focus Area**: Women entrepreneurship, business planning, MVP development, financial modeling, and microgrant application

## Eligibility
- Women living in the Transcarpathian region of Ukraine, including internally displaced women, military/veteran family members, low-income/single mothers, and women with a microbusiness idea

## Description
SILab Ukraine is inviting women in the Transcarpathian region to apply for the Starting Point 4-day intensive business training program in Uzhhorod, offering hands-on business modeling, mentorship, and microgrant funding pathways.
