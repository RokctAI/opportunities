# Grant Opportunity: Portishead Economic Development Fund for Local Business Growth (UK)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: North Somerset Council
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: Up to £315,000 total pool
- **Focus Area**: Local Economic Growth, Business Expansion, Infrastructure Investment

## Eligibility
- Commercial businesses, startups, and local enterprises based in or expanding within Portishead and Portbury, United Kingdom.

## Description
The Portishead Economic Development Fund offers business grants to local enterprises seeking to expand operations, improve commercial property infrastructure, and create sustainable employment.
