# Grant Opportunity: Agricultural Commercialization Innovation Fund Matching Grants Programme (Malawi)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: TRADE Programme & Ministry of Agriculture Malawi
- **Deadline**: Ongoing
- **Funding Amount**: Up to USD 100,000
- **Focus Area**: Agribusiness, Agri-Tech, Commercial Agriculture, Supply Chain

## Eligibility
- Small and medium agricultural enterprises, agribusiness cooperatives, and youth/women-owned commercial agriculture businesses in Malawi.

## Description
The Innovation Fund Matching Grants Programme aims to commercialize smallholder agriculture by providing matching grants to commercial agribusinesses to upgrade processing infrastructure and strengthen agricultural supply chains.
