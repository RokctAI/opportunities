# Grant Opportunity: AI Innovation Challenge to Transform Water Systems

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: The Water Council
- **Deadline**: 2026-04-03
- **Funding Amount**: Not specified (Innovation Challenge)
- **Focus Area**: AI, Water Systems, Cognitive Water Ecosystem, Resource Stewardship

## Eligibility
- Innovators developing modular technologies integrating into a cognitive water ecosystem using AI. Solutions should be at or above Technology Readiness Level 3 (functional prototype/lab-validated). Relevant to private tech companies/startups in the water sector.

## Description
The Water Council is seeking innovative AI-native technologies that can strengthen how water systems are sensed, treated, moved, recovered, and managed to build a more reliable and sustainable water ecosystem. The challenge focuses on developing AI-native components for a cognitive water ecosystem, advancing molecular intelligence, enabling autonomous infrastructure, and improving resource stewardship.
