# Grant Opportunity: INFO Grants to Support Innovative Companies with Technological Potential and Scalability

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Institute for the Promotion of the Region of Murcia
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-25
- **Funding Amount**: Maximum €80,000 per beneficiary in each call for proposals
- **Focus Area**: General

## Eligibility
- Eligible Countries: Spain (specifically the Region of Murcia)
- Innovative companies with technological potential and/or scalability established no more than four years before submitting the application
- Activities must be conducted within the Region of Murcia through a workplace located there
- Company activity must fall within the region's Smart Specialisation Strategy priority areas
- Eligible themes: product/process innovation, productive investment, digitalization, product/service promotion, internationalization projects
