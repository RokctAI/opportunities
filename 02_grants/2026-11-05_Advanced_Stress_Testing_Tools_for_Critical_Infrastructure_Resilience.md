# Grant Opportunity: Advanced Stress Testing Tools for Critical Infrastructure Resilience

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: European Commission
- **Deadline**: 2026-11-05
- **Funding Amount**: €9,670,000 (Individual projects around €4,835,000)
- **Focus Area**: Critical infrastructure, stress testing, simulation tools

## Eligibility
- Any legal entity may participate, including those from non-associated third countries or international organisations, provided Horizon Europe eligibility requirements are met. Specifically targets operators and authorities of critical asset infrastructure.

## Description
The European Commission is inviting grant applications to strengthen stress testing of critical infrastructure systems, improving their resilience to a wide range of threats and disruptions. The programme aims to provide tools and processes that allow operators and authorities to perform virtual and physical stress tests of critical assets.
