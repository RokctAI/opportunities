## Eligibility
- The Forest Sector Investment and Innovation Program is inviting grant applications to support investment and innovation projects that enhance productivity, competitiveness, and market growth within Ontario’s forest sector. Funding support of up to 30 percent of eligible project costs is available in the form of grants, with a maximum funding amount of $3 million.
