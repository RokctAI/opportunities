# Grant Opportunity: TribePreneurs Idea Quest for Tribal Startups (India)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Startup India
- **Deadline**: 2026-06-15
- **Funding Amount**: Rs. 7 Lakhs (1st), Rs. 5 Lakhs (2nd), Rs. 4 Lakhs (3rd)
- **Focus Area**: Health, nutrition, education, AgriTech, Technology/AI

## Eligibility
- Startups with 51 percent or more shareholding by Scheduled Tribes entrepreneurs in India. Must be focusing on innovative solutions for economic development.

## Description
The TribePreneurs Idea Quest is an initiative aimed at building a supportive entrepreneurial ecosystem for Tribal-led startups through structured support and innovation opportunities across India.
