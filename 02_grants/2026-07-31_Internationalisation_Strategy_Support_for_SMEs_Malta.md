# Grant Opportunity: CFAs: Internationalisation Strategy Support for SMEs (Malta)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: FONDI.eu / Government of Malta
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Maximum grant value up to €20,000 per undertaking from an allocated €500,000 budget
- **Focus Area**: Internationalisation, export readiness, overseas market entry, global feasibility analysis

## Eligibility
- Micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) registered and trading in Malta

## Description
The Internationalisation Strategy Support for SMEs scheme provides grant support to Maltese SMEs to develop detailed market entry and export expansion strategies for global markets.
