## Description
The program is designed for domain experts building value-generating technologies in any industry, with a particular emphasis on robotics, energy, applied and physical AI, and materials science, while remaining open to innovation across sectors. Participants engage in a three-month, mentorship-driven accelerator experience, combining remote programming with three in-person offsites in major startup hubs to foster community and connection. Eligible applicants are early-stage startups with founders based in or able to operate within American time zones, who are developing innovative solutions and seeking to accelerate their business growth.
