# Grant Opportunity: Industry Driven Research and Development Programme 2026 (Sri Lanka)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: National Science Foundation (NSF) Sri Lanka
- **Deadline**: 2026-07-31
- **Funding Amount**: Matching grant / R&D funding support for industry-academic joint projects
- **Focus Area**: Industry-driven R&D, technology development, commercialization, innovation partnerships

## Eligibility
- Sri Lankan companies, industrial enterprises, startups, and collaborative academic/research partners

## Description
The Industry Driven Research and Development Programme 2026 invites expressions of interest from companies and researchers to foster industry-driven innovation and technology commercialization in Sri Lanka.
