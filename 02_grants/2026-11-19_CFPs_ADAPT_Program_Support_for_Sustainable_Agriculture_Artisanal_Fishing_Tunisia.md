# Grant Opportunity: CFPs: ADAPT Program Support for Sustainable Agriculture & Artisanal Fishing (Tunisia)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Italian Agency for Development Cooperation (AICS)
- **Deadline**: 2026-11-19
- **Funding Amount**: EUR 8,000,000 total pool
- **Focus Area**: Sustainable Agriculture, Fisheries, Circular Economy, Agro-Industry

## Eligibility
- Private micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs), agricultural partnerships, and fisheries cooperatives operating in Tunisia.

## Description
The ADAPT Program supports private enterprises and cooperatives in Tunisia to invest in eco-friendly agricultural techniques, sustainable aquaculture, and climate-resilient food production systems.
