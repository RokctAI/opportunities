# Grant Opportunity: iHatch Cohort 5 Startups Incubation Programme (Nigeria)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: iHatch (ONDI)
- **Deadline**: 2026-05-27
- **Funding Amount**: Training, mentorship, and access to funding/resources
- **Focus Area**: Fintech, agritech, healthtech, edtech, cleantech

## Eligibility
- Open to Nigerian early-stage startups, tech-enabled founders, and problem-driven innovators. Must be ready to execute solutions with strong local relevance.

## Description
iHatch is a nationwide incubation programme aimed at strengthening innovation hubs and supporting early-stage startups across Nigeria. The 10-month programme consists of five months of hub training and five months of startup incubation, including MVP development and legal incorporation.
