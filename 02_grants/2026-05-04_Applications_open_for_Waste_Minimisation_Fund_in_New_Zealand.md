# Grant Opportunity: Waste Minimisation Fund in New Zealand

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: Whangārei District Council
- **Deadline**: 2026-05-04
- **Funding Amount**: Standard grants ranging from $1,000 to $5,000 and special project grants from $5,000 to $30,000
- **Focus Area**: Waste Minimisation, Resource Efficiency, Community Participation

## Eligibility
- Eligible organisations must operate for not-for-profit or charitable purposes. Although it mainly targets not-for-profits, the fund "fosters business-community partnerships" which opens eligibility to private sector companies working in collaboration.

## Description
The Waste Minimisation Fund is seeking applications to support innovative projects that reduce waste, improve resource efficiency, and encourage community participation, education, and long-term behaviour change across the Whangārei District. The fund focuses on complementing and enhancing existing waste minimisation programmes and creating new opportunities for reducing waste.
