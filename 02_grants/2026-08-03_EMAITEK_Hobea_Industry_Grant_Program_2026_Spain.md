# Grant Opportunity: EMAITEK Hobea Industry Grant Program 2026 (Spain)

## Quick Stats
- **Organization**: SPRI (Business Development Agency of the Basque Country)
- **Deadline**: 2026-08-03
- **Funding Amount**: €62.1 million total funding (up to 100% subsidy of eligible non-economic activity costs)
- **Focus Area**: Industrial competitiveness, strategic technology development, technological leadership, and knowledge transfer to Basque businesses

## Eligibility
- Multi-Focused Technology Centers that are part of the Basque Science, Technology and Innovation Network (RVCTI)

## Description
SPRI and the Basque Government have launched EMAITEK Industria Hobea 2026 with a budget of €62.1 million to strengthen the non-economic R&D activities of Multi-Focused Technology Centers, driving strategic technologies and transferring innovation to the Basque industrial sector.
